Electrical installation measurement protocol for the building authority

When a building is accepted into use, point 53 of LBN 261-15 requires a protocol with documents to be attached to the acceptance statement, if the relevant works have been carried out. These are corrected drawings, equipment passports, concealed works records, measurement reports, switching diagrams, an operating description and training records. The electrician’s measurements are in sub-point 53.4, and they are carried out by a certified electrical specialist (point 55).

Point 54 requires insulation resistance to be tested before acceptance. If a diagram is also needed, it is separate work: as-built diagram.

Electrical installation test report for the periodic inspection

In the fire safety regulations currently in force (Cabinet Regulation No. 238), the report form is given in Annex 6. The form has fields for each type of measurement, the instrument data and its calibration certificate number. The report is signed by the person who did the work. Regulation No. 238 does not name a construction specialist’s certificate for them. However, the report in Annex 6 comes with a copy of the electrical safety group card of the person doing the work, a copy of the instrument’s calibration or verification certificate and the electrical installation diagram (points 1.1–1.3 of the notes to the annex).

Together with it, the Annex 7 report on the thermal camera inspection of contact connections is prepared. A measurement report drawn up after 19 April 2016 is valid until the next inspection (Regulation No. 238, point 525), so keep both reports.

The confirmation for Sadales tīkls is not a measurement protocol

For a new connection or a capacity increase, Sadales tīkls requires a confirmation on e-st.lv that the customer’s electrical installation has been built and is ready. Point 99 of Cabinet Regulation No. 635 calls it a written confirmation that the electrical installation is ready to be energised. That is a different document from a measurement protocol. If Sadales tīkls asks for an attachment in your case, ask which one exactly. More: confirmation for Sadales tīkls.

An electrician’s test report: what it should show

Whatever the name, check that the document contains these things. Without them the report is hard to use at the building authority or at the next inspection.

  • the property address and the date of the test;
  • which distribution boards and circuits were tested;
  • the type of each measurement, the measured value and the comparison with the standard;
  • the measuring instrument and its calibration certificate number (in Annex 6 of Regulation No. 238 this is a field on the form);
  • the name and signature of the person who did the work, and for building acceptance also the certificate data.

Construction practice certificates are checked in the BIS register of construction specialists, while BLEEA and LEB certificates are checked using the issuer’s data and the four Sadales tīkls criteria. How to do this is explained on the page how to check an electrician’s certificate.

What to prepare so that the electrician can draw up the report

  • Who is asking for the document and in what form: building authority, property manager, tenant, buyer.
  • The existing diagram or design, earlier reports.
  • Access to all distribution boards and rooms.
  • If another electrician installed the wiring, the papers they handed over.

The documents are prepared and signed by a certified electrical specialist after a site visit and measurements. We do not sign a report without testing on site.

Questions about these documents

Are a report and a protocol the same thing?

The content may be similar, but the regulation determines the name. Regulation No. 238 uses “test report”, while LBN 261-15 speaks of a protocol with documents and “approved reports”.

Does Sadales tīkls require a measurement protocol for a new connection?

Sadales tīkls requires a confirmation on e-st.lv that the installation is ready. If you are asked for an additional attachment, ask Sadales tīkls which one and write it in the form.

Can you draw up a report with an earlier date?

No. The report records the measurements taken during the site visit and test, with that day.
