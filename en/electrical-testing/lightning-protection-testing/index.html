The fire safety regulations currently in force (Cabinet Regulation No. 238) count lightning protection devices as part of the electrical installation, which is inspected once every 10 years. These regulations are in force until 1 January 2027.

What the regulations say about testing a lightning protection system

Point 54 of Regulation No. 238 requires the electrical installation, including the earthing and lightning protection device, to comply with the construction intention documentation and to be kept in working order. Point 56 sets an inspection once every 10 years, once every two years in an explosive environment and once a year in a chemically aggressive environment.

Point 57 lists what has to be recorded in the test report (Annex 6): insulation resistance, total impedance of the phase-neutral loop, earthing installation resistance, earthing conductor continuity and the results of the lightning protection system test. So lightning protection is not a separate document but part of one report.

The Annex 6 form also has fields for the measuring instrument data and its calibration certificate number, and the report is signed by the person who did the work. Keep the report until the next inspection, because it shows when the inspection was carried out and what the results were.

Checked on 25 November 2026: Cabinet Regulation No. 238 of 19 April 2016 “Fire Safety Regulations”, points 56–58, likumi.lv/ta/id/281646. The regulations are in force until 1 January 2027.

When lightning protection measurements are relevant

The test applies to buildings that have a lightning protection system. Whether a building must have one is decided by the building design, not the periodic inspection. The test is ordered by the building owner or property manager: for an apartment building, a production or warehouse building, an office or a private house that has a system built.

If the property has an explosive or chemically aggressive environment, Regulation No. 238 sets shorter intervals. More about this for businesses: electrical installation testing for businesses.

How lightning protection testing goes

  1. Site visit The electrician looks at where the system’s down conductors reach the ground and where connections are accessible for measurement.
  2. Testing The earth electrode resistance and the continuity of the connections are measured.
  3. Report The results are recorded in the test report together with the electrical installation measurements.

In the report in Annex 6 of Regulation No. 238, the resistance of the lightning protection system is measured from the air terminal to the test joint at the earthing installation. A damaged down conductor or earth electrode is repaired by a company that does such work, and then the measurement is repeated.

Lightning protection and earthing in one site visit

A lightning protection system carries current into the ground through an earth electrode, so its measurements are close to the building’s earthing measurements. If the whole building needs a periodic inspection, it is more convenient to establish the scope of both the electrical installation and the lightning protection in one site visit. Then one Annex 6 report covers everything point 57 requires, and the results of two inspections do not have to be reconciled.

Together with the periodic inspection, point 58 also requires a thermal camera inspection of the contacts in distribution boards. It can be ordered on the same occasion, and a separate Annex 7 report is prepared for it.

What changes after 1 January 2027

Regulation No. 238 is repealed on 2 January 2027. The text of the new fire safety regulations had not yet been published on 25 November 2026, so the 10-year interval should not be treated as permanent. If you are planning an inspection in 2027, check the new regulations on likumi.lv and write in the form who requires the report and in what form.

What to prepare

  • The building design or lightning protection diagram, if it has survived.
  • The previous test report, so that it can be seen when the inspection was last carried out.
  • Access to the places where the down conductors reach the ground, including behind a fence or in locked rooms.

Questions: Lightning protection testing

Does lightning protection need a separate report?

Under point 57 of Regulation No. 238, the lightning protection system results are in the same test report (Annex 6) as insulation, loop and earthing.

How often must lightning protection be tested?

The fire safety regulations currently in force (Cabinet Regulation No. 238) set once every 10 years, once every two years in an explosive environment and once a year in a chemically aggressive environment. Regulation No. 238 is in force until 1 January 2027.

Can lightning protection testing be combined with electrical installation testing?

Yes. Point 57 of Regulation No. 238 provides for both results in one test report, so they can be ordered together.
