Electrical installation testing in an apartment building

Electrical installation testing in an apartment building covers the shared part of the building: the incoming distribution board, floor and stairwell boards, common-area lighting, the cellar, earthing and lightning protection, if there is any.

Stairwell distribution board testing

If the stairwell boards hold the flat meters and the protection of the flat supplies, the load of the whole floor flows through them. A thermal camera inspection is particularly useful here, because a loose connection heats up under load. That is why a time when the flats are drawing load is chosen for the thermal camera inspection; we arrange it after the site visit.

The meters and seals belong to Sadales tīkls. Sealed parts are not opened without permission from Sadales tīkls.

Electrical installation testing for a property manager: deadlines and documents

The fire safety regulations currently in force (Cabinet Regulation No. 238) set an inspection once every 10 years regardless of the form of ownership of the property. The regulations are in force until 1 January 2027, and the text of the new regulations had not yet been published on 25 November 2026. A property manager does well to keep both reports with the building documents, so that next time it can be seen what has been tested.

If a flat owner wants to increase capacity, Sadales tīkls requires the property manager’s written consent. That is a moment when a property manager does well to know the condition of the incoming and stairwell distribution boards.

After renovation or electrical installation alterations

If the risers, stairwell boards or incoming supply in the building have been replaced, it is no longer just a periodic inspection. Point 52 of LBN 261-15 requires testing before putting into use and after significant changes, and point 53 sets the documents attached to the acceptance statement. More about this: alteration and renovation.

How the testing goes

  1. Request The building address, number of stairwells and floors, whether there is lightning protection, the year of the previous report.
  2. Site visit A visit to the property costs €40. The electrician looks at the incoming and stairwell distribution boards.
  3. Notice to residents For some measurements the common-area circuits are switched off. The property manager warns residents in advance.
  4. Measurements and reports After the measurements, the reports in Annexes 6 and 7 are prepared. An electrician puts the shortcomings right, for example electrical installation repairs in Riga.

What to prepare

  • Keys to the incoming distribution board room, stairwell boards, cellar and loft.
  • The building’s electrical installation diagram or design, if it has survived.
  • Earlier test reports.
  • A contact person who can accompany the electrician around the building.
  • A list of common equipment that must not be switched off without warning, for example the lift, heating substation or door system.

Flat owners can order electrical installation testing in a flat separately for their own flat.

Questions about these documents

Can the testing be done stairwell by stairwell instead of the whole building in one day?

Yes, in a large building the work can be split by stairwell or distribution board, so that the common-area circuits are switched off for a shorter time. We agree the order after the site visit, and the reports cover the whole building.

Does the building test also cover the flats?

The building test covers the shared part. An owner can order testing of the flat’s internal installation separately.

Will you open the meter seals in the stairwell boards?

No. The meters and seals belong to Sadales tīkls, and sealed parts are not opened without its permission.

How often must the electrical installation of an apartment building be tested?

The fire safety regulations currently in force (Cabinet Regulation No. 238) set once every 10 years. The regulations are in force until 1 January 2027.

Who orders testing in an apartment building?

The property manager or the flat owners’ association that manages the building. State the client in the form.
