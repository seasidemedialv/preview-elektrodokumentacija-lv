Insulation resistance measurements

Point 54 of LBN 261-15 requires this test before electrical installations are accepted into use, and it is also part of the periodic inspection under the fire safety regulations. The result is compared with the minimum value given in the regulations.

When insulation resistance measurements of the electrical installation are required

The building standard names this measurement twice. Point 52 requires electrical equipment to be tested before putting into use and after significant changes, while point 54 specifically requires an insulation resistance test before acceptance. So the measurement is needed for a new house, an altered installation and a flat after complete rewiring.

The fire safety regulations currently in force (Cabinet Regulation No. 238, point 57) include insulation resistance in the test report prepared at the periodic inspection. Regulation No. 238 is in force until 1 January 2027.

Cable insulation resistance measurements are the same measurement for a single cable or line, for example if a new cable has been laid to an outbuilding and the client wants a result for it separately.

Insulation resistance limits under LBN 261-15

For newly installed electrical installations the minimum values are in Table 2 of Annex 6 to LBN 261-15. For the periodic inspection report they are given in point 6 of the notes to Annex 6 of Regulation No. 238: at least 0.5 MΩ in safety and protective extra-low voltage circuits up to 50 V and at least 1.0 MΩ in the others. The test voltage depends on the rated voltage of the circuit. Ordinary 230/400 V circuits in a house or flat are in the “up to 500 V” row.

Circuit Test voltage Minimum insulation resistance
SELV and PELV (extra-low voltage safety circuits) 250 V more than 0.5 MΩ
Up to 500 V 500 V more than 1.0 MΩ
Above 500 V 1000 V more than 1.0 MΩ

If the measured value is lower than the one set in the table, the circuit does not meet the standard. An electrician first has to repair such a circuit, and only then can the measurement be repeated and entered in the document.

Checked on 25 November 2026: LBN 261-15 (Cabinet Regulation No. 294 of 9 June 2015), likumi.lv/ta/id/274674.

Which document the insulation result goes into

One and the same measurement can be part of different documents. That determines what else has to be measured on the same occasion.

Who asks Document What else is measured
The building authority, when accepting the building into use A protocol with the documents under point 53 of LBN 261-15 Phase-neutral loop, earth loop, connection between earth electrodes and the parts to be earthed
Periodic inspection under Regulation No. 238 Test report following Annex 6 Phase-neutral loop, earthing installation, earthing conductor connection, lightning protection, if there is any
The owner or a buyer Measurement report As needed; a single measurement is also possible

If you do not know which row you are in, write in the form who is asking for the document and why. After the site visit we tell you which measurements are needed.

How the measurement goes on site

  1. Site visit The electrician looks at the distribution board, counts the circuits and finds out who is asking for the document: the building authority, a periodic inspection or you yourself.
  2. Circuits without voltage Insulation is measured with the circuit switched off, so expect a power cut on the circuits being measured. Connected appliances are unplugged from the sockets so that they do not affect the result.
  3. Measurement circuit by circuit Each circuit is measured with the test voltage shown in the table and the result is compared with the minimum value.
  4. Document The results are entered in a report or protocol in the form the regulation requires, for example in the report in Annex 6 of Regulation No. 238.

What to prepare before the measurements

  • Access to the distribution board and, if it is in the stairwell, a key or the property manager’s permission.
  • The existing electrical installation diagram or the previous report, if there is one.
  • Information on who is asking for the document and in what form.
  • A time when a brief power cut does not cause trouble, for example for a fridge or servers.

Questions: Insulation resistance measurements

What is the insulation resistance limit for 230 V circuits in a flat?

Under Annex 6 of LBN 261-15, newly installed circuits up to 500 V are measured with a 500 V test voltage, and the insulation resistance must be more than 1.0 MΩ.

Is an insulation measurement enough for the building authority?

No, if the building authority requires the documents under point 53 of LBN 261-15. Sub-point 53.4 also names phase-neutral loop and earthing tests at building acceptance. Ask the building authority which documents are needed in your particular case.

What should be done if a circuit does not meet the limit?

An electrician has to find and remove the fault. After the repair the circuit is measured again, and only then can the result be entered in the document.
