Point 58 of the fire safety regulations currently in force (Cabinet Regulation No. 238) requires it together with the periodic electrical installation inspection, and the results are set out in a test report on electrical installation contact connections under Annex 7.

What Regulation No. 238 requires for the thermal camera inspection

Point 58 requires the quality of electrical installation contact connections to be inspected with a thermal camera, for example in a junction box, an electrical distribution cabinet (distribution board) and at protective device locations. The inspection is carried out together with the periodic inspection named in point 56, and a separate report is prepared for it (Annex 7).

So the periodic inspection has two documents: the Annex 6 report on measurements and the Annex 7 report on contact connections. Regulation No. 238 is in force until 1 January 2027.

Checked on 25 November 2026: Cabinet Regulation No. 238 of 19 April 2016 “Fire Safety Regulations”, points 56–58, likumi.lv/ta/id/281646. The regulations are in force until 1 January 2027.

Electrical installation thermography: what it shows and what it does not

A thermal camera shows surface temperature. A loose or oxidised connection heats up more while current flows, and it stands out in the image. That is why distribution board thermography is carried out when the circuits are under load, not in an empty house with the equipment switched off.

A thermal camera does not replace insulation, loop and earthing measurements. It does not see wires in the wall and does not check whether the protective device will operate. That is why the regulations provide for it together with the measurements, not instead of them.

Who needs a thermal camera inspection of electrics

  • Buildings that undergo the periodic inspection under Regulation No. 238: apartment buildings, offices, shops, production sites.
  • Distribution boards with a heavy load, where the owner wants to know the condition of the connections between inspections.
  • After a distribution board is rebuilt, when you need to make sure the new connections do not heat up.

Where the thermal camera is pointed

Point 58 of Regulation No. 238 names three kinds of place, and the inspection looks at all of them that are accessible on the property.

Location What is checked
Distribution board (electrical distribution cabinet) Wire connections to circuit breakers and residual current devices, busbars, incoming terminals
Protective device locations Fuses and circuit breakers outside the main distribution board, for example in floor boards
Junction boxes Accessible boxes where wires are joined

In an apartment building these are the incoming distribution board and the stairwell boards, and in a business also the boards of production equipment. A thermal camera does not see concealed junction boxes behind finishes. Write in the form how many distribution boards there are and where they are, so that the scope can be assessed during the site visit.

Order of the inspection

  1. Access to the distribution board The board door and, if needed, the protective cover are opened so that the connections are visible.
  2. Images under load The board and connections are recorded with the thermal camera while the equipment is running.
  3. Report The results and the places where connections heat up are recorded in the Annex 7 report.

If a connection heats up, an electrician tightens or replaces it. For this work there are electrician services in Riga.

Thermal camera inspection after 1 January 2027

The thermal camera requirement is in the fire safety regulations currently in force (Cabinet Regulation No. 238), which are repealed on 2 January 2027. The text of the new regulations had not yet been published on 25 November 2026. If you are planning an inspection after this date, check what the new regulations require.

What to prepare

Give the number of distribution boards and their locations. Arrange a time when the usual equipment on the property is running, so that the load is close to the everyday load. If the boards are in locked rooms, provide keys.

Questions about these documents

Can a thermal camera inspection be done without measurements?

It can, if you only need to know the condition of the connections. For the periodic inspection, point 58 of Regulation No. 238 provides for it together with the tests of point 56.

Why does the inspection need a load?

A loose connection heats up when current flows through it. Without a load the difference may not be visible in the thermal image.

Does a thermal camera find faults in the walls?

No. A thermal camera shows surface temperature, so it sees in an open distribution board and accessible boxes. The condition of wires in walls is checked with insulation measurements.

Is a thermal camera inspection needed for a private house too?

The periodic inspection under Regulation No. 238 applies to properties regardless of the form of ownership, and point 58 provides for the thermal camera inspection together with it. So if you carry out the periodic inspection of a house, the thermal camera report is part of it.

Which document remains after the inspection?

A test report on electrical installation contact connections under Annex 7 of Regulation No. 238.
