Periodic electrical installation testing

Under the fire safety regulations currently in force (Cabinet Regulation No. 238), the periodic electrical installation inspection must be carried out once every 10 years, once every two years in an explosive environment and once a year in a chemically aggressive environment.

These regulations are in force until 1 January 2027, and the text of their successors had not yet been published on 25 November 2026. The result of the inspection is two reports: on measurements and on contact connections.

The electrical installation inspection once every 10 years: what it includes

Point 56 of Regulation No. 238 applies to the electrical installation, including earthing and lightning protection devices. The inspection must be carried out regardless of the form of ownership of the property, so for a flat, a private house, an apartment building and a business building.

Part What has to be tested Document
Measurements (point 57) Insulation resistance, total impedance of the phase-neutral loop, earthing installation resistance, earthing conductor continuity, lightning protection system Test report, Annex 6
Contact connections (point 58) Quality of contact connections with a thermal camera in junction boxes, distribution boards and at protective device locations Test report, Annex 7

Checked on 25 November 2026: Cabinet Regulation No. 238 of 19 April 2016 “Fire Safety Regulations”, points 54–58, likumi.lv/ta/id/281646. In force until 1 January 2027, repealed on 2 January 2027.

Intervals: 10 years, 2 years and 1 year

For an ordinary flat, house or office the interval is once every 10 years. If there is an explosive environment on the property or site, the inspection must be carried out once every two years, and in a chemically aggressive environment once a year. Which environment your property has follows from its activity; if you are not sure, write in the form what goes on at the property.

The ten-year interval should not be treated as a permanent law. Regulation No. 238 is repealed on 2 January 2027, and the new regulations may change it. If you are planning the next routine electrical installation inspection in 2027, check the text of the new regulations on likumi.lv.

Who can carry out the periodic inspection

Regulation No. 238 does not name a construction specialist’s certificate for the person carrying out the inspection. However, the report in Annex 6 comes with a copy of the electrical safety group card of the person doing the work, a copy of the instrument’s calibration or verification certificate and the electrical installation diagram (points 1.1–1.3 of the notes to the annex). The documents are prepared and signed by a certified electrical specialist.

What to do before and after the inspection

  1. Find the previous report It shows when the inspection was last carried out and which distribution boards were tested.
  2. Describe the property in the form Address, type of property, approximate floor area, number of distribution boards, whether there is lightning protection.
  3. Site visit A visit to the property costs €40. After it we tell you which measurements are needed and quote the price.
  4. Measurements and thermal camera The measurements and the thermal camera inspection are carried out on one occasion, as point 58 requires.
  5. Shortcomings If a result does not meet the standard, it is recorded in the report as a non-conformity. An electrician puts the shortcoming right, for example electrician services in Riga, and then the measurement is repeated.

What to prepare for the periodic inspection

If there is no previous report, write that in the form; this inspection will then be a new reference point. Access is needed to all distribution boards, the junction boxes that are accessible, and the earthing and lightning protection points. For the thermal camera inspection the equipment has to be running in normal mode. During the measurements some circuits will be without power, so at a business agree a time when you can afford that.

Questions: Periodic electrical installation testing

Does the 10-year inspection apply to a flat too?

Yes. Point 56 of Regulation No. 238 applies to the electrical installation regardless of the form of ownership of the property. The regulations are in force until 1 January 2027.

From what point are the 10 years counted?

The reference point is the previous inspection, shown in the previous report. If there is no report, it is worth carrying out an inspection so that there is a new reference point.

What happens to the 10-year interval after 2026?

Regulation No. 238 is repealed on 2 January 2027. The text of the new fire safety regulations had not yet been published on 25 November 2026, so the interval may change.

Is the periodic inspection report also useful when selling a flat or house?

It can be useful: the buyer sees when the installation was tested and with what results. If a buyer or bank asks for a document, ask which one exactly.

Is the thermal camera inspection a compulsory part?

Under point 58 of Regulation No. 238, contact connections are inspected with a thermal camera together with the inspection under point 56, and the Annex 7 report is prepared.
