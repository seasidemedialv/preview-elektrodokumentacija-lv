The electrical installation measurements that make up the test

Electrical measurements are not a single figure. LBN 261-15 and the fire safety regulations currently in force (Cabinet Regulation No. 238) name several separate measurements, and each has its own meaning.

  • Insulation resistance measurements: whether the wire insulation meets the minimum values set in Annex 6 of LBN 261-15. The building standard requires this measurement before putting into use.
  • Phase-neutral loop measurements: the total impedance of the circuit from the phase conductor to the neutral conductor, which determines whether the protective device will disconnect the circuit at the moment of a fault.
  • Earthing resistance measurements: the resistance of the earthing installation and the connection between the earth electrode and the parts to be earthed.
  • Residual current device (RCD) testing: whether the residual current device disconnects the circuit at the specified residual current.
  • Lightning protection testing: for buildings that have a lightning protection system.
  • Thermal camera inspection of a distribution board: the quality of the contact connections in the board, which Regulation No. 238 requires to be checked together with the periodic inspection.

Electrotechnical measurements for a single line, for example insulation only or earthing only, are possible. Sub-point 53.4 of LBN 261-15 and the periodic report under Regulation No. 238 include several measurements together, so first find out who will ask for the document.

Who may carry out measurements and who signs

Point 55 of LBN 261-15 states that the tests in sub-point 53.4 (wire and cable resistance, phase-neutral loop, earthing and the connection between earth electrodes) are carried out by a certified electrical specialist certified by an accredited body in line with LVS EN ISO/IEC 17024. For the periodic inspection, Regulation No. 238 does not name a construction specialist’s certificate. However, the report in Annex 6 comes with a copy of the electrical safety group card of the person doing the work, a copy of the instrument’s calibration or verification certificate and the electrical installation diagram (points 1.1–1.3 of the notes to the annex).

The documents are prepared and signed by a certified electrical specialist. How to check any electrician’s certificate is explained on the page about a certified electrician for documents.

Questions: Electrical installation testing

How does electrical installation testing differ from measurements?

Measurements are individual values: insulation, phase-neutral loop, earthing, RCD. Testing is the set of these measurements together with an inspection and a document in which the results are recorded.

Is the site visit free of charge?

No. The site visit costs €40. After it we tell you which documents are needed and quote the price.

Which document does the building authority need?

For putting a self-built Group 2 residential building into use, Cabinet Regulation No. 529 requires electrical installation test documentation. It is better to ask the building authority what exactly it expects in your case before ordering, and to write that in the form.

Can I order just one measurement?

Yes, for example insulation resistance only or earthing only. We quote the price after the site visit, because it depends on the number of circuits.
