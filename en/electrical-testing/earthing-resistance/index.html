Earthing resistance measurements - loop and connection

Earthing resistance measurements are needed when it has to be shown that a building’s earthing works: an earth electrode in the ground with low enough resistance and a continuous connection to the distribution board and the metal parts of the building.

In a private house with its own earth loop it is one of the measurements for putting into use. The result is entered in an approved report or in the periodic inspection report.

Two measurements: loop and connection

Sub-point 53.4 of LBN 261-15 requires two separate reports on earthing at building acceptance. The first is a test of the leakage electrical resistance of the earth loops, that is, how well the earth electrode carries current into the ground. The second is a test of the electrical resistance of the connection between the earth electrodes and the elements to be earthed, for example the distribution board casing and protective conductors. For the periodic report, Annex 6 of Regulation No. 238 also sets limits: the earth loop resistance must not exceed 10 Ω, and the earthing conductor continuity resistance 0.4 Ω.

Point 57 of the fire safety regulations currently in force (Cabinet Regulation No. 238) names earthing installation resistance and earthing conductor continuity in the periodic report. These are the same two questions in other words.

Earth loop passport or test report

Clients often ask for an “earth loop passport”. In the laws and regulations this page relies on, the results are set out as a report (LBN 261-15, sub-point 53.4) or as a test report under Annex 6 of Regulation No. 238. If you are specifically asked for a passport, ask who is asking and what content they expect, and write that in the form.

If the loop is new, sub-point 53.3 of LBN 261-15 also names concealed works acceptance records at acceptance. If the loop has already been backfilled, the record cannot be prepared later, and the building authority should be asked which document it will accept. More about this: concealed works records.

Earthing, lightning protection and RCD in a private house

Point 56 of the fire safety regulations currently in force (Cabinet Regulation No. 238) also counts earthing and lightning protection devices as part of the electrical installation at the periodic inspection. If the house has lightning protection, its earth electrode can be measured on the same occasion, and the results go into one Annex 6 report.

Since 1 July 2025 point 28.² of LBN 261-15 has required, in a new or altered installation, a 300–500 mA residual current device on the main incoming supply in the main incoming distribution board of a single-family residential building, or an alternative protection solution. The condition of the earthing and this device are looked at together in a test, because both relate to protection against fault current.

Who needs earthing measurements

  • A private house before putting into use, together with insulation and the phase-neutral loop.
  • A building at its periodic inspection under Regulation No. 238, which is in force until 1 January 2027.
  • After an earth loop is built or rebuilt, when the work done has to be tested (LBN 261-15, point 52).
  • Before buying a property, where the measurement can be useful as part of a general check.

How earth loop resistance is measured

  1. Finding the loop During the site visit the electrician finds out where the earth electrode is and where the earthing conductor is connected to the distribution board.
  2. Loop resistance The resistance of the earth electrode is measured with an earth tester.
  3. Connection The connection resistance between the earth electrode and the parts to be earthed is tested.
  4. Report The results are set out in the document the building authority or the periodic inspection requires.

If the loop cannot be found or the result is poor, an electrician has to build or repair the earthing. Electricians in Riga do this, and after the work the measurement is repeated.

What to prepare for earthing measurements

Access to the distribution board and to the place where the earthing conductor leaves the building. If there are photos of the loop construction, a diagram or a previous report, attach them to the form.

Questions: Earthing resistance measurements

Are earthing resistance measurements needed in a flat?

If the flat has no earth loop of its own, the flat test measures the connection of the protective conductors to the distribution board. The building’s earthing installation is tested in the apartment building test.

Are an earth loop passport and a test report the same thing?

The laws and regulations use the names “report” (LBN 261-15) and “test report” (Regulation No. 238). Ask the person who requires the document what content they expect.

Can an earthing measurement be ordered without the rest of the test?

Yes, the price is then given after the site visit. If the building authority or a periodic inspection requires the document, the regulations also require insulation and loop measurements, so first ask which document is needed.

What should be done if there is no earth loop?

An electrician has to build the earthing. After it is built, the loop resistance and the connection are measured and the document is prepared.
