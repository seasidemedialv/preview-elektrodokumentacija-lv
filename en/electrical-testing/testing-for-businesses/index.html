An office, shop, warehouse or production site has several distribution boards, larger loads and sometimes an environment with shorter inspection intervals. The result is the Regulation No. 238 reports; a measurement report drawn up after 19 April 2016 is valid until the next inspection (point 525).

Who in a business is responsible for the electrical installation

Point 44 of Cabinet Regulation No. 635 states that the electricity user is responsible for the technical condition of their electrical installation and its qualified servicing. The fire safety regulations currently in force (Cabinet Regulation No. 238) apply the periodic inspection to properties regardless of the form of ownership, so to business premises as well.

If the premises are rented, the lease may set out who orders the inspection: the owner or the tenant. Check the lease before ordering and write in the form who the client will be.

Electrical installation testing for an office and shop

For an office and a shop without an explosive or chemically aggressive environment, the interval is once every 10 years. The test includes insulation, phase-neutral loop, earthing, lightning protection if the building has it, and a thermal camera inspection of the distribution boards. In a shop the circuits of refrigeration and lighting equipment are also looked at, because they run all the time, and that is exactly when the thermal camera shows the condition of the connections.

So that the shop or office does not have to be closed, the measurements can be split by distribution board and circuit. We agree how to do this in your premises after the site visit.

Electrical installation testing in a warehouse and production site

Point 56 of Regulation No. 238 sets shorter intervals: if there is an explosive environment on the property or site, an inspection once every two years, and if a chemically aggressive environment, once a year. Point 27.11 of LBN 261-15 names premises with a fire or explosion hazard among the locations with special requirements for electrical installations.

A warehouse and a production site also have high-power equipment, several distribution boards and long cable lines. Write in the form what goes on at the property, how many distribution boards there are and whether there is lightning protection, so that the scope can be assessed during the site visit.

Checked on 25 November 2026: Cabinet Regulation No. 238 of 19 April 2016 “Fire Safety Regulations”, points 54–58, likumi.lv/ta/id/281646. In force until 1 January 2027, repealed on 2 January 2027.

Intervals by type of premises

Premises Interval under point 56 of Regulation No. 238 What to watch
Office Once every 10 years Socket circuits at workstations, server room
Shop Once every 10 years Refrigeration and lighting circuits that run all the time
Warehouse Once every 10 years; once every two years in an explosive environment Long lines, door and heating equipment
Production site Once every 10 years; once every two years in an explosive environment; once a year in a chemically aggressive environment High-power equipment, several distribution boards

The intervals are from the fire safety regulations currently in force, which are in force until 1 January 2027.

Order of work at a business

  1. Request Business name, address, type and floor area of the premises, number of distribution boards, year of the previous report.
  2. Site visit A visit to the property costs €40. The electrician looks at the distribution boards and agrees which circuits can be switched off and when.
  3. Price and plan After the site visit we quote the price and tell you which measurements and reports are needed.
  4. Measurements and thermal camera Measurements are carried out board by board, and the thermal camera inspection is done while the equipment is running.
  5. Reports The Annex 6 report on measurements and the Annex 7 report on contact connections.

If the premises also need documents after alterations or for putting into use, see the page electrical documents for commercial premises.

What to prepare

  • A floor plan or electrical installation diagram, if there is one.
  • Earlier test reports.
  • A responsible person on site who can open the distribution boards and rooms.
  • A list of equipment that must not be switched off without warning.

Questions about these documents

Will the power have to be switched off in the office during testing?

For some measurements the circuit has to be switched off. The measurements can be split by circuit and distribution board, and we agree the order after the site visit.

How often must a production site’s electrical installation be tested?

Under Regulation No. 238, once every 10 years, once every two years in an explosive environment and once a year in a chemically aggressive environment. Regulation No. 238 is in force until 1 January 2027.

Can just one distribution board be tested?

Yes, if you need to know the condition of one board. The report under Regulation No. 238 states whether the whole property or separate parts were tested, but within the interval of point 56 the whole installation has to be tested.

Who has to order testing in rented premises?

The lease determines that. If it does not say, agree with the owner before ordering.
