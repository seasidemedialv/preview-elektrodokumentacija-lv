What is tested in a flat

Flat electrical installation measurements cover what lies within the flat: the flat’s distribution board, the circuits for sockets, lighting and fixed appliances, for example the cooker or water heater.

  • insulation resistance of each circuit;
  • phase-neutral loop impedance, to see whether the circuit breakers will operate at the moment of a fault;
  • the connection of the protective conductors to the distribution board;
  • residual current devices (RCD): whether the socket and lighting circuits have them and whether they operate. Since 1 July 2025 point 28.¹ of LBN 261-15 has required this for new and altered installations; for an older installation the report records where there is no such device.

The building’s incoming distribution board, stairwell boards and building earthing are the shared part of an apartment building. They are tested in electrical installation testing in an apartment building, which the property manager or flat owners’ association orders.

Electrical installation testing for a rental flat

Electrical installation testing for a rental flat can be useful; if an insurer or tenant asks for a document, ask which one exactly. The periodic inspection once every 10 years under the fire safety regulations currently in force (Cabinet Regulation No. 238) applies to the property regardless of who lives in it, and the owner is responsible for it.

An electrical report for a rental flat can be useful at two moments: before handing the flat to a tenant, so that the owner knows its condition, and after the tenant moves out, if doubts about damage arise. A change of tenant is also a convenient time for the periodic inspection, because the flat is empty and access to all rooms is simple.

How testing in a flat goes

  1. Form Write down the address, floor, approximate floor area, location of the distribution board (in the flat or the stairwell) and who is asking for the report.
  2. Site visit A visit to the property costs €40. The electrician looks at the distribution board and circuits and says which measurements are needed.
  3. Testing For some measurements circuits are switched off, so the flat will be without power for a while.
  4. Report The results are recorded in the report. If a circuit does not meet the standard, an electrician has to repair it first.

What to prepare for flat testing

  • Access to all rooms, including the storeroom and balcony, if there are sockets or lighting there.
  • If the meter or distribution board is in the stairwell and locked, arrange access with the property manager.
  • A diagram received during renovation, if there is one.
  • A photo of the flat’s distribution board to attach to the form; from it the number of circuits can be estimated even before the site visit.

Questions about these documents

Does the power have to be off in the whole flat for the test?

For the insulation measurement the circuit being measured has to be switched off. For how long and which circuits depends on the distribution board, and we tell you that during the site visit.

Is an electrical report compulsory for a rental flat?

The owner has the duty of a periodic inspection once every 10 years under Regulation No. 238 (in force until 1 January 2027), and a report can also be useful in dealings with a tenant. If someone asks for a particular document for renting out, ask which one.

Will you test the stairwell distribution board too?

The flat test covers the flat’s part. Stairwell distribution boards are the shared part of the building, and the property manager orders their testing.

Will I also receive a diagram of the flat’s electrical installation after the test?

A diagram is separate work: as-built diagram. We quote its price after the site visit, because it depends on the number of circuits and whether there is an old diagram.

Can you prepare a report after renovation work done by another electrician?

Yes, after a site visit and measurements. If the measurements show shortcomings, they have to be put right before the report.
