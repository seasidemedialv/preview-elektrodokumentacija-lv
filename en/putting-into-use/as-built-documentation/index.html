It is attached when a building is accepted into use, and its parts are prepared by different people: the designer, the installer, the equipment supplier and the electrician who carries out the measurements. We prepare the measurement and diagram part in Riga and the Riga region.

List of as-built documents under LBN 261-15

Point 53 of LBN 261-15 lists the documents attached to a building acceptance statement if the relevant works have been done. The table shows whom to ask for each part.

Point Document Whom to ask for it
53.1 A corrected set of drawings of the construction design The designer, as part of author’s supervision
53.2 Equipment data sheets and material conformity declarations The installer or supplier
53.3 Concealed works acceptance records Drawn up before the works are covered, while they are still visible
53.4 Reports on wire and cable resistance, phase-neutral loops, earthing and bonds A certified electrical specialist (point 55)
53.5 Automatic control and switching diagrams The installer or electrician, according to what was built
53.6 Operating description and safety instructions The installer or equipment supplier
53.7 Staff training protocols For systems operated by staff

Separately, point 54 requires insulation resistance to be tested before acceptance into use; the minimum values are in Table 2 of Annex 6.

Checked on 25 November 2026: LBN 261-15 points 53–55 and Annex 6 (likumi.lv/ta/id/274674), Cabinet Regulation No. 529 points 120, 157.3, 167.5 (likumi.lv/ta/id/269164).

As-built documentation and the construction log

In the Building Construction Regulations (Cabinet Regulation No. 529), as-built records go together with the construction log: point 157.3 requires them for Part II of the explanatory note, and point 167.5 in the case of a building permit. The construction log is kept by the construction works manager. Under point 120, no log is kept in the cases in point 107 (including for an owner-built house). Nor is one kept in simplified renovation, in new construction or placement of a seasonal building, in a change of use without alteration, and in works carried out under the notification procedure.

The electrical part is one of several in this set. If you do not have a construction works manager, for example because you built the house yourself, see the page self-built house.

Which parts of the as-built documentation we prepare

  • Measurements under sub-point 53.4: wire and cable resistance, phase-neutral loop, resistance of the earthing loop and bonds.
  • The insulation resistance test under point 54.
  • A diagram of the electrical installation as built, if the installer has not handed one over or it does not match: as-built diagram.

We do not issue equipment data sheets or material certificates; they are provided by whoever supplied or installed the equipment. Concealed works records are drawn up while the works are still visible. If they are already covered, the record is no longer drawn up, and the building authority has to be asked what it accepts instead.

If the installer handed over a diagram, we compare it with what was built. Where the diagram does not match, this is noted, and the measurement results are linked to the circuits that are actually in the distribution board.

What to prepare before requesting as-built documents

  1. Gather what exists The design, the papers handed over by the installer, equipment data sheets, if you have them.
  2. Check what is missing Compare with the point 53 list and mark what is not there.
  3. Write it in the form What you have, what is missing, and which building authority or construction works manager is asking for the documents.
  4. Site visit After it we tell you which parts we can prepare and what the price will be.

Questions about these documents

Are as-built documentation and an as-built diagram the same thing?

No. The as-built diagram is one part of the set; as-built documentation also includes measurement reports, equipment data sheets, concealed works records and other point 53 documents.

The installer disappeared without handing over the papers. What should I do?

The measurements and diagram can be prepared afresh after a site visit. Equipment data sheets can be requested from the supplier or manufacturer by equipment model, but concealed works records are not recreated later.

Does an office need staff training protocols?

Point 53.7 mentions them only if such works have been done, for example for systems operated by staff. The building authority or the designer says what is needed for your property.

Who signs the measurement reports in the as-built documentation?

Point 55 of LBN 261-15 requires the sub-point 53.4 tests to be carried out by a certified electrical specialist certified by an accredited body. The documents are prepared and signed by a certified electrical specialist.
