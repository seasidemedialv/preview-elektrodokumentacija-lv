How a garden house differs from an ordinary private house

Point 27 of LBN 261-15 lists places where electrical installations have special requirements. They include agricultural and horticultural areas (27.5), outdoor lighting equipment (27.9) and rooms with a shower (27.1). A garden house may have all three at once: a socket by the greenhouse, a lamp at the entrance, a shower in an annexe.

In a new or altered installation, since 1 July 2025, point 28.¹ has required a residual current device (RCD) with a tripping current of no more than 30 mA for outdoor sockets for portable equipment up to 32 A and for household lighting circuits. An RCD disconnects the circuit if current leaks to earth, for example through a damaged cable or a person.

Checked on 25 November 2026: LBN 261-15 points 27, 28.¹, 52–55 (likumi.lv/ta/id/274674).

Which building authority process applies to a garden house

The Building Construction Regulations (Cabinet Regulation No. 529) provide three routes: a construction notification, an explanatory note or a building permit. Which of them applies to your garden house depends on the size of the building, its group and the type of work, and the building authority determines it.

  • Construction notification: completion is confirmed in BIS and reports on utility network readiness are attached (Cabinet Regulation No. 529, point 156.¹); for a new Group 1 residential building or auxiliary building, also an as-built survey plan and façade photos.
  • Explanatory note: the documents listed in point 157 are attached to Part II. If the owner built the residential house, they include electrical installation test documentation.
  • Building permit: the point 167 documents, including equipment test protocols if they are required.

Ask the building authority whether it regards your garden house as a residential house. This determines whether electrical installation test documentation is expressly required. The testing of electrical equipment before putting into use under point 52 of LBN 261-15 applies to all buildings.

First connection and electrical papers

If the garden house is getting a new connection, Sadales tīkls requires the customer to submit on e-st.lv a confirmation that the internal installation is ready. The internal installation and the cable up to the distribution board are prepared by a qualified electrical specialist or an electrical installation company. After the confirmation, Sadales tīkls makes contact within 2 working days to install the meter.

The fee for connecting the customer’s installation is €117.21 (0.4 kV), or €217.52 with additional installation work, according to the Sadales tīkls price list, checked on 25 November 2026. The confirmation and the measurement protocol are different documents. More about this: Sadales tīkls confirmation.

How to prepare for a garden house test

  1. List What is connected: house, greenhouse, pump, outdoor lighting, sauna.
  2. Find A design or sketch, if there is one, and the building authority’s letter with the required documents.
  3. Photograph The distribution board and the place where the cable enters the house, and attach the photos to the form.
  4. Provide access The electrician needs access to the distribution board, the earthing point and all connected buildings.

If the building authority has already sent a list of required documents, attach it to the form. After the site visit we can then say which of them concern the electrical part and what other specialists prepare.

Questions: Putting a garden house into use

Does a small garden house need electrical documents at all?

If the house has electrical installations, point 52 of LBN 261-15 requires them to be tested before putting into use. Which documents to attach depends on the building authority process.

Does a greenhouse socket need an RCD?

In a new or altered installation, point 28.¹ of LBN 261-15 requires 30 mA protection for sockets up to 32 A to which portable equipment used outdoors is connected. If you connect such equipment in the greenhouse, the requirement applies to it too.

What does Sadales tīkls ask of a garden house owner before the meter is installed?

A confirmation on e-st.lv that the internal installation is ready. You will see which annexes to add on e-st.lv when submitting; after the confirmation, Sadales tīkls makes contact within 2 working days.

Does periodic testing also apply to a garden house?

The fire safety regulations currently in force (Cabinet Regulation No. 238) require electrical installation testing once every 10 years, regardless of the form of ownership of the property. These regulations are in force until 1 January 2027, and the new ones have not yet been published.
