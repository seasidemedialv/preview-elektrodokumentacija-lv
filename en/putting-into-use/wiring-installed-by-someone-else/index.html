This helps if the installer cannot be reached, did not hand over the papers, or they do not match what was built. We test installations built by others and prepare the documents in Riga and the Riga region.

Why another specialist can carry out the measurements

Point 55 of LBN 261-15 separates two things: the electrical installation is put in under the direction of a certified building specialist, while the sub-point 53.4 tests (wire and cable resistance, phase-neutral loop, earthing, bonds) are carried out by a certified electrical specialist. The regulations do not require this to be the same person who built the installation.

So for an electrical installation without documents, the measurement part can be prepared afresh. Point 54 requires an insulation resistance test before putting into use, and it can be carried out at any time while the installation is accessible.

Checked on 25 November 2026: LBN 261-15 points 53–55 (likumi.lv/ta/id/274674), Sadales tīkls: preparing a property to receive electricity (sadalestikls.lv/lv/sagatavot-ipasumu-elektroenergijas-sanemsanai).

What can and cannot be restored if there are no installation documents

Document Can it be prepared later Why
Measurement reports (53.4) Yes The existing installation is measured
Insulation resistance test (54) Yes The existing installation is measured
As-built diagram Yes Drawn from what was built in the board and the rooms
Concealed works records (53.3) No Covered-up work can no longer be examined
Equipment data sheets and conformity declarations (53.2) Partly They are provided by the supplier or manufacturer, not by the electrician who measures

Restoring electrical installation documents therefore means new documents on the current condition, not copies of old papers. The new documents state the test date and what exactly was measured, so they describe the installation as it is at the moment of testing, with all later changes. Old papers, if they turn up, can be attached as history.

A record of concealed works is not drawn up retroactively, and the building authority has to be asked what document replaces it. We do not prepare documents in other electricians’ names or with an earlier date.

If the measurements reveal defects

If a circuit fails the insulation measurement or the loop resistance is too high, documents on conformity cannot be prepared until the defect is put right. For a new or altered installation this also applies to the residual current device (RCD), which point 28.¹ of LBN 261-15 has required since 1 July 2025.

An electrician puts the defect right: either the one who built the installation or another, for example electricians for installation and repair work. We do not do installation or repairs. After the correction, the corrected circuit is measured again, and then the documents can be prepared.

What to prepare if there are no papers

  1. Gather what you have Invoices or a contract with the installer, a design, photos from the construction period, if there are any.
  2. Find out the requirement Who is asking for the documents: the building authority, Sadales tīkls, a buyer or a bank, and what name they use.
  3. Request In the form, write the address, who did the installation and which papers are missing. Attach a photo of the distribution board.
  4. Site visit After it we tell you which documents can be prepared and what the price will be.

If the documents are needed for a new connection, bear in mind that Sadales tīkls requires a confirmation on e-st.lv that the installation is ready, and it is built by a qualified electrical specialist or an electrical installation company.

Questions about these documents

May the previous electrician sign the documents later?

Yes, if they hold the required certificate and carry out the measurements themselves. If they cannot be reached, another certified electrical specialist can carry out the measurements.

Will you fix another electrician’s mistakes?

No. We test and prepare the documents. An electrician puts the defects right, and then the corrected items are measured again.

The installation has old reports, but much has changed since. Are they valid?

A report describes the installation as it was at the time of testing. If significant changes have been made since, point 52 of LBN 261-15 requires testing after the changes.

Can documents be prepared if I bought the house already finished?

Yes, after a site visit and measurements. The documents are prepared and signed by a certified electrical specialist.
