We prepare measurements and documents for office, shop and other commercial premises in Riga and the Riga region.

How commercial premises differ in the documents

The list of documents in point 53 of LBN 261-15 is the same as for a residential building. In commercial premises, however, the works covered by its last items have more often been done: automatic control and switching diagrams (53.5), a system operating description and safety instructions (53.6) and staff training protocols (53.7).

If the premises are accepted under a building permit, point 167 of the Building Construction Regulations (Cabinet Regulation No. 529) also requires test protocols, acceptance records and conformity declarations for the process equipment and special systems in the construction design. This applies if equipment safety rules require them and they are not attached to the construction log. Once all the documents in points 167 and 168 are received, the building authority agrees an acceptance date with the construction initiator, no later than 10 working days from submission of the documents (point 171).

Checked on 25 November 2026: LBN 261-15 points 53–55 (likumi.lv/ta/id/274674), Cabinet Regulation No. 529 points 167, 171 (likumi.lv/ta/id/269164), Cabinet Regulation No. 238 points 56–58, in force until 1 January 2027 (likumi.lv/ta/id/281646).

Electrical installation in commercial premises: incoming supply and capacity

If an office or shop needs more capacity, it is applied for to Sadales tīkls on e-st.lv. Without network alteration only the connection fee of €117.21 (0.4 kV) is paid, according to the Sadales tīkls price list, checked on 25 November 2026; if the network has to be altered, construction costs are added.

For a higher-capacity connection, the incoming supply, distribution board and protection have to be adapted to the new capacity before the documents are prepared. More about this: increasing capacity.

Putting shop premises into use: electrics and equipment

In a shop, the electrical part also takes in display case lighting, freezers, tills and advertising equipment. Write in the form which equipment is connected, because in the test report each circuit’s result is linked to the diagram and the circuit names in the distribution board.

Part What is documented Basis
Distribution boards and circuits Insulation, phase-neutral loop, earthing, bonds LBN 261-15 points 53.4, 54
Diagrams Switching and distribution board diagrams of what was built LBN 261-15 point 53.5
Contact joints in distribution boards Thermal camera inspection report (Annex 7) Regulation No. 238, point 58
Lightning protection, if there is any Testing together with the electrical installation Regulation No. 238, points 56, 57

Periodic testing after opening

The fire safety regulations currently in force (Cabinet Regulation No. 238) require testing of the electrical installation, earthing and lightning protection once every 10 years. The interval is once every two years if the property has an explosive atmosphere, and once a year in a chemically aggressive environment. Together with this test, contact joints in distribution boards are checked with a thermal camera (point 58).

Regulation No. 238 is in force until 1 January 2027, and the new regulations have not yet been published. For businesses there is a page on periodic tests, electrical installation testing for a business.

What to prepare for a commercial premises request

  1. Premises Area, number of floors, how many distribution boards and roughly how many circuits.
  2. Process Whether the premises are accepted under a building permit, an explanatory note or a notification, and who the construction works manager is.
  3. Design The electrical design and the installer’s diagrams, if there are any, so they can be compared with what was built.
  4. Time When circuits in the premises can be switched off for measurements, if people already work there or finishing is under way.

If you rent the premises, also write who is asking for the documents: the building authority, the landlord or your company.

Questions: Putting office premises into use

Do office premises need a thermal camera inspection?

Point 58 of Cabinet Regulation No. 238 requires it together with periodic electrical installation testing, and an Annex 7 report is drawn up on it. Ask the building authority whether it is also needed for putting into use.

Can measurements be taken while people are working in the office?

Circuits are switched off briefly during the measurements. Write in the form when this can be done, and after the site visit we agree on a time.

The premises are rented. Who orders the documents?

This is determined by the lease and by who the construction initiator is. Either the owner or the tenant can be the client; what matters is that there is access to the premises and distribution boards.
