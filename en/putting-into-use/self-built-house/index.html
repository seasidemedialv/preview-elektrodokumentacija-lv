They name this requirement precisely for the case where owners build their own house. We carry out the measurements and prepare this documentation in Riga and the Riga region.

Why this document is required for an owner-built house

Section 19.¹, paragraph three of the Construction Law allows owners to build their own residential house of up to 400 m² on their own plot of land, acting as the builder. In that case construction supervision does not have to be organised, so there is also no construction supervisor to look over the electrical work.

It is for exactly this case that the Building Construction Regulations (Cabinet Regulation No. 529) require electrical installation test documentation: point 157.7.1 for Part II of the explanatory note and point 168.1 for the building permit case. Together with it, in the explanatory note process, point 157.7 also requires a test report on heating appliances and chimneys.

Checked on 25 November 2026: Construction Law (likumi.lv/ta/id/258572), Cabinet Regulation No. 529 points 157.7.1, 160, 168.1 (likumi.lv/ta/id/269164).

What the electrical installation test documentation includes

Cabinet Regulation No. 529 does not spell out the content of the documentation. This is determined by points 53–55 of LBN 261-15, which apply to every building with electrical installations at acceptance into use.

Part What it is Point
Insulation resistance test The insulation of each circuit is measured and the result compared with the minimum value in Annex 6 LBN 261-15 point 54
Phase-neutral loop measurements Show whether the breaker will disconnect the circuit in case of a fault LBN 261-15 point 53.4
Earthing and bond resistance Resistance of the earthing loop and the connection to the parts that must be earthed LBN 261-15 point 53.4
Diagram An electrical installation diagram matching what was built LBN 261-15 points 53.1, 53.5 (if the works were done)
Equipment data sheets and conformity declarations Papers for the distribution board equipment and materials, if there are any LBN 261-15 point 53.2

Under point 55, the sub-point 53.4 tests are carried out by a certified electrical specialist. If you ran the wiring yourself, the tests and documents are prepared by another person holding such a certificate.

The order from the site visit to the building authority’s mark

  1. Request In the form, write the address, the area of the house and whether the electrical installation is already fully finished.
  2. Site visit The electrician examines the distribution board, circuits and earthing. Then we tell you which documents can be prepared and quote the price.
  3. Measurements Insulation, phase-neutral loop, earthing and bonds are measured. If any value does not comply, an electrician has to put the defect right before the documents.
  4. Documentation The documents are prepared and signed by a certified electrical specialist.
  5. Building authority You attach the documentation to Part II of the explanatory note. Point 160 of Cabinet Regulation No. 529 provides for the building authority’s mark within 10 working days.

What the owner has to prepare

  • Access to the incoming distribution board, all rooms and the earthing point.
  • A design or sketch, if the house was built from one, and the papers for materials or equipment, if they have been kept.
  • Information on whether the electrical installation is already connected to Sadales tīkls.

Point 160.¹ of Cabinet Regulation No. 529 allows a Group 2 single-flat house to be accepted if the general construction works and utility networks are fully completed, the façade is finished and the area on the street side is landscaped. In addition, at least one living room, a kitchen and a bathroom must be fitted out, and the sanitary fittings must be connected to the utility networks. Write in the form what is already finished.

Questions about these documents

I ran the wiring myself. Can it be documented?

Yes, if the installation passes the measurements. The tests under point 55 of LBN 261-15 are carried out by a certified electrical specialist, not by the builder. Defects have to be put right before the documents are prepared.

Is this documentation also needed for a house built by a construction company?

Point 157.7.1 of Cabinet Regulation No. 529 requires it specifically for an owner-built house. If a building contractor built it, ask the building authority which electrical documents to attach in your process.

Can the house be accepted if the upper floor is not yet finished?

Point 160.¹ of Cabinet Regulation No. 529 allows this if the general construction works, utility networks and façade finish are completed and the area on the street side is landscaped. In addition, at least one living room, a kitchen and a bathroom with connected fittings are fitted out. The building authority decides on the particular house.

Must the house be connected to Sadales tīkls before the measurements?

If there is no connection yet, write that in the form. After the site visit we will say which measurements can be taken and which after connection.
