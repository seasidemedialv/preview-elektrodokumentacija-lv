Why measurements are needed after rewiring a flat

Point 52 of LBN 261-15 requires electrical equipment to be tested after significant changes have been made. Replacing the whole installation of a flat during renovation is a clear example of such changes: new cables, a new distribution board, different circuits.

The fire safety regulations currently in force (Cabinet Regulation No. 238) require electrical installation testing once every 10 years regardless of the form of ownership, and this applies to flats too. Regulation No. 238 is in force until 1 January 2027; the new regulations have not yet been published. A report after rewiring shows the condition in which the installation was handed over for use.

Checked on 25 November 2026: LBN 261-15 points 8.3, 27.1, 28.¹, 52 (likumi.lv/ta/id/274674), Cabinet Regulation No. 238 point 56 (likumi.lv/ta/id/281646), Sadales tīkls: Load changes (sadalestikls.lv/lv/slodzes-izmainas).

What is tested in an altered flat

Place Requirement Point
Sockets up to 32 A Residual current device (RCD) of no more than 30 mA LBN 261-15 point 28.¹
Lighting circuits The same 30 mA protection for household lighting LBN 261-15 point 28.¹
Bathroom, shower A place with special requirements for electrical installations LBN 261-15 point 27.1
All new circuits Insulation resistance, phase-neutral loop, protective conductor bond LBN 261-15 points 53.4, 54

The phase-neutral loop measurement shows whether the circuit breaker will disconnect the circuit in case of a fault. In a flat it is measured for each circuit, and the result is recorded against the circuit shown in the diagram.

If electric underfloor heating is installed during the work, that is another place with special requirements named in point 27 of LBN 261-15 (27.10). Write in the form which rooms have it.

Building manager, capacity and Sadales tīkls

If the flat needs more capacity as part of the work, an application is submitted to Sadales tīkls on e-st.lv, and the flat owner must attach the building manager’s written consent. If the connection already suits the capacity and the network does not have to be altered, only the connection of the installation is paid for: €117.21 (0.4 kV), according to the Sadales tīkls price list, checked on 25 November 2026.

In a residential building, an incoming supply with a protective device greater than 32 A is connected only as a three-phase supply (LBN 261-15 point 8.3). More: increasing capacity. Ask the building authority and the building manager whether the work also needs documents for the building authority, especially if it affects the stairwell distribution board or the riser.

What to prepare after a flat renovation

  1. Finished installation Sockets, switches and light fittings have to be connected, otherwise the circuits cannot be measured to the end.
  2. Distribution board A photo of the board with the door open and, if there is one, the installer’s diagram.
  3. Access To all rooms and to the flat’s distribution board; circuits will be switched off briefly during the measurements.
  4. Purpose Write in the form who the documents are for: yourself, a buyer, a tenant, the building manager or the building authority.

Questions: Flat rewiring

Is a report needed after partly replacing flat wiring?

If only a few circuits were changed, those can be measured. If the report has to show the whole flat, all circuits are measured; you decide this after the site visit.

Another electrician did the renovation. Can you prepare the report?

Yes, after a site visit and measurements. The documents are prepared and signed by a certified electrical specialist. If the measurements reveal defects, an electrician puts them right, and only then is the report prepared.

Does flat rewiring need building authority permission?

The building authority determines this according to the scope of the work. If the work affects the common parts of the building, for example the riser, start by asking the building authority.

Does a tenant have to be shown an electrical installation report?

The fire safety regulations currently in force (Cabinet Regulation No. 238) require periodic testing once every 10 years regardless of whether the flat is rented out. A report can also be useful for showing a tenant the condition of the installation.
