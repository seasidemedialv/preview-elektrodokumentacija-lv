In the BIS case: where electrical installation documents are attached

In the Construction Information System (BIS), each construction project has its own case. Point 5 of the Building Construction Regulations (Cabinet Regulation No. 529) states that each municipality’s building authority examines cases in its own territory. In Riga this is the City Development Department of Riga State City Municipality. In cases started after 31 December 2019, acceptance can be requested electronically in BIS.

Process Where the electrical documents are attached Point
Explanatory note To Part II of the explanatory note; the construction log and as-built records are attached if a log has to be kept Regulation No. 529, point 157

If the building authority has already left notes in the BIS case about missing documents, paste that text into the form. After the site visit we can then say which of the missing items concern the electrical part.

Explanatory note Part II: the electrical part

Point 157 of Cabinet Regulation No. 529 lists what is attached to Part II of the explanatory note. This covers reports on utility network readiness, as-built drawings if anything was changed, the construction log with as-built records (except in the cases in point 120, including an owner-built house), an as-built survey plan and a temporary energy performance certificate. If the owner built the house, sub-point 157.7.1 additionally requires electrical installation test documentation.

Within 10 working days the building authority checks that there is no unauthorised construction and makes a mark on completion of the works (point 160). For an owner-built house, see the page self-built house.

Electrician’s report for the building authority and the measurement protocol

The word “report” in this context comes from sub-point 53.4 of LBN 261-15: approved reports on wire and cable resistance testing, phase-neutral loop measurements, and the resistance of the earthing loop and bonds are attached to the acceptance statement. In essence these are measurement results with a conclusion that they meet the standards.

Under point 55, these tests are carried out by a certified electrical specialist. A report on utility network readiness is a different document: it is issued by the body that gave the technical conditions, for example Sadales tīkls. Ask the building authority whether in your case it is a separate paper or is generated in BIS.

Confirmation that the building is ready for use

With a building permit, the confirmation that the building is ready for use is approved in BIS by the construction initiator, the building contractor, the construction works manager, the construction supervisor and the author’s supervisor (Cabinet Regulation No. 529, point 167.¹). Once all the documents in points 167 and 168 are received, the building authority agrees an acceptance date with the construction initiator, no later than 10 working days from submission of the documents (point 171).

Questions: Electrical installation documents

What should I know before requesting documents for the building authority?

The address, which document the construction started with (explanatory note, notification or building permit), who built the electrical installation and what the building authority has already asked for. If you write all this in the form, what is needed can be checked right at the site visit.

The building authority asks for an “electrical installation act”. Which one is that?

The name used in the authority’s letter may differ from the one in the regulations; the regulations speak of measurement reports under sub-point 53.4 of LBN 261-15 and an insulation test under point 54. If the letter mentions a specific name, attach it to the form, and after the site visit we will say what to prepare.

Do building authorities in the Riga region ask for different electrical documents than in Riga?

The regulations (Cabinet Regulation No. 529 and LBN 261-15) are the same throughout Latvia. Each building authority examines cases in its own territory, so check the specific requirements with your building authority.
