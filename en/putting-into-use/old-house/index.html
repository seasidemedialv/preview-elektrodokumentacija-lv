What the building authority looks at for an old house

For an old house, you first need to find out its status at the building authority: whether it is a registered structure or whether alterations were made without documents. Point 160 of the Building Construction Regulations (Cabinet Regulation No. 529) provides that the building authority, when examining Part II of the explanatory note, checks that there is no unauthorised construction. The building authority tells you which process applies to your house.

If an old house is accepted into use, the same LBN 261-15 points apply to the electrical part as to other buildings at acceptance into use. These are the insulation resistance test (point 54) and the sub-point 53.4 measurements, carried out by a certified electrical specialist (point 55).

Checked on 25 November 2026: Cabinet Regulation No. 529 point 160 (likumi.lv/ta/id/269164), LBN 261-15 points 28.², 53–55 (likumi.lv/ta/id/274674), Sadales tīkls: Reconnection and price list (sadalestikls.lv).

Electrical installation without a design or papers

If there is no design, the electrical installation diagram is prepared from a site visit: it is traced where each circuit from the distribution board goes, and the protective devices are marked. Such an as-built diagram is also used in the test reports, because each circuit’s result has to be linked to a specific circuit.

A concealed works record is not drawn up later for covered work, so the building authority has to be asked what it accepts instead. An old house may have cables from different years in its walls, so the documents describe what was measured, not what cannot be seen.

In the main incoming distribution board of a detached residential house, point 28.² of LBN 261-15 has since 1 July 2025 required a 300–500 mA residual current device or an alternative solution. Ask the building authority whether this applies to your old house without alteration.

If the house has extensions or a shed with electricity, they are included in the same diagram, so that the building authority can see everything connected to one incoming supply.

If the house has long been disconnected from the network

Sadales tīkls recommends this: if the electricity supply has been interrupted for a long time or the internal installation has not been tested for a long time, have it tested before applying. If the installation is not fit for use, Sadales tīkls does not restore the connection.

  • Application within 30 days after the previous user’s contract ends: reconnection without a connection fee.
  • Later: a connection fee of €34.76 (0.4 kV) and the capacity maintenance fee for the time since the contract ended, for no longer than 24 months, according to the Sadales tīkls price list, checked on 25 November 2026.
  • Sadales tīkls issues the invoice within 3 working days and restores the connection within 2 working days after payment.

What to prepare for an old house site visit

  1. Documents Anything that has survived: an old design, an inventory file, earlier reports, Sadales tīkls invoices.
  2. Building authority The authority’s letter or the BIS case, if the process has already started, so that we know which document is required.
  3. Access The distribution board, attic, basement and all buildings on the plot that have electricity.
  4. Photos A photo of the distribution board and meter in the form, so that the scope can be assessed.

If the measurements reveal damage, an electrician has to put it right before the documents. This is done by electricians for installation repair and construction work; we test and prepare the papers.

Questions: Putting an old house into use

The house has no electrical papers at all. Where do I start?

With a site visit: the electrician examines the distribution board and circuits, and after that we tell you which documents can be prepared and what the price will be. In parallel, find out at the building authority which process applies to your house.

Can old aluminium wiring be documented?

The documents describe the measurement results. If the circuits pass the insulation and loop measurements, they can be included in the report; if they fail, an electrician has to put the defects right before the documents.

Are measurements needed before reconnection?

Sadales tīkls recommends testing the installation if it has not been used or tested for a long time, and it may refuse the connection if the installation is not fit for use.
