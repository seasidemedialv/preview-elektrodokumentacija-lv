We carry out measurements and prepare test reports, as-built diagrams, installation reports and the papers for Sadales tīkls. We do not repair or install: we test the existing electrical installation and prepare the documents.

Electrical installation measurements and test reports

Testing covers insulation resistance, the phase-neutral loop (the total impedance of the circuit, which determines whether the circuit breaker will trip on a fault), earthing and, where needed, the residual current device (RCD) and lightning protection. The results are set out in a report or protocol under the name that the relevant regulation requires. More: electrical installation measurements and testing.

Electrical installation measurements and report for a flat: from €170. A single measurement, for example earthing only: price after the site visit.

Documents for putting a house into use

Point 53 of LBN 261-15 lists what is attached to the building acceptance statement if the relevant works have been carried out: drawings, equipment passports, concealed works records and approved reports on the measurements. If you built the house yourself, Cabinet Regulation No. 529 requires electrical installation test documentation. More: putting a house into use: electrics.

Measurements and documentation for putting a private house into use: from €230. As-built diagram: price after the site visit.

Papers for Sadales tīkls

For a new connection or a capacity increase, Sadales tīkls asks the customer to submit on e-st.lv a confirmation that their electrical installation has been built and is ready. Sadales tīkls states that this work is done by a qualified electrical specialist or an electrical installation company. An electrician is also needed for a switch to 3 phases and for moving the meter, and before reconnection Sadales tīkls recommends having the installation checked. More: Sadales tīkls documents for a connection.

Meter relocation, the electrician’s work: from €70. Other Sadales tīkls documents: price after the site visit, with Sadales tīkls fees according to its price list charged separately.

Solar panel and battery paperwork

For a microgenerator (up to 11.1 kW on a three-phase or 3.7 kW on a single-phase connection), Sadales tīkls accepts an inverter settings protocol only from a specialist whose certificate covers generating equipment. Sadales tīkls also requires that the installation and inverter setup be carried out by a construction specialist with a certificate for generating equipment. We do not install panels. More: putting solar panels into use.

Solar panel paperwork and inverter settings protocol: price after the site visit. The Sadales tīkls assessment fee of €78.66 is separate.

Reports on the condition of the electrical installation

The report describes the condition of the electrical installation: after a fire or flooding, in an old house, before a purchase or when a third party asks for it. A bank or a buyer may ask for such a document, and it can be useful when making a decision. More: electrical installation report.

Electrical installation report: from €180.

Who may carry out measurements and sign documents

Point 55 of LBN 261-15 states that the tests referred to in sub-point 53.4 (wire and cable resistance, phase-neutral loop, earthing) are carried out by a certified electrical specialist certified by an accredited body in line with the standard LVS EN ISO/IEC 17024. The documents are prepared and signed by a certified electrical specialist. How to check any electrician’s certificate is explained on the page certified electrician in Riga.

LBN 261-15 (Cabinet Regulation No. 294), points 53–55, likumi.lv. Checked on 25 November 2026.

What to prepare before the site visit

  • The address of the property and whether it is a flat, a private house, an outbuilding or commercial premises.
  • Who is asking for the document and what it is called in the letter or requirements (building authority, Sadales tīkls, bank, buyer).
  • Existing papers: the electrical design, an old diagram, earlier test reports, if there are any.
  • Photos of the distribution board with its door open and of the meter.
  • Access to the distribution board and the rooms on the day of the visit.

The more precisely you know which paper you need, the easier it is to state the scope of work and the price after the site visit.

What we do not do

  • We do not install solar panels, inverters, batteries or electric car chargers.
  • We do not repair faults or build electrical installations. If the test finds a defect, an electrician puts it right, for example electrician repairs and electrical installation work in Riga; after that the documents can be prepared.
  • We do not sign documents without an inspection and do not backdate concealed works records.

Questions about these documents

How quickly will you get in touch after a request?

After you send the form, we contact you within 5 minutes. Then we agree on the site visit.

Is the site visit free of charge?

No. The site visit is paid: €40. During it the electrician looks over the electrical installation on site, and afterwards we tell you which documents are needed and what they cost.

Who signs the documents?

The documents are prepared and signed by a certified electrical specialist. Construction practice certificates can be checked in the BIS register of construction specialists, while BLEEA and LEB certificates are checked using the issuer’s data.

Does Sadales tīkls require a measurement protocol for a new connection?

For a new connection or a capacity increase, Sadales tīkls asks for a confirmation on e-st.lv that the electrical installation is ready. That is a different document from a measurement protocol. To see what has to be attached in your case, check the application on e-st.lv.

Do you also work outside Riga?

Yes, in Riga and the Riga region, for example in Mārupe, Ķekava, Salaspils and Ādaži. Give the property address in the form.

Can you prepare documents if another electrician built the installation?

Yes, we test the existing electrical installation regardless of who built it. If the measurements do not meet the requirements, the defects have to be put right first, and only then can the documents be prepared.

Which document are you being asked for?

Fill in the form: name, phone and address. After you send the form, we contact you within 5 minutes.
