Five service groups

Electrical installation measurements and testing: insulation resistance, phase-neutral loop, earthing, residual current device (RCD), lightning protection and thermal camera inspection of the distribution board. The result is a report or protocol, for example for the periodic inspection under the fire safety regulations currently in force.

Documents for putting into use: electrical installation test documentation for the building authority, as-built diagram and other electrical papers for a new build, an alteration, a sauna or an old house.

Documents for Sadales tīkls: electrician’s work and papers for a new connection, a capacity increase, a switch to 3 phases, meter relocation and reconnection.

Solar panel paperwork for Sadales tīkls: inverter settings protocol and confirmation for a microgenerator or battery, if an installer has already installed the panels.

Electrical installation report: condition assessment before buying a house, for a bank, after a fire or flood, for old wiring and for an electric car charger connection.

Questions about these documents

Do you also build or repair electrical installations?

No. We test the existing electrical installation and prepare the documents. If the test finds a defect, an electrician puts it right, and after that the papers can be prepared.

Who signs the prepared documents?

The documents are prepared and signed by a certified electrical specialist.

Which places do you work in?

In Riga and the Riga region. The list of towns and neighbourhoods is on the page areas.
