This covers measurements and test reports, papers for putting a building into use, as-built diagrams, documents for Sadales tīkls and reports on the condition of electrical installations.

Which documents we prepare

  • We measure insulation resistance, the phase-neutral loop and earthing, test residual current devices (RCD) and prepare a report or protocol.
  • We prepare electrical papers for putting a house, sauna or commercial premises into use.
  • We draw an as-built diagram (electrical installation diagram) from what is actually on site.
  • We prepare the customer-side papers for Sadales tīkls: new connection, capacity increase, phases, meter relocation.
  • We prepare the inverter settings protocol for solar panels and batteries installed by an installer.
  • We write a report on the condition of the electrical installation.

The documents are prepared and signed by a certified electrical specialist.

What this service does not include

We do not install solar panels, inverters, batteries or chargers and do not repair faults. Installation is done by the installer; we test and prepare the papers. If the test finds a defect, an electrician puts it right first, for example electrician repairs in Riga. We do not sign documents without an inspection.

From request to documents

We accept requests only through the form, in Latvian, English or Russian. After you send the form, we contact you within 5 minutes. Then comes the site visit, which is paid: €40. After the site visit we tell you which documents are needed and quote the price.

Prices are public: measurements and a report for a flat from €170, measurements and documentation for putting a private house into use from €230, an installation report from €180. Questions: About Elektrodokumentācija

Can you also repair what the test finds?

No, we test and prepare documents. An electrician puts the defect right, and after that the measurements can be repeated and the papers prepared.

Can the site visit be skipped?

No. The documents describe a particular electrical installation, so we do not sign them without an inspection. The site visit is paid: €40.

Do you work with businesses?

Yes, with businesses and also with managers of apartment buildings. We quote the price for these properties after the site visit.
