The fire safety regulations currently in force (Cabinet Regulation No. 238, in force until 1 January 2027) do not name a construction specialist’s certificate for the periodic inspection once every 10 years. However, the report in Annex 6 comes with a copy of the electrical safety group card of the person doing the work, a copy of the instrument’s calibration or verification certificate and the electrical installation diagram (points 1.1–1.3 of the notes to the annex). If a property manager or another client asks for the report, ask what kind of person they will accept to do the work.

What certificates electricians have

  • Construction practice certificate in the design of electrical installations (Cabinet Regulation No. 169, sub-point 8.5): for preparing an electrical design.
  • Construction practice certificate in managing and supervising electrical installation construction work (Cabinet Regulation No. 169, sub-point 8.11). These certificates are registered in the BIS register of construction specialists.
  • Certificates in the non-regulated sphere, for example “Elektroietaišu montāža” (installation of electrical equipment) or “Elektroietaišu izbūve” (construction of electrical installations), issued by the BLEEA or LEB certification bodies.
  • Generation specialisation: the certificate programme name includes power stations or generating equipment. This is exactly what Sadales tīkls requires for microgenerator papers.

An electrical safety group card is an occupational safety document. Sadales tīkls states that it does not treat it as a certificate for installing generating equipment.

How to check an electrician’s certificate

  1. BIS register Open the register of construction specialists on bis.gov.lv and search by name or certificate number. Sadales tīkls points to it as well. BLEEA and LEB certificates are checked using the issuer’s data.
  2. Scope Compare the scope of the certificate with the work. A designer’s certificate is not the same as a construction work manager’s certificate.
  3. Validity Make sure the certificate is valid.
  4. Generating equipment For solar panels, Sadales tīkls recommends checking four things: the issuer is BLEEA or LEB, the programme name includes the scope and the generation direction, the period of validity, and that the data match the person.

So that we understand which certificate and which document are needed in your case, include the following in the form:

  • the address and type of the property (flat, private house, sauna, commercial premises);

Questions: Certified electrician in Riga

May any electrician carry out measurements for putting a house into use?

Point 55 of LBN 261-15 states that the tests referred to in sub-point 53.4 are carried out by a certified electrical specialist certified by an accredited body. Without such a certificate, these measurements are not carried out for the acceptance statement.

Is an electrical safety group card enough for solar panels?

No. Sadales tīkls states that an electrical safety group card is not a certificate for microgenerator work. A BLEEA or LEB certificate with a generating equipment specialisation is needed.

Where can an electrician’s certificate be seen?

A construction practice certificate is shown in the BIS register of construction specialists on bis.gov.lv, while BLEEA and LEB certificates are checked using the issuer’s data. You can ask the electrician for the certificate number and check the scope and validity.

Can a certified electrician sign a report without an inspection?

No. A measurement report describes a particular electrical installation on a particular day, so we do not sign documents without a site visit and measurements.
