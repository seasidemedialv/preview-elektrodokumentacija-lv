Privacy policy

This privacy policy describes how the website elektrodokumentacija.lv processes the personal data you send through the request form and what rights you have under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) (EU) 2016/679.

Prepared by Elektrodokumentācija. Updated 25 November 2026. Sources: 3.

The website has no user accounts and no payments; you provide data only through the request form; technical data and cookies are described further down this page.

Controller

Controller of personal data: Elektrodokumentācija (elektrodokumentacija.lv).

If you have questions about data processing or wish to exercise your rights, get in touch using the form on the contact page.

Which data we process

  • In the request form: name, phone number, address, chosen service, when the work is needed, and the description you write (for example, the type of property, who is asking for the document, the name of the document).
  • Technical data: IP address, browser and device information and the time of the request, which the hosting provider processes to keep the website running and secure.
  • Cookies and browser storage, as described in the “Cookies” section below.

Please do not enter data in the form that is not needed to assess the work, especially health or other sensitive data. If you later send the electrician photos, for example of the distribution board or meter, make sure they show no people, documents, screens or other things you do not want to show.

Why and on what basis

Purpose Data Legal basis
Replying to the request, getting in touch, quoting the price and agreeing on the work Form data Steps taken at your request before entering into a contract, GDPR Article 6(1)(b)
Carrying out the work Form data and property address Performance of a contract, GDPR Article 6(1)(b)
Meeting accounting and tax requirements Transaction data Legal obligation, GDPR Article 6(1)(c)
Keeping records of requests, resolving disputes, website security Form and technical data Legitimate interests, GDPR Article 6(1)(f)
Visit statistics, if used Cookie data Consent, GDPR Article 6(1)(a)

Who receives the data

  • The electrician who carries out the site visit and prepares the documents. The request form data is passed to this electrician so that they can contact you and do the work; they receive only the data needed for that.
  • Processors we use to run the website and handle requests: providers of website hosting, form processing and automation, data storage and messaging services. They process the data only on the controller’s instructions and in line with GDPR Article 28.
  • State authorities only where laws and regulations require it.

We do not sell, publish or pass your data to third parties for marketing purposes.

Data transfers outside the European Economic Area

Some service providers may process data outside the European Economic Area. In such cases the transfer takes place only in compliance with the requirements of GDPR Chapter V, for example on the basis of a European Commission adequacy decision or standard contractual clauses.

How long we keep the data

  • Requests that have not led to work are kept for no longer than 24 months after the last contact and then deleted.
  • Data related to completed work is kept for as long as accounting and tax laws require, or until the limitation period for possible claims ends.
  • Technical data is kept for as long as necessary for website security.

You may ask for the data to be deleted earlier if the law does not require it to be kept.

Your rights

  • to access your data and receive a copy of it;
  • to correct inaccurate data;
  • to ask for the data to be deleted;
  • to ask for processing to be restricted;
  • to object to processing based on legitimate interests;
  • to receive your data in a portable format;
  • to withdraw consent at any time, if the processing is based on it.

We answer a request without undue delay and within one month at the latest, as the GDPR provides. If you believe your data is being processed incorrectly, we invite you to contact us first; you also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Data State Inspectorate (www.dvi.gov.lv).

Cookies

A cookie is a small text file that a website stores in your browser. The browser’s local storage works in a similar way, and the same rules apply to it.

At present the website elektrodokumentacija.lv does not use cookies: there are no statistics, advertising or social network tools, and the request form works without cookies. That is why no cookie notice is shown on the website. If we add visit statistics in the future, they will work only with your consent, a cookie notice will appear on the website and this page will be updated.

In your browser’s privacy settings (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge) you can view, delete or block cookies.

Storing information on a user’s device is permitted with the user’s consent; consent is not required if it is necessary to provide a service requested by the user. Source: Law on Information Society Services, Section 7.¹ (likumi.lv). Checked on 25 November 2026.

Is providing data compulsory?

Providing data is voluntary, but without a phone number or other contact details we cannot reply to a request. We do not carry out automated decision-making or profiling that would produce legal effects for you.

Changes

We may supplement this policy if the services used or the laws and regulations change. The current version is available on this page. Version: 25 November 2026.
