When electrical installations have to be tested

When electrical installations have to be tested is determined by three things: the deadline in the regulations, changes to the installation itself and your situation, for example a purchase or a reconnection.

The fire safety regulations currently in force (Cabinet Regulation No. 238) require testing once every 10 years, and the construction standard before putting into use and after significant changes. In other cases testing can be useful for knowing the condition of the installation.

How often electrical installations have to be tested

Point 56 of Cabinet Regulation No. 238: electrical installations, including earthing and lightning protection devices, are tested once every 10 years. If the property has an explosive atmosphere, once every two years; if a chemically aggressive environment, once a year. Together with this, distribution board contacts are checked with a thermal camera (point 58).

Regulation No. 238 is in force until 1 January 2027 and ceases to have effect on 2 January 2027. The text of the new regulations had not yet been published on 25 November 2026, so the 10-year interval may change. How this test is carried out: periodic electrical installation testing.

Checked on 25 November 2026: Cabinet Regulation No. 238 (likumi.lv/ta/id/281646), LBN 261-15 (likumi.lv/ta/id/274674), Sadales tīkls “Reconnection” (sadalestikls.lv/lv/piesleguma-atjaunosana).

When to test the wiring in a house after a long interruption

If a house has been without electricity for a long time, Sadales tīkls recommends choosing a certified electrician and testing the internal electrical installation before applying. If it is not fit for use, Sadales tīkls does not restore the connection.

There is no charge for reconnection if the application is submitted within 30 days after the previous user has ended the contract with the electricity supplier. Later you pay a connection fee of €34.76 (0.4 kV) and the capacity maintenance fee for the time since the contract ended, for no more than 24 months, according to the Sadales tīkls price list, checked on 25 November 2026. More: reconnection.

Before a new connection, a capacity increase or solar panels

For a new connection or a capacity increase, Sadales tīkls requires the customer’s installation to be prepared by a qualified electrical specialist or an electrical installation company, and the customer submits a readiness confirmation on e-st.lv. This is the moment when it is worth testing the existing installation too, because the load will grow after connection.

For solar panels and a battery up to 11.1 kW (three phases) or 3.7 kW (one phase), Sadales tīkls accepts the inverter settings protocol only from a specialist whose certificate covers generating equipment. It also requires such a specialist to carry out the installation and adjust the inverter. More: connecting a microgenerator.

Before buying or renting out

When buying a house or flat, electrical installation testing can be useful: you know the condition before the transaction and can judge whether you will need to invest in repairs. If a bank, insurer or seller asks for a document, ask which one exactly. There is a page on this, report before buying a house. When renting out a flat, a report can be useful as a recorded condition before the tenant moves in.

Signs that testing is needed: when not to wait for the deadline

Some signs mean an electrician is needed now, not according to the schedule:

  • protective devices trip repeatedly for no visible reason;
  • a socket, switch or distribution board is warm, has changed colour or smells of burning;
  • walls, the distribution board or cables have been affected after a flood or fire;
  • the house has old wiring without earthing and without residual current devices.

If anything is getting hot or burning, that is a repair matter: contact an electrician, for example electrician services in Riga. Testing and documents are the next step after the repair. There are separate pages for after flooding and after a fire.

How to prepare for electrical installation testing

  1. Find out who the document is for The building authority, periodic testing, Sadales tīkls or a buyer. This determines which measurements and which document are needed.
  2. Find the papers The previous report, a design or diagram, the documents the electrician handed over after a renovation.
  3. Photograph the distribution board A photo with the door open, and of the meter, to attach to the form.
  4. Provide access Keys to distribution boards, the basement, the attic and outbuildings. Arrange access to the stairwell board with the building manager.
  5. Plan for a power cut Circuits are switched off for some of the measurements. Save your computer work and think about the fridge, heating or server.

What the electrician needs during the visit: free access to the distribution board, the rooms with sockets and the earthing point, and a person who knows which circuits supply what.

Questions about these documents

How often does a flat’s electrical installation have to be tested?

Under the fire safety regulations currently in force (Cabinet Regulation No. 238), once every 10 years. The regulations are in force until 1 January 2027.

Is testing needed after replacing sockets?

Point 52 of LBN 261-15 speaks of testing after significant changes. A new circuit or a distribution board replacement is a change after which testing is worthwhile. If you are unsure whether your work is a “significant change”, ask the building authority.

Does the electrical installation have to be tested before connecting solar panels?

Sadales tīkls requires a confirmation of microgenerator readiness and an inverter settings protocol. Testing the installation can be useful for knowing the condition of the distribution board and earthing beforehand.

What if there is no previous report?

Then it is not known when testing was done or with what results. A new test becomes the reference point for the next deadline.
