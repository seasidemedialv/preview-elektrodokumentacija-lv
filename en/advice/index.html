Questions about these documents

How often is the advice updated?

Each article has the date on which the regulations were checked. If the regulations change, for example when the new fire safety regulations come into force, the article has to be checked again.

Does the advice replace an answer from the building authority or Sadales tīkls?

No. If the question depends on the particular building or municipality, ask the building authority or Sadales tīkls.

Where can I find the text of the regulations?

Each article has a link to the source: likumi.lv or sadalestikls.lv.
