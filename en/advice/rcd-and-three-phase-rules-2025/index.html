LBN 261-15 amendments 2025 - RCD and three-phase incoming supply

The 2025 amendments to LBN 261-15 (Cabinet Regulation No. 355 of 17 June 2025, in force from 1 July 2025) affect two things that concern house and flat owners: residual current devices (RCDs) and incoming supplies above 32 A.

In a new or altered installation, sockets and lighting need a 30 mA device, a private house incoming supply a 300–500 mA device or an alternative solution, and in a residential building a supply above 32 A is connected only with three phases.

What changed, in brief

Point Requirement What it applies to
28.¹ Residual current protection of no more than 30 mA General sockets up to 32 A, sockets for outdoor equipment up to 32 A, household lighting circuits
28.² A 300–500 mA device on the common incoming supply or an alternative solution The main incoming distribution board of detached residential houses
28.³ The class of the device is chosen according to the type of equipment to be connected All circuits with a residual current device
8.3 An incoming supply with a protective device above 32 A only as a three-phase supply Residential buildings

Checked on 25 November 2026: LBN 261-15 points 28.¹–28.³ as worded by Cabinet Regulation No. 355 of 17 June 2025, and point 8.3, likumi.lv/ta/id/274674.

RCD requirements 2025: where 30 mA is needed

An RCD, or residual current device, disconnects a circuit if part of the current leaks outside the conductors, for example through a person or damaged equipment. Point 28.¹ names three types of circuit that must be protected with no more than 30 mA:

  • general-purpose sockets up to 32 A used by people who are not specially trained in electrical matters;
  • sockets up to 32 A for connecting portable equipment used outdoors;
  • lighting circuits in household electrical installations.

Note that the list also includes household lighting circuits, not only sockets.

A 300–500 mA device on a private house incoming supply

Point 28.² applies to detached residential houses. In the main incoming distribution board, residual current protection of 300–500 mA has to be installed on the common incoming supply, or an alternative technical protection solution provided. This device does not replace the 30 mA devices on the circuits; these are two different levels.

The point does not list which solution is suitable as an alternative. If a designer or electrician proposes another solution, ask for it to be described in the documentation.

Three-phase incoming supply if the protection is above 32 A

Point 8.3: in a residential building, an electrical installation with a main protective device greater than 32 A is connected to the system operator’s network only as a three-phase supply. If a single-phase incoming supply needs more than 32 A, for example because of a heat pump or charger, you have to switch to three phases.

A capacity change is applied for to Sadales tīkls on e-st.lv. If the network does not have to be altered, a connection fee of €117.21 (0.4 kV) is paid for the capacity increase according to the Sadales tīkls price list, checked on 25 November 2026. A flat owner must attach the building manager’s written consent to the application. Steps and documents: from 1 phase to 3 phases.

Checked on 25 November 2026: Sadales tīkls “Load changes” (sadalestikls.lv/lv/slodzes-izmainas) and the price list (sadalestikls.lv/lv/cenradis).

Device class: point 28.³

Point 28.³ requires the class of the residual current protective device to be chosen according to what equipment will be connected to the circuit. This matters for circuits with an inverter, an electric car charger, a heat pump or other equipment with electronics. The required class may be stated in the equipment manufacturer’s instructions; check this before the device is installed and keep the instructions with the documents.

Switching to three phases: what to submit to Sadales tīkls

A capacity increase is a paid service. The amount depends on whether Sadales tīkls has to alter the network. The application is submitted on e-st.lv.

After the electrician has prepared the incoming supply and distribution board, the customer submits on e-st.lv a confirmation that the installation is ready. This is not a measurement protocol; the difference is explained on the page confirmation for Sadales tīkls.

Does an existing installation have to be altered

The amendments are in a construction standard that is applied when installing or altering electrical installations. Point 52 requires testing before putting into use and after significant changes. If you are altering the distribution board or adding new circuits, plan them to the new requirements.

These points do not say directly whether a house where nothing is being changed has to be altered. Ask the building authority about this if it matters, for example before putting a house into use.

What a house owner should do

  1. Look at the distribution board Whether the socket and lighting circuits have a residual current device and whether the incoming supply has a 300–500 mA device.
  2. Check the incoming supply What the main protective device is and how many phases there are. If you plan more than 32 A, three phases are needed.
  3. Order a test Residual current device testing shows whether the devices are in place and trip.
  4. Installation Missing devices are installed by an electrician, for example electrical installation work in Riga. We test and prepare the papers.

Questions: LBN 261-15 amendments 2025

Do lighting circuits also need an RCD?

Yes. Point 28.¹ (the wording in force from 1 July 2025) requires protection of no more than 30 mA for household lighting circuits too.

Does a flat need a 300–500 mA incoming device?

Point 28.² speaks of detached residential houses. The 30 mA requirements of point 28.¹ apply to a new or altered flat installation.

Can a single-phase incoming supply be increased to 40 A?

In a residential building, under point 8.3 an incoming supply with a protective device above 32 A is connected only with three phases.

Does a 300–500 mA device replace the 30 mA devices?

No. The point 28.² device is on the common incoming supply, while the 30 mA protection of point 28.¹ is needed for socket and lighting circuits. These are two separate levels.

How much does switching to three phases cost at Sadales tīkls?

If the network does not have to be altered, the connection fee is €117.21 (0.4 kV) according to the Sadales tīkls price list, checked on 25 November 2026. The electrician’s work and documents are separate; we name their price after the site visit.
