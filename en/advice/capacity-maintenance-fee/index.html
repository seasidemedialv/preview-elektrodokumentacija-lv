Capacity maintenance fee - when it starts and how to change it

The capacity maintenance fee is the monthly Sadales tīkls charge for the connection capacity reserved for you, regardless of how much electricity you use.

It starts when you submit the confirmation of installation readiness on e-st.lv, even if the voltage has not yet been switched on. So it is worth deciding when to submit the confirmation and what capacity you really need.

Why there is a capacity maintenance fee even if you use no electricity

Sadales tīkls keeps the connection ready for the capacity you have applied for. So the fee is calculated for the connection, not for kilowatt-hours. The specific amount is set by the Sadales tīkls tariff; before deciding, check the current tariff at sadalestikls.lv.

When Sadales tīkls starts calculating the fee

Situation What happens to the fee
New connection Starts after the confirmation is submitted on e-st.lv, even if the voltage has not yet been switched on
Reconnection within 30 days after the contract ends Reconnection takes place without a charge for it
Later reconnection A connection fee of €34.76 (0.4 kV) and the capacity maintenance fee for the whole time since the contract ended, for no more than 24 months
Capacity reduction Takes effect the day after the application, with no charge for the reduction
Restoring capacity after a reduction A paid change

Capacity maintenance fee for a new connection

This has to be borne in mind for a new build or on an empty plot of land. Sadales tīkls builds its part, and if the owner submits the confirmation while the house is not yet occupied, the capacity maintenance fee starts from that moment.

If no network has to be built, the capacity is provided within 15 days after the invoice is paid. Once the confirmation is submitted, Sadales tīkls makes contact within 2 working days about installing the meter. These are Sadales tīkls deadlines, and they help to plan when the confirmation is really needed.

The confirmation means that the installation is ready for the voltage to be switched on. So submit it when the installation on your side is really finished. If you need electricity for construction work, compare with a temporary electricity connection for a building site, which requires a valid building permit. More on the steps: electricity connection for a land plot.

An inherited or empty house: 30 days and 24 months

If the previous user ended the contract with the electricity supplier and you apply for reconnection within 30 days, Sadales tīkls restores the connection without a charge for it. Later you have to pay the connection fee and the capacity maintenance fee for the period since the contract ended, but for no more than 24 months. Reconnection is possible if the network is technically suitable, the capacity is available and neither the load nor the number of phases is increased.

So with an inheritance or a purchase it is worth watching the deadline from the very start. Sadales tīkls also recommends testing a long-unused installation before reconnection: restoring an electricity connection.

How to reassess connection capacity

  1. Add up the loads Write down which large appliances run at the same time: heating, cooker, water heater, charger.
  2. Compare with the connection The Sadales tīkls advice says that a typical private house connection is three phases at 20–25 A (13.8–17.3 kW) and that a smart electric car charger with dynamic load management often makes it possible to manage without increasing capacity.
  3. Apply for a reduction On e-st.lv; it takes effect the next day.
  4. Think about the future If you need the capacity back later, it will be a paid increase.

For a flat, capacity changes require the building manager’s written consent.

Checked on 25 November 2026: Sadales tīkls, “Load changes” and “Charging an electric car at a private house” (sadalestikls.lv).

Solar panels, flats and the capacity fee

A solar panel owner has to choose capacity not only for consumption but also for the system: Sadales tīkls allows capacity to be reduced only as far as the phases and capacity needed by the system are kept. Sadales tīkls states that there is no distribution fee for electricity that you generate and consume immediately.

For a flat, capacity changes are likewise applied for on e-st.lv, but the building manager’s written consent is attached to the application.

Checked on 25 November 2026: Sadales tīkls, “Load changes” and “Settlement options for electricity producers” (sadalestikls.lv).

When you need an electrician before deciding

If you reduce capacity, the main protective device becomes smaller; make sure the large loads in the house will not trip it. If you increase capacity, it has to be checked whether the incoming cable and distribution board can carry the larger current, and above 32 A a residential building needs three phases (LBN 261-15 point 8.3).

Questions: Capacity maintenance fee

Why pay for capacity if nobody lives in the house?

The fee is for the connection capacity that Sadales tīkls maintains, not for consumption. If the house stands empty for a long time, check in the Sadales tīkls tariff whether a smaller connection capacity would cost you less.

Does the capacity maintenance fee start as soon as there is a contract with Sadales tīkls?

Sadales tīkls states that it starts after the confirmation is submitted on e-st.lv, even if the voltage has not yet been switched on.

Can capacity be reduced temporarily?

A reduction takes effect the next day, and there is no charge for it. Increasing it back is paid. For connections in the 0.4 kV network above 100 A and in the 6–20 kV network there is also a temporary reduction for up to 12 months, after which the load is restored without charge if the Sadales tīkls conditions are met.

For how many months must I pay when restoring a long-disconnected connection?

For the period since the contract ended, but for no more than 24 months, if you apply for reconnection later than 30 days.
