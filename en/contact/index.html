Contact

You contact us through the request form. After you send the form, we contact you within 5 minutes, clarify the situation and agree on the site visit.

Send your request

The site visit is paid: €40.

After you send the form, we contact you within 5 minutes

By sending the form you agree to the processing of your data so that we can contact you. Privacy policy

You can fill in the form in Latvian, English or Russian.

What to write in the request

  • Address: the town or parish and the street. We work in Riga and the Riga region.
  • Situation: for example, the house has to be put into use, more capacity is needed, solar panels are installed, the periodic inspection is due.
  • Which document is required and by whom: building authority, Sadales tīkls, property manager, bank or buyer. If there is a letter with requirements, copy the name of the document.
  • Photos of the distribution board with its door open, if you can send them after we have been in touch.
  • When the document is needed: choose the timing in the form.

The form also has a choice of service. If you do not know which service suits you, choose “Other” and describe the situation.

What happens after the request

  1. Contact We get in touch and clarify which document is needed and for whom.
  2. Site visit We agree on the site visit. The visit is paid: €40.
  3. Documents and price After the site visit we tell you which documents are needed and quote the price.

Prices cover the electrician’s work and the documents. Sadales tīkls and building authority fees are separate. Price list: prices.

Questions: Contact

Why is there no phone number?

We accept requests only through the form. With it, the address, situation and required document are written down straight away, and nothing gets lost. After you send the form, we contact you within 5 minutes.

Which languages can I write in?

Latvian, English or Russian.

Do I need to know the exact name of the document?

No, it is enough to describe who is asking for the document and why. If the name is in a letter or on e-st.lv, copy it; that helps.

Can I ask for just a price, without a site visit?

In the form you can choose “Later, just the price for now”. We quote the final price after the site visit, because the scope can only be seen on site. The published prices are on the prices page.
