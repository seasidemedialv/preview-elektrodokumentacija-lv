Sadales tīkls recommends having a certified electrician check the internal installation before applying, if it has not been tested for a long time. We carry out this test in Riga and the Riga region and prepare documents on the condition of the installation.

30 days decide the fee

If the reconnection application is submitted within 30 days after the previous user terminated the contract with the supplier, Sadales tīkls restores the connection without charging a fee. Later you have to pay the connection fee, €34.76 for a 0.4 kV low-voltage connection, and the capacity maintenance fee for the whole time since the contract was terminated, but for no more than 24 months.

Reconnection is possible if the network is technically suitable, the capacity is available and neither the load nor the number of phases is increased. If more capacity is needed at the same time, that is a capacity increase.

According to the Sadales tīkls price list and the page “Reconnection”, checked on 25 November 2026 (sadalestikls.lv).

Reconnection for an inherited house

An inherited house or one unoccupied for years is exactly the case Sadales tīkls describes: if the electricity supply was interrupted for a long time or the internal installation has gone untested for long, choose a certified electrician and check it before applying. If the installation is not fit for use, Sadales tīkls cannot restore the connection.

In an old house the test looks at the distribution board, incoming supply, insulation resistance and earthing, and we record what has been found. If a written assessment is needed, for example, to sell the house, see old wiring. An electrician puts defects right before the voltage is switched on.

The fire safety regulations currently in force (Cabinet Regulation No. 238, in force until 1 January 2027) require electrical installation testing once every 10 years with a report under Annex 6. If the inherited house has no such report or it is old, the test before reconnection can be done so that it also serves this duty, see periodic testing.

Checked on 25 November 2026: Cabinet Regulation No. 238 of 19 April 2016, points 56 and 57, likumi.lv/ta/id/281646.

How disconnected electricity is restored

  1. Installation test We test the internal installation if it has not been used or tested for a long time.
  2. Supplier and application You choose an electricity supplier and apply to receive electricity, and the supplier submits the reconnection application to Sadales tīkls. You submit the e-st.lv application yourself if you want to reduce the load or number of phases, or if the property has no meter.
  3. Invoice Sadales tīkls issues an invoice within 3 working days.
  4. Reconnection After payment, Sadales tīkls restores the connection within 2 working days.
  5. Supplier If you have no agreement with a supplier yet, sign a supply contract; until then, electricity is delivered as supply of last resort.

If the meter is installed but there is no voltage because a seal has been fitted, removal of the seal and its replacement are requested separately; Sadales tīkls gets in touch within 2 working days, and replacing the seal is paid. The deadlines are those of Sadales tīkls.

What to prepare

  • Property documents and access to your e-st.lv account.
  • Information on how long the house was without electricity and whether there was flooding, frost or damage.
  • Access to the distribution board and all rooms.
  • If you know it: when the installation was last tested.

Questions: Restoring an electricity connection

Is an installation test compulsory before reconnection?

Sadales tīkls recommends it if the installation has not been tested for a long time, and does not connect it if the installation is not fit for use. Plan the test before you apply for electricity.

What will I pay if the house was disconnected for three years?

According to the Sadales tīkls price list: the connection fee of €34.76 (0.4 kV) and the capacity maintenance fee for no more than 24 months.

Can I reconnect with more capacity straight away?

Reconnection assumes the same load and phases. More capacity is a separate capacity increase application.

What happens if the test shows the installation is not fit for use?

Sadales tīkls does not connect an unfit installation. An electrician puts the defects right, and the installation is then tested again.
