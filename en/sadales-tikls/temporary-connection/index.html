Building site electricity connection: why it is different

Point 27 of LBN 261-15 lists places where special requirements according to standards apply to electrical installations, and one of them is construction sites (sub-point 27.4). On a building site the cables are outdoors, equipment is moved around and many people work with it. So the condition and protection of the site distribution board have to be checked before work begins.

For outdoor sockets up to 32 A used to connect portable equipment, point 28.¹ of LBN 261-15 requires a residual current device (RCD) with a rated operating current of no more than 30 mA.

Checked on 25 November 2026: LBN 261-15 sub-point 27.4 and point 28.¹, likumi.lv/ta/id/274674.

From a temporary to a permanent connection

The temporary connection ends, but the house needs a permanent one. Its steps are described on the page new Sadales tīkls connection: application, construction, your installation and the confirmation on e-st.lv. Request it in good time, because a permanent connection has its own steps and Sadales tīkls deadlines.

When the house is put into use, the documents under point 53 of LBN 261-15 are attached for the building authority, if the relevant work has been done. If the installation is covered by finishing, the concealed works records must be drawn up before it is covered, because they cannot be drawn up later. More: concealed works records.

If you build the house yourself as the builder (Construction Law Section 19.¹, paragraph three), the Building Construction Regulations (Regulation No. 529) require “electrical installation test documentation” for putting it into use (sub-points 157.7.1 and 168.1). More on this: self-built house.

Temporary power supply for construction: how it goes

  1. Building permit Make sure the building permit is valid; without it the temporary connection is not granted.
  2. Application on e-st.lv You request the temporary connection and the capacity needed.
  3. Site distribution board An installer or electrician installs the distribution board; we check it.
  4. Application for the permanent connection Request it in good time, before the temporary connection period ends.

What to prepare

  • The building permit and its validity period.
  • The capacity needed: cranes, concrete mixers, heaters.
  • The place where the site distribution board will stand.
  • If there is one: the design of the house’s electrical installation.

Questions about these documents

Can I get a temporary connection without a building permit?

No. Sadales tīkls grants a temporary connection for a building site with a valid building permit.

How long can a temporary connection be used?

For a building site, up to 24 months. For a short event, up to 31 days, applying 10 working days in advance.

Can a temporary connection be turned into a permanent one?

A permanent connection is a separate application with its own steps. Request it in good time, before the temporary connection period ends.

How much does a temporary connection for a building site cost?

According to the Sadales tīkls price list, checked on 25 November 2026: €117.21, or €217.52 with additional installation work. The electrician’s work on your side is separate.

Does the site distribution board need a residual current device?

Point 28.¹ of LBN 261-15 requires a device of no more than 30 mA for sockets up to 32 A to which portable equipment used outdoors is connected.
