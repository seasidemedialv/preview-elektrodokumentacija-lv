If a network has to be built to the land plot

  1. Technical requirements Sadales tīkls issues the technical requirements and approximate costs within 3 days; it charges no fee for them.
  2. Designer Within 60 days you choose a network designer.
  3. BIS and the building authority Sadales tīkls creates the construction intention in BIS, and the building authority approves the design.
  4. Builder and payment Sadales tīkls chooses a builder (about 15 days), you pay the invoice, and only then does construction work start.

The deadlines are those of Sadales tīkls. The design is approved by the building authority of the municipality where the land plot is located, for example, in Ādaži or Olaine.

When to submit the confirmation for an empty land plot

After Sadales tīkls has built its distribution cabinet, the next step is yours: the cable from it to the house distribution board and the internal installation. A certified electrician builds it, and then you submit the confirmation on e-st.lv.

Sadales tīkls states that after the confirmation is submitted, the capacity maintenance fee starts to be charged, even if the voltage has not yet been switched on. If there is no house yet but you need electricity for construction work, check whether a temporary electricity connection for a building site suits you; it needs a valid building permit.

An electricity connection for a flat and for land is different

A flat usually does not need a new connection: only the capacity changes, and the building manager’s written consent is attached to the application, see capacity increase. For a land plot without a building, what matters is the distance to the line, the capacity and when construction will start.

How many amperes to request

The construction fee rises with the number of amperes, so it is worth choosing the capacity according to the planned appliances rather than “with a margin”. The Sadales tīkls advice mentions that a typical private house connection has three phases with 20–25 A (13.8–17.3 kW).

If the main protective device in a residential building is to be above 32 A, LBN 261-15 point 8.3 requires a three-phase connection. If you are planning solar panels, the microgenerator limit is 11.1 kW on three phases and 3.7 kW on one phase.

Checked on 25 November 2026: LBN 261-15 point 8.3 (likumi.lv/ta/id/274674); Sadales tīkls, “Electric car charging at a private house” and “Connecting a microgenerator”.

What to prepare

  • The cadastral number or address of the land plot.
  • The planned capacity: whether there will be a heat pump, electric heating, a charger, solar panels.
  • The planned location of the house and distribution board.
  • The Sadales tīkls reply, if an application has already been submitted.

How much does the electrician’s work for a connection cost

  • Documents and electrician’s work for Sadales tīkls: price after the site visit. The price depends on: whether the incoming supply and distribution board already suit the new capacity; whether you need to change the main protective device or switch to 3 phases; whether measurements are needed; Sadales tīkls fees according to its price list (separate).
  • Site visit: €40.

Prices cover the electrician’s work and the documents. Sadales tīkls and building authority fees are separate.

Questions: Electricity connection for a land plot

How can I find out the distance to the Sadales tīkls line?

For connections up to 100 A and 300 m, Sadales tīkls offers a map on which you can calculate the fee yourself. An e-st.lv application gives the exact answer.

Can a connection for a land plot be requested before there is a building permit?

A permanent connection is requested on e-st.lv. For a temporary connection for a building site, Sadales tīkls requires a valid building permit.

Does Sadales tīkls cover part of the construction?

Sadales tīkls states that it co-finances 50 % of the construction costs if the customer agrees to the co-financing conditions.
