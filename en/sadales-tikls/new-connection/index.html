New Sadales tīkls connection - what to do on your side

A new Sadales tīkls connection happens in two parts: Sadales tīkls builds the network up to its own distribution cabinet, while you prepare the installation from that cabinet to the house and submit the confirmation.

New Sadales tīkls connection step by step

  1. Application on e-st.lv You state the address and the required capacity. For connections up to 100 A and up to 300 m from a low-voltage line, you can calculate the fee yourself on the Sadales tīkls map.
  2. Reply from Sadales tīkls If the network does not have to be rebuilt, within 3 days Sadales tīkls offers the final costs and a contract. If it does, within 3 days it issues technical requirements and approximate costs.
  3. Design, if something has to be built Within 60 days you choose a network designer, Sadales tīkls creates the construction intention in BIS, the building authority approves the design, and then Sadales tīkls chooses a builder (about 15 days).
  4. Payment and construction Construction work starts only after the invoice is paid. If the network does not need to be built, the capacity is provided within 15 days of payment.
  5. Your installation and the confirmation A certified electrician prepares the internal installation and the cable to the distribution cabinet, and you submit the confirmation on e-st.lv.
  6. Meter After the confirmation, Sadales tīkls gets in touch about installing the meter within 2 working days.

Checked on 25 November 2026: Sadales tīkls, “Permanent connection” (sadalestikls.lv/lv/pastavigs-pieslegums). The deadlines are those of Sadales tīkls.

What Sadales tīkls expects from the customer side

Sadales tīkls writes that the customer’s installation may be built only by a qualified electrical specialist or an electrical installation company. You are responsible for the incoming cable from the Sadales tīkls distribution cabinet to the house distribution board, the board itself and the circuits. The confirmation means that all of this has been built and is ready for voltage to be switched on.

We check this part before the confirmation: whether the main protective device matches the requested capacity, whether the distribution board is finished, and whether there is earthing and residual current devices (RCD) where LBN 261-15 requires them. Installation is done by an installer or electrician; we test and prepare the papers.

Electricity connection for a new build: two document routes

A new build needs electrical papers for two institutions. For Sadales tīkls the confirmation on e-st.lv is enough. When the house is put into use, point 53 of LBN 261-15 requires a protocol with test conclusions for the building authority, and point 55 lays down that the phase-neutral loop, earthing and conductor resistance tests are carried out by a certified electrical specialist.

If the main protective device in the new build is to be above 32 A, LBN 261-15 point 8.3 requires a three-phase connection. It is worth deciding this already in the application, so that you do not have to request a capacity increase a year later. The measurements for putting into use can be done during the same site visit, see putting a house into use.

Checked on 25 November 2026: LBN 261-15 (Cabinet Regulation No. 294 of 9 June 2015), points 8.3, 53 and 55, likumi.lv/ta/id/274674.

Sadales tīkls fees for a new connection

According to the Sadales tīkls price list, checked on 25 November 2026: connecting the customer’s installation €117.21 (0.4 kV), with additional installation work €217.52. Building the network is charged per ampere: €101.36 per A up to 50 m, €132.68 per A from 51 to 150 m and €184.15 per A from 151 to 300 m. Sadales tīkls co-finances 50 % of the construction costs if the customer agrees to the co-financing conditions.

After the confirmation is submitted, the monthly capacity maintenance fee starts to be charged, even if the voltage has not yet been switched on. That is why the confirmation is submitted when the installation is really ready.

What to prepare before the site visit

  • The Sadales tīkls reply or contract from e-st.lv and the requested capacity in amperes.
  • The design or diagram of the house’s electrical installation, if there is one.
  • Access to the Sadales tīkls distribution cabinet and the house distribution board.
  • Information on whether the incoming cable has already been laid and who laid it.

Questions: New Sadales tīkls connection

Does Sadales tīkls run the cable to the house itself?

Sadales tīkls builds the network up to its own distribution cabinet. The cable from it to the house distribution board is part of the customer’s installation, and an electrician prepares it.

When should the confirmation for a new connection be submitted?

When the internal installation and the incoming cable are ready. After submission the capacity maintenance fee starts, and Sadales tīkls gets in touch about the meter within 2 working days.

Does a new connection need a measurement protocol?

The public Sadales tīkls description does not require it for an ordinary connection. Measurements with a test report are needed for putting a house into use under LBN 261-15.
