It is submitted on e-st.lv after a new connection has been built or capacity increased; for a new connection the meter is installed only after that. Before you submit it, we test the installation so that the confirmation states what is true.

What readiness of an electrical installation for switching on voltage means

Point 99 of the Electricity Trade and Use Regulations (Cabinet Regulation No. 635) allows the system operator to check the user’s electrical installation before connection and to request “written confirmation that the electrical installation is ready for voltage to be switched on”. Sadales tīkls implements this with the confirmation on e-st.lv. Point 44 lays down that the user is responsible for the technical condition of the electrical installation.

With the confirmation you state that the installation is ready for voltage to be switched on (Regulation No. 635, point 99). Sadales tīkls writes that the electrical network may be built only by a qualified electrical specialist or an electrical installation company.

Sadales tīkls recommends looking for the list of certified specialists in the BIS register of construction specialists. BLEEA and LEB certificates are checked using the issuer’s data. How to check a certificate and what its scope means is explained on the page how to check an electrician’s certificate.

Checked on 25 November 2026: Cabinet Regulation No. 635 of 7 November 2023, points 44 and 99, likumi.lv/ta/id/347235; Sadales tīkls, “Permanent connection” and “Preparing a property to receive electricity”.

A conformity confirmation for an electrical installation is not the same

The word “confirmation” appears in several documents. Sub-point 53.2 of LBN 261-15, on putting a building into use, mentions “conformity confirmations of the materials used”: these are the manufacturer’s or supplier’s documents about materials. For putting a building into use, BIS has the “confirmation of the building’s readiness for use”, approved by the participants in construction. The Sadales tīkls confirmation is a third one: your statement that the installation is ready.

The measurement protocol is also a different document. The Sadales tīkls public pages do not name it as an annex for an ordinary connection. Measurements with a test report are required when putting a building into use under LBN 261-15 and in the periodic test; see test protocol.

Checked on 25 November 2026: LBN 261-15 point 53 (likumi.lv/ta/id/274674); Regulation No. 529, point 167.¹ (likumi.lv/ta/id/269164).

What happens after submission

  • For a new connection Sadales tīkls gets in touch about installing the meter within 2 working days.
  • The monthly capacity maintenance fee starts to be charged, even if the voltage has not yet been switched on.
  • The price list sets no separate fee for the confirmation itself; the price list charges for connecting the installation, €117.21 for a 0.4 kV connection (according to the Sadales tīkls price list, checked on 25 November 2026).

What to prepare before the test

  • The Sadales tīkls contract or reply on e-st.lv.
  • Information on who built the installation and whether there is a diagram.
  • Access to the incoming cable, distribution board and circuits.

Questions about these documents

Who signs the confirmation for Sadales tīkls?

The customer submits the confirmation on e-st.lv. A microgenerator needs an inverter settings protocol as an annex, signed by a specialist with a certificate for generating equipment.

Can I submit the confirmation before the installation is finished?

The confirmation means that the installation is ready. After submission the capacity maintenance fee starts, and Sadales tīkls may check the installation (Regulation No. 635, point 99). Submit it when the installation is finished.

Is the confirmation also valid for the building authority?

No. When a house is put into use, the building authority needs the electrical installation test documents under LBN 261-15, and that is a separate job.

Do I have to pay Sadales tīkls for the confirmation?

There is no separate fee for the confirmation in the price list. The charge is for connecting the installation: €117.21 (0.4 kV) according to the Sadales tīkls price list, checked on 25 November 2026.
