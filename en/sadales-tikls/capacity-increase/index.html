If the existing connection is suitable and the network does not have to be rebuilt, Sadales tīkls is paid only for connecting the installation, but on your side the incoming supply and distribution board must withstand the new current. We check whether your installation is ready for more capacity and prepare the electrician’s part.

Why the new capacity also has to be checked in the house

Sadales tīkls can increase the main protective device up to the boundary, but beyond it are your incoming cable, distribution board and circuits. If they were built for a lower current, a larger main circuit breaker does not protect the cable. In the check we look at whether the cable cross-section, the distribution board and the protective devices suit the new capacity.

Point 44 of the Electricity Trade and Use Regulations (Cabinet Regulation No. 635) lays down that the user is responsible for the technical condition of their electrical installation. So the Sadales tīkls reply “the capacity can be increased” does not mean that the house distribution board is ready. New capacity may be needed, for example, for a heat pump, electric heating or an electric car charger.

If the main protective device in a residential building is to be above 32 A, LBN 261-15 point 8.3 requires a three-phase connection. The capacity increase then also becomes a switch from 1 phase to 3 phases.

Checked on 25 November 2026: LBN 261-15 point 8.3 (as worded by Cabinet Regulation No. 355 of 17 June 2025), likumi.lv/ta/id/274674.

Increasing electrical capacity in a flat

A flat owner has to attach the building manager’s written consent to the application. So start by talking to the building manager, who can tell you whether the common installation of the building allows a larger load for your flat.

The distribution board check and measurements for the flat can be combined with electrical installation testing in a flat.

When more capacity is not needed at all

The Sadales tīkls advice on electric car charging mentions that a typical private house connection has three phases with 20–25 A (13.8–17.3 kW) and that a smart charger with dynamic load management often makes it possible to manage without increasing capacity. Before applying, it is worth adding up what will really run at the same time.

Sadales tīkls carries out a capacity reduction on the day after the application and charges no fee for it, but returning to higher capacity is paid. Solar panel owners may reduce capacity only as far as the phases and capacity needed by the system are kept.

What to prepare before the test

  • The present rated current of the main protective device in amperes and the number of phases (shown on the main circuit breaker).
  • A list of new appliances: heat pump, cooker, charger, workshop equipment.
  • Access to the incoming distribution board and the house distribution board.
  • For a flat: the building manager’s contact or the consent already received.

Questions: Increasing electrical capacity

Does a capacity increase need an electrician if Sadales tīkls does everything itself?

Sadales tīkls changes its part up to the boundary. Whether your incoming cable and distribution board withstand a higher current has to be checked by an electrician, because the user is responsible for the condition of the installation (Regulation No. 635, point 44).

Does a capacity increase need a design?

If Sadales tīkls has to rebuild the network, it issues technical requirements and a design is needed. If the existing connection is suitable, Sadales tīkls increases the capacity and the customer pays the connection fee of €117.21 (0.4 kV) according to the price list.

How long does a capacity increase take?

It depends on whether Sadales tīkls has to rebuild the network. If it does, a design and construction work are needed first. After applying, ask Sadales tīkls for the timeframe for your connection.

Can I increase capacity for the winter only?

The capacity can be reduced later, and Sadales tīkls does this on the next day. Increasing it again is paid again, so compare both fees before the season changes.

Can a solar panel owner reduce capacity?

Yes, but only as far as the phases and capacity needed by the system are kept.
