In a residential building three phases become mandatory when the main protective device exceeds 32 A. Before applying, you need to check whether the incoming supply and distribution board of the house are ready for three phases, and we check and document this part.

When the regulations require three phases

LBN 261-15 point 8.3: in residential buildings, an electrical installation whose main protective device has a rated current above 32 A is connected to the system operator’s low-voltage networks “only in a three-phase arrangement”. So if single-phase 32 A is no longer enough, a bigger single phase is not a solution.

Common reasons: a heat pump or electric heating, an electric car charger, three-phase workshop equipment or solar panels. For a microgenerator the limit on a single-phase connection is 3.7 kW and on three phases 11.1 kW, so the number of phases also determines how large a solar system can be connected as a microgenerator.

The Sadales tīkls advice on electric car charging mentions that a typical private house connection already has three phases with 20–25 A (13.8–17.3 kW). If your house still has one phase, the switch gives more capacity without a very large main circuit breaker.

Checked on 25 November 2026: LBN 261-15 point 8.3, likumi.lv/ta/id/274674; Sadales tīkls, “Connecting a microgenerator” (sadalestikls.lv).

Switching from 1 phase to 3 phases on e-st.lv

Sadales tīkls describes phase changes together with load changes. A switch from three phases to one with a higher rated current of the main protective device is a paid capacity increase, submitted as a free-form application on e-st.lv. Whether your switch to three phases needs network rebuilding and a design, Sadales tīkls answers after the application.

If the network does not have to be rebuilt, the connection fee in the price list is €117.21 (0.4 kV), according to the Sadales tīkls price list, checked on 25 November 2026. If it has to be rebuilt, construction costs are added. A flat needs the building manager’s written consent.

Three-phase connection for a private house: what changes in the distribution board

Sadales tīkls replaces its part up to the boundary. On your side the incoming cable, the main device and the distribution board must be suitable for three phases, and the large loads have to be spread across the phases. If they all stay on one phase, the gain is small.

During the check we record the existing incoming supply, distribution board and circuits and tell you what has to be changed before the confirmation. The alteration work itself is done by an electrician, for example, electrical installation work in Riga. After that the installation can be tested with measurements.

Since 1 July 2025 LBN 261-15 has also required a 300–500 mA residual current device (RCD) on the main incoming supply of a single-family house, or an alternative solution (point 28.²). If the distribution board is being rebuilt, it is worth taking this into account straight away. More: RCD and three-phase supply requirements 2025.

How the switch goes

  1. List of loads We add up which appliances will run at the same time and which of them need three phases.
  2. Site visit We check the incoming cable, distribution board and circuits and tell you what has to be changed.
  3. Application on e-st.lv The owner requests the change of phases and capacity; Sadales tīkls answers whether the network has to be rebuilt.
  4. Alteration on your side The electrician rebuilds the incoming supply and the distribution board.
  5. Test and confirmation We test the finished installation; you submit the confirmation.

What to prepare

  • Photos of the main circuit breaker and the distribution board.
  • A list of appliances with their power.
  • The existing diagram or design, if there is one.
  • For a flat: the building manager’s consent.

Questions: 1 phase to 3 phases with Sadales tīkls

May a single-phase connection be increased to 40 A?

Not in a residential building: above 32 A, LBN 261-15 point 8.3 requires a three-phase connection.

Do solar panels need three phases?

On a single-phase connection the microgenerator limit is 3.7 kW, on three phases 11.1 kW. A larger system as a microgenerator needs three phases.

Does switching to three phases cost more than a capacity increase?

The Sadales tīkls fee depends on whether the network has to be rebuilt. Without rebuilding, the connection fee in the price list is €117.21 (0.4 kV); the electrician’s work on your side is separate.

Can a flat switch to 3 phases?

For changes to a flat’s connection Sadales tīkls requires the building manager’s written consent. First ask the building manager whether the common installation of the building allows it.
