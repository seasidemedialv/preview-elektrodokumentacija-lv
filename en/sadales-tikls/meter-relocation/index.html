Moving the meter within a house or flat is done by a certified electrician, and after the work Sadales tīkls puts the seal back. If the meter has to be moved outside the house, the process is longer and a design may be needed.

Outside the house means, for example, to a stairwell, an external wall or another place on the land plot. The deadlines are those of Sadales tīkls.

Checked on 25 November 2026: Sadales tīkls, “Meter operations” and “Moving, altering or dismantling an object” (sadalestikls.lv).

Removing a meter seal: why only with permission

The meter and its seal are part of the Sadales tīkls metering. Sadales tīkls writes that, after permission, a certified electrician removes the seal and does the work. So submit the application before the work starts.

An electrician may also submit the application for moving the meter within the property.

Electricity meter relocation step by step

  1. Site visit We look at the existing and new meter location, the incoming cable and the distribution board.
  2. Application Removal of the seal and the relocation are requested on e-st.lv.
  3. Permission You receive permission to remove the seal immediately after applying, and two days from submission are allowed for the work.
  4. Work The electrician removes the seal and moves the meter to the new location.
  5. Replacing the seal Sadales tīkls replaces the seal no later than 3 working days after the work.

If one house has two meters

Meters can also be combined. Sadales tīkls describes that the electrician prepares the installation, Sadales tīkls removes the surplus meter and charges no fee for reducing the capacity of the surplus connection. Before that, it is worth adding up the total load so that the combined connection has enough capacity. If it does not, see increasing electrical capacity.

Testing after relocation

Point 52 of LBN 261-15 requires electrical equipment to be tested not only before putting into use but also after significant changes. If the meter relocation requires the incoming cable to be re-laid or the distribution board rebuilt, it is worth taking measurements after the work and getting a report, see test protocol.

If you combine the relocation with more capacity, these are two applications: removing the seal and changing the capacity. Both can be prepared during one site visit.

Checked on 25 November 2026: LBN 261-15 point 52, likumi.lv/ta/id/274674.

What to prepare

  • Photos of the existing meter location and of the place where you want it.
  • Information on whether the relocation is inside the house or to an external wall, stairwell or land plot.
  • For a flat: whether the meter is in the stairwell and who the building manager is.
  • Access to the distribution board on a working day.

How much does meter relocation cost

  • Meter relocation, the electrician’s work: from €70. The Sadales tīkls sealing fee is separate.
  • Site visit: €40.

For relocation outside the house we quote the price after the site visit. Prices cover the electrician’s work and the documents. Sadales tīkls and building authority fees are separate.

Questions: Meter relocation with Sadales tīkls

May I move the meter myself?

No. Sadales tīkls writes that a certified electrician removes the seal and does the work, and only after permission.

How long will the meter be without a seal?

After the work Sadales tīkls replaces the seal within 3 working days. This is a Sadales tīkls deadline.

Is moving the meter to an external wall the same thing?

No. For relocation outside the house the application is submitted on the technical portal, Sadales tīkls issues a design brief, and a construction design may be needed.

Who covers the costs if the meter is moved outside the house?

Sadales tīkls states that for relocation outside a house or flat the customer covers all costs. Sadales tīkls issues the design brief without charging a fee for it.
