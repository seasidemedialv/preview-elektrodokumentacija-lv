Where the Sadales tīkls work ends and your responsibility begins

Sadales tīkls states that the user’s electrical installation begins where the incoming cable is connected to the Sadales tīkls distribution cabinet. The meter is usually at this boundary. Everything beyond it is yours: the incoming cable to the house distribution board, the board itself and the circuits.

The Electricity Trade and Use Regulations (Cabinet Regulation No. 635) lay down that the user is responsible for the technical condition of their electrical installation (point 44). Before connection the system operator may check the user’s electrical installation and request “written confirmation that the electrical installation is ready for voltage to be switched on” (point 99). In practice this is done on the e-st.lv portal.

Sadales tīkls writes that the customer’s installation may be built “only by a qualified electrical specialist or an electrical installation company” and that the list of certified specialists can be found in the Construction Information System (BIS).

Checked on 25 November 2026: Cabinet Regulation No. 635 of 7 November 2023, points 44 and 99 (likumi.lv/ta/id/347235); Sadales tīkls, “Permanent connection” and “Preparing a property to receive electricity” (sadalestikls.lv).

Does Sadales tīkls need a measurement protocol

The confirmation and the measurement protocol are different documents. The customer submits the confirmation on e-st.lv, confirming that the installation has been built and is ready for voltage to be switched on. The Sadales tīkls public pages do not name a measurement protocol as an annex for an ordinary connection.

Measurements with a test report are required in other cases: when putting a building into use under LBN 261-15 and in the periodic test under the fire safety regulations. If you are connecting a new house, these papers can be prepared in one go, see putting into use. If we find defects during the test, an electrician puts them right before the papers are prepared, for example electrical installation repairs in Riga.

Questions: Sadales tīkls connection documents

Can I submit the confirmation to Sadales tīkls myself?

Yes, the customer submits the confirmation on e-st.lv. Sadales tīkls requires the installation to have been built by a qualified electrical specialist or an electrical installation company, so it is worth testing the installation before submitting.

Why do payments start after the confirmation if there is no electricity yet?

Sadales tīkls states that after the confirmation is submitted, the monthly capacity maintenance fee starts to be charged, even if the voltage has not yet been switched on. More: capacity maintenance fee.

Do you submit the e-st.lv application on my behalf?

The owner submits the application and the confirmation in their own e-st.lv account, while reconnection is usually requested by the electricity supplier. For meter relocation, Sadales tīkls also allows the electrician to submit the application. We prepare the electrician’s part and tell you what has to be stated in the application.
