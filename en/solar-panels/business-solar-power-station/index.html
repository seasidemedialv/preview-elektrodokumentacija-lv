Connecting a solar power station to Sadales tīkls: who does what

If the existing connection suits the power station, Sadales tīkls issues technical requirements, a contract and a connection fee invoice within 20 days. If a new connection has to be set up or the load changed, within 20 days it issues technical requirements, an approximate cost estimate and an agreement, and then a designer prepares the construction design in BIS. The station is installed and the test documents are signed by a construction specialist whose certificate meets the Sadales tīkls requirements.

Construction specialists’ certificates are governed by Cabinet Regulation No. 169: electrical installation design (sub-point 8.5) and “Elektroietaišu izbūves darbu vadīšana” (management of electrical installation construction work) and “Elektroietaišu izbūves darbu būvuzraudzība” (construction supervision of that work) (sub-point 8.11). Current certificate data is in the BIS register of construction specialists, so the certificates of the designer and the work manager can be checked there.

Checked on 25 November 2026: Cabinet Regulation No. 169 of 20 March 2018, sub-points 8.5 and 8.11, likumi.lv/ta/id/298177.

For a solar station of up to 50 kW whose inverter meets the requirements of standard LVS EN 50549, test measurements are not needed. For a station above 50 kW, however, the test is carried out by accredited laboratory specialists under a programme agreed with Sadales tīkls. Sadales tīkls assesses the submitted documents and prepares the permission for parallel operation within 30 days at the latest. Who signs the documents for a particular property and which certificate that requires, we establish during the site visit, before we quote the price. We do not do panel installation.

Solar power station documentation in a business building

Besides the station, a business building has duties concerning the installation itself. The fire safety regulations currently in force (Cabinet Regulation No. 238, in force until 1 January 2027) require testing of the electrical installation, earthing and lightning protection once every 10 years, in an explosive environment once every two years and in a chemically aggressive environment once a year. The testing is recorded in a report under Annex 6, and distribution boards get a thermal camera inspection under Annex 7.

Measurements for businesses are described in more detail on the page electrical installation testing for businesses.

Checked on 25 November 2026: Cabinet Regulation No. 238 of 19 April 2016, points 56–58, likumi.lv/ta/id/281646.

Settlements for a business

The net billing system is available to households and businesses up to 999.99 kW, the universal service up to 50 kW. The other option is to sell electricity to a supplier. Until there is a contract with a supplier, the electricity produced is accepted into the network without payment.

Checked on 25 November 2026: Sadales tīkls, “Settlement options for electricity producers” (sadalestikls.lv).

What to prepare

  • The planned or installed capacity in kW and a list of inverters.
  • The design or BIS case number, if there is one.
  • The number of distribution boards in the building and the existing measurement reports.
  • A contact person on site for access.

Questions about these documents

Can a 15 kW system for a business be a microgenerator?

No. The microgenerator limit is 11.1 kW on a three-phase connection; a larger system is a power station.

Does a business station need a State Environmental Service permit?

Sadales tīkls states that it is needed only from 500 kW.

Do you design power stations?

No. The design is developed by a designer with a certificate for electrical installation design. We prepare the measurements and documents on the property side.

What if the business system is up to 11.1 kW?

Then it is a microgenerator with a simpler process: a confirmation with an inverter settings protocol on e-st.lv. Legal entities have to choose an electricity supplier after permission.
