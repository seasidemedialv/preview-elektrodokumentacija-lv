Solar panels without documents: what happens to the electricity produced

Without Sadales tīkls permission only a brief test run is allowed. After permission a settlement option has to be chosen; until there is a contract with a supplier, the electricity produced is accepted into the network without payment. The old net metering has been closed to new participants since 1 May 2024, so it is no longer available to a system that is not yet connected.

Off-grid systems not connected to the network do not need Sadales tīkls permission.

Sadales tīkls checks the submitted documents and may inspect the property on site. It may also ask for a calculation showing that the voltage rise up to the network boundary does not exceed 2 %, or 4.6 V. If the installer has not left such a calculation, it is worth preparing it before the confirmation.

Solar panel documents after installation: how the check goes

  1. What you already have You show the status of the e-st.lv application and the installer’s documents, if there are any.
  2. Site visit We compare the inverter with the application and the list, and check the circuit breaker of up to 16 A between the inverter and the meter, the phases and the protection settings.
  3. Mistakes found We tell you what does not meet the Sadales tīkls requirements. The correction is done by an installer, for example, solar panel installation.
  4. Protocol and annexes If the system complies and a certified building specialist installed it and set up the inverter, we prepare the annexes; you submit the confirmation on e-st.lv.

Sadales tīkls requires that the installation, the inverter set-up and the protocol are all done by a construction specialist with a certificate for generating equipment.

Legalising solar panels and the building authority

The Sadales tīkls permission concerns the connection to the network. Whether anything has to be submitted to the building authority depends on whether the installation required construction work, for example, strengthening load-bearing structures or new networks. Ask your building authority about your particular house; Sadales tīkls also recommends finding out the municipality’s requirements in good time.

What to prepare

  • Access to the e-st.lv account or the application number.
  • The inverter model, capacity and a photo of the nameplate.
  • The installer’s invoice, diagram or contract, if there is one.
  • Access to the inverter and distribution board.
