What Sadales tīkls calls a microgenerator

A microgenerator is generating equipment of up to 16 A per phase: 3.7 kW on a single-phase or 11.1 kW on a three-phase connection. A battery also counts as generating equipment, and the total inverter capacity must stay within these limits. A larger system is a power station with a different process.

The inverter must be on the list of inverters approved by Sadales tīkls (standard LVS EN 50549-1). Sadales tīkls decides separately on an inverter that is not on the list, and this can take up to 5 working days longer.

Sadales tīkls accepts the protocol only from the holder of a BLEEA or LEB certificate with a specialisation in power stations or generating equipment. An electrical safety group card is not considered a certificate. Sadales tīkls requires that the installation and inverter set-up are also carried out by a construction specialist with a certificate for generating equipment.

Checked on 25 November 2026: Sadales tīkls, “Connecting a microgenerator” and “Certification requirements for installers of generating equipment” (sadalestikls.lv).

The net billing system for solar panels

The old “net metering” has been closed to new participants since 1 May 2024. Existing participants can use it until 28 February 2029, but changes, for example, of the user, settlement option or connection, can end participation. It is worth checking this before an expansion or inverter change.

New producers have two options: the net billing system or selling electricity to a supplier. The net billing system also includes a universal service up to 50 kW: the purchase price is the Nord Pool Latvia price minus no more than €0.02/kWh, not lower than 0, with a contract of at least 6 months. Until there is a contract with a supplier, the electricity produced is accepted into the network without payment.

Checked on 25 November 2026: Sadales tīkls, “Settlement options for electricity producers” (sadalestikls.lv/lv/norekinu-veidi-elektribas-razotajiem).

Do panels need the building authority

That depends on whether the installation requires construction work, for example, strengthening load-bearing structures or new networks. Sadales tīkls recommends finding out the local municipality’s requirements in good time, so ask your building authority about your particular house. The panels are installed by an installer, for example, solar panel installation.

Questions: Putting solar panels into use

May solar panels be switched on before Sadales tīkls gives permission?

Without permission only a brief test run is allowed. Off-grid systems with no connection to the network do not need permission.

Do you install solar panels?

No. Installation is done by an installer; we test the system and prepare the papers for Sadales tīkls.

Who may sign the papers for solar panels?

Sadales tīkls accepts the inverter settings protocol only from the holder of a BLEEA or LEB certificate with a generation specialisation. The documents are prepared and signed by a certified electrical specialist.

Can I still join the old net metering?

No, it has been closed to new participants since 1 May 2024. For new systems there is the net billing system or selling electricity to a supplier.
