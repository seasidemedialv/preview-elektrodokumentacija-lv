Inverter settings protocol

Sadales tīkls accepts it only from a specialist whose BLEEA or LEB certificate covers power stations or generating equipment. We check the inverter on site and prepare the protocol.

Who may sign the protocol

For microgenerators of up to 11.1 kW, Sadales tīkls requires a certificate issued by the BLEEA or LEB certification centre, with a specialisation in power stations or generating equipment. The field must be “Elektroietaišu montāža” (installation of electrical equipment), “Elektroietaišu izbūve” (construction of electrical installations) or, in the regulated sphere, “Elektroietaišu izbūves darbu vadīšana” (management of electrical installation construction work) or “Elektroietaišu izbūves darbu būvuzraudzība” (construction supervision). An electrical safety group card is not considered a certificate. Sadales tīkls requires that the installation and inverter set-up are also carried out by a construction specialist with a certificate for generating equipment.

The check Sadales tīkls suggests to the customer: the certificate has been issued by an accredited body (BLEEA or LEB), the programme name includes the field and the generation direction, the certificate is valid and the data matches the person.

Checked on 25 November 2026: Sadales tīkls, “Certification requirements for installers of generating equipment” (sadalestikls.lv).

What the protocol confirms

The protocol records the inverter protection settings that meet the Sadales tīkls general technical requirements. These mention, for example, a maximum voltage setting of 255 V with disconnection in no more than 1.5 s. They also mention a maximum frequency setting from 52 Hz with disconnection in no more than 0.5 s, as well as disconnection when there is no voltage in the network.

  • One protocol for each inverter, including a hybrid inverter with a battery.
  • Attached are a photo of the inverter nameplate and a photo of the installed equipment.
  • A copy of the construction specialist’s certificate is submitted together with the protocol.

Checked on 25 November 2026: Sadales tīkls, “General technical requirements for connecting a microgenerator”, “Connecting a microgenerator”, “Connecting a battery” (sadalestikls.lv).

Connecting an inverter to the grid: how the protocol is made

  1. Data check We compare the inverter model with the e-st.lv application and the Sadales tīkls list of approved inverters.
  2. Settings We check whether the inverter protection settings meet the Sadales tīkls requirements.
  3. Connection We check the circuit breaker between the inverter and the meter and the phases to which the inverters are connected.
  4. Protocol If the system and the installer’s certificate meet the requirements, we prepare the protocol; you attach it to the confirmation on e-st.lv.

Changing the inverter: what Sadales tīkls requires

If you replaced the inverter after paying the assessment fee, Sadales tīkls allows you to submit the confirmation with the data of the originally applied-for inverter and attach the protocol for the new inverter. This applies if the new one is on the approved list and its capacity does not exceed the one applied for. Otherwise a new application is needed.

After permission for parallel operation, the settings must not be changed without the consent of Sadales tīkls, because Sadales tīkls may disconnect the microgenerator. If the inverter of a working system has to be changed, request the change on e-st.lv as a change of the technical parameters of the microgenerator.

When a new protocol is needed

  • You add a battery with its own inverter: a protocol for each inverter.
  • You replace the inverter with another model: a protocol for the new inverter.
  • You add another single-phase inverter: it is connected to a different phase and needs its own protocol.

Each of these cases is an application to Sadales tīkls on e-st.lv, and the protocol is its annex. More about batteries: connecting a battery.

What to prepare

  • Access to the inverter menu or app account.
  • The e-st.lv application data: the inverter model and capacity.
  • The installer’s documents, if there are any.

Questions: Inverter settings protocol

Can any electrician sign an inverter settings protocol?

No. Sadales tīkls accepts it only from the holder of a BLEEA or LEB certificate with a generation specialisation; an electrical safety group is not a certificate.

Is a separate protocol needed for each inverter?

Yes, Sadales tīkls requires a settings protocol for each inverter.

May I change the inverter settings myself after permission?

No, the settings must not be changed without the consent of Sadales tīkls.

Where can I check whether a specialist has the required certificate?

Construction practice certificates are checked in BIS, while BLEEA and LEB certificates are checked using the issuer’s data and the four Sadales tīkls criteria: issuer BLEEA or LEB, field and generation direction in the programme name, validity and personal data.
