Connecting a microgenerator

A microgenerator is a system of up to 3.7 kW on a single-phase or 11.1 kW on a three-phase connection. We test the installed system and prepare the protocol and annexes; installation is done by an installer.

Microgenerator application and the other steps

  1. Application on e-st.lv You state the inverter and capacity. If the inverter is on the Sadales tīkls list of approved inverters (standard LVS EN 50549-1), the reply comes within 3 working days; Sadales tīkls decides on one not on the list up to 5 working days longer.
  2. Assessment fee €78.66 according to the Sadales tīkls price list, checked on 25 November 2026; it is not refunded if the application is withdrawn.
  3. Installation A construction specialist with a certificate for generating equipment installs the system and sets up the inverter within 9 months of approval; the deadline can be extended once by up to 6 months.
  4. Confirmation with annexes On e-st.lv you submit the confirmation that the microgenerator is ready for parallel operation, with a copy of the certificate, the inverter settings protocol and photos of the nameplate and equipment.
  5. Sadales tīkls check Sadales tīkls checks the documents, may inspect the property and issues permission for parallel operation within 3 working days.
  6. Settlement option You choose the net billing system or selling electricity to a supplier.

Checked on 25 November 2026: Sadales tīkls, “Connecting a microgenerator” (sadalestikls.lv/lv/mikrogeneratora-pieslegsana). The deadlines are those of Sadales tīkls.

What has to be checked before the confirmation

The Sadales tīkls general technical requirements lay down, for example, that there must be a circuit breaker of no more than 16 A (preferably with a B characteristic curve) between the inverter and the meter. The inverter must also disconnect when there is no voltage in the network, and the voltage rise up to the Sadales tīkls boundary must not exceed 2 %, or 4.6 V. Sadales tīkls may ask for a calculation of this rise.

Several single-phase inverters are connected to different phases. Island mode needs synchronisation automation. We check these points on site before we sign the protocol. The protocol is signed by a construction specialist whose certificate covers generating equipment.

Without permission for parallel operation, Sadales tīkls allows only a brief test run. Off-grid systems not connected to the network do not need permission.

Checked on 25 November 2026: Sadales tīkls, “General technical requirements for connecting a microgenerator” (sadalestikls.lv).

Increasing solar panel capacity

The system remains a microgenerator as long as the total inverter capacity does not exceed 3.7 kW on a single-phase or 11.1 kW on a three-phase connection. If an expansion exceeds this, it becomes a power station with a different process, see solar power station for a business. On a single-phase connection, a larger system first needs a switch to 3 phases.

Changes to microgenerator parameters are requested on e-st.lv with an application to change the technical parameters of the microgenerator. After permission the inverter settings must not be changed without the consent of Sadales tīkls. If you are in the old net metering, check before making changes whether they end your participation.

Microgenerator with Sadales tīkls for a business

Legal entities have to choose an electricity supplier after permission. Until there is a contract with a supplier, the electricity produced is accepted into the network without payment. The net billing system is available to households and businesses up to 999.99 kW.

What to prepare

  • The Sadales tīkls approval of the application and its date.
  • The inverter model and serial number, and the battery data, if there is one.
  • Access to the inverter, protective circuit breaker and meter.
  • The installer’s diagram, if there is one.

Questions: Connecting a microgenerator

How long does connecting a microgenerator take in total?

Sadales tīkls writes that the process can take 1–2 months.

May I install an inverter that is not on the Sadales tīkls list?

Sadales tīkls decides on such an inverter separately, and this can take up to 5 working days longer. A laboratory certificate, specification and manual are needed.

What should I do if the 9 months are running out?

The deadline can be extended once by up to 6 months. Ask Sadales tīkls how to request the extension before the deadline has passed.

Is the €78.66 refunded if I withdraw the application?

No. Sadales tīkls states that the assessment fee is not refunded if the application is withdrawn.
