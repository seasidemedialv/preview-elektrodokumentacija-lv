Bauska Municipality building authority: what to submit for a house in Iecava

Iecava has a compact centre, apartment buildings, detached-house areas, and production and farming properties in the surroundings. Construction matters in Iecava are handled by the Bauska Municipality building authority, and the municipality states that since 1 January 2020 the construction administrative process can be started only electronically in BIS.

The Bauska Municipality document list (updated 6 February 2026) includes, for putting residential houses and outbuildings (Group 2) into use, electrical installation test documentation prepared by a certified electrical specialist, if an electricity supply has been installed. Confirm with the building authority whether your garage or shed counts as an outbuilding on this list. The list also includes reports from utility network holders if external connections lie outside the property boundary and have no other construction documentation.

Production or farm building: who may sign the measurements

Iecava’s functions are a local service centre, production and the farming surroundings. A workshop, grain dryer or livestock barn may have more distribution boards than a house, and equipment may run on three phases. The owner therefore needs to know not only the result but also who may sign the measurements.

Point 55 of LBN 261-15 states that the tests in sub-point 53.4 of the standard, namely measurements of conductor and cable resistance, the phase-neutral loop, earthing loops and bonding resistance, are carried out by a certified electrical specialist. The specialist’s certificate comes from an institution accredited in Latvia under standard LVS EN ISO/IEC 17024.

Checked 25 November 2026: Latvian Construction Standard LBN 261-15 “Internal Electrical Installations of Buildings” (Cabinet Regulation No. 294 of 9 June 2015), sub-point 53.4 and point 55, likumi.lv.

In your request, state the number of buildings, the number of distribution boards in each and whether there are explosive or dusty zones. More: electrical installation testing for businesses.

Lightning protection for buildings on the rural edge: when it is tested

Iecava lies on the Zemgale plain by the Iecava river, and the town borders farmland. If a farm building, warehouse or production facility has a lightning protection system, its condition has to be checked just like the installation: whether the air terminals, down conductors and earth electrodes are connected and whether the earthing resistance is compliant.

The fire safety regulations currently in force (Cabinet Regulation No. 238, in force until 1 January 2027) include testing of the lightning protection system in the periodic electrical installation test, and the results are entered in the same report. Whether the building needs lightning protection at all is determined by the design, not by the test report.

Before the site visit, find out whether there is a lightning protection design or diagram, and state the building’s height and roof covering. For how this test is carried out, see the page lightning protection testing.

More capacity for a business on the A7: application and papers

The A7 (Via Baltica) links Iecava with Ķekava, Riga, Bauska and the route to Lithuania. If a business on this road expands production and the existing connection is not enough for new equipment, an increase of permitted capacity has to be requested from Sadales tīkls. It is a paid service applied for on e-st.lv or through the electricity supplier.

If the network does not need rebuilding, the fee for connecting the installation in the Sadales tīkls price list is €117.21 (0.4 kV, checked 25 November 2026). If network rebuilding is needed, construction costs are added. The electrician’s work is on the business side: checking whether the incoming supply, cable and distribution board will carry the new capacity, and preparing an installation for whose readiness the business submits a confirmation on e-st.lv.

In your request, give the existing main protective device, the planned capacity and a list of new equipment. The process is described on the page electricity capacity increase.

Questions about these documents

Who in Bauska Municipality may prepare electrical installation documentation?

The Bauska Municipality list says that electrical installation test documentation is prepared by a certified electrical specialist. Point 55 of LBN 261-15 specifies that the certificate is issued by an institution accredited in Latvia under LVS EN ISO/IEC 17024. Check such a certificate with its issuer, for example BLEEA or LEB; the BIS register holds construction practice certificates.

Does a farm production building need a lightning protection report?

If the building has a lightning protection system, the fire safety regulations currently in force (Cabinet Regulation No. 238, in force until 1 January 2027) include its testing in the periodic electrical installation test. Whether a building must have the system is determined by the design and construction standards; ask the designer.

How is a capacity change requested for a business in Iecava?

The application is submitted on the Sadales tīkls portal e-st.lv or through the electricity supplier. An increase is a paid service. Before that, the electrician checks whether the business’s incoming supply and distribution board suit the new capacity, and after the work a confirmation of readiness is submitted.
