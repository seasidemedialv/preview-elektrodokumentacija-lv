The owner of a house who built it themselves has to submit electrical installation test documentation to the building authority with the other documents for putting into use. For the owner or tenant of a warehouse or office, the reports are by distribution board and serve the business itself. If there are already panels on the roof, the third recipient is Sadales tīkls.

A self-built detached or terraced house: the Mārupe list

Mārupe has detached houses, terraced houses, newer apartment building projects, offices, warehouses and logistics properties. On 31 October 2025 Mārupe Municipality explained what you need to know for a self-built house to be ready for putting into use. Documents are submitted to the Building Authority, and the list includes electrical installation test documentation.

The same list includes reports from the issuers of technical conditions on readiness, with AS “Sadales tīkls” given as an example, and test protocols for special systems and equipment. So the electrical part has two sides: the Sadales tīkls report on the connection and the electrician’s protocol on the house’s internal installation.

If each section of a terraced house has its own meter and distribution board and the sections belong to different owners, the protocol can be drawn up for each section; ask the building authority whether separate documents are needed. In your request, state how many sections are to be tested. More: electrical installation test documentation for a self-built house.

From dense quarters to rural areas: one building authority, one BIS

Mārupe’s development ranges from dense quarters to villages and rural areas. A house may stand on a new street near the airport or on a rural property at the edge of the municipality, but the same Mārupe Municipality building authority accepts the documents. The Construction Law states that since 1 January 2020 the construction administrative process is started electronically in BIS.

The municipality’s list also contains papers unrelated to electricity: registration of decentralised sewerage and an address plate on the building. Prepare them in parallel so that one missing document does not delay the whole set.

Mārupe has drainage ditches and lower-lying areas. If there has ever been water in the house’s utility room or basement, say so before the test: insulation resistance measurement shows whether it has affected cables and the distribution board.

Streets in new areas can be narrow with varying surfaces. In your request, give the cadastral number if the address is not yet on maps. How the electrical papers fit into a building authority case is described on the page electrical installation documents for the building authority.

Warehouse or office near the airport: reports for putting premises into use

Mārupe’s functions are living, the airport and aviation services, logistics, warehouses and offices. In a warehouse with racking and doors, the electrical installation may be split between several distribution boards, each with its own part in the protocol. If premises are put into use after alteration, electrical measurements and, if the work requires it, a diagram are part of the execution documents. If the warehouse has a forklift charging point or doors with their own automation, their lines get a separate entry, and the automation diagram is attached if installed.

If several companies rent the warehouse, agree who orders testing of the common part and who the circuits of their own premises. State the floor area, the number of distribution boards and whether the building has lightning protection. More: putting office premises into use.

Panels on the roof but Sadales tīkls will not allow them: certificate scope

If your detached or terraced house in Mārupe has panels on the roof that the installer has connected, but the Sadales tīkls papers are not in order, one possible reason is an inverter settings protocol signed by a person without the required certificate.

Sadales tīkls accepts a microgenerator protocol only from a specialist with a BLEEA or LEB certificate specialising in power stations, that is, generating equipment. An electrical safety group card is not considered a certificate. Sadales tīkls recommends checking:

  • whether the certificate was issued by an accredited institution, BLEEA or LEB;
  • whether the programme name includes the certification field and the generation direction;
  • whether the certificate is valid;
  • whether the details match an identity document.

Checked 25 November 2026: Sadales tīkls, “Certification requirements for installers of generating equipment”, sadalestikls.lv.

Under Sadales tīkls requirements, a construction specialist holding a certificate has to do both the mounting and inverter set-up and the protocol. The process is described on the page solar panels installed, Sadales tīkls papers not sorted out.

Questions about these documents

What does Mārupe Municipality require for a self-built house?

The list in the municipality’s explanation of 31 October 2025 includes electrical installation test documentation, reports from the issuers of technical conditions on readiness, for example from Sadales tīkls, and test protocols for special systems and equipment. Others prepare the remaining documents, such as sewerage registration.

Does a terraced house section need separate measurements?

If each section has its own meter and distribution board, the measurements are essentially separate, and a protocol can be drawn up for each section. Whether the building authority wants one joint document or several depends on how the terraced house is registered in the construction case.

Which certificate field is needed for a solar panel protocol?

For a microgenerator up to 11.1 kW, Sadales tīkls accepts a BLEEA or LEB certificate in electrical installation mounting, construction or management of construction works with a specialisation in power stations, that is, generating equipment. A general electrician’s certificate or electrical safety group is not enough.
