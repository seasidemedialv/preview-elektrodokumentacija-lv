If you are building a house in the new quarters, you need two different papers: a confirmation for Sadales tīkls before the voltage is switched on and test documentation for the building authority before putting it into use. Both can be prepared after one site visit.

A flat in a Baloži apartment building: a report after rewiring

Baloži has apartment buildings, detached houses and wooded areas. After renovation, a flat owner has a narrow question: are the new circuits installed correctly and is there a paper to show it. The measurement report shows this circuit by circuit: insulation resistance, phase-neutral loop, earthing and residual current device tripping.

The report can be used by the owner, the building manager or the next buyer. If only the kitchen or bathroom has been rewired, say so in your request: the measurements will then mark which circuits are new and which remain old. If an electric cooker or water heater has its own line, it also gets a separate entry in the report.

Electricity in the flat is switched off briefly during the measurements, so access is needed only to the flat’s distribution board. If the flat’s protective devices are in a stairwell board, agree in advance who will open it. The process is described on the page electrical installation testing in a flat.

A new house on the Titurga side: documents for the building authority and reports

Titurga and other nearby residential areas extend Baloži beyond the historic centre, and new detached houses are being built there. If you build a second-group residential house yourself, the Building Construction Regulations require electrical installation test documentation for putting it into use. However, the Ķekava Municipality building authority list also includes other reports that should not be confused with the electrician’s papers.

The Ķekava Municipality list includes reports from the issuers of technical conditions on the readiness of utility networks, with AS “Sadales tīkls” given as an example. That is the network operator’s paper about the connection, not a measurement protocol. The list also includes the amended part of the building design and a chimney sweep’s report on the flues. On the electrical side we prepare the measurements and diagram; others prepare the remaining documents.

This order saves time: first the connection and the confirmation to Sadales tīkls, then measurements with the voltage on, then documents for the building authority. Without voltage, phase-neutral loop impedance and residual current device tripping cannot be measured, so a test protocol before connection would be incomplete.

If the location of the distribution board or the number of circuits has changed from the design, say so before the measurements: the changes have to show in the diagram. More: electrical installation test documentation for a self-built house.

Before the voltage is switched on: confirmation of readiness

If your house in the new detached-house quarters of Baloži has a new connection, a different paper matters. Point 99 of the Electricity Trade and Use Regulations (Cabinet Regulation No. 635) provides that before connecting, the system operator may check the installation and require a written confirmation that the electrical installation is ready to be energised. This confirmation is submitted to Sadales tīkls on the e-st.lv customer portal.

Checked 25 November 2026: Electricity Trade and Use Regulations (Cabinet Regulation No. 635 of 7 November 2023), point 99, likumi.lv.

Before submitting the confirmation, make sure that:

  • the cable from the Sadales tīkls cabinet to the house distribution board is laid and connected;
  • the distribution board has incoming protection matching the capacity applied for;
  • the circuits are connected and labelled;
  • the installation has been done by a qualified electrical specialist or electrical installation company, as Sadales tīkls puts it.

Sadales tīkls states that the monthly capacity maintenance fee starts after the confirmation, even if the voltage has not yet been switched on. So do not submit it too early. How the confirmation differs from a measurement protocol is explained on the page confirmation of readiness of the electrical installation.

The shared wiring of the whole building: risers, stairwell, basement

In Baloži apartment buildings, flat installations begin after the common part: the incoming distribution board, risers and stairwell circuits. This part is tested when ordered by the building manager or the flat owners’ community, not by an individual flat owner.

If a flat owner wants to increase capacity, Sadales tīkls requires the building manager’s written consent to be attached. The manager in turn needs to know whether the risers can carry it. Measurements and a diagram of the common part help answer that question with figures.

In your request, state the number of floors and stairwells, how many incoming distribution boards the building has and whether there is a basement or attic with its own circuits. More: electrical installation testing in an apartment building.

Questions: Electrical papers in Baloži

When may the network operator ask for a written confirmation of readiness?

Before the installation is connected. Point 99 of Cabinet Regulation No. 635 allows the system operator to check the user’s electrical installation and require a written confirmation that it is ready to be energised. For a new connection or capacity increase, it is submitted to Sadales tīkls on e-st.lv.

Does a Baloži flat need a report after rewiring?

The construction standard LBN 261-15 requires electrical equipment to be tested after significant changes. A measurement report is the paper with which this can be shown to a building manager or buyer. If only some circuits have been replaced, the report shows which are new.

What reports besides the electrical documents does Ķekava Municipality require?

For putting a house into use, the Ķekava Municipality list includes reports from the issuers of technical conditions on utility network readiness, for example from Sadales tīkls, a chimney sweep’s report on flues and ventilation, and the amended parts of the building design, if any.
