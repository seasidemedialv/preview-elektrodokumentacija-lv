The protocol gives, for each circuit, insulation resistance, phase-neutral loop impedance, the earthing result and residual current device tripping. The as-built diagram, or electrical installation diagram, shows how the distribution board and circuits are actually built. Putting a house into use with the building authority needs both; for a business they serve as a basis for future tests.

A detached house or flat in Ķegums: what to measure before putting into use

Ķegums has detached houses, apartment buildings, technical and energy facilities and wooded outskirts. For a detached house, measurements are part of putting it into use; for a flat they are needed after rewiring. In both cases each circuit is measured separately, so electricity is switched off briefly during the measurements.

For a house, take the measurements when all circuits are connected and protective devices installed. If a room is not yet finished, its circuit will be marked as not connected in the protocol and will later have to be measured separately. If the house earth electrode is in the yard, say where it is: earthing resistance is measured there. If the property is by the Daugava or the hydroelectric reservoir and a sauna or boathouse on the bank has its own line, it should also be included in the protocol and diagram.

If the house has no connection yet and has to be connected to Sadales tīkls, measurements that need voltage can only be taken after connection. More: electrical installation testing in a house.

Workshop, warehouse or technical building: reports for a business

Ķegums is shaped by the hydroelectric station, the railway and the A6 road. If your business has a workshop, warehouse or service station here, electrical measurements are needed first of all by the owner, to know the condition of the installation, and by the tenant if the lease requires them.

A business building may have several distribution boards and three-phase equipment, so each board has its own part in the protocol. Before the site visit, prepare a list of distribution boards and say which equipment must not stop during measurements. If the building has lightning protection, it can be tested on the same visit.

Drawings and switching diagrams: what LBN 261-15 requires to be attached

Point 53 of LBN 261-15 states that a protocol with documents is attached to the acceptance statement of a building with electrical installations, if the relevant works have been carried out. These include a set of construction intention documentation drawings corrected under author’s supervision (53.1) and the installation’s automatic control and electrical switching diagrams (53.5).

Checked 25 November 2026: Latvian Construction Standard LBN 261-15 “Internal Electrical Installations of Buildings” (Cabinet Regulation No. 294 of 9 June 2015), point 53, likumi.lv.

Ogre Municipality building authority for Ķegums: if a contractor built the house

In Ķegums, construction matters are handled by the Ogre Municipality building authority. Its conditions for accepting a residential house say: if a building contractor builds the house, the construction log with as-built documentation and the test protocols for planned equipment are also submitted.

In that case the electrical measurements and the diagram, if one has been prepared, are part of the as-built documentation that the contractor attaches to the log. If the contractor has not handed over the electrical part or the firm no longer operates, the measurements can be taken again. If you built the house yourself, the list is different, and you submit the electrical installation test documentation as the builder. In your request, write who built the house and what papers there already are. How the electrical part fits into the building authority case is described on the page electrical installation documents for the building authority.

Questions: Electrical measurements in Ķegums

Which drawings and diagrams have to be attached when putting a new installation into use?

Point 53 of LBN 261-15 mentions a corrected set of drawings and automatic control and electrical switching diagrams, if the relevant works have been carried out. For a house without a design, an as-built diagram drawn from the actual installation is useful instead.

Does a Ķegums house need a certified electrician for putting into use?

Yes. Under the Ogre Municipality conditions, electrical installation test documentation is issued by a certified electrician, and point 55 of LBN 261-15 requires loop, earthing and resistance measurements to be taken by a certified electrical specialist. Ask the electrician to name the issuer and certificate number and check them with that institution; the BIS register shows construction practice certificates.

How do I request testing for a property on a wooded outskirt?

Give the exact address or cadastral number, how to get there, whether the road is passable all year and whether the property has electricity. If nobody lives in the house, write who will open the door and the distribution board. Without voltage some measurements cannot be taken, so also say whether the connection is switched on.
