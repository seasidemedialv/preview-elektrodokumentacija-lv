If you built a detached house in the town yourself, putting it into use requires electrical installation test documentation. If a rural property also has a sauna or garage, those circuits should be included in the same test. Detached house or inherited country house: testing before putting into use

Baldone is a small town with detached houses, wooded surroundings and rural properties on the periphery; it has a historic spa town heritage. Requests from Baldone can therefore concern two very different homes: a new detached house still to be put into use, and a country house where nobody knows when the wiring was last replaced.

For a new house, testing is part of putting it into use: insulation resistance, phase-neutral loop, earthing and residual current devices circuit by circuit, plus an as-built diagram if the building authority asks for it. In an old house, the same measurements show which circuits still comply and which need putting right before use. If the test reveals defects, an electrician fixes them (see, for example, electrical installation repair and rewiring), and only then can the documents be drawn up.

Point 160.1 of the Building Construction Regulations allows a second-group single-dwelling house to be accepted once general construction works and utility networks are complete, the facade is finished and the street side is landscaped. At least one living room, a kitchen and a bathroom must also be fitted out, with the sanitary fixtures connected to the networks. The electrical installation is a utility network, so it must be installed even if some rooms are not yet finished. More: electrical installation testing in a house.

Checked 25 November 2026: Building Construction Regulations (Cabinet Regulation No. 529 of 2 September 2014), point 160.1, likumi.lv.

Sauna, garage and shed away from the house: what has to be measured

The Baldone area has forest and hilly terrain, damper spots and scattered rural buildings. If an outbuilding stands away from the house, electricity reaches it by a buried cable or an overhead line. Such a building has its own supply line, its own protective device and may also have its own distribution board.

If the building is in a damper spot or has had water in it, insulation resistance measurement is the first thing that shows it. The house price does not apply to work on a sauna or garage: we state it after the site visit, depending on the number of lines and distribution boards. How a sauna and garage are included in the documents is covered on the page sauna, garage and outbuilding.

Ķekava Municipality building authority for Baldone properties: BIS only

Baldone is linked with Ķekava, Iecava and the Riga bypass by regional roads, but administratively it belongs to Ķekava Municipality. Construction matters in Ķekava, Baloži and Baldone are handled by the Ķekava Municipality building authority. There is no separate Baldone building authority, so the papers are also addressed to Ķekava Municipality.

Ķekava Municipality states that construction applications, explanatory notes and construction documentation are submitted only in BIS. The electrical protocol and diagram therefore need to be received electronically so that they can be added to the case in BIS. What the electrical part looks like in a BIS case is described on the page electrical installation documents for the building authority.

Electricity off for a long time: testing before reconnection

Properties in Baldone are spread out, and road surface and access can differ between the town centre and the rural part of the municipality. If a country house has stood without electricity for several years, Sadales tīkls recommends choosing a certified electrician and having the internal installation tested before reconnection. If the installation is not fit for use, Sadales tīkls does not reconnect it.

At the site visit the incoming supply and distribution board are checked and insulation and earthing measured, after which it is clear whether you can ask the electricity supplier to request reconnection or repairs are needed first. In your request, describe the access if the house is on a gravel or forest road, and say whether someone will be there to open the house. The reconnection process is described on the page electricity reconnection.

Questions about these documents

Can a Baldone house be put into use if the interior finish is not complete?

Point 160.1 of the Building Construction Regulations allows this, but only with all conditions together: general construction works and utility networks ready, facade finished, street side landscaped, a kitchen, a bathroom with connected fixtures and at least one room fitted out. The electrical installation must therefore be installed and measured. Ask the Ķekava Municipality building authority whether it is enough to list the circuits of unfinished rooms in the protocol.

What document does a sauna with electricity need?

If the sauna is part of the same construction case, its lines are included in the house measurement protocol and diagram. If you are building the sauna separately, the building authority determines the document list. On the electrical side, measurements with insulation and residual current device testing are needed.

Which building authority receives documents for a Baldone property?

Baldone is in Ķekava Municipality, and its construction matters are handled by the Ķekava Municipality building authority. The municipality states that applications, explanatory notes and construction documentation are submitted only in BIS, so the electrical papers have to be added to the case electronically.
