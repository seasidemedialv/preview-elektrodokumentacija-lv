If you have an old wooden house and do not know when the wiring was replaced, a report on its condition is the first useful step. A wooden house with wiring of unknown age: the electrical report and report validity

Jūrmala has historic wooden houses, detached houses, apartment buildings, hotels and seasonally used properties. In a wooden house altered piece by piece, one distribution board may have circuits from different decades. The electrical installation report describes what is in the board and the walls and what needs fixing first; we state the scope of measurements after the site visit.

The fire safety regulations currently in force (Cabinet Regulation No. 238, in force until 1 January 2027) require an electrical installation test once every 10 years. Their point 525 states that reports drawn up after 19 April 2016 are valid until the next test. If the house has such a report, check its date first: it determines whether a new test is needed or the existing report is enough.

Checked 25 November 2026: Fire Safety Regulations (Cabinet Regulation No. 238 of 19 April 2016), points 56 and 525, likumi.lv. The regulations are in force until 1 January 2027.

Jūrmala has sandy soils and pine forests. In sand, the earth electrode resistance can be higher than in damp clay, so in a house protocol the earthing resistance line is one of the most important. If the house has its own earth electrode in the yard, say where it is and whether anyone has changed it.

In a wooden house it is especially important to mark connections in the attic and under the cladding. For how this work is done, see the page old wiring survey.

A summer house after alteration: when papers for putting into use are needed

If you convert a seasonally used property into a year-round home, for example by insulating it, installing electric heating or increasing capacity, the question of putting it into use arises. Whether it has to go through putting into use afterwards depends on the construction case and is decided by the building authority. In Jūrmala, construction legality is ensured by the municipality, whose Urban Planning Administration includes the Building Inspectorate (page updated 23 December 2025).

The Construction Law states that since 1 January 2020 the construction administrative process is started electronically in BIS. The electrical papers therefore need to be prepared so that they can be added to the case in BIS. Before the measurements, check:

  • whether all new circuits are connected and labelled in the distribution board;
  • whether the electric heating and water heater lines have their own protective devices;
  • whether there is an old diagram or design to compare with what was built;
  • whether the house will have electricity in winter, if you plan the test out of season.

Guest house or hotel: a report an insurer may ask for

Jūrmala’s functions are a resort, tourism, living, hospitality and health services. An insurer may ask the owner of a guest house or hotel for a document on the condition of the electrical installation. What exactly is needed for this is set by the insurer, so find out what the insurer wants to see: a periodic test report or an electrical installation report with recommendations.

A periodic test report shows the measurement results. An electrical installation report additionally describes what is visible in the distribution board, what defects were found and what has to be done. If the guest house has several floors or buildings, say so in your request: the scope depends on it. More: electrical installation testing for insurance. If there is a previous test report or installation report, attach a photo of it.

A house that has stood without electricity: reconnection process and access

The A10 and the electrified railway line link Jūrmala with Riga, and in summer traffic and parking near the beach differ from winter. If you plan the site visit in summer, say in your request where a car can be parked near the house.

If the connection to a house has been cut off for a long time, after a reconnection application Sadales tīkls issues an invoice within 3 working days and restores the connection within 2 working days after payment. The reconnection application is submitted by the electricity supplier you choose. Beforehand it is worth testing the internal installation, because Sadales tīkls does not reconnect an installation that is not fit for use.

At the site visit, insulation and earthing are measured and the distribution board is checked. If everything is compliant, you can ask the electricity supplier to request reconnection. The process is described on the page electricity reconnection.

Questions about these documents

Does a guest house in Jūrmala need periodic electrical installation testing?

The fire safety regulations currently in force (Cabinet Regulation No. 238, in force until 1 January 2027) require an electrical installation test once every 10 years regardless of the property’s form of ownership, so for a guest house too. The results are recorded in a test report.

What should be considered when buying a building in Jūrmala that has not been put into use?

On 13 August 2020 the Jūrmala municipality building authority reminded that for a buyer acquiring a structure not yet put into use, the time allowed for construction works does not change. So before buying, find out how much time is left; an electrical test can be useful to learn what still needs sorting out.

How should access be described if the property is far from a station?

Jūrmala is long, and Majori, Bulduri, Kauguri and Ķemeri are far apart, so give the exact address in your request, the entrance from the street and, if the house is deep in a yard, how to find it. In summer, note where a car can be parked.
