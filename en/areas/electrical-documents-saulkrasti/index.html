A summer house or detached house by the sea: what to check before the season

Saulkrasti has detached houses, summer houses, guest accommodation, apartment buildings and seasonally used properties. In a summer house that has stood unheated over winter, the installation works in different conditions from a house lived in all year. If you convert a summer house into a permanent home, the load changes too: electric heating, a water heater, perhaps a heat pump.

Before such changes, measurements are useful: insulation, phase-neutral loop, earthing and residual current devices circuit by circuit. They show whether the old circuits can still be used with a larger load. If the alteration has a building authority case, the same measurements with a diagram form the document for putting into use. If the summer house has a single-phase supply with a small protective device, that is recorded too, as it determines whether new equipment can be connected without a capacity increase.

Saulkrasti stretches along the sea and the A1, so addresses can be far apart. In your request, give the exact address and say whether the summer house has electricity in winter. More: putting a garden house into use.

Electricity disconnected long ago: 30 days and the reconnection fee

The population of Saulkrasti changes with the seasons. If electricity has not been used on your property for a long time and the connection has been cut off, a Sadales tīkls rule matters. If reconnection is requested within 30 days after the previous user ended the contract with the supplier, Sadales tīkls charges nothing for it. Later, a connection fee of €34.76 (0.4 kV) and the capacity maintenance fee for the time since the contract ended, for no more than 24 months, have to be paid (per the Sadales tīkls price list, checked 25 November 2026).

Checked 25 November 2026: Sadales tīkls, “Reconnection” and price list, sadalestikls.lv.

  1. Find out the date When the previous user ended the contract with the supplier: it determines whether you are within the 30 days.
  2. Site visit The electrician checks the incoming supply and distribution board and measures insulation and earthing.
  3. Putting it right If the installation is not fit for use, it is repaired first: Sadales tīkls does not reconnect an unfit installation.
  4. Supplier and application Choose an electricity supplier, who requests reconnection from Sadales tīkls; capacity and phases are not increased this way.
  5. When you apply yourself You apply on e-st.lv yourself when you want a lower load or fewer phases, and also if you do not own a meter.

If the distribution board is on an outside wall or in an unheated veranda exposed to sea air, the terminals and condition of the protective devices are also checked at the site visit. The process is described on the page electricity reconnection.

Saulkrasti Municipality building authority: what to attach for a self-built house

Construction matters in Saulkrasti are handled by the Saulkrasti Municipality building authority. Its list says: if the builder builds a second-group residential building for their own needs, electrical installation test documentation and an inspection report on heating appliances, chimneys and ventilation also have to be submitted. If a contractor builds the house, the construction log with as-built documentation and test protocols and reports for equipment and special systems also have to be submitted.

The Construction Law states that since 1 January 2020 the construction administrative process is started electronically in BIS. So prepare the electrical protocol and diagram so that they can be added to the case. If the house also has a sauna or garage with electricity, their lines are included in the same protocol so that all electrical documents are in one set. Another specialist prepares the chimney and ventilation report. What the electrical part looks like in a case is described on the page electrical installation documents for the building authority.

A seaside guest house: a report an insurer may ask for

Saulkrasti has sea wind, salty air, dunes and pine forests. If a guest house has outdoor sockets, outdoor lighting lines or a distribution board on an outside wall, the report covers them separately, as they are directly exposed to the weather. If a guest house insurer asks for a document on the electrical installation, first find out exactly what is needed. The report describes the condition of the distribution board and circuits, the defects found and recommendations for fixing them; we say which measurements are needed after the site visit.

The report can also be useful to the owner before the season, if the guest house has several rooms with their own circuits or a sauna. In your request, state the number of rooms and buildings and whether there are outdoor boards or sockets. More: electrical installation testing for insurance.

Questions: Electrical documents in Saulkrasti

What if electricity in a Saulkrasti summer house was disconnected long ago?

Find out when the previous user ended the contract: within 30 days Sadales tīkls charges no reconnection fee; later there is a connection fee and a capacity maintenance fee. Before applying, have the installation tested, because Sadales tīkls does not reconnect an unfit one.

Can a summer house report be useful to an insurer?

It can. An insurer may ask for a document on the installation’s condition, and the report describes it and gives recommendations. Ask the insurer what exactly it accepts before ordering.

What papers does the Saulkrasti building authority require for a self-built house?

On the Saulkrasti Municipality list, for a second-group residential building built for your own needs, electrical installation test documentation and an inspection report on heating appliances, chimneys and ventilation also have to be submitted.
