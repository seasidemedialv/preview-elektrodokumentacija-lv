A Vangaži apartment building: the common part separate from the flats

Vangaži is a small town of industrial origin with an apartment building stock, detached houses and wooded surroundings. In an apartment building the electrical installation splits into two parts. The common part is the incoming distribution board, risers, and stairwell, basement and attic circuits, ordered by the building manager or flat owners’ community. Flat circuits beyond the flat distribution board are each owner’s responsibility.

So for a block only the common part can be tested, leaving the flats alone. This is easier if not all owners are ready to let an electrician in. If a flat owner wants their own report, they order it separately, and it can be done on the same visit. For the shared incoming distribution board, through which the whole building’s load passes, a thermal camera inspection is also useful: it shows heated connections that measurements alone do not reveal.

In your request, state the number of stairwells and floors, how many incoming distribution boards the building has and whether there is a separate board in the basement. If there is an old report or diagram, attach a photo. More: electrical installation testing in an apartment building.

A detached house you built yourself: a test report under point 168.1

For Vangaži detached houses built by a private person themselves, the Ropaži Municipality guidance note’s document list includes the building’s electrical installation test documentation, or test report, under point 168.1 of the Building Construction Regulations. The municipality states that in this case documents for acceptance into use are submitted in BIS. If you built the house in stages and some circuits were connected later, write that in your request: the report has to include all circuits in the house at the time of putting into use.

  1. Check readiness All circuits connected and labelled, distribution board closed, connection switched on.
  2. Site visit The electrician looks at the distribution board and circuits and says whether measurements can be taken straight away.
  3. Measurements Insulation, phase-neutral loop, earthing and residual current devices for all circuits.
  4. Report and diagram Results in the test report, an as-built diagram of the distribution board.
  5. BIS You add the report and diagram to your case with the other documents.

If the test reveals defects, an electrician fixes them first, and the report is drawn up afterwards. More: electrical installation test documentation for a self-built house.

Vangaži in Ropaži Municipality: which building authority and what other reports

Vangaži is part of Ropaži Municipality, so construction matters are handled by the Ropaži Municipality: the building authority and the Development and Construction Department. There is no separate Vangaži building authority, and documents have to be addressed to Ropaži Municipality.

Besides the electrical report, the Ropaži guidance note includes reports from the issuers of technical conditions on utility network readiness and a chimney sweep’s report. On the electrical side we prepare the test report and diagram; the connection report comes from the network holder, and the chimney sweep’s report from another specialist. The connection report needs to be obtained in good time, as the set is incomplete without it.

The A2 and the railway link Vangaži with Riga and Sigulda, and local access may depend on crossing these routes. In your request, say which side of the road the property is on. How the electrical part fits into a case is described on the page electrical installation documents for the building authority.

Panels on a block or house roof: when to agree with the building authority

For Vangaži apartment buildings and detached houses there is one question about panels: whether the building authority is needed. A Ropaži Municipality publication of 16 January 2024 says that solar panels are equipment that can sometimes be installed without construction works, but mostly works are needed and have to be agreed with the building authority; if in doubt, ask the authority.

Checked 25 November 2026: Ropaži Municipality publication on solar panels (16 January 2024), ropazi.lv.

For panels on an apartment building roof, talk to the building manager and flat owners first. After installation Sadales tīkls needs a confirmation of readiness of the microgenerator and an inverter settings protocol. Order the work from an installer with a generating equipment certificate, as Sadales tīkls also requires it for setting up the inverter. We check and document what has been installed. Questions: Electrical documents in Vangaži

Are documents for a Vangaži property submitted in Ropaži Municipality?

Yes. Vangaži is part of Ropaži Municipality, and construction matters are handled by the Ropaži Municipality building authority. A private person who built the house themselves submits the documents for acceptance into use in BIS.

Which electrical report is required of a private person who built the house themselves?

The Ropaži Municipality guidance note includes the building’s electrical installation test documentation, or test report, under point 168.1 of the Building Construction Regulations. It contains circuit-by-circuit measurements, with the distribution board diagram attached.

Can only the common areas be tested in an apartment building?

Yes. The common part, that is, the incoming supply, risers and stairwell and basement circuits, is ordered by the building manager or owners’ community, and the report covers only that. Each owner can have the flat installation tested separately. Both reports can be prepared on one site visit.
