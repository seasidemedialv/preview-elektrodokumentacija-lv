An apartment building in Jelgava: the shared installation and its reports

Jelgava has a historic centre, apartment building districts, detached-house areas, universities and production facilities. In an apartment building the shared installation is the incoming distribution board, risers, and stairwell and basement circuits. The building manager or flat owners’ community is responsible for it, not an individual flat owner.

In a periodic test, the insulation resistance of the common part, phase-neutral loop impedance, earthing and earth conductor continuity are measured, and lightning protection too if the building has it. The results are entered in a test report. If the flat distribution boards are in the stairwell, agree whether the test includes them or only the building’s part.

For a building manager, the request should state the number of floors and stairwells, the number of incoming distribution boards and the date of the previous report, if there is one. More: electrical installation testing in an apartment building.

Production facility, warehouse or teaching building: measurements by distribution board

Jelgava’s functions are a regional centre, education and science, production, agricultural services and trade. A production facility or warehouse may have a main distribution board and several workshop boards, each with its own set of protective devices. The protocol therefore has a separate part for each board, showing what needs fixing in which workshop.

If you rent the premises, agree with the owner which part each of you has tested. Who orders testing of the workshop boards and who orders the incoming supply and main board follows from the lease. Before the site visit, prepare a list of distribution boards with locations and old diagrams, if any.

Measurements in a production facility should be planned so that one circuit can be switched off at a time, not the whole workshop. Tell us which equipment must not stop. More: electrical installation testing for businesses.

Jelgava building authority: completing construction in BIS and the on-site inspection

Structures in Jelgava are accepted into use by the Building Authority of the Jelgava state city municipal institution “Central Administration”. Documents are submitted in BIS using the service “Completion of construction works” or as a confirmation of readiness for use; the municipality also lists other submission methods (information updated 3 December 2025).

After receiving the documents, the Jelgava building authority inspects the property, and the structure is accepted by a building inspector and an architect. So before submitting, make sure the protocol and the diagram, if there is one, match what was built. Depending on the structure, the documents specified in the special construction regulations have to be submitted.

If the property has several buildings or entrances, write in your request in which of them the distribution boards are and who can open them during the site visit. What the electrical part looks like in a building authority case is described on the page electrical installation documents for the building authority.

Distribution board contacts with a thermal camera: together with the periodic test

Point 58 of the fire safety regulations currently in force (Cabinet Regulation No. 238, in force until 1 January 2027) states that the quality of contact connections, for example in junction boxes, distribution boards and at protective devices, is checked with a thermal camera. The check is done together with the periodic electrical installation test, and the results are recorded in a report following Annex 7.

Checked 25 November 2026: Fire Safety Regulations (Cabinet Regulation No. 238 of 19 April 2016), points 56–58, Annexes 6 and 7, likumi.lv. The regulations are in force until 1 January 2027; the new regulations have not yet been published.

Report What it records When it is drawn up
Test report (Annex 6) insulation, phase-neutral loop, earthing, earth conductor bonding, lightning protection at the periodic test
Contact connection report (Annex 7) thermal camera results for distribution boards and connections on the same visit

If the thermal camera shows a heated connection, it is recorded in the report with its place in the distribution board and the measured temperature. An electrician fixes the connection, and the same spot can then be photographed with the thermal camera again so that the report shows the result after the work.

A thermal camera shows a heated contact only when current is flowing through it. In a production facility the check is therefore planned during working hours when equipment is running, and in an apartment building at a time when residents are using electricity. More: thermal camera inspection of a distribution board.

Questions about these documents

How are documents for completion of construction works submitted in Jelgava?

The Jelgava municipality lists the BIS service “Completion of construction works” or a confirmation of readiness for use, as well as other submission methods. After receiving the documents, the building authority inspects the property, and the structure is accepted by a building inspector and an architect.

Does a production facility need the thermal camera report to be done with the measurements?

Yes. The fire safety regulations currently in force (Cabinet Regulation No. 238, in force until 1 January 2027) require the thermal camera check of contacts to be done together with the periodic electrical installation test, with a separate report following Annex 7.

Which papers does the building manager of an apartment building in Jelgava keep?

After a periodic test, the building has a test report on insulation, loop, earthing and lightning protection and a report on the thermal camera check of contact connections. A diagram of the common part is also useful. With these papers the building manager can show when the test was done and with what result.
