Ikšķile has detached-house areas, newer residential development, low-rise projects and a historic centre. A new detached house needs electrical installation test documentation for the Ogre Municipality building authority. If the house already has an owner and a history, papers after other electricians’ work, a panel protocol or a meter relocation may be useful.

A new detached house in Ikšķile: documentation and the building authority’s 10 working days

Construction matters in Ikšķile are handled by the Ogre Municipality building authority. Its conditions for accepting a residential house into use (published 18 August 2021) include electrical installation test documentation issued by a certified electrician. It contains a measurement protocol for all circuits and a distribution board diagram. If the house has two floors or a basement, say so in your request: the number of floors determines how long tracing the circuits for the diagram takes.

If the house was built under a building permit, point 171 of the Building Construction Regulations states that once the building authority has received all documents, it sets the acceptance date, in agreement with the construction initiator, no later than 10 working days. The period starts only with a complete set, so the electrical papers need to be ready before submission, not after a reminder from the building authority.

Checked 25 November 2026: Building Construction Regulations (Cabinet Regulation No. 529 of 2 September 2014), point 171, likumi.lv; Ogre Municipality, “Conditions for accepting a residential house into use”.

If you built the house yourself, the list of papers is slightly different; it is described on the page electrical installation test documentation for a self-built house.

Another electrician ran the wiring and there are no papers: how to get them now

In newer Ikšķile development and low-rise projects, several contractors may have built a house: one pulled the cable, another assembled the distribution board, a third connected the circuits. If nobody left measurements or a diagram, the documents can be prepared afresh from the actual condition.

  1. Collect what there is Old protocols, the design, photos of the distribution board, receipts for cables and devices.
  2. Site visit The electrician looks at the distribution board and circuits and says whether measurements can be taken straight away or defects have to be fixed first.
  3. As-built diagram Cable routes and circuits are traced on site and the as-built diagram is drawn.
  4. Measurements and protocol Insulation, phase-neutral loop, earthing and residual current devices for all circuits.
  5. The question of concealed works A concealed works record is not recreated once the work has been covered up; ask the building authority what it accepts instead.

Panels on a house on a slope or by the reservoir: permit and protocol

Ikšķile is close to the Daugava and the reservoir, with steep slopes in places. If panels are placed on the ground at the foot of a slope or on an outbuilding, the cable to the house distribution board can be long, and its route has to show in the diagram.

On 11 May 2022 the Ogre Municipality building authority explained that if no construction works are required, such as strengthening the load-bearing roof structures or building a utility network lead-in, a building authority permit is not needed to place solar panels. If construction works are needed, ask the building authority before installation.

Sadales tīkls meanwhile expects its own papers: a confirmation of readiness of the microgenerator for parallel operation and an inverter settings protocol. It matters who mounts the panels: Sadales tīkls allows mounting and inverter set-up only by a construction specialist holding a certificate in the generation field. We then check the system and handle the documents. How this works is described on the page putting solar panels into use.

Moving the meter inside the house: seal and deadlines

In a detached house after alteration, the meter may be in the way, for example if the distribution board is moved to a utility room. If the meter is moved inside the house, Sadales tīkls sets out this procedure. The application is submitted on e-st.lv, permission to remove the seal is given immediately after the application, 2 days from the application are allowed for the work, and Sadales tīkls restores the seal within 3 working days.

The seal is removed and the work done by a certified electrician, who can submit the application on behalf of your property. If the meter is to be moved outside the house, for example to an outer wall or fence post, that is a different process through the Sadales tīkls technical portal, and a building design may be needed. The sealing fee follows the Sadales tīkls price list and is not included in the electrician’s price. If you combine relocation with a capacity increase, for Sadales tīkls they are two separate applications.

The railway line and the Daugava bank limit the roads between some parts of Ikšķile, so give the exact address in your request. The process is described on the page meter relocation.

Questions: Electrical documents in Ikšķile

How long does the Ogre Municipality building authority have to examine acceptance documents?

For a building built under a building permit, point 171 of the Building Construction Regulations provides that after receiving all documents, the building authority and the initiator set the acceptance date, no later than 10 working days. If a document is missing, the period does not start yet.

What if several electricians worked on the house?

Collect everything each of them left behind: protocols, diagrams, photos. Then one electrician measures the whole installation and draws the diagram from the actual condition. For concealed works records not drawn up in time, ask the Ogre Municipality building authority.

Do panels on an Ikšķile house roof need a building authority permit?

On 11 May 2022 the Ogre Municipality building authority stated that a permit is not needed if no construction works are required, such as strengthening the load-bearing roof structures or building a lead-in. The Sadales tīkls microgenerator papers are needed in any case if the panels will operate in parallel with the grid.
