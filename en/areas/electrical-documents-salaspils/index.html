A flat in a Salaspils apartment block district: a report before a sale or renovation

Salaspils has apartment block districts, detached-house areas, research institutes and commercial properties. A report can be useful to a flat owner at two moments: before a sale, so the buyer sees the installation’s condition in figures, and before renovation, to know which circuits can stay and which have to be rebuilt.

The report gives, for each circuit, insulation resistance, phase-neutral loop impedance and the earthing result. If the flat has residual current devices, their tripping is recorded too. If a circuit has no earth conductor, the report shows it straight away.

A buyer or bank may ask for such a report, and it can be useful as a fact about the home. In your request, write the floor, whether the distribution board is in the flat or the stairwell and whether the wiring has ever been replaced. The process is described on the page electrical installation testing in a flat.

A garden house on the outskirts by the reservoir: papers and access

The outskirts of Salaspils have forests and allotment garden areas, with the Daugava and the hydroelectric reservoir nearby. If you convert a garden house into a year-round home or it needs a new connection, the question of papers arises. Whether a garden house has to be put into use is determined by its construction case, and the Salaspils Municipality building authority confirms it. If there has been damp or water damage in the garden house after winter, say so: insulation resistance measurement shows whether it has affected the cables.

If putting into use is needed, the electrical part starts with circuit-by-circuit measurements; ask the building authority about a diagram. Before the site visit, find out:

  • whether the garden house has its own Sadales tīkls connection and meter;
  • whether there is a design or explanatory note with an electrical part;
  • whether a shed, sauna or greenhouse is also connected;
  • whether the road to the house is passable in winter and who will open the distribution board.

More about this case: putting a garden house into use.

A detached house for your own needs: the Salaspils list and BIS

Construction matters in Salaspils are handled by the Salaspils Municipality building authority, and the municipality states that since 1 January 2020 construction documents circulate only electronically in BIS. The Salaspils list for a detached house built by the builder for their own needs includes electrical installation test documentation; the test is carried out by a certified specialist or electrician.

The same list includes reports from the issuers of technical conditions on the readiness of connected utility networks and changes to the building design, if there are deviations. If the distribution board or circuits are built differently from the design, the diagram has to match what was built and the changes have to be marked. How the electrical part gets into a BIS case is described on the page electrical installation documents for the building authority.

One phase is no longer enough: a capacity change with Sadales tīkls

In Salaspils detached-house areas, one phase may not be enough if a heat pump, electric heating or an EV charger is added. Sadales tīkls states that increasing permitted capacity is a paid service, applied for on e-st.lv or through the electricity supplier. Switching from three phases to one with a larger main fuse is also a paid increase, requested with a free-form application.

Checked 25 November 2026: Sadales tīkls, “Load changes”, sadalestikls.lv. Sadales tīkls assesses a switch from one to three phases based on the application.

Point 8.3 of the construction standard LBN 261-15 states that in residential buildings an incoming supply with a protective device larger than 32 A is connected only on three phases. Sadales tīkls determines which application your switch from one to three phases needs and whether the network has to be rebuilt. On the house side, the electrician checks whether the distribution board and incoming supply are ready for three phases. Before applying, list the largest appliances with their power, for example a heat pump, cooker and charger: it shows how many amps to request for the incoming supply. More: 1 phase to 3 phases with Sadales tīkls.

Questions about these documents

What is submitted to the Salaspils building authority for a house built for your own needs?

The Salaspils list for a detached house built by the builder for their own needs includes electrical installation test documentation, reports from the issuers of technical conditions on utility network readiness and changes to the building design, if there are deviations. Everything is submitted in BIS. The electrical part has a measurement protocol for all circuits and a distribution board diagram.

Is increasing permitted capacity a paid service?

Yes. Sadales tīkls lists an increase as a paid service applied for on e-st.lv or through the supplier. Sadales tīkls charges no fee for reducing capacity, but restoring it is paid again. The electrician’s work on the house side is charged separately.

When does a garden house have to be put into use?

It depends on the documents under which the garden house was built or altered. The Salaspils Municipality building authority can say whether putting into use is needed in your case. If it is, a measurement protocol is prepared for the electrics and, if the building authority asks, a diagram too.
