The Ādaži Municipality building authority asks for it when a house is put into use, and Sadales tīkls when there are solar panels on the roof. The building authority needs electrical installation test documents; Sadales tīkls needs a confirmation and an inverter settings protocol. Below, each case sets out what has to be prepared and when.

Ādaži Municipality building authority: papers via BIS, properties beyond the Gauja bridges

Construction matters in Ādaži Municipality are handled by the Ādaži Municipality building authority. It states that since 1 January 2020 all construction documentation can be submitted digitally in BIS, where progress can also be followed. The electrical papers therefore need to be received in a form that can be uploaded to the existing construction case.

The A1 links Ādaži with Riga and Saulkrasti, but the Gauja bridges and local roads determine how to reach properties towards Carnikava and Kadaga. In your request, give the exact address or cadastral number and the access route if the house is on a new street not yet on maps.

The electrical part gets into BIS in this order:

  1. Site visit The electrician looks at the distribution board, incoming supply and circuits and says which documents the house needs and what they will cost.
  2. Measurements Insulation resistance, phase-neutral loop, earthing and residual current devices, circuit by circuit.
  3. Protocol and diagram The results are compiled in a protocol, and an as-built diagram of the distribution board is prepared if there is no current one.
  4. Upload to BIS The owner or their authorised representative adds the files to the case together with the other documents for putting into use.
  5. Examination by the building authority The Ādaži Municipality building authority examines the whole set; its deadlines and decisions are the authority’s responsibility.

How to prepare the electrical part specifically for the building authority is explained on the page electrical installation documents for the building authority.

A house you built yourself: what the Building Construction Regulations and the Ādaži list require

Ādaži has newer detached-house development, apartment building projects, commercial properties and public infrastructure. If you built a detached house yourself, without a building contractor, a separate requirement applies to this case.

Sub-point 157.7.1 of the Building Construction Regulations states that if the owner has built a second-group residential house under an explanatory note, electrical installation test documentation is added to Part II of the explanatory note. The Ādaži Municipality document list says the same for a second-group house built for your own needs: electrical installation test documents and an inspection report on heating appliances, chimneys and ventilation ducts are also submitted. Another specialist prepares the chimney report.

Checked 25 November 2026: Building Construction Regulations (Cabinet Regulation No. 529 of 2 September 2014), sub-point 157.7.1, likumi.lv; Ādaži Municipality page “Documents for accepting structures into use”.

Take the measurements when the distribution board is assembled and all circuits are connected, but before the building authority’s inspection. If a circuit is not yet finished, it will not be in the protocol, and the measurements will then have to be repeated. More: electrical installation documentation for a self-built house.

Solar panels for a new house: papers for Sadales tīkls after installation

Ādaži is the municipal centre, and suburban development here is growing fast. If panels are planned for the new house from the start, be aware that the papers will go in two directions. If the panels and inverter are connected to the grid, the house’s internal installation and the microgenerator have to be sorted out in two places: with the building authority for the house and with Sadales tīkls for the generating equipment.

Sadales tīkls defines a microgenerator as equipment of up to 11.1 kW on three phases or 3.7 kW on one phase. The application is submitted on e-st.lv, and after installation the confirmation of readiness for parallel operation and the inverter settings protocol are attached there. Sadales tīkls requires the installer to hold a generating equipment certificate both for mounting and for setting up the inverter. If this requirement is met, we check what has been installed and prepare the protocol with attachments.

If you are still putting the house into use, it is more convenient to prepare the measurements and the panel protocol during one site visit. The whole sequence is described on the page microgenerator connection.

Shop, office or warehouse in Ādaži: construction log and equipment protocols

If a commercial property or apartment building project is built by a contractor, the document list is different. Under the Ādaži Municipality list, more is submitted if a building contractor does the work on a residential building. These are the construction log with as-built documents and the test protocols and reports for planned technological equipment and special systems, if they are not attached to the log.

In commercial premises, electrical measurements are therefore one of the execution documents the construction manager attaches to the log. If a subcontractor installed the wiring and has not handed over their reports, the measurements can be taken again and a diagram prepared from the actual installation.

In your request, state the floor area, the number of distribution boards and who the construction manager is. More: putting commercial premises into use.

What house measurements and panel papers cost

Measurements and documentation for putting a private house into use cost from €230. For panel papers for Sadales tīkls we state the price after the site visit: it depends on the number and type of inverters and whether the inverter is on the Sadales tīkls list of accepted models. The Sadales tīkls assessment fee of €78.66 is separate (per the Sadales tīkls price list, checked 25 November 2026). For commercial premises the price is given after the site visit and depends on the area and number of distribution boards. The site visit is paid: €40.

Prices cover the electrician’s work and the documents. Sadales tīkls and building authority fees are separate.

Questions about these documents

What does the Ādaži building authority require for a house built by the owner?

According to the Ādaži Municipality list, for a second-group residential house built for your own needs, electrical installation test documents and an inspection report on heating appliances, chimneys and ventilation ducts also have to be submitted. The electrical part includes the measurement protocol and the distribution board diagram.

Is the document list different for a house built by a contractor?

Yes. If a building contractor does the work, the Ādaži list additionally includes the construction log with execution documents and the test protocols for technological equipment and special systems. Electrical measurements are then part of the execution documents prepared on the contractor’s side.

What has to be submitted in BIS before a building is put into use in Ādaži?

The full list depends on the construction route and the Ādaži Municipality building authority. On the electrical side, the measurement protocol, diagram and, if there is one, the Sadales tīkls report on connection readiness are uploaded to BIS. Other documents, such as the chimney report, are prepared by other specialists.
