Olaine has apartment buildings, detached-house areas, production, warehouse and railway properties. The Olaine building authority has separate document lists for garden houses and detached houses, and both include test documentation from a certified electrician. A production facility needs papers by distribution board.

A garden house in Olaine: its own list and acceptance conditions

Olaine Municipality uses separate document lists for detached houses and garden houses. In the Olaine building authority list, accepting garden houses (Group II buildings) with or without outbuildings requires electrical installation test documentation provided by a certified electrician. So even a small garden house has the same electrical papers as a house: measurements and a diagram.

The list also sets out when a garden house can be accepted: if general construction works and utility systems are complete, the facade is finished, the street side landscaped and at least one living room, a kitchen and a bathroom fitted out. The electrical installation is one of the utility systems, so it has to be complete and measured before the application.

If a garden house has an outbuilding with electricity, such as a shed or sauna, its line is included in the same protocol. If the garden house is connected through a gardening society’s network rather than its own Sadales tīkls connection, say so in your request: then it first has to be established where your installation begins. More: putting a garden house into use.

A detached house you built yourself: sub-point 168.1 of the Building Construction Regulations

Construction matters in Olaine are handled by the Olaine Municipality building authority, and the municipality states that since 1 January 2020 construction process documentation is only digital, in BIS. The Olaine list for detached and single-dwelling houses also includes electrical installation test documentation from a certified electrician.

The basis for this requirement is in the Building Construction Regulations. If a house was built under a building permit by the owner, sub-point 168.1 states that electrical installation test documentation is submitted to the building authority. If a contractor built the house, electrical measurements are part of the contractor’s as-built documentation.

Checked 25 November 2026: Building Construction Regulations (Cabinet Regulation No. 529 of 2 September 2014), sub-point 168.1, likumi.lv; Olaine Municipality document lists for detached houses and garden houses.

Industrial zone and warehouses: a protocol by distribution board

Olaine’s functions are industry, logistics and warehouses, living and local services. In an industrial building measurements cover the whole building, but the protocol arranges them by distribution board and circuit. This shows which workshop or part of the warehouse has what non-compliance, and repairs can be planned in parts. Keep the protocol with the building documents: you can compare the next test’s results with it and see which board has changed.

If a production facility has an explosive or chemically aggressive environment, the testing frequency under the fire safety regulations differs from an ordinary building, so say so in your request. Also write the number of distribution boards, whether a thermal camera inspection is needed and which equipment must not stop. More: electrical installation testing for businesses.

Earth electrodes and down conductors near forests and bogs: lightning protection measurements

Around Olaine there are forests and bogs, industrial areas and drained land. Soil composition affects earth electrode resistance: in peat, sand and clay it can differ, so in a lightning protection test the earthing measurement is the key figure.

From the ground, the down conductors along the facade and connections are inspected, and their continuity and the earth electrode resistance measured. The report form starts the measurement at the air terminal on the roof. The fire safety regulations currently in force (Cabinet Regulation No. 238, in force until 1 January 2027) include lightning protection in the periodic electrical installation test, and the result goes into the same report.

Before the site visit, find the lightning protection design or diagram and say whether the roof is accessible. If there is no design, the system is sketched during the test so the report shows which down conductors and earth electrodes were measured. More: lightning protection testing.

Questions about these documents

Which electrical documents have to be submitted for an Olaine garden house?

In the Olaine building authority list, a garden house with or without outbuildings has electrical installation test documentation provided by a certified electrician. It contains a measurement protocol for all circuits and a diagram. It is submitted in BIS.

In an industrial zone, are measurements taken for the building or each distribution board?

The whole building is measured, but each board and circuit separately, and the protocol arranges them by distribution board. If several companies rent the building, agree who orders the common part and who their own boards.

What is the basis for electrical documents for a self-built house?

The Building Construction Regulations: for a second-group residential house built by the owner, electrical installation test documentation is required by sub-point 157.7.1 under an explanatory note and sub-point 168.1 under a building permit.
