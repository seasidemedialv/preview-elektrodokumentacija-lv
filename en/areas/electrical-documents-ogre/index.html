A house owner with solar panels needs the Sadales tīkls papers and an inverter settings protocol. If the house was built long ago and not put into use, the starting point is a diagram and measurements for the Ogre Municipality building authority.

Ogre Municipality building authority: BIS or a confirmation form

Ogre has apartment block districts, detached-house areas, a historic centre and wooded terrain. Construction matters for all these properties are handled by the Ogre Municipality building authority. It states that the confirmation form has to be filled in if documents are not submitted through BIS. So documents can be submitted in BIS, and then the form does not have to be filled in separately.

On the electrical side this means the measurement protocol and diagram need to be received in a form that can be added to a BIS case, or as a printout if you submit on paper with the form. Before ordering, decide which way you will submit. What the electrical part looks like in a building authority case is described on the page electrical installation documents for the building authority.

The railway and the linear shape of the town create different access in northern and southern Ogre, so in your request give the exact address and the side of the railway.

A flat in an Ogre apartment block district: a report for one owner

In Ogre’s apartment block districts, flat measurements are ordered by the flat owner, not the whole building. The report describes only the flat’s installation: circuits from the flat distribution board, their insulation, phase-neutral loop impedance, earthing and residual current devices. The shared risers and stairwell distribution board remain the building manager’s responsibility.

If the flat distribution board is in the stairwell, arrange with the building manager or neighbours who will open it. If the old circuits in the flat have no earth conductor, the report records it, and this is an important fact when planning renovation. Before the site visit, note which switch feeds which room if the board has no labels.

The process is described on the page electrical installation testing in a flat.

Panels on a river bank or hill: Sadales tīkls steps and deadlines

Ogre is shaped by the banks of the Daugava and the Ogre river, hills and forests. If a house stands among trees, panels may be placed on the ground or an outbuilding roof rather than on the house. For Sadales tīkls this does not change the connection procedure: a microgenerator up to 11.1 kW on three phases or 3.7 kW on one phase follows the same route.

If you change the inverter after payment, Sadales tīkls allows the confirmation to be submitted with the details of the originally declared inverter and the new inverter’s protocol attached, if the new one is on the list and its capacity does not exceed that declared. Before choosing an installer, ask for their BLEEA or LEB certificate: without a generation specialisation they may not install a microgenerator or set up the inverter. We do not mount panels; we check what has been installed and draw up the papers. More: microgenerator connection.

An older house or one not put into use: the building authority’s reminder and the first step

If your house in an Ogre detached-house area or the historic centre was built long ago or has not been fully put into use, start early. The Ogre Municipality building authority reminds owners that sorting out documents for putting into use can take several months, and states that the tax rate for a structure past its construction deadline is 3 % after 1 January 2026.

The electrical part for such a house starts with a site visit. If there is no design or old diagram, the as-built diagram is drawn from the actual installation, and all circuits are then measured. If measurements reveal defects, an electrician fixes them, and only then can the documents be drawn up. The building authority determines which other papers are needed.

In your request, write roughly when the house was built, whether there is any design and whether the wiring has been changed since. More: putting an old house into use.

Questions: Electrical papers in Ogre

In how many working days does Sadales tīkls reply about a microgenerator?

If the inverter is on the Sadales tīkls list of accepted models, the reply to the application comes in 3 working days. After installation and submission of documents, Sadales tīkls issues the permit for parallel operation within 3 working days. The assessment fee under the price list is €78.66.

How do I put a house built many years ago into use in Ogre?

First find out from the Ogre Municipality building authority what the construction status of your house is and which documents are missing. The building authority reminds owners that sorting this out can take several months. On the electrical side the start is a measurement protocol; ask the building authority about an as-built diagram.

Can one owner order measurements for a flat?

Yes. The flat installation beyond the flat distribution board is the owner’s responsibility, and they can order the report themselves. If the shared risers or stairwell board also need testing, the building manager or the flat owners’ community orders it.
