The owner of a house who built it themselves has to submit electrical installation test documentation to the Sigulda Municipality building authority via BIS. For a hotel, guest house or sports facility owner the test is periodic, and its reports stay with the owner. Both routes are set out step by step below.

Sigulda Municipality building authority: documents in BIS after the works are complete

Sigulda has detached houses, apartment buildings, tourism, sports, hospitality and public properties. Structures in Sigulda are accepted into use by the Sigulda Municipality building authority, and after the works are complete the documents are submitted in BIS. If your property is on the slope of the ancient river valley, where the access road is steep or narrow, describe it in your request along with the address.

This follows from the Construction Law: a person initiates construction by submitting documents in BIS, and since 1 January 2020 the construction administrative process is started electronically in BIS (Section 14 and paragraph 21 of the Transitional Provisions). The electrical protocol and diagram therefore have to be added to the same BIS case as the other documents.

Checked 25 November 2026: Construction Law, Section 14 and paragraph 21 of the Transitional Provisions, likumi.lv; Sigulda Municipality service description, e.sigulda.lv.

The Sigulda service description states that for a second-group residential house built by the owner (sub-point 107.1 of the Building Construction Regulations), electrical installation test documentation and a report on flues and ventilation ducts are also submitted to the building authority. For first-group structures, completion is accepted by a mark in the explanatory note, submitting its Part II with attachments. More: electrical installation documents for the building authority.

Hotel, guest house or sports base: periodic test reports

In Sigulda’s tourism, sports and hospitality properties, the installation is larger than in a house, and for its owner testing is not one-off. The fire safety regulations currently in force (Cabinet Regulation No. 238, in force until 1 January 2027) require the electrical installation, earthing and lightning protection to be tested once every 10 years, in an explosive environment once every two years and in a chemically aggressive environment once a year. Together with this, distribution board contacts are checked with a thermal camera.

Property What the test covers What to state in the request
Hotel or guest house all distribution boards, room circuits, earthing, thermal camera number of floors and rooms, number of distribution boards
Sports facility hall and changing room circuits, outdoor lines, lightning protection whether there is a pool or sauna
Apartment building common part: incoming supply, risers, stairwells number of stairwells
Detached house all circuits, earthing, residual current devices whether there are separate buildings

In a hotel, measurements are planned floor by floor or board by board so that electricity is not switched off for guests in the whole building at once. If a sports facility has a pool or sauna, point 27 of LBN 261-15 sets special requirements for such places under the applicable standards, so these rooms’ circuits are listed separately in the report. If there is a previous report, attach a photo of it. More: periodic electrical installation testing.

Buildings on the valley bank: lightning protection measurements and access

The terrain of the Gauja valley and protected areas create different access and construction conditions from the flat Riga region. If a hotel or sports facility with lightning protection stands on a slope, the earth electrodes may be at different levels and have to be reached with the measuring instrument. If the earth electrodes are under paving or lawn and their locations are unknown, attach old plans; otherwise they have to be found on site, and the site visit takes more time.

The test inspects visible down conductors and measures continuity of connections and the resistance of each earth electrode. The results go into the same periodic test report. In your request, say whether the earth electrodes can be reached without ladders or machinery and whether there is a lightning protection design. More: lightning protection testing.

Solar panels in a protected area: what to ask before installation

Sigulda’s protected areas and valley landscape mean that for panels on a roof or the ground, you first need to ask the Sigulda Municipality building authority. Whether panels need building authority approval depends on whether construction works are required and on the rules of the location.

The Sadales tīkls side is the same across Latvia: for a microgenerator up to 11.1 kW, a connection is requested on e-st.lv, and after installation the confirmation and inverter settings protocol are submitted. Without Sadales tīkls permission for parallel operation, only brief test operation is allowed. Sadales tīkls entrusts mounting and inverter set-up only to a specialist whose certificate covers generating equipment. We check and document an installed system. How this works is described on the page putting solar panels into use.

What testing for a house, hotel and panels costs

Measurements and documentation for putting a private house into use cost from €230. For a hotel, sports facility or apartment building we state the price after the site visit: it is determined by the floor area, the number of distribution boards and circuits, lightning protection and the thermal camera inspection. Panel papers are priced after the site visit, depending on the number and type of inverters. The site visit is paid: €40.

Prices cover the electrician’s work and the documents. Sadales tīkls and building authority fees are separate. The documents are prepared and signed by a certified electrical specialist.

Questions about these documents

Does a hotel in Sigulda need a periodic test report?

Yes, the fire safety regulations currently in force (Cabinet Regulation No. 238, in force until 1 January 2027) require a test once every 10 years and a report on its results, as well as a thermal camera report on contacts. If the hotel has an explosive or chemically aggressive environment, the frequency is higher.

How are house acceptance documents submitted in Sigulda?

After the works are complete, the documents are submitted in BIS to the Sigulda Municipality building authority. A house built by the owner additionally needs electrical installation test documentation and a report on flues and ventilation ducts.

In a protected area, should the building authority be asked about solar panels?

Yes, ask the Sigulda Municipality building authority before installation: the answer depends on whether construction works are needed and on the area’s rules. The Sadales tīkls microgenerator papers are needed regardless of the building authority’s answer.
