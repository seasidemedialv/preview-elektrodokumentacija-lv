What is the same everywhere

Sadales tīkls connection rules are the same across the whole country, so the papers for a new connection, a capacity increase or solar panels are the same in Riga and the Riga region. Construction regulations and LBN 261-15 also apply nationwide.

The building authority differs: in Riga, putting a building into use is handled by the City Development Department of the Riga State City Municipality, and in the Riga region by each municipality’s building authority in its own territory. For questions about building authority procedure, ask the authority where you will submit the documents.

Cabinet Regulation No. 529 “Building Construction Regulations”, likumi.lv; Riga municipality service description, riga.lv. Checked 25 November 2026.

Questions about these documents

Are the documents in the Riga region different from those in Riga?

The electrical installation documents and Sadales tīkls requirements are the same. What differs is the building authority where you submit the documents for putting the building into use.

Where are documents for putting a building into use submitted in Riga?

In Riga this is handled by the City Development Department of the Riga State City Municipality; the application can also be submitted in BIS.

Is the price higher outside Riga?

Distance from Riga can change the price. We state it after the site visit for the particular property.
