Installation report on a villa’s old electrical installation

The typical development of Mežaparks is historic villas, detached houses and low-rise buildings. In such a house the electrical installation may have been replaced piece by piece: new circuits on one floor, old wiring on another, a distribution board moved without a diagram. The installation report describes what is on site now, the condition of the insulation and protection and what has to be put in order before the installation is used further.

The installation report is useful before deciding on repairs, before buying a villa and when a new owner has no papers at all about the wiring. It is not a building authority document and does not replace measurements after alterations. For what it includes, see installation report on old wiring. If damage is found during the site visit, an electrician fixes it; electrical installation repairs in Riga are suitable for that, while we test and prepare the papers.

Villa renovation: residual current protection at the incoming supply and the new diagram

Mežaparks is shaped by forest, the closeness of Lake Ķīšezers and a protected cultural and historical character. In a renovation the facade and room layout may stay as they were while the electrical installation is built anew. The papers then have to describe the new condition, not the historic house, and they have to be prepared after the work, when it is known what exactly has been built.

One requirement applies specifically to the incoming supply of detached houses. Sub-point 28.2 of LBN 261-15 states that in the main incoming distribution board of individual residential buildings, protection against residual current with a 300–500 mA tripping current is installed on the common incoming supply, or an alternative technical protection solution is provided. When rebuilding the board, agree with the electrician or designer which option is chosen, so that it is also visible in the diagram.

Checked on 25 November 2026: Cabinet Regulation of 9 June 2015 No. 294, LBN 261-15 “Internal Electrical Installations of Buildings”, sub-point 28.2.

After the work all circuits are measured; the as-built diagram, that is, an electrical installation diagram showing what has actually been built, helps to link the measurements to the lines and is useful if the building authority asks for it. Which documents go into the building authority case is explained on the page electrical documents after alterations or renovation. Order the measurements while the distribution board is still open and the circuit labels match what has been built.

Panels on a villa roof: approval in Riga and papers for Sadales tīkls

On 23 March 2022 the Riga municipality explained that installing solar panels does not need building authority permission if no construction work, such as strengthening load-bearing structures, is required. Outside the historic centre the placement has to be approved by the City Development Department if the panels will be on a roof or facade facing the street (explanation of 28 July 2022). If the building is a cultural monument or is in a monument area, approval from the National Heritage Board is also needed.

For Mežaparks villas it is worth clarifying this before ordering panels, because approval depends on the roof direction and the building’s status. The panels are mounted and the inverter set up by an installer who, under the Sadales tīkls requirements, must hold a certificate for generating equipment. The inverter settings protocol is signed under the same field, and we prepare it together with the other enclosures if this requirement has been met. For the whole route, see the section solar panel papers for Sadales tīkls.

EV charger at a detached house: is the existing capacity enough

In its advice on charging at a private house, Sadales tīkls writes that no application is needed if the existing connection capacity is enough, and that a charger with dynamic load management may make it possible to do without a capacity increase. The check compares the charger power with the main protective device and the other load and assesses the cable and protection. The charger is fitted by an installer. For what the work includes, see electric car charger connection check.

Mežaparks is reached by tram, Meža prospekts and Kokneses prospekts, but traffic changes considerably during events. In the form, give several suitable dates, so that the site visit can be set on a day without a large event. Attach photos of the main distribution board and the marking on the main protective device; then it is clear before the visit what capacity has to be reckoned with.

Questions about these documents

What residual current protection is required at the incoming supply of a single-family house?

Sub-point 28.2 of LBN 261-15 requires a residual current device (RCD) with a 300–500 mA tripping current, or an alternative technical solution, in the main incoming distribution board of an individual residential building. Which of them your villa has is seen during the site visit from the board and the diagram.

Does a villa renovation need a new diagram?

If the distribution board or circuits have been changed, the old diagram no longer matches what has been built. A new as-built diagram shows the actual condition and is useful both for the measurement report and for later work. Whether the building authority requires it in your case depends on the scope of the work; ask the building authority.

Does panel placement in Mežaparks have to be approved by the municipality?

According to the Riga municipality’s explanation (28 July 2022), outside the historic centre panels on a roof or facade facing the street have to be approved. On the courtyard side and on the ground no approval from the department is needed, but the spatial plan must be observed. For a monument, approval from the heritage board is also needed.
