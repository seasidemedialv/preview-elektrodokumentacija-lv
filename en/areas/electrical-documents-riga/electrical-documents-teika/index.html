A pre-war detached house: a report before big decisions

The typical development of Teika is pre-war detached houses, low-rise and apartment buildings, office complexes and commercial premises. In a detached house that has changed owners over the decades, the installation may be partly new and partly original. If the wiring in your house has not been replaced or you do not know when it was done, a report is useful before a capacity increase or meter relocation.

In the report the electrician describes the distribution board, incoming cable, protective devices and visible installation, and writes what is suitable and what needs putting right. We say which measurements the house needs after the site visit. With this paper you can plan work in the right order, instead of applying for capacity first and then finding out that the board does not suit it.

Useful for the report: photos of the incoming supply and board, information on the existing permitted capacity and number of phases shown on e-st.lv, and a list of the appliances you plan to add. Then the report can say straight away whether the board suits the planned load.

If the report mentions faults, the electrician fixes them; we test and prepare the papers. For how the report is made, see the page old electrical installation report.

An office building and more permitted capacity

People live in Teika, and there are offices, technology companies, shops and service outlets here. In an office that adds a server room, air conditioners or electric car charging for staff, the existing permitted capacity can become a bottleneck. A capacity increase is applied for from Sadales tīkls on e-st.lv, and it assesses whether the existing connection is suitable or the network has to be rebuilt.

Before applying, find out whether the incoming supply, main protective device and main distribution board suit the new capacity. If something has to be changed, an electrician does it, and after the work the customer submits a confirmation on e-st.lv that the internal installation is ready. We test the installation and prepare the papers for the electrical part.

If the building has several tenants, find out who holds the Sadales tīkls contract: the application is submitted by that party or with its approval. For the steps, see the page increasing the permitted capacity.

Meter to an outside wall: a design brief from Sadales tīkls

In a detached house the meter may be indoors, in a hallway or in the basement. If you want to move it to an outside wall or another place on the plot, this is no longer a move within the room. Sadales tīkls has a separate procedure for this case:

  1. Application on the technical portal The application is submitted on the Sadales tīkls technical portal, not on e-st.lv.
  2. Design brief Sadales tīkls examines the application within three working days on average and charges no fee for examining it. You receive the brief as an electronically signed EDOC document.
  3. Construction design, if building work is needed If the move requires building work, expect to have a construction design drawn up and construction work done.
  4. Work according to the brief The electrician carries out the work according to the conditions of the brief. All costs are borne by the customer.

Checked on 25 November 2026: Sadales tīkls “Meter operations” (sadalestikls.lv). In most cases the conditions of the design brief are valid for at least two years.

Before applying, photograph the existing meter and incoming supply and mark on a plan where you want it. For the electrician’s work, see the page moving the meter.

Solar panels on a detached house by a major street

Teika is a residential and office neighbourhood close to the centre along Brīvības gatve, and some houses stand right by the street. Riga municipality explained on 28 July 2022 that outside the historic centre, panels on facades and roofs facing the street have to be approved by the City Development Department. On a roof that does not face public outdoor space, or on the ground, approval from the department is not needed; the spatial plan has to be followed in any case.

If the detached house is a cultural monument or is located on the territory of a monument, according to the same explanation the placement also has to be approved by the National Cultural Heritage Board.

After installation a microgenerator needs the Sadales tīkls permit for parallel operation and an inverter settings protocol signed by a specialist whose certificate covers generating equipment. Sadales tīkls also requires the installation itself and the inverter adjustment to be done by a specialist with such a certificate. We do not fit panels, but we check what has been done and document it. More: microgenerator connection papers.

Questions: Electrical documents in Teika

Can the meter be moved from a hallway to an outside wall without Sadales tīkls?

No. Removing seals fitted by Sadales tīkls without permission is prohibited. To move a meter from inside the house to an outside wall, an application has to be submitted on the Sadales tīkls technical portal, and the work is done according to the design brief it issues.

Which papers are needed if capacity is increased for an old house?

On the Sadales tīkls side, an application on e-st.lv and, after the work, a confirmation that the internal installation is ready. For an old house, a report on the condition of the installation is useful beforehand: it shows whether the board and incoming supply have to be changed. If they are changed, a measurement report on the new state is useful after the work.

Does an office building need electrical installation testing after a set period?

The fire safety regulations currently in force (MK 238, in force until 1 January 2027) provide for electrical installation testing once every 10 years regardless of the form of ownership of the property, so for an office building too. These regulations cease to be in force on 2 January 2027, and the interval after that date was not yet known on 25 November 2026.
