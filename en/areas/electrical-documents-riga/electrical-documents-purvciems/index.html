A flat in a Soviet-era estate or a new development: what is measured

In Purvciems, Soviet-era estates, newer developments, shops and office buildings stand side by side. For a flat this means two different starting points. If the building is a newer development, ask the developer or manager whether the flat circuit diagram from construction has been kept: the electrician compares what is installed against it. If the flat is in a series-built block and the wiring has not been replaced, there may be no diagram, and the circuits are identified on site.

  • insulation resistance for each circuit: whether there is a leakage path between conductors and to earth;
  • the phase-neutral loop: the impedance of the circuit from the distribution board to the socket, used to judge whether the circuit breaker will trip in a fault;
  • protective conductor continuity, if the flat has a protective conductor;
  • operation of the residual current device (RCD), if one is fitted in the distribution board.

The results go into a report with a list of circuits. How this work goes from application to report is described on the page electrical installation testing in a flat.

After rewiring a kitchen or bathroom: protection and new circuits

If the kitchen or bathroom in the flat has been rewired, the new circuits have to be measured, and the electrician needs to see whether they are protected as the construction standard requires. LBN 261-15 sub-point 28.1 sets residual current protection with an operating current of no more than 30 mA. It covers three types of circuit: general-use sockets up to 32 A used by people without electrical training, sockets up to 32 A for portable equipment used outdoors, and household lighting circuits. This wording has been in force since 1 July 2025 (Cabinet Regulation of 17 June 2025 No. 355).

For a flat on Deglava iela, Dzelzavas iela or Stirnu iela, the address alone may not be enough in your request. In large blocks, state the building section, stairwell and floor, and attach a photo of the flat distribution board: then it is visible before the site visit whether the protection is there. Which papers the owner keeps after the work is explained on the page documents after rewiring a flat.

Checked on 25 November 2026: Latvian Construction Standard LBN 261-15 “Internal Electrical Installations of Buildings”, sub-point 28.1, as worded by Cabinet Regulation of 17 June 2025 No. 355 (likumi.lv/ta/id/274674).

Stairwell and incoming distribution boards are shared property of the building

Purvciems is one of the largest apartment-building residential neighbourhoods in Riga. So in one building two different people may order testing, and each has their own part. The flat owner is responsible for the circuits from their flat distribution board. The incoming distribution board, stairwell boards, basement and common-area lines are the shared part of the building, and its testing is decided by the manager or the flat owners’ community.

If you represent the building, write in your request the number of stairwells and floors and whether there is a copy of the previous report. To learn what testing the shared part covers and which reports come out of it, read the page electrical installation testing in an apartment building. Testing inside a flat and testing the building are separate jobs with separate reports. Meters and seals belong to Sadales tīkls, and sealed parts are not opened without its permission.

A charger in the courtyard parking area: which distribution board should feed it

Large inner courtyards and busy main roads are typical of Purvciems. If you want an electric car charger in the parking area by the building, the first question is the power source, ahead of the charger model: a flat circuit, the building’s shared installation or a separate connection. Each option means a different party to agree with and a different paperwork route.

Sadales tīkls states that a flat owner changing the permitted load must attach the building manager’s written consent to the application. During the site visit the electrician assesses the connection point, protection and cable route and says whether the existing capacity is enough. The installation is done by the installer; we test and prepare the papers. What this work includes is described on the page electric car charger connection test and documents.

Questions about these documents

Can only some circuits be tested in a panel-block flat?

Yes, if needed, for example only the rewired kitchen circuits. The report then contains only these circuits and does not describe the whole flat, so it is worth saying so to the person you give the report to. In your request, write which circuits and why; the scope is refined at the site visit.

Which measurements follow a kitchen rewiring?

For the new circuits, insulation resistance, the phase-neutral loop and protective conductor continuity are measured, and operation of the residual current device is tested. The electrician checks whether socket and lighting circuits are protected by an RCD of up to 30 mA, as set by LBN 261-15 sub-point 28.1.

What is checked if a charger is wanted in the parking area by the building?

First, which part of the installation would feed the charger and whether the existing capacity is enough. If it is fed from the flat and the permitted load has to change, Sadales tīkls requires the building manager’s written consent. After the site visit we tell you whether a capacity increase is needed and which papers there will be.
