Someone else installed the wiring, but there are no papers

The typical buildings of Aplokciems are detached houses, pockets of allotment and low-rise development and a few newer residential buildings. In a newer house the electrical installation may have been done by a builder or electrician who left no papers after the work. First ask them what has been kept: a diagram, a measurement protocol, concealed works records on the installation of electrical equipment. Even part of it makes the work easier.

If nothing can be obtained, the measurements and the as-built diagram are prepared anew from what is on site. A concealed works record is not recreated once the works are covered up; ask the building authority what it accepts instead. If the measurements find faults, an electrician repairs them, and only then are the documents drawn up. This situation is described in more detail on the page wiring installed by someone else, no papers.

If the house has already been put into use and the papers are needed for a sale or a bank, the building authority is no longer the recipient. Then a measurement protocol or a report showing the current condition can be useful, and the buyer or bank can compare it with their requirements. If the house has not yet been put into use, the documents have to match what the building authority asks for, and that is a wider set.

In your request, write who installed the wiring and when, whether the house has already been put into use and who is asking for the papers now: the building authority, a buyer or a bank.

What the test protocol contains and who may sign it

Aplokciems is a quieter residential area of northern Riga near Lake Ķīšezers and the Jaunciems area, and for putting a detached house into use the papers cover the whole building and its distribution board. For putting a house into use, the construction standard LBN 261-15, sub-point 53.4, names the tests whose results are attached to the acceptance documents:

  • electrical resistance test of wires and cables;
  • measurements of the total impedance of the phase-neutral loop;
  • test of the dissipation resistance of earthing loops;
  • test of the electrical resistance of the bonding between earth electrodes and the elements to be earthed.

Point 55 of the same standard provides that these tests are carried out by a certified electrical specialist who has been certified by a body accredited in Latvia and complying with the standard LVS EN ISO/IEC 17024. So a protocol without such a signature will not do for putting into use, even if the measurements are correct. For what the protocol looks like and what is entered in it, see the page test protocol.

Checked 25 November 2026: Cabinet Regulation No. 294 of 9 June 2015, “LBN 261-15 Internal Electrical Installations of Buildings”, sub-point 53.4 and point 55.

An allotment cabin with electricity

Cabins in allotment areas are small, but they too may have a distribution board, outdoor socket outlets and a cable laid in the ground. If you are selling or buying a cabin, or its connection is being restored, a measurement report shows whether the insulation and the protective conductor are in order. Building authority papers may not be needed for such a cabin, but if it is converted into a residential building or put into use, the building authority decides what it will ask for.

For outdoor outlets that garden equipment is plugged into, a residual current device matters, and the measurements check whether it trips. For what garden house papers contain and how they are prepared, see the page garden house electrical documents. In your request, write whether the cabin has its own meter and how far it is from the road.

After water damage: test before switching on

Near Aplokciems there are waterfronts, forests and lower-lying land. If your cellar has flooded or water has reached the distribution board or junction boxes, do not switch on the affected circuit until it has been tested. Moisture can remain in the insulation even after the surfaces have dried, and an insulation resistance measurement shows it.

A report after water damage describes which circuits were affected and what has to be replaced; we tell you which measurements to make after inspecting the property. An insurer may find it useful, but ask them what exactly they want to see before you order. For how this work is done, see the page electrical installation testing after flooding. In your request, write how high the water was and which rooms were affected.

Questions: Electrical documents in Aplokciems

Who may measure the phase-neutral loop for putting into use?

A certified electrical specialist certified by a body accredited in Latvia under the standard LVS EN ISO/IEC 17024 (LBN 261-15, point 55). Ask which body issued the certification and check its validity there; the BIS register of construction specialists serves for construction practice certificates.

What paper should you ask the previous installer of the house for?

The as-built diagram or design diagram, the measurement protocol if one was drawn up, and concealed works records on the installation of electrical equipment. If something is missing, the measurements and diagram can be prepared anew, but concealed works records are not recreated after covering up; ask the building authority about a substitute.

Does a damp cellar call for an extra test?

If there is a distribution board, boxes or cables in the cellar and there has been water there, these circuits are worth measuring before use. An insulation resistance measurement shows whether moisture has remained in the insulation. If the cellar is dry and has had no water in it, a separate test is not needed.
