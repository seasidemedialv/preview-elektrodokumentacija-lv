For a self-built house the building authority requires electrical installation test documentation for handover, and the house can be put into use even if not all rooms are finished. For a garden house the question is simpler: whether its installation is safe and whether it suits the connection. In both cases you start with a site visit.

A self-built detached house: handover with one finished room

The typical development of Vecdaugava is detached houses, low-rise housing, gardens and the closeness of natural areas. If the house has been built by the owner, points 157.7.1 and 168.1 of Building Construction Regulations require electrical installation test documentation for acceptance into use. If you want to put the house into use before everything inside is finished, the regulations allow it on specific conditions.

Point 160.1 of Building Construction Regulations sets when a second-group single-flat house can be accepted into use. General construction work and engineering networks must be complete, the facade finished and the street side landscaped. At least one living room, a kitchen and a bathroom must be fitted out, and sanitary fixtures connected to the engineering networks. For the electrical part this means that measurements must cover the whole installation built, not only the finished rooms.

Checked on 25 November 2026: Cabinet Regulation of 2 September 2014 No. 529 “Building Construction Regulations”, points 157.7.1, 160.1 and 168.1.

  1. Finish the required minimum One room, kitchen, bathroom, facade and street side; engineering networks built.
  2. Order the measurements The electrician measures all circuits built, including lines in unfinished rooms, and prepares the test documentation.
  3. Gather the other documents The rest of the list is set by the building authority; in Riga this is the municipality’s City Development Department.
  4. Submit in BIS In cases started after 31 December 2019, in Riga the acceptance application can be submitted in BIS.

For what the electrical documents contain in this case, see the page electrical documents for a self-built house. Mark the circuits of unfinished rooms separately in your request, so that they are visible in the protocol.

Garden house: safety and papers without the building authority

In Vecdaugava there are gardens and garden houses alongside detached houses. If a garden house is not being put into use as a residential building, you may need electrical testing for yourself: before reconnection, before selling or after wiring done by your own hand. Then a measurement report showing the condition of the circuits and earthing can be useful.

If a garden house is converted into a residential one, the route changes, and the electrical documents have to be what the building authority requires. For the difference between the two cases, see the page electrical documents for a garden house. In your request, write whether the house has its own meter or is fed from the dwelling house distribution board.

If you wired the garden house yourself, there is nothing to hide: tell what was done and when. Measurements will show where the problems are, and the electrician fixes them before the report is drawn up.

Earthing measurements on a waterside property

Vecdaugava is a northern waterside neighbourhood between Vecāķi, Mangaļsala and Vecmīlgrāvis. Earthing resistance depends on the soil and its moisture, and moisture changes with the seasons. So the protocol records the result as well as the measurement date and method, and these details matter when comparing with a previous measurement.

The measurement checks earth electrode resistance and protective conductor continuity from the main earthing busbar to the sockets. If the earth electrode was built by yourself and its location is unknown, write that, because then the connection point has to be found first. For how the work goes, see the page earthing resistance measurements.

If the property has a sauna, boat shed or another building with its own electricity line, its protective conductor also has to be connected to earthing. Write in your request how many such buildings there are and whether they have their own boards, so that the measurements can be planned for one visit.

Panels near a nature reserve: what to find out beforehand

Vecdaugava has a nature reserve, wetland areas and water close by. If your plot lies within or next to the reserve, ask the municipality before ordering which restrictions apply to panels on the ground or roof. Riga municipality explained on 23 March 2022 that a building authority permit is not needed for panels if no building work is required, but this does not cover other requirements.

Near a nature reserve, as elsewhere, Sadales tīkls requires a construction specialist with a certificate in the generation field to install the microgenerator and adjust the inverter. On this basis we prepare the protocol and attachments. For assessing a microgenerator connection Sadales tīkls charges €78.66 under its price list, checked on 25 November 2026. For the whole route, see the section solar panel papers for Sadales tīkls.

Questions: Electrical documents in Vecdaugava

Can a house be put into use if only one room, the kitchen and bathroom are ready?

A second-group single-flat house can, if the other conditions of point 160.1 of Building Construction Regulations are also met: general construction work and engineering networks complete, facade finished and street side landscaped. Electrical installation measurements must cover the whole installation built.

Where is the earthing measurement result recorded?

In the measurement protocol or test report prepared by the electrician, with the measurement date, method and measuring instrument details. For putting a house into use this result is part of the electrical installation test documentation; in periodic testing under MK 238 (in force until 1 January 2027) it is entered in the test report.

Do panels near a nature reserve need any approval?

This is determined by the municipality and the nature protection requirements for the particular place, so ask the municipality before ordering panels. Sadales tīkls papers are a separate question, and they are needed in any case if the system will run in parallel with the grid.
