Detached house in Jugla: a report on the whole installation from the incoming supply

The typical development of Jugla is apartment estates, detached houses, retail sites and educational institutions. Unlike a flat owner, a detached house owner owns the whole installation from the Sadales tīkls boundary: the incoming cable, the incoming board, circuits, the earth electrode and any outbuildings. So a house report contains more than circuit measurements: also the earthing resistance and a check of the incoming protection.

If the house has several distribution boards or a sauna or garage connected by cable, write this in your request, because each board is a separate part of the report. If there is an old diagram or design, attach it: the electrician compares it with the actual condition. The work is described in more detail in electrical installation testing in a private house. If you are selling or buying a house, the report can be useful to the parties as an independent description. It shows the condition on the day of testing and states which values do not comply, but it is not a building authority document if the house is not being put into use.

If there has been water in the basement or on the ground floor

Around Jugla there is a lake, forests and a busy main road. If your house or flat has had water damage, whether the water came from outside or from the plumbing, the electrics in the circuits that got wet have to be assessed before they are switched on again. The document in this case is an installation report: it can be useful to you, to know what may be switched on, and to your insurer, if it asks for a description.

Reconnecting a house or flat after a long interruption

Jugla is a large eastern residential neighbourhood by Lake Jugla and Brīvības gatve, and it has both houses and flats that may have stood unused for a long time, for example after an inheritance. If the previous user’s contract with the supplier has ended and the electricity has been off, the reconnection application to Sadales tīkls is submitted by the electricity supplier you choose.

Sadales tīkls advises that, if the power supply has been interrupted for a long time or the internal installation has not been tested for a long time, you choose a certified electrician and test the installation before applying for reconnection. If the installation is not fit for use, Sadales tīkls does not restore the connection. So the test is a recommendation rather than a protocol required by Sadales tīkls, but it prevents the reconnection stalling over an unfit installation.

Once the supplier has submitted the application, Sadales tīkls issues the invoice within 3 working days and restores the connection within 2 working days after payment. In your request, write how long the property has been unused and whether the meter is in place. Read how electricity reconnection works on a separate page.

Flat in a Jugla estate: circuit report and the shared part

For a flat in the apartment estates of Jugla the measurements cover the circuits from the flat distribution board: insulation resistance, the phase-neutral loop and the tripping of the residual current device (RCD), if there is one. The stairwell and incoming supply are the shared part of the building, tested when the building manager orders it. The measurement results are arranged by circuit in the report, so that you can see which room or line is the problem.

If a flat has been flooded from the floor above, only the affected circuits are assessed, but if water has run through the stairwell distribution board, the building manager must be told. In your request, write which rooms are affected and whether the electricity in the flat is currently on. The work in a flat is described on the page electrical installation testing in a flat.

Questions about these documents

What does Sadales tīkls advise before restoring a connection that has been off for a long time?

Sadales tīkls advises choosing a certified electrician and testing the internal installation if the power supply has been interrupted for a long time or the installation has not been tested for a long time. If the installation is not fit for use, the connection is not restored. So it is worth testing before you apply.

What should you do with the electrics after water damage in the basement?

Do not switch on the circuits that got wet, and photograph the distribution board and the damaged spot. Then order insulation measurements for the affected circuits. If the insurer asks for a document, attach its written question to your request, so that the installation report answers exactly that.

Does a flat need an installation report after flooding?

An installation report can be useful for an insurance case or if losses have to be agreed with neighbours. If the water affected only a few circuits, a measurement report for them may be enough. During the site visit we assess which document suits your case.
