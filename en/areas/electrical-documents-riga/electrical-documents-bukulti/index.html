A garden house or summer cottage: first a question for the building authority

The typical buildings of Bukulti are detached houses, gardens and newer low-rise residential buildings. For a garden house or summer cottage the content of the electrical documents is similar to that for a house: measurements, report and diagram. What differs is whether and how the building authority asks for them, because that depends on the building group and on whether it was built under an explanatory note, a building permit or a construction notification.

The Building Construction Regulations expressly name electrical installation test documentation for a second-group residential house that the owner has built themselves. For a garden house that is not such a house, find out the requirements from the building authority; in Riga that is the City Development Department. If you will use the garden house all year or plan to convert it into a residential house, ask this before the work.

In your request, write the floor area, whether the building has its own distribution board and which document the building authority has asked for. More on this case: putting a garden house into use.

A new low-rise house you built yourself

If you built the new house in Bukulti yourself as the owner on your own land, the building authority needs electrical installation test documentation at acceptance (Building Construction Regulations, sub-points 157.7.1 and 168.1). It consists of measurements: insulation resistance, phase-neutral loop (the total impedance of the circuit, used to judge whether a circuit breaker will trip), earthing and testing of residual current devices (RCD), as well as a report and an electrical installation diagram.

Under LBN 261-15 these tests are carried out by a certified electrical specialist. The measurements are best made when the board and circuits are finished but still accessible, because repairs are harder after the finishes. For construction cases opened in Riga after 31 December 2019, the documents are added to the BIS case. Everything about this case: electrical installation test documentation for a self-built house.

Solar panels: who may sign the inverter protocol

Bukulti serves housing, leisure and suburban services, and there may be solar panels on detached house roofs. For a microgenerator of up to 11.1 kW on a three-phase or 3.7 kW on a single-phase connection, a confirmation with attachments is submitted to Sadales tīkls on e-st.lv, and one of them is the inverter settings protocol. Sadales tīkls sets which specialist’s signature is accepted:

Document or card Accepted for the protocol?
BLEEA certificate in the assembly or installation of electrical equipment with a generating equipment (microgenerator) specialisation Accepted
LEB certificate with a specialisation in the electrical part of power stations or generating equipment Accepted
Electrician certificate without a generation specialisation Not accepted
Electrical safety group card Not accepted: Sadales tīkls does not treat it as a certificate

Sadales tīkls replies to the application within 3 working days if the inverter is on the recognised list; after approval the unit must be installed within 9 months, and after receiving the documents the permission for parallel operation is issued within 3 working days. This requirement also applies to mounting and to inverter set-up. How connecting a microgenerator works is described on a separate page.

Checked 25 November 2026: Sadales tīkls “Certification requirements for installers of generating equipment” (sadalestikls.lv/lv/sertifikacijas-prasibas-elektroenergijas-razosanas-iekartu-uzstaditajiem).

Checking a certificate if the electrician comes from the neighbouring municipality

Bukulti is a north-eastern residential neighbourhood between Lake Jugla, the Baltezers direction and the city boundary, so the electrician or panel installer may come from the neighbouring municipality. Sadales tīkls requirements are the same throughout the country, and the same certificate is valid on both sides of the boundary. Only the building authority to which the building documents are submitted differs.

The BIS register of construction specialists, which Sadales tīkls also points to, shows construction practice certificates, while those issued by BLEEA and LEB are checked with the certification body itself. For a generating equipment certificate, Sadales tīkls advises checking four things: BLEEA or LEB issued it, the programme name includes the generation field, the certificate is valid and the details match the person. For how to do this step by step, read the page electrician certificate.

Questions: Electrical documents in Bukulti

What certificate is needed for an inverter settings protocol?

A BLEEA or LEB certificate in the assembly or installation of electrical equipment with a specialisation in generating equipment or microgenerators. A general electrician certificate without this specialisation and an electrical safety group card are not accepted by Sadales tīkls. The documents are prepared and signed by a certified electrical specialist with this field.

Is a garden house asked for the same as a private house when put into use?

Not in every case. The Building Construction Regulations expressly name electrical installation test documentation for a self-built second-group residential house. For a garden house the requirements depend on the building group and how it was built, so ask the building authority before you order documents.

Where can you check an electrician’s certificate?

It depends on the type: a construction practice certificate is searched for in the BIS register of construction specialists by name or number, while a BLEEA or LEB certificate is checked against the issuer’s data. For a generation certificate, check the issuer (BLEEA or LEB), the specialisation in the programme name, the expiry date and whether the details match the person.
