Terminal or warehouse handover: measurements and switching diagrams

The typical development of Kundziņsala is port terminals, warehouses, infrastructure sites and a small historic residential area. If the electrical installation in a terminal or warehouse has been newly built or altered, acceptance into use needs more than a measurement report. Point 53 of LBN 261-15 states that a protocol with documents is attached to the acceptance statement if the relevant work has been carried out, and among them are:

  • a set of drawings corrected under designer’s supervision and detailed drawings;
  • automatic control and electrical switching diagrams of the installation;
  • reports on testing the resistance of wires and cables, the phase-neutral loop and earthing;
  • descriptions of how to use the systems and staff training protocols.

Checked on 25 November 2026: Cabinet Regulation of 9 June 2015 No. 294, LBN 261-15 “Internal Electrical Installations of Buildings”, point 53.

We prepare the electrical part: measurements and as-built diagrams showing what has actually been built. If the site has automation cabinets, write whether their diagrams come from the manufacturer or the designer, because they are attached rather than drawn anew. For how the work goes in company premises, see electrical installation testing for businesses.

If several tenants use a terminal, each may have its own distribution board after a shared incoming supply. Then first agree with the owner who orders documents for the shared part and who for their own premises. In your request, also state whether the construction manager is organising the handover, because then the prepared measurements and diagrams go into their document set.

Distribution boards under load: thermal camera inspection in cargo handling

The island serves the port, logistics, cargo handling, warehousing and infrastructure. In such sites distribution boards work with heavy loads, and weak connections in them heat up. A thermal camera shows this only when current flows through the connection, so the inspection is planned during working hours, when cranes, conveyors or cooling are switched on, not when the warehouse stands empty.

The fire safety regulations currently in force (MK 238, in force until 1 January 2027) provide for a thermal camera inspection together with the periodic electrical installation test and a separate report on its results. For how this work is prepared and what the report contains, see thermal camera inspection of distribution boards. In your request, write the number of distribution boards and which of them carry the heaviest load.

If the thermal camera shows an overheated connection, the report records the location and temperature, and an electrician has to tighten or replace the connection. After the repair a second image shows that the problem has been fixed, and it can be attached to the report.

Lightning protection for port buildings by the Daugava

Kundziņsala is surrounded by the Daugava, port activity and an industrial environment. Large warehouses and terminal buildings may have a lightning protection system, and its test is entered in the same periodic test report as the electrical installation measurements. If the system has several down conductors, the connection to the earth electrode is measured for each.

The report form provides for measuring the circuit from the air terminal to the test joint. More: lightning protection testing. If the site has a lightning protection diagram or an earlier report, attach it: then it is clear how many measuring points there are and where they are.

Historic house in the island’s residential area: an installation report

The island has a small historic residential area. If the wiring in your house has not been replaced for a long time or there are no papers on it at all, an installation report shows what is on site. It shows what the insulation is like, whether the protection and earthing work and what needs to be changed. An installation report is not a building authority paper, but it is useful before repairs, a sale or reconnecting the supply.

If damage is found, an electrician repairs it, and we prepare the papers afterwards. For what the installation report contains, see installation report on old wiring. The residential area is also reached by port roads and the bridge, so write in the form whether a pass is needed to drive in.

A historic house may still have old porcelain plug fuses or aluminium wiring. If that is the case in your house, write it in your request, because the installation report then describes specifically which circuits can be kept and which are recommended for replacement.

Questions about these documents

Which documents do the fire safety regulations require at an industrial site?

The fire safety regulations currently in force (MK 238, in force until 1 January 2027) require a test report on the electrical installation, earthing and lightning protection (Annex 6) and a thermal camera test report (Annex 7). The interval is once every 10 years, but once every two years in an explosive environment and once a year in a chemically aggressive one.

Can an installation report be prepared for a historic house on the island?

Yes. An installation report describes the condition of the existing installation and what needs to be changed; we tell you which measurements it needs after the site visit. It requires access to the distribution board and all rooms, and if a pass is needed to drive in, it has to be arranged before the site visit.

How do the access rules in the port area affect the site visit?

The site visit can start only once entry and access to the electrical rooms have been agreed. In the form, write whether a pass is needed and what details it requires, who will meet us at the entrance and which rooms will be open. We agree the date once this is known.
