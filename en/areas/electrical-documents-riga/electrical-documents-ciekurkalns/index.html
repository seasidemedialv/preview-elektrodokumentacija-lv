The building determines which document is needed in the end. For a house it is a measurement report or papers for putting into use, for a warehouse a report on each distribution board and, if more capacity is needed, also the electrical part of an application to Sadales tīkls.

A wooden or masonry house: measurement report and papers for putting into use

The typical buildings of Čiekurkalns are wooden and masonry low-rise houses, apartment buildings, warehouses and office complexes. In a private house, measurements show whether wire insulation, phase-neutral loop and earthing meet the standard. If the house has been rebuilt or has to be put into use after renovation, these measurements become part of the package submitted to the building authority.

In a wooden house it is important to know where the wires run: over the cladding, under it or in conduits. That does not change the measuring method, but it shows which places need closer attention at the site visit. If you know who replaced the wires and when, write it in your request together with a photo of the distribution board.

If the house is divided into flats with separate meters, a report can be drawn up for each flat or for the whole house together. Say which option you need and who the paper is for.

For what a private house test includes and which report stays with you in the end, see the page electrical installation testing in a private house.

A warehouse or small workshop: a report for each distribution board

People live in Čiekurkalns, and there are offices, warehouses, small manufacturing and services here. A warehouse with lifts, cold rooms or workshop equipment has three-phase circuits, larger protective devices and several distribution boards. A report for such premises is longer: separate results for each board and circuit, and separate details for the incoming supply.

In a warehouse the same is measured as elsewhere: insulation resistance, phase-neutral loop resistance and earthing. If the building has lightning protection, that too. Thermal camera inspection of heavily loaded boards can be ordered together with the measurements. If the premises have an explosive or chemically aggressive environment, say so in your request: the testing frequency under the fire safety regulations currently in force (Cabinet Regulation No. 238, in force until 1 January 2027) is then different.

For the process and preparation, see the page electrical installation testing for businesses.

More capacity for a warehouse or house: what Sadales tīkls sets

If new equipment is added to a warehouse or a heat pump to a house, a higher permitted capacity may be needed. The application is submitted to Sadales tīkls on e-st.lv. The timing and fee depend on whether the existing connection can take the new capacity or the network has to be rebuilt.

Before applying, make a list of equipment and its power figures. From it the electrician can work out which main protective device is needed, and you do not ask for more amps than necessary, because you also pay a monthly capacity maintenance fee for the permitted capacity.

The electrician’s part is to check whether the incoming supply and distribution board are suitable and to prepare the installation for the confirmation. For the steps, see the page increasing permitted capacity.

An old workers’ house without a design

Čiekurkalns is a historic workers’ and residential neighbourhood between the railway, Brīvības gatve and Mežaparks. If your house is old and neither a design nor a diagram has survived, or extensions were built without a building authority case, the electrical part can be described anew: an as-built diagram after a site visit, measurements and a report.

An old house may have several incoming supplies or meters from the times when several families lived in it. Write how many meters there are and whether all are still in use. Sadales tīkls allows meters to be combined if an electrician prepares the installation, and that changes what has to be drawn in the diagram.

A concealed works record is not recreated once the works are covered up; ask the building authority what it accepts instead. In Riga the municipality’s City Development Department puts buildings into use. More: electrics of an old or never-accepted house.

Questions: Electrical measurements in Čiekurkalns

What does the Sadales tīkls connection cost if the network does not need rebuilding?

If the network does not have to be rebuilt for a capacity increase, Sadales tīkls charges only the connection fee: €117.21 at 0.4 kV under the Sadales tīkls price list, checked 25 November 2026. The electrician’s work and documents are separate from this fee.

What is measured in a warehouse working with increased capacity?

Insulation resistance, phase-neutral loop resistance and earthing are measured for each distribution board and circuit, and lightning protection if there is any. For heavily loaded boards a thermal camera inspection during working hours is also useful, when equipment is switched on and current flows through the connections.

Can a diagram be prepared for an old wooden house without a design?

Yes. An as-built diagram, or electrical installation diagram, can be drawn after a site visit: the electrician traces the circuits from the distribution board to the rooms and extensions. The price depends on the floor area, the number of storeys and boards and how many cable routes have to be traced on site.
