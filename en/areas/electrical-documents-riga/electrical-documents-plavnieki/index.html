The fire safety regulations currently in force (MK 238, in force until 1 January 2027) provide for electrical installation testing and a report with set columns. A flat owner may find their own report useful for selling or letting, but orders it themselves, separately from the building.

What a flat owners’ community orders in a large Soviet-era estate building

The typical development of Pļavnieki is large Soviet-era housing estates, schools, shopping centres and newer projects. For a building in an estate, testing the shared installation covers the incoming distribution board, stairwell boards, basement and attic lines and common-area lighting. The more stairwells, the more boards and circuits, and that is what determines the scope of work.

In a newer project the situation differs: if the building was put into use recently, the manager may have measurement protocols from the handover, and they should be attached to the application. The decision on testing the building is made by the community or the manager, not by an individual flat owner. Write the number of stairwells, where the incoming distribution board is and whether the building has lightning protection. How this work goes across the whole building is described on the page testing the shared installation in an apartment building.

Periodic test report: which columns have to be filled in

Pļavnieki is a densely populated eastern neighbourhood of apartment buildings, and in a building with several stairwells the report has many rows. So it is worth the manager knowing what a complete report looks like before accepting it. Point 57 and Annex 6 of the fire safety regulations currently in force (MK 238, in force until 1 January 2027) set what has to be entered in it:

Column in the report What it shows
Insulation resistance Whether line insulation is undamaged; a separate value for each tested line
Phase-neutral loop impedance Whether the protective device will disconnect the circuit quickly enough in a fault
Earthing installation resistance Resistance of the building’s earth electrode, measured on site
Earthing conductor continuity bond Whether the protective conductor is continuous from the earth electrode to the distribution boards
Lightning protection system The result, if such a system has been built for the building
Measuring instruments Instrument details and calibration or verification certificate number

The intervals and who has to be tested are explained on the page periodic electrical installation testing.

Checked on 25 November 2026: Cabinet Regulation of 19 April 2016 No. 238 “Fire Safety Regulations”, point 57 and Annex 6 (likumi.lv/ta/id/281646). The regulations are in force until 1 January 2027; the text of the next regulations has not yet been published.

Flat or ground-floor premises: which report suits

Pļavnieki serves housing, retail, education, health and everyday services. For a document this means a simple question: how the premises are used. If someone lives in the flat, the measurements cover the circuits from the flat distribution board, and the report can be given to a buyer, tenant or manager. If ground-floor premises are used for a shop or service, they are assessed as company premises, and the scope is set after the site visit. In a sale or letting the report can be useful as information for the other party; if they ask for a particular document, ask them to name it in writing, so that you know what has to be tested.

A flat owner does not have to wait for the building test to order their own. In your request, write how many rooms the flat has, whether the wiring has been replaced and who the report is needed for. Read how flat electrical installation measurements work on a separate page.

Thermal camera for stairwell distribution boards

Together with the periodic test, the contact connections in the distribution boards are inspected with a thermal camera, and a separate report is drawn up on this. A loose connection heats up only when current flows through it, so the inspection is planned for a time when there is load in the flats.

Pļavnieki is reached by Deglava iela and Lubānas iela and by public transport, but access to the distribution boards themselves matters more for the test. In your request, state where the incoming board is (in the basement or a separate room), who has the keys and whether the stairwell boards are locked. How a thermal camera inspection of a distribution board goes is described on its page.

If the thermal camera shows an overheated connection, the report notes the board and spot. Fixing it is repair work done by an electrician, for example by ordering electrical installation repairs in Riga. After the repair that spot can be checked again, so that the report has a record of the finding being fixed.

Questions: Electrical documents in Pļavnieki

What does the periodic test report state about earthing?

Two things: the resistance of the earthing installation, measured at the building’s earth electrode, and the earthing conductor continuity bond to the distribution boards. The report also has the measuring instrument details and the calibration or verification certificate number. This is set by Annex 6 of MK 238; these regulations are in force until 1 January 2027.

Does the flat owners’ meeting need a price before the site visit?

For testing a building, a figure before the site visit would not be honest, because the scope depends on the number of distribution boards and circuits. The site visit costs €40. After it we quote the price, and the manager can put it to the meeting for a decision.

How do stairwell testing and flat testing differ?

The stairwell distribution boards, incoming supply and common-area lines are the shared part of the building, and the manager or community orders its testing. Flat testing covers only the circuits from the flat distribution board, and the owner orders it. Each part has its own report.
