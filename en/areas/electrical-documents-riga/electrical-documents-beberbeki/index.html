Putting a self-built house into use: what to write in your request

The typical buildings of Beberbeķi are detached houses, gardens, forest areas and a few commercial properties. If the owner built a second-group residential house themselves, the Building Construction Regulations (points 157.7.1 and 168.1) require electrical installation test documentation for acceptance. In Riga, for cases opened after 31 December 2019, the application for acceptance can be submitted in BIS, and the municipality’s City Development Department carries out the acceptance.

So that the measurements can be planned in one visit, write in your request:

  • whether the house already has a case in BIS and at what stage it is;
  • how many distribution boards there are and whether any outbuildings have electricity;
  • whether you installed the electrics yourself or someone else did;
  • whether there is a design diagram and whether what was built differs from it.

Beberbeķi has forest, the nearby airport and a sparser street network. If the house stands at the forest edge far from the street, the supply cable from the Sadales tīkls distribution cabinet to the house may be long, and it is part of your installation. It is measured together with the house circuits, and the diagram shows its length and protection.

For the full list of documents in this case, see the page electrical documents for a self-built house.

A commercial property near the airport: the electrical part of the set for putting into use

Beberbeķi serves housing, logistics, services connected with the nearby airport and land management. If you are building or rebuilding a warehouse, a service building or an office, the set for putting it into use is wider than for a house. The Riga municipality’s document list for putting into use also includes acceptance records for significant structures and concealed works, the construction log and a statement on the readiness of the utility connection.

The electrical part of this set is the measurements, the as-built diagrams and the concealed works records on the installation of electrical equipment, which are drawn up during the work, not after it. If a site manager runs the project, agree with them when the installation will be ready for measurements. For how commercial premises are put into use, see the page putting commercial premises into use.

If concealed works records on the electrical installation were not drawn up while the cables were still visible, they are not recreated after covering up; ask the building authority what it accepts instead. So for a commercial property, arrange the electrician’s visit before ceilings and walls are closed up.

Panels installed, no papers: when the inverter is not on the list

Detached houses at the forest edge may have solar panels that someone else installed while the papers for Sadales tīkls were left unfinished. The first thing to check is the inverter. Sadales tīkls accepts inverters from its list of recognised inverters that comply with the standard LVS EN 50549-1. Sadales tīkls decides on an unlisted inverter separately, and that can take up to 5 working days more.

A warehouse with an office: one diagram or two

In a commercial property near Kārļa Ulmaņa gatve, the warehouse part and the office may share an incoming supply but have separate distribution boards. The as-built diagram (an electrical installation diagram showing what was actually built) can be one for the whole property, with each board shown on its own sheet. If the office is let to another user, they benefit from their own diagram sheet with their circuits and protection.

The scope of the diagram depends on the number of distribution boards and circuits and on how many cable routes have to be traced on site. If there is a design, attach it, and then only the differences are checked. For how the diagram is prepared, see the page as-built diagram.

Questions: Electrical papers in Beberbeķi

What if the inverter is not on the Sadales tīkls list?

Sadales tīkls decides on this separately, and the assessment can take up to 5 working days more. The inverter’s laboratory certificate, specification and manual are submitted for it. If the inverter is not approved, it may have to be replaced with a listed one.

Does the office part of a warehouse need its own diagram?

If the office has its own distribution board, it is shown separately in the diagram, for example on its own sheet. A separate diagram is needed if the office is let to another user or has its own meter. Otherwise one shared diagram may do, if the party asking for the document accepts it.

How do you request measurements for putting a house into use in Beberbeķi?

In the form, write the address, whether the house already has a case in BIS, how many distribution boards there are, whether any outbuildings have electricity and who installed the electrics. Attach a photo of the board and the design diagram if there is one. After you send the form, we contact you within 5 minutes.
