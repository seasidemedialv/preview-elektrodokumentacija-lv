The first is the electrical installation test report with the insulation, phase-neutral loop, earthing and lightning protection results. The second is the contact connection test report, drawn up after the distribution boards have been inspected with a thermal camera. Both are provided for by the fire safety regulations currently in force (MK 238, in force until 1 January 2027), and both are done in one visit.

Shop, car service or office: a premises report for a business

Krasta rajons serves retail, offices, car services, logistics, catering and housing. For each of these types of premises the content of the measurements is the same, but the way the work is organised differs. A shop and a catering outlet have refrigerators and equipment that must not be switched off for long; a car service has three-phase equipment and work zones; an office has many circuits with computers.

A line is disconnected for the insulation measurement, so write in your request which equipment has to stay on and at what times there are no customers in the premises. If you rent the premises, attach the owner’s contact or permission to access the incoming distribution board, because it may be outside your premises. The measurement results are arranged in the report by distribution board and circuit. The procedure in company premises is explained on the page electrical installation testing for businesses. The report can also be useful to a landlord or insurer if they ask for proof of the condition of the installation.

New or altered retail and office premises

The typical development of Krasta rajons is shopping centres, offices, warehouses, apartment buildings and development land. If premises have been newly fitted out or altered with construction documents, the electrical papers are part of acceptance into use. In Riga this is handled by the City Development Department of the Riga State City Municipality, and in cases opened after 31 December 2019, acceptance can be requested in BIS.

The Riga municipality’s list of documents for acceptance also includes acceptance records for concealed works, the construction log and a statement on the readiness of the utility connections. For the electrics this means measurement reports, an as-built diagram and concealed works records drawn up before the wires are covered. A concealed works record is not recreated after the work has been covered up; ask the building authority what it accepts instead. More: putting office premises into use.

Thermal camera report: what it proves and what it does not

In shopping centres and shops with long opening times the distribution boards work under load all day. A thermal camera shows which connection heats up more than the others, but this is visible only when current flows through it. So the inspection is planned during working hours, while the insulation measurements, which require lines to be disconnected, are planned for when the premises are closed.

The thermal camera inspection is done together with the periodic test, and it does not replace insulation measurements. For how it works, read the page thermal camera inspection of a distribution board. If a connection heats up more than others, the report names the particular distribution board and spot; fixing it is the electrician’s job, and afterwards that spot is checked again under load.

More capacity for rented premises: who applies

Krasta rajons is reached by Krasta iela, Salu tilts bridge and Dienvidu tilts bridge, and this means that sites in an application need to be found by building and entrance, not just by street. If a tenant who needs different capacity moves into premises, the paperwork starts with the question of whose name the connection is in. An increase of the permitted capacity is a paid Sadales tīkls service, applied for at e-st.lv or through the electricity supplier. Sadales tīkls receives the application from the user of the connection; if the connection is in the building owner’s name, the tenant has to agree with the owner before applying.

The electrician’s job on your side is to check whether the incoming supply and distribution board suit the new capacity and whether the main protective device has to be changed. How soon more capacity will be available depends on whether the network has to be rebuilt, and Sadales tīkls can tell you that. What has to be prepared is described on the page electricity capacity increase.

Questions: Electrical measurements in Krasta rajons

How should a shop that stays open late prepare for a thermal camera inspection?

Plan the thermal camera inspection during working hours, when the equipment is running and the distribution boards are under load. Plan the insulation measurements, which require lines to be disconnected, for when the shop is closed. Provide access to all distribution boards and write which equipment must not be switched off.

Which reports does a tenant of rented retail premises keep?

It depends on the lease: whoever is responsible for the installation orders the test and keeps the reports. Under the fire safety regulations these are the electrical installation test report and the thermal camera report. If the tenant built the installation, they may also hold the as-built diagram.

Does the tenant or the owner order a capacity increase?

The user of the connection submits the application to Sadales tīkls. If the connection is in the building owner’s name, the tenant has to agree with the owner. If you are not sure whose name the connection is in, you can find out at e-st.lv or from the electricity supplier.
