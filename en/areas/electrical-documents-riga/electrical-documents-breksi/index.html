Someone else installed the wiring, but there are no papers

The typical buildings of Brekši are detached houses, gardens, small residential projects and sparsely built areas. If the electrical installation in your house was done by an electrician or company you are no longer in contact with. That is no obstacle with the building authority if the documents are prepared anew: the installation is measured, a report filled in and a diagram drawn from the actual state.

Until the measurements have been made, do not add new circuits or change devices in the board: the electrician needs to see the installation in the state in which you will hand it over. If the site visit finds places that do not comply, they are repaired first, then the circuits concerned are measured again, and only then is the report added to the building authority application.

Sub-point 168.1 of the Building Construction Regulations provides that for a house built under a building permit by the owner, electrical installation test documentation is submitted to the building authority. If the house was built under an explanatory note, sub-point 157.7.1 requires the same. A concealed works record is not recreated once the works are covered up; ask the building authority what it accepts instead. The process is described in more detail on the page electrical installation documents when someone else built it.

Checked 25 November 2026: Building Construction Regulations (Cabinet Regulation No. 529 of 2 September 2014), sub-points 157.7.1 and 168.1 (likumi.lv/ta/id/269164).

Earthing resistance for a house and an outbuilding

The resistance of an earthing system is measured on site for each earth electrode, because the value depends on the electrode itself and the soil. Brekši lies near a lake, forests and lower-lying land, but that does not change how the measurement is done: the result cannot be taken from a neighbour’s house or from the design; it is obtained only by measuring.

If there are several buildings on the plot, it is important to know whether each has its own earth electrode or whether they are earthed through the house board by a protective conductor. In the first case each electrode is measured, in the second also the continuity of the protective conductor to the outbuilding. In your request, write how many buildings are connected and where the cables run. The measurement itself is explained in earthing resistance measurements. If the earthing value does not comply, the report notes it, and improving the earth electrode is installation work, after which the measurement is repeated.

A new connection on an out-of-the-way plot

Brekši is reached by Biķernieku iela and local roads, and the choice of public transport is more limited. For a new connection, the Sadales tīkls fee for network construction depends on the distance to the low-voltage line, so the application on e-st.lv has to give the exact place where you want the connection. On your side, the cable from the Sadales tīkls distribution cabinet to the house and the indoor installation have to be built, which a qualified electrical specialist or electrical installation company does.

Once the installation is ready, you submit a confirmation on e-st.lv. The capacity maintenance fee starts after it, even if no electricity is used yet, so it is not worth submitting the confirmation prematurely. The procedure is described in more detail in Sadales tīkls new connection.

A sauna, garage or workshop in the garden

Next to a house on the garden plots of Brekši there may be a sauna, garage or workshop whose electricity comes from the house distribution board or from its own board. For the building authority a sauna or garage is a separate structure, and if it is put into use, the electrical documents relate to its installation. For locations with sauna heaters, LBN 261-15, sub-point 27.3, sets special requirements under the applicable standards.

Measurements for a sauna or garage are a smaller job than for a house, but they cannot be priced at the house price: the price depends on the number of distribution boards and circuits and on whether there is an old diagram. In your request, write whether the building is connected to the house board. For what putting such a building into use requires, read the page sauna, garage and outbuilding electrical documents.

Price and what to prepare for the site visit

So that one trip is enough, prepare:

  • the exact address or cadastral designation and directions for driving in;
  • photos of the incoming distribution board and, if there is one, of the sauna or garage board;
  • the design or an old diagram, if one has survived;
  • the building authority or Sadales tīkls letter naming the document needed.

Questions: Electrical papers in Brekši

What should you do if an electrician wired the house but left no papers?

Order new measurements and an as-built diagram. The installation is measured, the results are entered in a report and the diagram is drawn from the actual state. Concealed works records are not recreated after covering up; ask the building authority where you will submit the documents what it accepts instead.

Is earthing measured for an outbuilding too?

If the outbuilding has its own earth electrode, its resistance is measured. If the building is earthed through the house distribution board, the continuity of the protective conductor to it is tested. In both cases the result can be obtained only on site, so list all connected buildings in your request.

What information is needed for a site visit at an out-of-the-way address?

The exact address or cadastral designation, directions for driving in, photos of the distribution boards, an old diagram or design if there is one, and the letter in which the building authority or Sadales tīkls names the document needed. That way the electrician knows before the trip what has to be tested.
