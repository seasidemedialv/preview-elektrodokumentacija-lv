Flat in a series-built block or pre-war building

The typical development of Ķengarags is Soviet-era apartment estates, fragments of pre-war buildings, retail and public buildings. The measurements for a flat in a series-built block and a flat in a pre-war building are the same, but the starting point differs. In an estate block the circuits can be identified from the flat distribution board. In a pre-war building the installation may have been altered several times, and the electrician has to trace where each line goes.

If the wiring in your flat has not been replaced, the measurements show what condition it is in. They cover insulation resistance between conductors and to earth, the phase-neutral loop (the total impedance of the circuit, used to judge whether the circuit breaker will disconnect a fault) and the continuity of the protective conductor, if there is one. If there is a residual current device (RCD) in the board, its tripping is checked too.

In your request, write the building type if you know it, when the wiring was last replaced and who the report is needed for, and attach a photo of the distribution board. The process is described on the page electrical installation testing in a flat.

The building’s shared installation and report validity

In an estate block the stairwell distribution boards, incoming supply and common-area lines are the shared part, and its testing is ordered by the building manager. The fire safety regulations currently in force (MK 238, in force until 1 January 2027) provide for such testing once every 10 years. Point 525 of these regulations also matters:

  • a test report drawn up after 19 April 2016 is valid until the next test;
  • this applies to the report on insulation, the phase-neutral loop, the earthing installation, the earthing conductor bond and lightning protection;
  • if nothing has changed and the time for the next test has not come, a new report is not needed just because the manager has changed;
  • if the installation has been altered, the altered part needs new measurements.

These regulations cease to apply on 2 January 2027, and a new text of the regulations had not yet been published on 25 November 2026. To find out what testing the shared part covers, read the page electrical installation testing in an apartment building.

Checked on 25 November 2026: Cabinet Regulation of 19 April 2016 No. 238 “Fire Safety Regulations”, points 56 and 525 (likumi.lv/ta/id/281646). In force until 1 January 2027.

After renewal or alterations: which paper you keep

If the electrical installation in a flat or building has been renewed or altered, measurements of the new part are needed after the work, and a diagram showing how it was built is useful. Which work needs the building authority to be involved depends on whether it affects the shared part of the building; find this out from the building authority, in Riga the City Development Department, before the work starts.

Ķengarags is a large linear neighbourhood along the Daugava, reached by Maskavas iela, by tram and by rail. In a long neighbourhood with many similar buildings, write the block and stairwell in your request in addition to the address, and attach photos of the distribution board before and after the work, if there are any. If another electrician did the work, ask them for a list of the new circuits. More: electrical installation documents after alterations.

If there has been a fire in the stairwell or flat

In an apartment estate a fire in one flat or stairwell may also affect shared lines running through that spot. So after a fire it helps to separate two things: the flat circuits, which the owner decides on, and the stairwell distribution board and risers, which the building manager decides on.

In your request, write which rooms or floors are affected, whether the electricity is currently off and who switched it off, and attach a photo of the distribution board. If the insurer has already sent questions, attach those too, so that the installation report answers exactly them.

Before the affected circuits are switched on again, they have to be examined and measured. The document here is an installation report: it can be useful for an insurance case and to the building manager when deciding on restoring the shared part. We tell you which measurements it needs after the site visit. The damaged parts are restored by an electrician, for example by ordering electrical installation repairs in Riga, and then they are measured. To read how such a report is prepared, see electrical installation testing after a fire.

Questions about these documents

What should you do about documents after renewing the electrics in a flat?

Order measurements for the renewed circuits and a diagram showing the new layout. Keep them together with the material data sheets, if there are any. If the work affected the shared part of the building, ask the building authority whether other documents are needed and inform the building manager.

Are measurements needed after a fire in a stairwell?

Before the affected lines are switched on again, it is worth examining and measuring them, because the fire may have damaged the insulation. The stairwell distribution board is a shared part of the building, so the building manager orders the work. Each owner orders testing of their own flat circuits.

Does an old report need renewing if nothing has changed?

If the report was drawn up after 19 April 2016, the fire safety regulations currently in force (MK 238, in force until 1 January 2027) recognise it as valid until the next test. If the installation has been altered in the meantime, the altered part needs new measurements.
