Ten-year inspection in a pre-war wooden or brick house

The typical development of Sarkandaugava is pre-war wooden and brick houses, apartment buildings, hospital complexes and industrial sites. For a residential house the most important deadline here follows from the fire safety regulations. The fire safety regulations currently in force (MK 238, in force until 1 January 2027) set in point 56 that the electrical installation, including earthing and lightning protection devices, is tested once every 10 years.

Checked on 25 November 2026: Cabinet Regulation of 19 April 2016 No. 238 “Fire Safety Regulations”, points 56 and 57. The regulations cease to be in force on 2 January 2027; the text of their successors had not been published on this date, so the interval cannot be treated as permanent.

The results are recorded in a test report according to Annex 6 of the regulations. It contains:

  • insulation resistance in each circuit;
  • phase-neutral loop impedance, which shows whether the protection will disconnect a fault;
  • earthing installation resistance and the earthing conductor continuity bond;
  • testing of the lightning protection system, if the house has one.

If there is no previous report or it is unknown when measuring was done, start there. For how testing goes in a house with several distribution boards, see the page periodic electrical installation testing. In a wooden house, attach a photo of the stairwell distribution board to your request and write how many flats the house has.

In a wooden house the shared part also includes the attic and basement lighting lines, if they run from the stairwell board. Write in your request whether there are any and whether the basement is accessible. If the house has a previous report, attach a copy: the new report is best kept to the same circuit numbering so that results can be compared.

Production or warehouse premises: a report on your own distribution board

The neighbourhood’s functions are housing, healthcare, the port, manufacturing, warehouses and services. If you rent part of a larger building, your premises may have their own distribution board, while the incoming supply and the other circuits belong to the owner. Then first agree which part the report will cover: only your board and its circuits, or the whole building. These are two different scopes and two different prices.

Testing for a business is ordered by whoever is responsible for operating the premises; the lease agreement may determine this. For what the work contains, see the page electrical installation testing for a business. In the form, state the floor area, the number of distribution boards, whether the building has lightning protection and when equipment may be switched off briefly, because a circuit has to be dead for insulation measurements.

If compressors, furnaces or other large loads run in production premises, testing can be supplemented with a thermal camera. Point 58 of the fire safety regulations provides for inspecting contact connections with a thermal camera together with the periodic test, and a separate report is drawn up on this according to Annex 7. A thermal camera shows an overheated connection only when current flows through it, so this part is planned during working hours, when the equipment is on.

If the wiring in a wooden house has not been replaced: a report before renovation or purchase

In a pre-war house the flat wiring may come from different periods: some circuits relaid during renovation, some left from earlier years. If you are buying a flat or preparing for renovation, an electrical installation report shows which circuits can stay and which need rebuilding. It is an assessment of condition and differs from a periodic test report; both can be ordered together if you need a report and recommendations.

For what the report contains, see the page old electrical installation report. If the flat is in a wooden house, write whether the wiring is surface-mounted or inside the walls and whether the board has circuit breakers or screw-in fuses. This helps to judge how long the site visit will take.

A workshop after a long break: testing before reconnection

Port, production and warehouse sites lie close by in Sarkandaugava, and a workshop or warehouse may stand without electricity for a long time. Sadales tīkls advises: if the power supply has been interrupted for a long time or the internal installation has not been tested for ages, it should be checked by a certified electrician before applying. If the installation is not fit for use, Sadales tīkls does not restore the connection.

So order the testing first and then turn to your electricity supplier, because the supplier applies for reconnection. How reconnection works and what affects the Sadales tīkls fee is explained on the page reconnection after an interruption. In your request, write how long the property has been without power and whether anything in the distribution board has been changed during that time.

If the user of the premises has changed during this time, the new user chooses the supplier, and it is this supplier that submits the reconnection application to Sadales tīkls.

Questions about these documents

Does the fire safety inspection also apply to a wooden rental house?

Yes. The fire safety regulations currently in force (MK 238, in force until 1 January 2027) apply to properties regardless of the form of ownership, and a rental house is no exception. No separate interval is set for rental houses; it is the same, once every 10 years.

Who decides on testing the shared wiring in a building?

The shared distribution boards and rising mains are the common property of the building, so talk about the work and the fee with the building manager or administrator. An owner can also order testing of their own flat circuits separately.

What is needed if a workshop has stood without electricity for a long time?

First a test of the internal installation, as Sadales tīkls advises, and only then the reconnection application, which your chosen supplier submits. If the installation is not fit for use, Sadales tīkls does not connect it, so faults have to be fixed before applying.
