For a new warehouse or outbuilding, insulation resistance has to be measured before it is put into use, and the result goes into the handover documents. Existing buildings may need a test report and a diagram, and larger loads also papers for Sadales tīkls.

An outbuilding or shed with electricity

The typical development of Spilve is airfield infrastructure, warehouses, utility sites and rare fragments of housing. In an outbuilding or shed, electricity may serve only lighting and a few sockets, but even then it has its own distribution board or a line from another building. If the line runs underground or overhead from the main building, it is part of the installation and is measured with the rest.

What papers such buildings need depends on whether the building is being put into use, let or sold. Whoever asks for the document says what will be enough; for a small building it may be a measurement report and a one-page diagram showing where the supply comes from and what the circuits are. For how this work is prepared, see the page electrical documents for a sauna, garage or outbuilding.

If machinery or fuel is stored in the outbuilding, write that in your request. Then the site visit also assesses whether the board and sockets are placed in a suitable spot, and this is noted in the installation report. If the building is damp, for example unheated, insulation measurement results may differ between seasons, so the measurement date is written in the report.

New warehouse: insulation measurement before handover

The neighbourhood’s functions are logistics, warehouses, aviation heritage, events and land management. If you are building or altering a warehouse, point 54 of LBN 261-15 sets that insulation resistance of electrical installations is tested before acceptance into use, and the minimum values are in Annex 6 of the construction standard. This measurement is done before the installation is put to use, not a year later.

Stage What is measured Who needs it
Before putting into use Insulation resistance of all circuits For the handover documents for the building authority

For how testing of business premises goes, see the page electrical installation testing for a business. If a construction manager runs the building work, agree with them when the installation will be ready for measurements, so that these happen before finishing work and equipment connection.

Lightning protection in an open location

Spilve has low meadows, land drainage and the proximity of the Daugava, and buildings may stand in open locations. If a building has a lightning protection system, testing it belongs in the same periodic test report that records the electrical installation measurements. Earth electrode resistance depends on soil moisture, so the measurement date is written in the report.

In the report, the lightning protection measurement starts at the air terminal on the roof; see the page lightning protection testing. In your request, write how many down conductors are on the facades and whether there is a system diagram.

If there is no lightning protection, this is noted in the report, and the other measurements stay the same. Whether a building needs a system is determined by a designer, not by the test report. If there is a system but the location of the test joints is unknown, they are found first at the site visit.

More capacity for a warehouse: before applying to Sadales tīkls

At logistics sites the load can grow: cooling, electric forklifts with charging, new lines. Before an application for more capacity, the electrician checks whether the incoming supply, main distribution board and cables suit the new load and whether the main protective device has to be changed. If the network does not have to be rebuilt, the Sadales tīkls fee is only for connection; if it does, the timing and fee are different.

In the capacity application, state the existing main protective device, the power of the planned equipment and whether the load will be simultaneous. Forklift charging points may work at night, when other equipment is off, and that can mean that more capacity is not needed at all. This shows when the load schedule is compared with the incoming supply.

Access in Spilve is via Daugavgrīvas iela and the roads towards Kleisti, and it depends on the particular site. For the process, see the page increasing the permitted capacity.

Questions: Electrical documents in Spilve

Is insulation measured before switching on a new warehouse?

Yes. Point 54 of LBN 261-15 sets that insulation resistance of electrical installations is tested before acceptance into use. The measurement is done on dead circuits, and the results are compared with the minimum values in Annex 6 of the construction standard.

Does a shed with electricity need measurements?

If the shed has its own line or distribution board, it is measured with the rest of the installation, and the result is entered in the same report. A separate document for the shed can be prepared if someone asks for it, for example a tenant or buyer.

What to send about a site where the access road is hard to find?

The location on a map or coordinates, a description of the access road and whether the site has a gate that someone opens. Also attach a photo of the distribution board. We agree the time of the site visit once it is known how to get there.
