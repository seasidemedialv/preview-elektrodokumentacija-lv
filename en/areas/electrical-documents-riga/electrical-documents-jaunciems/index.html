Workshop or warehouse: a report you can show a tenant

Jaunciems serves housing, production, warehousing and local services. A workshop has different loads from a house: welding machines, compressors, woodworking machines, and some of them may be three-phase. The test report records the insulation resistance and the phase-neutral loop result for each circuit, and on three-phase lines it has to be measured on every phase.

In a workshop the measurements also show what is not seen day to day: whether extension leads and portable equipment are connected to circuits with the right protection, and whether earthing reaches every metal casing. If the installation has been added to piece by piece over time, the site visit first traces which line comes from which distribution board, and only then are measurements taken.

If you let out the workshop, the report shows the tenant the condition in which they receive the installation. If you are the tenant, ask the owner whether there already is a report and when it was drawn up. For what the work includes, see electrical installation testing for businesses. In your request, write the power of the largest machines and at what times they may be switched off.

Detached house and apartment building: what differs in testing

The typical development here is detached houses, apartment buildings, and industrial and warehouse sites. In a detached house the test covers everything: the incoming supply, the main distribution board, all circuits and, if there are any, the lines to outbuildings. In an apartment building a flat owner orders measurements only for their own circuits, while testing of the shared part is organised by the building manager.

For a detached house a measurement protocol may be needed for putting into use, after alterations or for yourself, to know the condition. For what a private house test includes, see private house electrical installation testing. If the workshop is next to the house and fed from the same distribution board, mention it: then both can be measured in one visit.

In an apartment building in Jaunciems the flat’s distribution board may be in the stairwell. In that case write in your request whether you have access to it or the manager holds the key, because the measurements cannot start without it.

Battery added to a solar system: a protocol for each inverter

A detached house or workshop may get a battery added to its solar system. Sadales tīkls treats a battery as generating equipment, so it needs permission in the same way as panels. Each inverter needs its own settings protocol, and the total inverter capacity must not exceed the microgenerator limit: 11.1 kW on a three-phase connection and 3.7 kW on a single-phase connection. A hybrid inverter is allowed.

Two meters on one property: relocating or merging

If the house and the workshop each have their own meter, you may want to merge them or move them to a more convenient place. To merge meters, an electrician prepares the installation and Sadales tīkls removes the surplus meter. For a move inside the building the application is submitted at e-st.lv, a certified electrician removes the seal with permission from Sadales tīkls, and Sadales tīkls restores it within 3 working days.

If the meter has to be moved outside the building, for example to the plot boundary, you need an application in the technical portal and a design assignment from Sadales tīkls. For the steps of both routes, see meter relocation. With your request, photograph both meters and the main protective devices.

When meters are merged, the permitted capacity may change. If the workshop needs as much capacity as before, make sure that the combined main protective device allows it, otherwise you will have to apply for a capacity increase after the merger.

Questions about these documents

Does a hybrid inverter with a battery need its own settings protocol?

Yes. Sadales tīkls requires a settings protocol for each inverter, including a hybrid inverter with a battery connected to it. If the system has two inverters, two protocols are needed, and the total capacity must not exceed the microgenerator limit.

Can measurements in a workshop be combined with measurements in the house?

Yes, if the workshop is fed from the house distribution board: then its circuits are measured together with the house circuits and entered in one report. If the workshop has its own meter and distribution board, the report describes it as a separate part, but the site visit can be made on the same occasion.

Who removes the meter seal?

When a meter is moved inside the building, the seal is removed by a certified electrician after Sadales tīkls has given permission. After the work Sadales tīkls restores the seal within 3 working days; the sealing fee follows the Sadales tīkls price list.
