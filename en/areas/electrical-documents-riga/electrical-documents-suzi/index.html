For putting a self-built house into use you need electrical installation test documentation: measurements and, if the building authority asks, also a diagram. It is easier to prepare while the walls are not yet closed. Panels have their own deadline: after Sadales tīkls approval the microgenerator has to be installed within 9 months. More permitted capacity is a third, separate question.

A self-built house: measure before finishing, not after

The typical development of Suži is detached houses, gardens, low-rise buildings and large natural areas. If you are building a house yourself, Building Construction Regulations (points 157.7.1 and 168.1) require electrical installation test documentation for acceptance into use. The most important advice here is about timing: call in the electrician while the cables in walls and ceilings are still visible.

Once the wires are covered, some information can no longer be checked by eye. A concealed works record is not recreated after the work has been covered up; ask the building authority what it will accept instead. So if you are still at the construction stage, photograph the cable routes before the cladding goes on, with a tape measure or marks on the wall. They are useful both for the diagram and for later work.

For the full list of documents in this case, see the page electrical documents for a self-built house. If the house is already finished and there are no photos, that is all right: measurements can be done now too, but the routes in the diagram are then traced from what is visible in the board and junction boxes.

An as-built diagram that also shows the sauna and shed

On detached house plots in Suži there may be a garden, sauna, garage or shed next to the house. The as-built diagram (an electrical diagram showing what was actually built) covers everything fed from your distribution board. If a cable runs from the house to the sauna or shed, the diagram also shows that line, its protection and the second board, if there is one. This matters, because years later no one will remember where the cable runs underground.

Useful for the diagram: design sheets, if there are any, a photo of the board with circuit labels and a rough plan of the plot with the buildings. For how the diagram is prepared and what it contains, read the page as-built diagram. If the line of some building is unknown, it is traced on site, and that affects the scope of work.

Panels on the house: 9 months after Sadales tīkls approval

Suži’s functions are housing, recreation and local services, and the roof of a detached house can become a place for solar panels. A microgenerator has its own deadline with Sadales tīkls that is easy to miss. After approval the microgenerator has to be installed within 9 months; this deadline can be extended once by up to 6 months.

  1. Application on e-st.lv Microgenerator application with inverter data; Sadales tīkls replies within 3 working days if the inverter is on the list of recognised inverters.
  2. Paying the invoice Assessment fee €78.66 under the Sadales tīkls price list, checked on 25 November 2026.
  3. Installation within 9 months Installation and inverter adjustment are done by a construction specialist with a certificate in the generation field; one extension of up to 6 months.
  4. Confirmation with protocol A confirmation is submitted on e-st.lv, attaching the inverter settings protocol signed by a specialist whose field covers generating equipment.

Checked on 25 November 2026: Sadales tīkls, “Connecting a microgenerator”; installation within 9 months after approval, one extension of up to 6 months.

If after payment the installer offers a different inverter, you do not have to start from scratch. Sadales tīkls accepts a confirmation with the data of the originally declared inverter and the new inverter’s protocol attached, if the new one is on the recognised list and its power is not greater than declared. Otherwise a new application is needed.

We check what has been installed and prepare the papers for Sadales tīkls; we do not install panels. For everything about this work, see the page connecting a microgenerator.

When a house needs more permitted capacity

Access to Suži is provided by Jaunciema gatve and local roads, and if a house is lived in all year round, a heat pump, electric heating or an electric car charger may appear. If the total simultaneous load exceeds the main protective device, the protection trips or appliances have to be switched on in turn. This is a signal to check whether more permitted capacity is needed.

Before an application to Sadales tīkls, the electrician assesses whether the board and incoming cable suit the new capacity and whether a move to three phases is needed. The fee to Sadales tīkls follows its price list and depends on whether the network has to be rebuilt. For the process, see the page increasing the permitted capacity. If you also plan panels at the same time, mention that in your request, because both questions concern the same incoming supply.

Questions: Electrical paperwork in Suži

How soon after the permit does a microgenerator have to be installed?

Sadales tīkls sets 9 months after the connection approval, and this deadline can be extended once by up to 6 months. After installation a confirmation with the inverter settings protocol is submitted on e-st.lv, and Sadales tīkls issues the permit for parallel operation within 3 working days of receiving the documents.

Do outbuildings also have to be shown in the diagram?

Yes, if they are fed from your distribution board. The as-built diagram shows everything that has actually been built, including the line to a sauna, garage or shed and its protection. If an outbuilding has its own board, the diagram shows that too.

When does a house need more permitted capacity?

When the planned appliances together with the existing ones need more at the same time than the main protective device allows, for example when adding a heat pump or charger. At the site visit the electrician compares the load with the incoming supply and board, and then it is clear whether an application to Sadales tīkls is needed.
