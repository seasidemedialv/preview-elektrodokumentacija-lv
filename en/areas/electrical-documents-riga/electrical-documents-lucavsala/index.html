There are few permanent buildings on the island, so the first question here may be a Sadales tīkls connection rather than the building authority. Below we describe what to prepare in each case.

Allotment or garden house: electricity and papers

The typical development of Lucavsala is recreation infrastructure, allotments, sports and event facilities and limited permanent building. For a garden house the electrical documents are the same as for a small building: measurements, a report and a simple diagram. Whether and how the building can be connected or put into use depends on who owns the land, what the rights of use are and how it was built, so find that out first from the landowner or land manager and the building authority.

If the cabin is connected to a shared allotment distribution board, your part starts after your protective device: the cable to the cabin, its distribution board and circuits. For sockets intended for portable equipment used outdoors, LBN 261-15 requires a residual current device (RCD) with a tripping current of no more than 30 mA, and an electrician checks it. More: putting a garden house into use. In your request, write where the cabin’s electricity comes from, how many circuits it has and who the landowner or manager is with whom the work must be agreed.

Temporary connection for an event or construction site

Lucavsala serves recreation, sport, events, seasonal services and area development. For these needs Sadales tīkls has two types of temporary connection:

  • for a construction site: a temporary connection for up to 24 months, which needs a valid building permit;
  • for a short event: a connection for up to 31 days, applied for at least 10 working days in advance;
  • in both cases your side needs a temporary distribution board and an electrician’s work to connect it;
  • installing a construction site connection costs €117.21, or €217.52 with additional installation work, under the Sadales tīkls price list, checked on 25 November 2026.

For construction sites and for exhibition, show and stand areas, LBN 261-15 sets special requirements according to standards, so the temporary distribution board and cables must be suitable for outdoor use. In your request, write the time of the event or construction work, the capacity needed and the location on the island. The procedure is described on the page temporary electricity connection for a construction site. For an event, list the stage, catering and lighting equipment with their power, so that the capacity requested matches the need.

Checked on 25 November 2026: Sadales tīkls “Temporary connection” (sadalestikls.lv/lv/pieslegums-uz-laiku) and price list (sadalestikls.lv/lv/cenradis).

After water: when the installation may be switched on

Lucavsala has Daugava floodplains and seasonal use of the land. If there has been water in your cabin, distribution board or cable entry, the affected circuits have to be measured before they are switched on. The main measurement is insulation resistance: it shows whether moisture has created a leakage path. Then the tripping of the residual current device is checked.

Do not switch on a distribution board that has had water in it before it is tested, and photograph it. If devices or cables are damaged, an electrician replaces them, for example by ordering electrical installation repairs in Riga, and then the circuits are measured again. An installation report can be useful to you and to the insurer; we tell you which measurements it needs after the site visit. To read how this work goes, see electrical installation testing after flooding. If the water was only outside, also check the cable entry into the cabin and the outdoor distribution board, because moisture can stay there longer than indoors.

Permanent connection for a new structure on the island

Lucavsala is an island in the Daugava with parks, event areas, allotments and development potential. If a new permanent structure is being created on the island, the connection is applied for to Sadales tīkls at e-st.lv. If the network does not have to be rebuilt, Sadales tīkls offers the final costs and a contract within 3 days; if it does, it issues technical requirements, and the network construction needs a design approved by the building authority.

On your side, a qualified electrical specialist or an electrical installation company builds the cable to the Sadales tīkls distribution board and the internal installation, and then you submit the confirmation at e-st.lv. In your request, write what stage the project is at and whether there are Sadales tīkls technical requirements. More: new connection with Sadales tīkls.

Questions: Electrical documents in Lucavsala

Can a temporary connection be set up for a short event?

Yes. Sadales tīkls has a connection for short events of up to 31 days, applied for at least 10 working days in advance. A construction site has a different type: up to 24 months with a valid building permit. In both cases your side needs a temporary distribution board and an electrician’s work.

What does a garden house need before electricity is connected?

First find out from the landowner or manager and the building authority whether the cabin may be connected. Then you need a cable from the distribution board, a board in the cabin with protection and a residual current device, and measurements before switching on that show the installation is in order.

Why is it worth testing the installation after floodwater?

Water can damage the insulation in the distribution board, junction boxes and cables, and this cannot be seen by eye. An insulation resistance measurement shows whether a leakage path has formed. So the affected circuits are measured before they are switched on, and damaged parts are replaced first.
