Car repair shop or workshop: a report for three-phase equipment

Alongside housing, Atgāzene has small businesses, car repair shops and services typical of the city’s outskirts. A car repair shop has lifts, compressors, welding and tyre equipment, some of it three-phase, working with high starting currents. A report for such premises describes each distribution board and circuit, protective devices and measurements, including whether equipment casings are connected to earth.

Compressors and welding equipment cause a current surge at start-up. If protective devices are chosen too small, they trip for no reason; if too large, they may fail to switch off during a fault. The phase-neutral loop measurement shows whether the protection will switch off a fault quickly enough.

If the workshop has a paint booth or other rooms the company considers explosive environments, say so in your request. The fire safety regulations currently in force (Cabinet Regulation No. 238, in force until 1 January 2027) provide for testing once every two years in an explosive environment.

Attach to your request the number of bays, a list of equipment with power figures, photos of the distribution boards and the previous report if there is one. For how business premises are tested, see the page electrical installation testing for businesses.

A detached house in Atgāzene: testing the whole plot

The typical buildings of Atgāzene are detached houses, low-rise residential buildings, commercial areas along the railway and a few apartment buildings. In a detached house the test covers the incoming supply, the main distribution board, all circuits and the earthing. If there are other buildings on the plot with a cable from the house board, they are also part of the installation.

If the house has an old single-phase incoming supply but a garage with a workshop has appeared on the plot, the test shows whether the supply can carry it all. Then you know whether more capacity has to be requested before the other papers.

If the house has been rebuilt and has to be put into use, the measurements are part of the building authority documents. If not, a report shows the condition of the installation to the owner, a buyer or a bank. For what a detached house test includes, see the page electrical installation testing in a private house.

Moving a meter inside the building: the Sadales tīkls procedure

In a house or workshop, the meter may end up in the wrong place after rebuilding: behind a new wall or in another room. If it is moved along the same wall, to another wall or to another room, Sadales tīkls has a simpler procedure than for moving it outside the building:

  1. Application on e-st.lv Once you know the exact start time of the work, you submit an application on e-st.lv and immediately receive permission to remove the seal. An electrician may also submit the application for the client’s property.
  2. Work deadline The time limit set for the work is two days from submitting the application.
  3. Work The seal is removed and the work is done by a certified electrician chosen by the client.
  4. Restoring the seal Sadales tīkls restores the seal no later than three working days after the work; the fee is calculated from the price list and included in the monthly bill.

Checked 25 November 2026: Sadales tīkls “Meter operations” (sadalestikls.lv). Removing seals without permission is prohibited.

For the electrician’s work and what has to be prepared, see the page meter relocation.

If you want to move the meter to an outside wall or elsewhere on the plot, a different procedure applies: an application on the Sadales tīkls technical portal and a design brief from Sadales tīkls.

A garage, sauna or shed with its own supply cable

Detached house plots in Atgāzene may have a garage, a sauna or an outbuilding with a separate cable from the house distribution board. Such a building has its own small distribution cabinet and its own circuits, and a sauna also has special conditions, as the construction standard lists locations with sauna heaters among locations with special requirements.

Before the site visit, write how long the cable from the house to the garage or sauna is and whether it runs underground or overhead. That determines how it is measured and what has to be looked at on site.

If the garage or sauna was built with a building authority case, putting it into use may also require its electrical papers. If not, measurements show that the cable and circuits are in order. For these buildings the price is set after the site visit, not the detached house price. More: sauna, garage and outbuilding.

Questions: Electrical papers in Atgāzene

How quickly does Sadales tīkls allow the seal to be removed for meter relocation?

When a meter is moved within the premises, permission to remove the seal is received immediately after the application on e-st.lv, and the time limit set for the work is two days from the application. Sadales tīkls restores the seal no later than three working days after the work.

Does a garage with its own electricity supply cable need measurements?

If the garage was built with a building authority case and is being put into use, measurements may be part of the papers for putting it into use; ask the building authority what exactly it requires. If there is no case, measurements show that the cable from the house and the garage circuits are safe, and the report is useful to the owner.

What should you attach to a request about car repair shop premises?

The number of bays, a list of equipment with power figures, photos of the distribution boards, the previous report if there is one, and whether any room has an explosive environment. Also write when the power can be switched off briefly so as not to stop work.
