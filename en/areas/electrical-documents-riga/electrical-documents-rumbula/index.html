In all three cases the answer starts with Sadales tīkls, where your side needs an electrician’s work and a confirmation. For the owner of a detached house the papers are simpler: measurements and a report for the house. Each case is covered separately below.

More capacity for a warehouse or workshop

Rumbula is a south-eastern outlying neighbourhood with industrial, logistics and low-rise residential functions. For a warehouse, workshop or car service the capacity need can change when new equipment, cooling or electric car charging appears. Increasing the permitted capacity is a paid Sadales tīkls service, applied for on e-st.lv.

If the network does not have to be rebuilt, you pay only the connection fee of €117.21 under the Sadales tīkls price list, checked on 25 November 2026; find out the implementation date for the warehouse from Sadales tīkls. If the network has to be rebuilt, construction costs are added. On your side the electrician has to check whether the incoming supply, main protective device and distribution board suit the new capacity.

In your request, write the existing and required capacity in amperes, which equipment you will connect and where the meter is. More detail: increasing the electricity capacity.

New connection: price per ampere by distance

Rumbula’s functions are logistics, warehouses, trade, manufacturing, car services and housing, and on the large, sparsely built areas a connection for a new site may have to be built from scratch. For a connection up to 100 A and up to 300 m from the low-voltage line, Sadales tīkls applies a fixed price per ampere that depends on the distance:

Distance to the Sadales tīkls line Price per ampere
up to 50 m €101.36 per A
51–150 m €132.68 per A
151–300 m €184.15 per A

Prices follow the Sadales tīkls price list, checked on 25 November 2026; check the current price list before applying. This is a Sadales tīkls fee, separate from the electrician’s work. On your side the cable from the Sadales tīkls distribution cabinet to the site and the internal installation have to be built, and then a confirmation submitted on e-st.lv. The procedure in more detail: new Sadales tīkls connection. From the moment of the confirmation Sadales tīkls starts charging the capacity maintenance fee, so for a warehouse still under construction submit it only when the installation is really ready for use.

Checked on 25 November 2026: Sadales tīkls price list (sadalestikls.lv/lv/cenradis) and “Permanent connection” (sadalestikls.lv/lv/pastavigs-pieslegums).

Detached house in Rumbula: measurements and report

The typical development of Rumbula is warehouses, trade, manufacturing, detached houses and large sparsely built areas. A detached house owner has a different paperwork route from a company: measurements are needed for the whole house installation from the incoming supply, plus a report with the results. If the house is being put into use and was built by the owner, these measurements are part of the electrical installation test documentation for the building authority.

The measurements include insulation resistance, the phase-neutral loop (the circuit impedance used to judge circuit breaker tripping), earthing installation resistance and testing of residual current devices (RCD). If the plot has a workshop or garage with its own distribution board, it is a separate part of the report. For how the work goes, read the page electrical installation testing in a private house.

A company solar power station on the roof or on the ground

For a company with a large roof or vacant land, a solar system may exceed the microgenerator limit. A microgenerator is up to 16 A per phase, that is, 11.1 kW on a three-phase or 3.7 kW on a single-phase connection. A larger system is a power station with a separate Sadales tīkls procedure. BIS requires a construction design if a new connection has to be set up or the load changed. The station is installed and the test documents signed by a specialist with a regulated-sphere certificate for generating equipment. If the system stays within the microgenerator limits, the process starts with an application on e-st.lv, and the Sadales tīkls assessment fee is €78.66.

Access to Rumbula is via Maskavas iela, the railway and the A6 direction, and the neighbourhood has open areas. If panels are planned on the ground, Riga municipality explained on 28 July 2022 that outside the historic centre and its protection zone they need no approval from the City Development Department, but the spatial plan has to be followed. A company station is installed by an installer holding the certificate Sadales tīkls requires; we prepare the measurements and documents on the site side. More detail: connecting a business solar power station.

Questions: Electrical paperwork in Rumbula

What does an ampere of a new connection cost in the Sadales tīkls price list?

For a connection up to 100 A: €101.36 per A up to 50 m from the line, €132.68 per A from 51 to 150 m and €184.15 per A from 151 to 300 m, under the Sadales tīkls price list, checked on 25 November 2026. The electrician’s work on your side is separate.

When is a solar power station no longer a microgenerator?

When its capacity exceeds 16 A per phase, that is, 11.1 kW on a three-phase or 3.7 kW on a single-phase connection. Then it is a power station with a separate Sadales tīkls procedure and higher requirements for the specialist’s certificate; a construction design in BIS is needed only in the case of a new connection or a load change.

Does a production building need a different confirmation from a house?

The confirmation to Sadales tīkls that the installation is ready is the same type, and it is submitted on e-st.lv. What differs is the capacity, the number of distribution boards and whether the network construction needs a construction design. The other documents for acceptance of the building into use are set by the building authority.
