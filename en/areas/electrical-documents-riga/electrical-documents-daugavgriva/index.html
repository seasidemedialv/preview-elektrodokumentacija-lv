The ten-year report for an apartment building: what it covers and who orders

The typical buildings of Daugavgrīva are low-rise residential buildings, apartment buildings, port properties and military heritage sites. Point 56 of the fire safety regulations currently in force (Cabinet Regulation No. 238, in force until 1 January 2027) provides that the electrical installation, including earthing and lightning protection devices, is tested once every 10 years. In an apartment building this means several parts with different clients.

Part Who orders Where it is entered
Incoming supply, stairwell distribution boards, risers, common lighting The managing company on the building’s behalf In the building test report
Lightning protection, if there is any The managing company In the same building report
Flat distribution board and circuits The flat owner, or the building if it has so agreed In the flat report or the building report
Distribution board connections with a thermal camera Together with the building test In a separate thermal camera report

Checked 25 November 2026: Cabinet Regulation No. 238 of 19 April 2016, “Fire Safety Regulations”, point 56. The regulations cease to be in force on 2 January 2027; the text of their successor has not been published on this date.

If you are the building’s authorised representative, first ask the managing company for a copy of the last report and find out whether the test also covered lightning protection. For how a building test is organised, see the page apartment building electrical installation testing.

A low-rise building with a few flats may have no management company, and then the owners order the test together themselves. In that case agree who will be the contact person, who will open the cellar and attic and how the cost will be shared. The report must state which parts were measured (the Annex 6 field “Darba norises vieta”, place of work), so that every owner can see what is covered.

A flat after renovation: measurements for its own circuits

In Daugavgrīva apartment buildings stand next to low-rise houses, and flat renovations in them happen each at its own time. If circuits or the board in a flat have been replaced, a measurement report shows that the work was done correctly: insulation resistance, phase-neutral loop result and residual current device (RCD) tripping. This report is about the flat, not the whole building.

The report is useful if you sell or let the flat, and it shows the condition of the flat’s circuits. For how the work goes, see the page electrical installation testing in a flat. In your request, write whether the renovation is finished, whether the board is in the flat or the stairwell and who did the wiring work.

If the renovation is still going on, it is better to measure after all circuits are connected but before the walls are finally finished. Then it is also possible to trace where the new cables run and enter that in a diagram, if one is needed.

Lightning protection for buildings near the sea and dunes

Daugavgrīva lies near the sea, the Daugava, dunes and protected nature areas. If a building has a lightning protection system, in the report following Annex 6 of Cabinet Regulation No. 238 its resistance is measured from the air terminal to the test joint at the earthing system. The result is entered in the same ten-year report, so there is no point ordering it on a separate occasion.

More on this test: lightning protection testing. If it is unknown whether the building has a system at all, the site visit shows it by the down conductors on the façade. If there is no system, the report notes it.

A port or leisure property after standing idle

The neighbourhood serves housing, the port, fishing traditions, leisure and nature tourism. A boathouse, warehouse or seasonal property may have had its power off for a long time. Before reconnection Sadales tīkls recommends testing an indoor installation untested for a long time, and it does not connect an unsuitable one. So first come testing and the necessary repairs, then you choose an electricity supplier, and the supplier submits the reconnection application to Sadales tīkls.

The Sadales tīkls fee depends on how long the connection has been interrupted and whether the application is submitted within 30 days after the previous user’s contract ended. This is explained in more detail on the page restoring a connection. Daugavgrīva is reached by Daugavgrīvas šoseja through Bolderāja, so state in the form how to drive to the property.

In port or military heritage properties the buildings may have old distribution boards without diagrams. The site visit then first traces which lines still work and which are disconnected, and only after that measures, so that the condition of the property is already known when you approach the supplier.

Questions: Electrical measurements in Daugavgrīva

After how many years does a building need a new lightning protection test?

Under the fire safety regulations currently in force (Cabinet Regulation No. 238, in force until 1 January 2027), lightning protection is tested together with the electrical installation once every 10 years. Cabinet Regulation No. 238 ceases to be in force on 2 January 2027, and as of 25 November 2026 no text has been published saying what the interval will be later.

Is one joint report enough for a building by the sea?

The building’s common part and lightning protection can be covered by one report. Flat circuits are included in it only if the building has agreed to have them measured and the flats are accessible. Otherwise the flat owner orders a separate report for their own circuits.

What is needed to restore a connection after a long idle period?

A test of the indoor installation, as Sadales tīkls recommends, the necessary repairs and then a reconnection application submitted by the supplier. If the installation is not suitable, Sadales tīkls does not connect it. The contract with the electricity supplier is concluded separately.
