Putting a building into use in Riga: the City Development Department and BIS

Property conditions in Riga differ considerably between the centre, the Soviet-era housing estates, the detached-house neighbourhoods on the outskirts and the Daugava islands. However, a single body puts a building or part of it into use across the whole city: the City Development Department of the Riga State City Municipality. The recipient does not change; what differs is how your construction case has been handled.

If the case was opened after 31 December 2019, putting into use can be requested electronically by submitting an application in BIS. If the case was handled outside BIS, the documents are submitted to the department electronically or on paper. Before ordering the electrical papers, open your case and check which system it is in: that determines whether the protocol and diagrams are uploaded to BIS or added to a paper set.

For a house built under an explanatory note, point 160 of the Building Construction Regulations provides that the building authority marks the completion of construction works within 10 working days. This is the building authority’s deadline, not the electrician’s. The electrical papers therefore need to be ready before the application goes in. How the electrical part fits into the application is explained on the page electrical installation documents for the building authority.

Checked 25 November 2026: Building Construction Regulations (Cabinet Regulation No. 529 of 2 September 2014), point 160, likumi.lv; Riga municipality service “Putting a structure or part of it into use”, riga.lv.

A flat in a housing estate or a central building: a report on your circuits

Riga has a dense historic centre, large apartment block districts, detached-house neighbourhoods, office centres, and industrial and port areas. In an apartment building you first need to know where the flat’s installation ends and the building’s common part with the risers and stairwell distribution board begins; ask the building manager about this boundary.

A report for a flat therefore describes only the flat’s circuits. If the question concerns the whole building, the client is the building manager or the flat owners’ community. In your request, say whether the wiring has been replaced, how many circuits the distribution board has and whether the board is in the flat or in the stairwell. For how flat measurements are carried out, see the page electrical installation testing in a flat.

Offices, the port and institutions: who orders the test

Riga’s functions include state administration, offices, the port and logistics, education, health care, retail and hospitality. In such buildings the owner and the user may be different parties. In an office centre a tenant may use only the floor distribution board, while the incoming supply and main distribution board serve the whole building.

The lease agreement determines which part the tenant has tested and which part the owner does, so before ordering, also agree who receives the report. If the owner is the client, the scope may include the incoming supply, risers and common areas. In a warehouse or a port area building with several distribution boards the protocol is longer, because each board and circuit has its own entry.

In your request, state the floor area, the number of distribution boards, whether the building has lightning protection and whether a thermal camera inspection of the boards is needed. More about this work: electrical installation testing for businesses.

Solar panels on roofs of different ages: municipal approval and Sadales tīkls

Buildings in Riga date from very different construction periods, so roof load-bearing capacity can differ completely within one street. On 28 July 2022 Riga municipality stated that before panels are installed, the building owner has to have a technical survey of the building to establish the load-bearing capacity of the structures. This is the work of a civil engineer, not an electrician.

Whether approval is needed depends on the location. In the historic centre and its protection zone the layout has to be approved by the City Development Department; elsewhere in Riga this applies if the panels are on a facade or roof facing the street. If the building is a cultural monument, approval from the National Cultural Heritage Board is also needed. A municipal explanation of 23 March 2022 says a building authority permit is not needed if no construction works are required, such as strengthening load-bearing structures.

On the electrical side there is Sadales tīkls. It requires the panels to be installed and the inverter set up by a construction specialist with a generating equipment certificate. If that has been done, we prepare the inverter settings protocol and attachments, while the owner submits the confirmation of readiness of the microgenerator on e-st.lv. How this works is described on the page putting solar panels into use.

Questions about these documents

How is putting a building into use requested in Riga?

For cases opened after 31 December 2019, the application is submitted in BIS. If the case was handled outside BIS, the documents are submitted to the City Development Department electronically or on paper. The municipality states a processing time of 10 working days, which under the Administrative Procedure Law can be extended to 4 months.

Do a Riga flat and a house need the same documents?

No. After rewiring, a flat benefits from a measurement report on the flat’s circuits, used by the owner, the building manager or the next buyer. For a self-built house, the Building Construction Regulations require electrical installation test documentation for putting it into use, and it is part of the application to the department.

When do solar panels in Riga need approval?

According to the municipal explanation of 28 July 2022, approval is needed in the historic centre and its protection zone, elsewhere in the city for roofs and facades facing the street, and for a cultural monument also from the National Cultural Heritage Board. A courtyard roof and ground-mounted panels outside the centre need no department approval, but the spatial plan requirements have to be observed.
