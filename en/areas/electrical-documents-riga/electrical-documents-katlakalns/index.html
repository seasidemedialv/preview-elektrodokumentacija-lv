If the house is new and self-built, you need papers for the building authority to put it into use. If the house is already lived in and solar panels have been installed on the roof, you need papers for Sadales tīkls, and there the settlement option also matters. In both cases an earthing measurement will be useful.

A new detached house you are building yourself

The typical development of Katlakalns is detached houses, low-rise housing, gardens and some commercial properties. If you are building the house yourself as the owner on your own land, the Building Construction Regulations require electrical installation test documentation when it is put into use. It consists of measurements, a report and an electrical installation diagram, and under LBN 261-15 the measurements are taken by a certified electrical specialist. Arrange the measurements when the installation is finished but the distribution board is still open, and state the BIS case number in your request if there already is one.

Katlakalns is a Riga neighbourhood, so the building is accepted by the City Development Department of the Riga State City Municipality, and in cases opened after 31 December 2019, acceptance can be requested in BIS. If a plot near the boundary already belongs to Ķekava Municipality, the documents are submitted to its building authority; check this in the cadastre data. More on this case: electrical installation test documentation for a self-built house, and for the neighbouring municipality electrical documents in Ķekava.

Solar panels installed, but the Sadales tīkls papers are not in order

Katlakalns is a southern Riga neighbourhood by the Daugava and the Ķekava boundary, and the Sadales tīkls requirements are the same on both sides of the boundary. If the panels are already on the roof but the installer has not submitted the confirmation or the inverter settings protocol, the system must not operate permanently without permission for parallel operation. If someone else installed the panels, first find out whether they held a certificate for generating equipment, because Sadales tīkls requires it for installation, inverter setup and the protocol.

Before the documents, find out the settlement option, because it affects what to do next:

  • the old net metering system has not accepted new participants since 1 May 2024;
  • if you are already in net metering, changing the user, the settlement option or the connection may end your participation, so check with Sadales tīkls before making changes;
  • new producers are left with the net billing system or selling electricity through a supplier;
  • until there is a contract with a supplier, the electricity produced is accepted into the grid without payment.

In your request, write the inverter model, the total panel capacity and whether the application at e-st.lv has already been submitted, and attach a photo of the inverter data plate, because it is one of the enclosures to the confirmation. What to do step by step is described on the page solar panels installed, Sadales tīkls papers not in order.

Checked on 25 November 2026: Sadales tīkls “Settlement options for electricity producers” (sadalestikls.lv/lv/norekinu-veidi-elektribas-razotajiem).

Earthing measurement for a detached house by the Daugava

Katlakalns is characterised by the closeness of the Daugava and lower-lying land. This does not change how earthing is measured: the earthing resistance is obtained only on site, by measuring the particular earth electrode, because the value depends on the electrode itself and the soil. A value from a design or from the neighbour’s house will not do for the report.

An earthing measurement is needed both for the documents for putting into use and for a solar system, because the solar system equipment may be connected to the house earthing. In your request, write whether you know where the earth electrode is and whether a sauna or garage is earthed separately. What the measurement involves is explained on the page earthing resistance measurements.

Meter relocation in the house or to the boundary

If the meter is moved inside the building, the application is submitted at e-st.lv. You receive permission to remove the seal immediately after applying, two days from submission are allowed for the work, a certified electrician removes the seal and moves the meter, and Sadales tīkls restores the seal within 3 working days. The sealing fee follows the Sadales tīkls price list. An electrician can also submit the application for your property.

If you want to move the meter outside the house, for example to the plot boundary, the application is in the technical portal, Sadales tīkls issues a design assignment and a building design may be needed. In your request, write where the meter is now and where you want it. More: meter relocation with Sadales tīkls.

What house papers, panel papers and meter relocation cost

Measurements and documentation for putting a private house into use cost from €230. Meter relocation, the electrician’s work, costs from €70; the Sadales tīkls sealing fee is separate. For solar panel papers the price is set after the site visit: it is affected by the number and type of inverters and whether the installer’s mistakes have to be corrected. The Sadales tīkls assessment fee of €78.66 is separate. The site visit costs €40. Prices cover the electrician’s work and the documents. Sadales tīkls and building authority fees are separate.

Questions about these documents

What does the Sadales tīkls microgenerator assessment cost?

€78.66 under the Sadales tīkls price list, checked on 25 November 2026. The fee is not refunded if the application is withdrawn. If the inverter is on the accepted list, Sadales tīkls answers the application within 3 working days. The electrician’s work and the protocol are separate, and we quote their price after the site visit.

What should you do if the solar panel installer has not submitted the papers?

Check at e-st.lv whether the application has been submitted and paid at all. If it has, what is missing is the confirmation with the inverter settings protocol, signed by a specialist with a certificate for generating equipment. Installation and inverter setup must also be done by a holder of such a certificate. After the site visit we tell you what we can do.

Which building authority gets the documents if the address is near the city boundary?

The municipality in whose territory the plot lies. In the Riga neighbourhood of Katlakalns buildings are accepted by the Riga City Development Department, but if the land is already in Ķekava Municipality, by the Ķekava Municipality building authority. The territory can be checked in the cadastre data.
