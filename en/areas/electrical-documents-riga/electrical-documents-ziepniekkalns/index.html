The types of measurement are the same, but the scope, who orders them and who keeps the report differ. A flat and a house have a public price, for a business it is set after the site visit. If a house or workshop needs more capacity or three phases, papers also come from the Sadales tīkls side.

A flat in a Ziepniekkalns estate

The typical development of Ziepniekkalns is apartment estates, detached houses, warehouses, manufacturing and retail sites. For an estate flat the measurements cover the circuits from the flat distribution board, and the flat owner orders them. The report serves as information for the other party in a sale or letting, to check new circuits after renovation, or simply to know the condition.

The measurements include insulation resistance for each circuit, the phase-neutral loop (the impedance from the board to the socket, used to judge whether the circuit breaker will trip in a fault), protective conductor continuity and operation of the residual current device (RCD). The stairwell and incoming supply are the shared part of the building, and the building manager orders their testing. In your request, write the number of rooms and attach a photo of the flat board. How it works: electrical installation testing in a flat. If the flat has new circuits after renovation, the report can mark them separately from the old ones.

Detached house: more measurements than a flat

A detached house owner owns the whole installation from the Sadales tīkls boundary, so house measurements include more than flat circuits: the incoming distribution board, earthing installation resistance, all boards and outbuildings, if connected. This explains why a house and a flat have different prices. If the plot has a sauna or garage with its own board, write that, because it is a separate part of the report.

If the house is being put into use and was built by the owner, the measurements are part of the electrical installation test documentation required by Building Construction Regulations; in Riga the house is accepted by the City Development Department. If the house has long been lived in, a report shows the condition before major renovation or sale. In your request, write the house area, number of boards and purpose. More detail: electrical installation testing in a private house.

Production and warehouse premises on the edge of the neighbourhood

Ziepniekkalns’ functions are housing, retail, manufacturing, logistics and everyday services, and the industrial edge lies next to residential blocks. A report for production premises needs measurements for all boards and lines, earthing and, if the building has it, lightning protection. Together with the periodic test, the fire safety regulations currently in force (MK 238, in force until 1 January 2027) also provide for inspecting distribution boards with a thermal camera.

In a plant, lines have to be disconnected for insulation measurements, so write in your request which equipment can be switched off and when. If there is a diagram or previous report, attach it: then it is clear how many boards there are and how to split the work into parts. The procedure for business premises is described on the page electrical installation testing for a business. If an explosive or chemically aggressive environment is set for part of the premises, their test interval is shorter than 10 years.

More capacity or three phases: what is applied for from Sadales tīkls

Access to Ziepniekkalns is via Vienības gatve, Ziepniekkalna iela and the Dienvidu tilts connections, and the property may be an estate flat, a house or a workshop. Capacity and phase changes with Sadales tīkls work as follows:

  1. Checking the incoming supply The electrician checks whether the incoming supply and board suit the new capacity and whether the main protective device has to be changed.
  2. Application An increase of permitted capacity is applied for on e-st.lv or with the electricity supplier; it is a paid service. A flat owner attaches the building manager’s written consent.
  3. Phase change Moving from three phases to one with a larger main fuse is also a paid increase, applied for with a written application in any form on e-st.lv. A move from one to three phases is assessed by Sadales tīkls after an application.
  4. Implementation If the network has to be rebuilt, construction costs are added; in both cases Sadales tīkls confirms the implementation time.

For a house that needs much more capacity, the phase question has to be assessed at the very start. Point 8.3 of LBN 261-15 sets that in a residential building an incoming supply with a protective device larger than 32 A is connected only in three-phase form. In your request, write the existing main fuse, number of phases and the appliances that create the need for capacity.

More about this case: 1 phase to 3 phases with Sadales tīkls.

Questions: Electrical measurements in Ziepniekkalns

Where is an increase of permitted capacity applied for?

On the Sadales tīkls customer portal e-st.lv or with the electricity supplier. It is a paid service. A flat owner must attach the building manager’s written consent to the application. Before applying it is worth checking whether the incoming supply and board suit the new capacity.

Does the measurement price differ for a flat and a private house?

Yes. For a flat, measurements and a report cost from €170, for a private house, measurements and documentation for putting into use from €230. More is measured in a house: the incoming supply, earthing installation, all boards and connected buildings. The site visit costs €40.

What is needed for a production premises report?

Measurements for all boards and lines: insulation, phase-neutral loop, earthing installation and earthing conductor bond, as well as lightning protection, if there is any, and a thermal camera inspection of the boards. In your request, write the number of boards and which equipment can be switched off.
