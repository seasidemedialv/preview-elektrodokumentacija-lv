For a house the report describes the condition of an old installation; for a warehouse or workshop it is the basis for a decision on using or rebuilding the premises. A buyer may find it useful before a deal, and the signatory’s certificate can be checked with the issuer or in the BIS register.

An old installation in a detached house or low-rise building

The typical buildings of Bišumuiža are detached houses, low-rise buildings, industrial and warehouse properties and a few apartment buildings. If the installation in your house was fitted long ago and it is unknown what has been changed and when, a report gives a written assessment: what complies, what needs repairing and what must be replaced before a new load.

The report is prepared after a site visit, and then we also say which measurements, for example insulation or earthing, the particular building needs. The assessment covers the condition of the distribution board, whether protective devices suit the wires and the places where wires are damaged. In your request, write the year the house was built, if you know it, and what has been changed. More detail: old electrical installation site visit.

A warehouse, workshop or converted building

Bišumuiža serves housing, manufacturing, warehousing, logistics and technical services. The fire safety regulations currently in force (Cabinet Regulation No. 238, in force until 1 January 2027) also provide for an electrical installation test report for business premises. However, if the building’s use has changed, for example a warehouse converted into a workshop, offices or housing, a measurement report may not be enough. The report then assesses whether the existing installation suits the new use: capacity, circuit layout, residual current protection and earthing.

Whether a change of use requires construction documents is decided by the building authority, in Riga the City Development Department. Here the report serves as the technical basis for that conversation. In your request, write how the premises were used before and how they will be used now. For how business premises are tested, see the page electrical installation testing for businesses.

Before buying a house or building: what a report gives and what it does not

Bišumuiža is a neighbourhood on the left bank of the Daugava with residential, industrial and historic areas, so both an old detached house and a former industrial building may be for sale. The buyer orders a report by choice: it can be useful for knowing how much work there will be after the purchase and for talks with the seller.

If the power in the building is off, that limits the test, and the report says so. For what exactly to test before a purchase, read the page electrical installation testing before buying a house.

Who may sign and how to check it

Under LBN 261-15, point 55, measurements for putting into use are carried out by a certified electrical specialist who has been certified by an accredited body. Ask which body issued the certificate: a BLEEA or LEB certificate is checked with the issuer, while construction practice certificates are shown in the BIS register of construction specialists, which Sadales tīkls also points to. In both cases look at the field and validity.

The documents are prepared and signed by a certified electrical specialist. If you will submit the report to a third party, for example the buyer’s bank, ask whether it needs a particular certificate field.

An as-built diagram for a building rebuilt several times

Bišumuiža is reached by Bauskas iela and over the Dienvidu Bridge, and the neighbourhood has both houses and production properties whose installation may have been extended in several stages. If the diagram does not match the actual state or there is none at all, the as-built diagram (an electrical installation diagram of the actual state) shows the incoming supply, distribution boards and circuits. The report can then use it to state which board and circuit each finding is in, and for putting into use it can be one of the documents. In your request, write how many distribution boards the building has and whether there is any old plan. More detail: as-built diagram for electrics.

Questions about these documents

Is an electrical report compulsory before buying a house?

A buyer orders a report by choice. It can be useful for knowing the condition of the installation and the rough amount of work after the purchase, and a bank or insurer may ask for it. If you order it before signing the contract, the results can be used in price talks.

How do you check who may sign measurements?

A construction practice certificate can be found in the BIS register of construction specialists by name or number, while those issued by BLEEA and LEB are checked against the body’s own data. Under LBN 261-15, point 55, measurements for putting into use are carried out by a certified electrical specialist who has been certified by an accredited body. Look at the certificate field and expiry date.

What is looked at if a building has been converted from a warehouse?

Whether the existing capacity and circuit layout suit the new use, whether there is residual current protection and what the earthing is like. The report assesses this, and the necessary measurements are named after the site visit. Ask the building authority whether the change of use needs construction documents.
