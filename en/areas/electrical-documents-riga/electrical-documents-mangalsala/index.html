The installation report describes the condition of the installation and what has to be put in order before it is used or reconnected. Sadales tīkls advises testing an installation long left untested before reconnection, and it does not connect an unfit one.

Summer house unoccupied in winter

The typical development of Mangaļsala is detached houses, summer house and low-rise development, and port and military heritage sites. If a summer house stands unheated through the winter, in spring it is worth checking what cannot be seen: insulation resistance in the circuits, the connections in the distribution board and the continuity of the protective conductor. This is a check for yourself, not a building authority requirement.

If you alter the summer house or put it into use as a dwelling house, the route is different, and the building authority determines which papers are needed. The electrical part then consists of measurements and a diagram. For how this works for a garden house, see garden house electrical documents.

If lines run from the summer house distribution board to a sauna, shed or garden lighting, they are included in the measurements too, because outdoor cables and junction boxes spend the winter in cold and damp. Write in your request how many such lines there are and whether they run underground or overhead.

Testing before reconnection

Some houses on the coast are visited seasonally. If your house has had no electricity contract for a while and the connection has to be restored, Sadales tīkls gives this advice. If the power supply has been interrupted for a long time or the internal installation has not been tested recently, choose a certified electrician and test it before applying. If the installation is not fit for use, Sadales tīkls does not connect it.

Checked on 25 November 2026: Sadales tīkls, “Reconnection”; a recommendation to test an internal installation long left untested before applying.

So the order is this: site visit and measurements, necessary repairs, then choosing a supplier, who submits the reconnection application to Sadales tīkls. You apply at e-st.lv yourself if you reduce the load or the number of phases or if there is no meter on the property. The Sadales tīkls fee depends on how long the connection has been interrupted; this is explained on the page reconnection. In your request, write roughly how long the house has been without voltage.

Sadales tīkls charges no reconnection fee if the application is submitted within 30 days after the previous user ended the contract with the supplier. Later you pay for connecting and for capacity maintenance for the time since the contract ended, for no more than 24 months. If you have only just bought the house, this deadline is worth knowing.

Lightning protection for a house near dunes and forests

Mangaļsala is surrounded by the sea, dunes, sand and coastal forests. If your house has a lightning protection system, its earth electrode and down conductor connections are outdoors, and the inspection checks whether they are intact and accessible for measurement. The result is entered in the same test report as the electrical installation measurements, so it is worth ordering both at once.

The report in Annex 6 of Regulation No. 238 provides for a measurement from the air terminal to the connection at the earth electrode; more detail: lightning protection testing. If there is a diagram of the system or an earlier report, attach it to your request.

If there is no lightning protection, the report notes this and the other measurements remain the same. Whether your house needs a system is a question for the designer, not for the test report.

Installation report before buying a seaside house

Mangaļsala is a coastal neighbourhood between the mouth of the Daugava, the sea and nature areas. When you buy a summer house or home here, the seller may have no papers at all about the wiring, especially if the house has been altered several times. An installation report before the transaction shows whether the installation can be used, what needs to be changed and whether the connection suits your needs.

The inspection needs the seller’s permission and access to the distribution board and all rooms. For what an installation report for a buyer contains, see electrical check before buying a house. Such a paper can be useful for a bank, but the bank itself decides whether it is needed.

If the house is a summer house with a single-phase connection but you plan to live in it all year round, the installation report can also record whether the existing capacity will be enough for electric heating or a heat pump. This helps you understand whether after buying you will need to go to Sadales tīkls for more capacity.

What papers for a summer house and home cost

For papers for Sadales tīkls before reconnection the price is also set after the site visit, because it matters whether the incoming supply and distribution board are suitable and whether measurements are needed. For a guest house or a port site building the measurement price depends on the floor area and the number of distribution boards. The site visit is paid: €40.

Questions about these documents

What should be checked in a summer house that is unoccupied in winter?

Insulation resistance in the circuits, the connections in the distribution board and the continuity of the protective conductor to the sockets. If the house has outdoor lines to a sauna or shed, those as well. The results are entered in a measurement report or installation report showing what has to be put in order.

Can you order an electrical check before buying a summer house?

Yes, if the seller allows access to the distribution board and the rooms. The installation report shows the buyer the condition of the installation and what needs to be changed. It is not a mandatory transaction document, but it can be useful in price negotiations and when planning repairs.

What happens if the installation is not fit for connection?

Sadales tīkls does not connect an unfit installation. An electrician first has to repair the damage, then the test can be repeated and the reconnection application arranged with the supplier. So order the test before applying, not after a refusal.
