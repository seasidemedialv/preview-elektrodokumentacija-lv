A detached house next to warehouses: what is measured and what goes into the protocol

The typical development of Šampēteris is detached houses, low-rise buildings, offices, warehouses and commercial areas. So one street can bring two quite different orders. A detached house has one incoming distribution board, a warehouse has several boards and more capacity. In your request, write which of these your property is, so that the electrician knows what scope to expect.

In a private house test the following is measured and entered in the protocol:

  • insulation resistance for each circuit, with all appliances disconnected;
  • phase-neutral loop impedance, which shows whether the protective device will operate in a short circuit;
  • earthing resistance and continuity of the earthing conductor to the sockets;
  • the operating current and time of residual current devices (RCD);
  • an as-built diagram, if the distribution board has no up-to-date diagram.

When each circuit is measured separately, the power is switched off for a short while. If the house has a heat pump, an aquarium or a server, say so in advance so that the shutdown can be planned. Before the site visit, a photo of the inside of the distribution board door is useful, as is the old diagram or design, if there is one.

If you built the house yourself, these measurements together with the diagram are part of the papers for the building authority. If the house has long been in use, the protocol shows which circuits comply and which need putting right. How it works: electrical installation testing in a private house.

Office, shop or hotel premises: concealed works records before the ceilings are closed

People in Šampēteris live, work in offices, trade, work in logistics and hospitality. If premises are altered with a building authority case, the electrical papers become part of acceptance. Riga municipality’s list of documents for acceptance into use includes acceptance records for significant structures and concealed works, the construction log and an opinion on the readiness of the engineering network connection.

A concealed works record confirms that cables in walls, in the floor or above suspended ceilings were laid correctly before they were covered. A concealed works record is not recreated after the work has been covered up; ask the building authority what it will accept instead. So call in the electrician while the cable routes are still visible, and photograph them with a tape measure alongside.

In a warehouse or logistics building with several distribution boards, each board has its own part in the protocol. If you rent the premises, agree with the owner whether only the circuits of your premises are tested or also the main board and incoming supply. This determines in whose name the report is issued and who keeps it.

In your request, state the floor area, whether there is a construction design and how many boards the premises will have. For how premises are handed over, read the page putting office premises into use.

A charger at the house or office parking space: will the existing connection be enough

The airport and main roads, Kārļa Ulmaņa gatve and Lielirbes iela, are close to Šampēteris. So detached houses and offices with their own parking face the same question: is the existing connection enough for an electric car charger, or does a capacity increase have to be applied for.

Sadales tīkls advice of 7 September 2026 says that a typical private house connection is three phases at 20–25 A, which is 13.8–17.3 kW. A smart charger with dynamic load management often makes it possible not to increase capacity, because it reduces charging when other large appliances are switched on in the house. This is Sadales tīkls advice, and it is not a legal document.

Checked on 25 November 2026: Sadales tīkls advice on charging an electric car at a private house (7 September 2026), sadalestikls.lv.

Before installation we check the existing distribution board, the incoming supply protection, the cable route to the charger and the protective devices. Questions: Electrical documents in Šampēteris

What is a typical private house connection capacity according to Sadales tīkls?

In its advice on charging an electric car at a private house, Sadales tīkls mentions a three-phase connection with a 20–25 A incoming supply, which corresponds to 13.8–17.3 kW. Your house connection is determined by the size of the incoming protective device and the number of phases, and these are inspected at the site visit.

Are concealed works records needed when office premises are put into use?

Riga municipality’s list for accepting a building or part of it includes concealed works acceptance records. They are drawn up during the work, while the cables are still visible. If the premises are already finished and there are no records, ask the City Development Department what it will accept instead.

What is checked before installing a charger in the yard of a house?

The size of the incoming protective device and the number of phases are checked. So are the spare space and condition of the distribution board, the cable route to the charger spot and whether the line will have its own protective device and residual current device. This shows whether the existing capacity is enough.
