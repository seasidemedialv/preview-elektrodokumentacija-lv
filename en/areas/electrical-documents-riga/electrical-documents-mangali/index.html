For a low-rise house such a paper is useful for putting into use, before the confirmation to Sadales tīkls or for an insurer. Which document you need depends on who asks for it, and the scope of work depends on that too.

Low-rise house: what a private house measurement protocol records

The typical development of Mangaļi is low-rise housing, apartment buildings and nearby industrial sites. For a low-rise house the protocol covers the whole building: the incoming supply, the main distribution board and all circuits, including outdoor and outbuilding lines if they run from the same board. The list of measurements includes:

  • insulation resistance of each circuit, measured with the power off;
  • phase-neutral loop impedance, which shows whether the circuit breaker will disconnect a fault in time;
  • earthing resistance and continuity of the protective conductor to the sockets;
  • tripping test of the residual current devices (RCD), if installed.

If the house has several floors or an extension with its own distribution board, write this in your request. For everything about this work, see private house electrical installation testing.

The protocol is the same whoever you will show it to, but the content may differ. For putting into use it is added to the other building authority documents. If you order it for yourself, for example after repairs, it shows the condition and serves as a starting point for later tests.

Confirmation at e-st.lv: when Sadales tīkls installs the meter

In Mangaļi the port, production and local services are next to housing, so a new connection may be needed for a house or for a small workshop. In both cases Sadales tīkls builds its part up to the distribution board, while the customer’s chosen electrician prepares the internal wiring. When it is ready, the customer submits a confirmation at e-st.lv that the installation has been built and is ready for voltage.

The boundary between the Sadales tīkls installation and yours is where the incoming cable is connected to the Sadales tīkls distribution board; the meter is at this boundary. Everything beyond it is your responsibility, including the cable to the house distribution board. So before the confirmation, make sure that this cable has also been installed and tested.

After the confirmation Sadales tīkls gets in touch within 2 working days to install the meter. The monthly capacity maintenance fee also starts to be charged after submission, even if the voltage has not yet been switched on, so submit it only when the internal work really is finished. The confirmation is not a measurement protocol: they are two different papers. More: confirmation to Sadales tīkls.

Installation report for an insurer: when it can be useful

Mangaļi is close to water, the port and the railway. If your house has had water damage, for example a flooded basement or water running through the roof onto the distribution board, the insurer may ask for an electrician’s assessment of the condition of the installation. Such a paper can be useful, but the insurer decides what exactly is needed, so first ask them which paper they want and about which part of the building.

The installation report describes what was examined and what damage was found, while the measurements needed are determined during the site visit. If there is damage, an electrician fixes it before the installation is used again. For how the installation report is prepared and what an insurer may look for in it, see electrical installation report for insurance.

After water damage, do not switch on circuits that have been wet until they have been tested. In your request, write which rooms had water and whether the distribution board was damaged too; then the site visit turns to these circuits first.

Meter relocation: inside the house or onto the facade

In a low-rise house the meter may be in an inconvenient place, for example in a hallway that is being renovated. Sadales tīkls distinguishes two cases. If the meter is moved inside the building, the application is submitted at e-st.lv, a certified electrician removes the seal and does the work with permission from Sadales tīkls, and Sadales tīkls restores the seal. If the meter has to be moved outside the house, onto an outer wall or elsewhere on the plot, the route is different: an application in the technical portal and a design assignment.

For a move inside, permission to remove the seals is received immediately after applying, 2 days from submission are allowed for the work, and afterwards the seal is restored within 3 working days; the sealing fee follows the Sadales tīkls price list. An electrician can also submit the application on your behalf.

Before applying, photograph the existing meter and the place where you want it. For the steps of both routes, see meter relocation.

Questions about these documents

When does Sadales tīkls install the meter after the confirmation?

Sadales tīkls states that after the confirmation is submitted at e-st.lv it gets in touch within 2 working days about installing the meter. The date is agreed with you. The capacity maintenance fee starts as soon as the confirmation has been submitted.

Does a low-rise building need a separate installation report for an insurer?

Only if the insurer asks for it; that is decided by the insurer, not the building authority. If you already have a recent measurement protocol, ask whether it is enough; if an assessment is needed after water damage, that is an installation report.

Is a meter on a house facade moved by an electrician or by Sadales tīkls?

Moving it onto an outer wall counts as moving it outside the house: Sadales tīkls issues a design assignment, and a building design may be needed. Inside the building a certified electrician does the work with permission from Sadales tīkls to remove the seal, and Sadales tīkls restores the seal.
