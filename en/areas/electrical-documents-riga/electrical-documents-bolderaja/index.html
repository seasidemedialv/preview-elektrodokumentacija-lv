Each of these cases has its own document and its own scope. One thing is common: all are based on measurements, and the most important of them is insulation resistance.

A standard-series flat: what insulation resistance above 1.0 MΩ means

The typical buildings of Bolderāja are apartment block estates, historic low-rise buildings, detached houses, and industrial and port properties. If the wiring in your standard-series flat has not been replaced since the building was built, the first question is the insulation: whether it still separates phase from neutral and earth. An insulation resistance measurement shows this, made on each circuit with the voltage off.

The minimum values are set by Table 2 of Annex 6 to LBN 261-15. In a network up to 500 V, as in a flat, insulation is tested with a 500 V test voltage, and the resistance must be greater than 1.0 MΩ. If a circuit does not reach this value, the report records it and recommends what to do: find the damaged spot or rebuild the circuit.

Checked 25 November 2026: Cabinet Regulation No. 294 of 9 June 2015, “LBN 261-15 Internal Electrical Installations of Buildings”, point 54 and Annex 6, Table 2.

The report assesses more than figures. In standard-series blocks the flat’s distribution board may have been replaced while the old wires remain in the walls, and then the result can be good on one circuit and low on another. Whether the report needs a measurement on every circuit, we tell you after the site visit.

For a flat, measurements and a report are the first step; the electrical installation report is a separate written assessment with recommendations. For how a flat test is carried out, see the page electrical installation testing in a flat. In your request, write whether the board has circuit breakers or old fuses and whether the flat has an electric cooker.

Production premises: what makes up the price of a report

The neighbourhood serves housing, the port, manufacturing, warehousing, retail and local services. For production premises the scope of the report is set not by floor area alone but by the number of distribution boards and circuits, the number of three-phase lines and whether there is lightning protection. Two premises of the same size can involve very different amounts of work if one has three boards with dozens of circuits and the other only one.

The price is also affected by whether equipment may be switched off during measurements and whether there is an existing diagram. Without a diagram the circuits have to be traced first, and that takes time. For the content of the work, see the page electrical installation testing for businesses. So that we can give the price straight after the site visit, state the number of boards in your request and attach photos of them.

A detached house or old low-rise house: measurements for the whole building

In Bolderāja, detached houses and historic low-rise buildings stand next to the apartment block estates. In a private house the test covers the whole building: the incoming distribution board, all circuits, the earthing and, if there are any, the lines to outbuildings. If the house has been rebuilt in several rounds, the site visit first establishes which circuits are new and which old, and insulation and loop are measured on each.

These measurements serve for putting into use or after rebuilding, while the scope of measurements for an electrical installation report is set at the site visit. For what a private house test includes, see the page private house electrical installation testing. If the house has old earthing whose location is unknown, write that, because it has to be found first.

If the house has several generations of wiring and some circuits run through timber structures, the report marks these places in particular, so that they can be put first in the renovation plan.

A report for a bank: how to prepare it

When lending for a house or renovation, a bank may ask for a paper on the condition of the electrical installation. It is not a requirement of the building authority or Sadales tīkls, so the bank itself decides which document will do. Bolderāja is further from the centre and is reached by Daugavgrīvas šoseja, so it is worth gathering everything needed in one visit instead of making several trips.

  1. Ask the bank Which document is needed, for which building and whether a measurement report will do or an electrical installation report is needed.
  2. Request in the form The address, type of building, a photo of the distribution board and the text of the bank’s requirement.
  3. Site visit and measurements All circuits, earthing, residual current devices.
  4. Report Condition, measurement results and recommendations; you submit it to the bank.

If the loan is for renovation in which the electrical installation will be changed, ask the bank whether it needs a paper on the current condition or on the condition after the work. These are two different documents, and the second needs measurements after the renovation.

For what such a report contains, see the page electrical installation report for a bank.

Questions about these documents

What does insulation resistance above 1 MΩ mean?

It is the minimum value that Table 2 of Annex 6 to LBN 261-15 sets for a network up to 500 V, measured at 500 V. If a circuit’s resistance is higher, the insulation meets the standard. If it is lower, the insulation is damaged or damp somewhere, and the circuit has to be examined more closely or rebuilt.

Can an electrical report on a private house be useful to a bank?

It can be useful if the bank asks for a paper on the condition of the electrical installation. The bank decides which document will do, so before ordering ask whether a measurement report is enough or a report with recommendations is needed.

How is the price worked out for production premises?

The main factors are the number of distribution boards and circuits, three-phase lines, lightning protection and whether there is a diagram. Floor area alone does not set the price. After the site visit we say which documents are needed and give the price.
