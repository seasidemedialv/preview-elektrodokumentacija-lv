A garden house or seasonal building: what the building authority may ask for

The typical buildings of Dārziņi are detached houses, gardens, seasonal buildings and low-rise residential buildings. For a garden house the content of the electrical documents is similar to that for a house: measurements, report and electrical installation diagram. Whether the building authority asks for them depends on the building group and how it was built, so find this out from the City Development Department in Riga before ordering documents. If the garden house is connected to the main house distribution board, its circuits can be included in the same report as the house circuits, with a separate note.

If you plan to convert the seasonal building into a residential house or use it all year, the scope of papers may change, because for a self-built second-group residential house the Building Construction Regulations expressly require electrical installation test documentation. In your request, write the floor area, whether the building has its own distribution board and whether its power comes from the main house. More detail: putting a garden house into use.

Restoring a connection for a garden property after a break

Dārziņi serves housing, gardening and local services, and garden properties may be used only in season. If your property has been unused for a long time and the previous user’s contract with the electricity supplier has ended, restoring the connection with Sadales tīkls works as follows:

  1. 30-day period If the reconnection application is submitted within 30 days after the previous user’s contract ended, Sadales tīkls charges no connection fee.
  2. Later: two fees After 30 days you pay a connection fee of €34.76 (0.4 kV) and a capacity maintenance fee for the time since the contract ended, for no more than 24 months.
  3. Installation test Sadales tīkls recommends that before applying, an installation untested for a long time is checked by a certified electrician; an unsuitable installation is not connected.
  4. Invoice and connection Sadales tīkls issues the invoice within 3 working days and restores the connection within 2 working days after payment. The application is submitted to Sadales tīkls by the electricity supplier you choose.

The fees follow the Sadales tīkls price list, checked 25 November 2026. Reconnection is possible if the network is technically fit and the capacity or number of phases is not increased. More on this procedure: restoring an electricity connection.

Checked 25 November 2026: Sadales tīkls “Reconnection” (sadalestikls.lv/lv/piesleguma-atjaunosana) and price list (sadalestikls.lv/lv/cenradis).

If there has been water in the cellar or building

Dārziņi lies near the Daugava, the reservoir and lower-lying land. If your building has had water damage in the cellar or ground floor, circuits that were wet need testing before they are switched on again. The main measurement here is insulation resistance: it shows whether moisture or damage has created a leakage path between wires or to earth.

Do not switch on the affected circuits before the test, photograph the distribution board and the damaged spot and write in your request how high the water was. If wires or devices are damaged, an electrician replaces them, and the circuits are then measured again. If the insurer asks for a document, an electrical installation report may be useful; we tell you the scope of measurements needed after the site visit. How this work is done is described on the page electrical installation testing after flooding.

An old or unknown installation in a remote property

Dārziņi is a remote south-eastern neighbourhood by the Daugava and the reservoir of the Riga hydroelectric power plant, reached by Maskavas iela and the local street network. If you have bought or inherited the property and do not know when and how the installation was fitted, an old electrical installation site visit gives a report. This is a written assessment of the condition with notes on places to be repaired; the inspection shows which measurements it needs.

As the trip is longer, list in your request all buildings on the plot that have electricity and what documents are available: old drawings, meter data, Sadales tīkls letters. Then one site visit can assess both the report and what is needed to restore the connection. More detail: old electrical installation site visit.

Questions about these documents

What does restoring a connection cost if more than 30 days have passed?

If reconnection is requested later than 30 days after the previous user’s contract ended, you pay a connection fee of €34.76 (0.4 kV). You also pay a capacity maintenance fee for the time since the contract ended, for no more than 24 months, under the Sadales tīkls price list, checked 25 November 2026.

Does a summer house need testing before winter?

Testing before winter may be useful to you. If the house stands unheated in winter and the power stays on, it is worth knowing the condition of the installation, and measurements show it. The fire safety regulations currently in force (Cabinet Regulation No. 238, in force until 1 January 2027) provide for testing once every 10 years.

Which measurements are made after a flooded cellar?

Mainly insulation resistance on the affected circuits, because it shows leakage caused by moisture or damage. After repairs the phase-neutral loop is also measured and residual current device (RCD) tripping is tested. The results can be drawn up as a test report or an electrical installation report.
