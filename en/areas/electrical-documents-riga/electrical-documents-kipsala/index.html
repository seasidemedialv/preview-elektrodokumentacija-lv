In a new apartment project the question is not about old wiring, but about what has changed since the developer handed over the building. In a historic wooden house or a detached house papers are needed for renovation, more capacity, solar panels or an electric car charger.

New flat, changed layout: a report and an up-to-date diagram

The typical development of Ķīpsala is historic wooden houses, detached houses, new apartment projects, university and exhibition complexes. In a new project the developer’s handover documents describe the flat’s installation as it was at the time of handover. If you have since moved walls or added circuits for the kitchen or underfloor heating, these papers no longer match what is actually there.

After such changes two documents are useful: a measurement report on the new and changed circuits and an updated as-built diagram showing where each circuit goes. The as-built diagram, or electrical installation diagram, is a drawing from the flat distribution board to each circuit. If you ever sell the flat, both papers will show the buyer what has been changed and how.

If the developer handed over the flat with a diagram, attach it to your request. Then only the changes have to be entered in the updated diagram, which is quicker than drawing everything anew.

Before the work, check whether the building manager requires changes to be agreed. For what you receive after alterations, see flat electrical installation alteration documents.

Renovating a historic wooden house: concealed works records in good time

Ķīpsala is an island in the Daugava opposite the city centre with residential, educational and exhibition functions. In the renovation of a historic wooden house with a building authority case, the electrical installation may be put in anew, and most of it ends up behind cladding. The Riga municipality’s list of documents for acceptance into use also includes acceptance records for significant structures and concealed works.

So the concealed works record on the electrical installation has to be drawn up while the wires are still visible. A concealed works record is not recreated after the work has been covered up; ask the building authority what it accepts instead. Plan the measurements in two stages: before the walls are closed and after the work is finished.

In your request, write the status of the building authority case and the work schedule. For what else this package includes, see documents after alterations or renovation.

Solar panels on a house by the bank of the Daugava

Ķīpsala has the Daugava bank, low-lying areas and wind. On 28 July 2022 the Riga municipality stated that before panels are placed, the owner must have a technical survey of the building carried out to establish the load-bearing capacity of the structures. If a roof is exposed to wind from the river side, it is in this survey that it is assessed whether the structure and fixings will withstand the panels.

Once the panels are installed, Sadales tīkls permits parallel operation only if the inverter is on its list of accepted inverters and a settings protocol has been submitted, signed by a specialist whose certificate covers generating equipment.

Whether approval from the City Development Department is needed depends on the location: it is required for roofs and facades facing the street, but not on the courtyard side or on the ground outside the historic centre and its protection zone. If the building is in the protection zone or is a cultural monument, the rules are stricter. Sadales tīkls also requires installation and inverter setup to be done by a specialist with the same generation certificate; if that is met, we prepare the papers for Sadales tīkls. More: solar panel papers for Sadales tīkls.

EV charger at a detached house: the capacity calculation first

If you plan an electric car charger at a detached house on the island, the first question is whether the existing capacity will be enough. The Sadales tīkls advice (it is not a legal document) states that a typical private house connection is 3 phases with a 20–25 A main protective device and that a smart charger with dynamic load management in most cases makes a capacity increase unnecessary. For a home charger, correctly chosen cables and protection matter.

Before installation it helps to know:

  • the existing permitted capacity and number of phases (shown at e-st.lv);
  • the distance from the distribution board to the charger location;
  • whether there is room in the board for a new protective device;
  • whether the chosen charger has load management.

Checked on 25 November 2026: Sadales tīkls advice “Elektroauto uzlāde privātmājā” on charging an electric car at a private house (sadalestikls.lv).

If it turns out that the capacity does have to be increased, this is applied for to Sadales tīkls at e-st.lv. Before applying, it is worth checking whether the distribution board and incoming supply suit the new load, so that nothing has to be redone after permission.

The charger is fitted by an installer; we check the connection and prepare the papers. More: electric car charger connection check.

Questions: Electrical documents in Ķīpsala

Does an electric car charger mean the house capacity has to be increased in every case?

No. In its advice Sadales tīkls writes that for most private houses the existing capacity is enough for charging, and with a smart charger with dynamic load management an increase is in most cases not needed. Whether that suits your house is shown by the existing capacity and the load of the other equipment.

Which documents do you receive after altering the electrics of a flat in a new project?

A measurement report on the changed and new circuits and, if you order it, an updated as-built diagram. The developer’s handover documents remain as history, while the new papers describe the current condition. Keep them together with the flat documents.

When must placing solar panels in Riga be approved by the municipality?

According to the Riga municipality’s explanation (28 July 2022), approval is needed in the historic centre and its protection zone, and in the rest of Riga on facades and roofs facing the street. On the courtyard side and on the ground outside these zones no approval from the department is needed.
