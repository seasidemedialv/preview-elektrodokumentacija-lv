A flat needs a measurement report on its circuits, an institution or shop a report on its premises, and after damage an installation report on the condition is needed. All three share one rule: the periodic testing currently provided for by the fire safety regulations.

Measurement report for a flat in an estate building

In the apartment estates of Mežciems the flat owner is responsible for the circuits from the flat distribution board, while the stairwell and incoming supply are the shared part of the building. A flat report covers only the owner’s part, and it can be ordered at any time, regardless of the building test. If you are preparing to sell or let the flat, the report can be useful as information for the other party. It describes the condition on the day of testing and does not replace the documents needed after rewiring.

During the site visit the electrician counts the circuits, looks at the protective devices and says whether measurements can be taken straight away or something has to be repaired first. The measurements include insulation resistance, the phase-neutral loop (the total impedance of the circuit, used to judge whether the circuit breaker disconnects) and a test of the residual current device (RCD). In your request, write when the wiring was last replaced, if you know, and who you will give the report to. The work is described step by step on the page electrical installation testing in a flat.

Clinic, pharmacy or sports premises: business reports

Mežciems serves housing, healthcare, retail, sport and services. For an institution or company the electrical installation test report under the fire safety regulations is the same as for any other building: insulation, phase-neutral loop, earthing, the earthing conductor bond and lightning protection, if there is any. What differs is the scope and how the work fits into the institution’s day.

In a large complex with several distribution boards it is worth planning a thermal camera inspection of contact connections together with the measurements: the regulations provide for it at the same time as the periodic test, with a separate report. For the inspection to be meaningful, write in your request at which hours the load in the premises is highest.

If the premises have equipment that must not be switched off, list it already in your request, because a line is disconnected for the insulation measurement. If a healthcare institution has sector requirements of its own for power supply, they have to be checked with the institution’s responsible authority or the designer; we test and document what is on site. To read how work in company premises is organised, see electrical installation testing for businesses.

Ten-year test: which properties it applies to

Point 56 of the fire safety regulations currently in force (MK 238, in force until 1 January 2027) provides for testing the electrical installation, including earthing and lightning protection devices, once every 10 years. The regulations apply to sites regardless of their form of ownership, that is:

  • to the shared installation of apartment buildings, run by the building manager;
  • to flats and detached houses, whose owner is responsible for the installation;
  • to the premises of institutions, shops and sports facilities;
  • a shorter interval applies in an explosive environment (once every two years) and a chemically aggressive environment (once a year).

MK 238 ceases to apply on 2 January 2027, so ten years should not be treated as a permanent norm; a new text had not been published on 25 November 2026. The large complexes of Mežciems are reached from Biķernieku iela and Hipokrāta iela; if there are several buildings, state in your request the block and entrance where the incoming distribution board is. The intervals are explained in more detail on the page periodic electrical installation testing.

Checked on 25 November 2026: Cabinet Regulation of 19 April 2016 No. 238 “Fire Safety Regulations”, point 56 (likumi.lv/ta/id/281646). In force until 1 January 2027.

If an insurer asks for an electrical document after damage

Mežciems is a green eastern residential neighbourhood by Biķernieki forest with detached houses as well as flats. So the insurer’s question differs: for a flat its circuits are assessed, for a detached house the whole installation from the incoming supply. If after a water leak, power surge or other damage the insurer asks for an electrical document, ask them to name in writing exactly what has to be described.

An electrical installation report suits such a case: a written assessment of the condition with notes on the damaged places; the measurements needed are determined during the site visit. It can be useful in an insurance case if the insurer asks for it. To read how this installation report is prepared, see electrical installation testing for insurance.

Questions about these documents

Which properties does the ten-year test apply to?

The fire safety regulations currently in force (MK 238, in force until 1 January 2027) apply it to sites regardless of the form of ownership: flats, detached houses, the shared part of apartment buildings and company premises. In an explosive environment the interval is two years, in a chemically aggressive environment one year.

Do medical premises need a separate electrical report?

The test report under the fire safety regulations is the same for medical premises as for other buildings. If an institution has sector requirements of its own for power supply, they have to be checked with the institution’s supervising authority or the designer. We measure and document the existing installation and enter the results in the report.

What should you do if an insurer asks for a document after damage?

Ask the insurer to name in writing what the document has to describe, and attach that to your request together with photos of the damage. During the site visit the electrician assesses whether a measurement report is enough or an installation report is needed. An installation report costs from €180, the site visit €40.
