A new site may need two more things: an as-built diagram showing what was built, and preparation of the customer’s part for the Sadales tīkls connection.

Test report for a warehouse or plant

Voleri’s functions are the port, logistics, warehouses, manufacturing and limited housing. For a warehouse or plant the test report is the paper required by the fire safety regulations currently in force (MK 238, in force until 1 January 2027) and one that can be shown to the owner or tenant. A report is only as good as the accuracy with which it describes the circuits, so before measuring, the boards need understandable circuit labels.

Useful to us in your request:

  • floor area and number of distribution boards, with a photo of each;
  • whether there is a previous report and diagram;
  • the time when equipment may be switched off for measurements;
  • whether the site has a pass system and what has to be stated for a pass;
  • whether the building has lightning protection.

For how the work goes in business premises, see the page electrical installation testing for a business.

New connection: price per ampere by distance to the line

The typical development of Voleri is low-rise housing, port, warehouse and industrial sites, and for a new site the connection fee depends on how far away the Sadales tīkls low-voltage line is. For a connection up to 100 A and up to 300 m from the line, the Sadales tīkls price list has a price per ampere. It is €101.36 per A up to 50 m, €132.68 per A from 51 to 150 m and €184.15 per A from 151 to 300 m (under the Sadales tīkls price list, checked on 25 November 2026).

Checked on 25 November 2026: Sadales tīkls, price list and “Permanent connection”. Check the current price list on the Sadales tīkls website before applying.

This is the Sadales tīkls fee for the network part, not for the customer side. The customer’s part is the cable to the site distribution board and the internal installation. When it is ready, the customer submits a confirmation on e-st.lv. So even before applying it is worth deciding how many amperes you really need, because capacity directly affects the Sadales tīkls fee. For the whole route, see the page new Sadales tīkls connection.

If you plan electric forklifts, cooling or later solar panels on the roof of a warehouse, mention this already in the connection application. Capacity can be increased later, but that is a separate application with its own fee, and sometimes it pays to choose the right incoming supply straight away.

Temporary connection for a construction site in the port zone

Access to Voleri is via Daugavgrīvas šoseja and the port access roads, and in some zones it is restricted. If a new warehouse is being built on the site, the construction site needs a temporary connection. Sadales tīkls sets it up for up to 24 months, and it requires a valid building permit. The construction site distribution board is the customer’s part, and before power is switched on it is worth testing it like a permanent installation.

If the construction site is on a territory with passes, arrange access both for the Sadales tīkls crew and for the electrician preparing the board. For how a temporary connection works, see the page temporary connection.

When construction is finished, the temporary connection is replaced by a permanent one, and then putting the site installation into use needs a full set of measurements, while ask the building authority whether an as-built diagram is needed. If the construction site board stays as part of the permanent installation, write that, because then it is measured with the rest.

When an as-built diagram is useful for a warehouse

In warehouses and industrial sites the installation may have been added to in parts: a new ramp, new racking with lighting, a new cold room. After a few years no one knows which line comes from which board. The as-built diagram (an electrical diagram showing what was actually built) sorts this out: boards, circuits, protective devices and cable directions.

The diagram is needed in several cases: after alterations, when the building authority or construction manager asks for as-built documents; before a test report, so that measurements can be tied to particular lines; and for a tenant who wants to know what they are getting. For how the diagram is prepared and what it contains, see the page as-built diagram.

If the site has an old design, attach it: then differences are checked on site, instead of drawing everything from scratch. If there is no design, the scope of work is determined by how many cable routes have to be traced on site.

Price for a warehouse, a connection and a diagram

For measurements and documents for a warehouse or plant the price is set after the site visit: it is determined by the floor area, the number of boards and circuits and lightning protection. For work and documents for Sadales tīkls the price is also set after the site visit, because what matters is whether the incoming supply and board suit the required capacity. The price of an as-built diagram is determined by the building area, the number of boards and whether there is an old design. The site visit is paid: €40.

Prices cover the electrician’s work and the documents. Sadales tīkls and building authority fees are separate.

Questions about these documents

Does the connection fee depend on the distance to the line?

Yes. For a connection up to 100 A and 300 m, the price per ampere in the Sadales tīkls price list depends on the distance. It is €101.36 per A up to 50 m, €132.68 per A up to 150 m and €184.15 per A up to 300 m, checked on 25 November 2026. For a longer distance or larger capacity Sadales tīkls sets the fee after assessing the application.

Who needs an electrical diagram in a warehouse?

The owner and tenant, to know which line comes from which board; the electrician doing the test; and the building authority or construction manager after alterations. Without a diagram a test report is hard to compare with the next one.

How to write an application if the site has a pass system?

Write what has to be stated for a pass, who will meet at the entrance and which premises will be open. We agree the time of the site visit once the pass procedure is known. After you send the form, we contact you within 5 minutes.
