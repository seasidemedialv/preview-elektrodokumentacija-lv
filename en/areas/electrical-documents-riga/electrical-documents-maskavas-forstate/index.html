Shop, warehouse or workshop by the market: what to know before a report

Alongside housing, Maskavas forštate has the market, retail, logistics, cultural and social services. A shop or warehouse installation differs from a flat: there is refrigeration equipment, lighting in large rooms, sometimes a three-phase supply and several distribution boards. A report for such premises covers each board and its circuits, so you first need to know how many there are and where they are.

If you rent the premises, find out whether the lease says who orders the electrical installation testing. If the owner already has an earlier report, a copy of it shows the circuit numbering and what was measured last time.

In your request, write the floor area, the number of distribution boards, whether there is lightning protection and at what times the equipment can be switched off briefly. For how company premises are tested, see electrical installation testing for businesses.

Old wooden house without handover papers

The typical development of Maskavas forštate is pre-war wooden and masonry buildings, courtyard buildings and commercial areas by the market and railway. If a house or its extension was once altered without a building authority case or has never been put into use, the electrical part may need documents on the current condition, such as an as-built diagram, measurements and a report. The building authority says which ones exactly.

Before the site visit, find everything that has survived about the house: the cadastral survey file, old plans, earlier electrical papers. They show which parts of the building are original and which extensions are later, and which part of the installation has to be described.

A concealed works record is not recreated after the work has been covered up; ask the building authority what it accepts instead. In Riga buildings are accepted into use by the municipality’s City Development Department, and that is where you need to find out which document set your case requires. We prepare the electrician’s part after the site visit. More: electrics of an old or never handed over house.

Empty premises after a long break: reconnecting the supply

If a building or shop premises by Maskavas iela have stood without a user for a long time, the electricity connection may have been cut off. The Sadales tīkls reconnection terms depend on how soon after the previous user left the electricity supplier you choose submits the reconnection application, and on whether the load or number of phases stays unchanged.

If you plan more capacity or more phases in the premises than the previous user had, this is no longer a simple reconnection. Then a load increase has to be applied for, and the terms and fees are different.

What the test protocol records in an old building

In dense historic development one address may have a street building, a courtyard building and several users with their own distribution boards. So the protocol says clearly which board and which circuits were measured, what was measured with what and what the results are. If these places are not in the description, it is hard to prove later what the protocol refers to.

In the protocol you will see a list of circuits with their protective devices, insulation resistance, phase-neutral loop impedance, earthing results and a conclusion on compliance. What each line means is explained on the page what a test protocol contains.

The protocol also has the date of measurement and the measuring instrument details, so that it can later be checked what was used and when. If a building has several users, each can have their own part prepared, and a tenant does not receive the results for other premises.

Questions: Electrical papers in Maskavas forštate

How soon after the previous user leaves can the connection be restored without paying for it?

Sadales tīkls restores the connection without a fee if the application is submitted within 30 days from the moment the previous user ended the contract with the supplier. Later a connection fee and a capacity maintenance fee, for no more than 24 months, have to be paid under the Sadales tīkls price list.

Which report may an insurer ask for on shop premises?

The insurer itself chooses the type of document for shop premises. Ask the policy issuer in writing which document they accept: a measurement report, an installation report or a thermal camera test report. Then exactly that can be prepared instead of one document after another.

How is the price of shop measurements set?

The price depends on the floor area, the number of distribution boards and circuits, whether there is lightning protection and whether a thermal camera inspection is needed too. After the site visit, which costs €40, we tell you which documents the premises need and quote the price.
