A report on an old installation in a brick house without a diagram

The typical development of Old Riga is brick buildings from the medieval, Renaissance, Baroque and later periods, now housing hotels, offices and commercial premises. If a building has had several renovations and cables run along old routes in thick walls, it may no longer be known which circuit feeds which room. In this situation what is needed first is not a new report form but a clear picture of what is in the walls and the distribution board.

Anything left from earlier work is useful before the site visit: an old design, a circuit list on the board door, a previous electrician’s invoice describing the work. Even an incomplete paper shortens the time spent tracing cables from the board to the rooms.

In the report the electrician describes the board, protective devices, visible wiring and the non-conformities found, and we tell you about the necessary measurements after the site visit. If needed, a diagram can also be drawn during the visit. For how this work goes and what the report contains, see the page old electrical installation report. If the report lists faults, the electrician fixes them before the next papers; we test and prepare the documents.

Hotel, restaurant or office: who needs the report

In Old Riga premises are used for tourism, hospitality, catering, culture, offices and retail, and people live in the same buildings. A hotel or restaurant with kitchen equipment uses electricity differently from a flat, and there may be several distribution boards. A company should know which premises and boards are its own and which belong to the building owner.

An electrical installation report and a measurement report are different papers. A measurement report fixes figures on a set form, while an installation report is a written assessment with a conclusion on the condition of the installation. For a company about to rent or buy premises in an old house, an installation report can be useful before signing the contract. If the administrator asks specifically for a periodic test report under the regulations currently in force (MK 238, in force until 1 January 2027), say so in your request.

In your request, write whether you need a report on the whole building or only on your own premises, and whether the building administrator can show earlier documents. For what the work contains and how to prepare, see the page electrical installation testing for a business.

Diagrams and drawings after alterations or renovation

If alterations or renovation took place in a building or part of it with a building authority case, a protocol with documents on the electrical installations is attached to the acceptance statement. Point 53 of the construction standard for internal electrical installations, LBN 261-15, mentions that it includes (if the relevant work has been done):

  • a set of drawings and detail drawings corrected under the designer’s supervision (53.1);
  • passports of the installed equipment and certificates of material conformity (53.2);
  • diagrams of the automatic control and electrical switching of the installation (53.5);
  • a description of how to use the systems and safety instructions (53.6).

Checked on 25 November 2026: Cabinet Regulation of 9 June 2015 No. 294 on the Latvian Construction Standard LBN 261-15 “Internal Electrical Installations of Buildings”, point 53, likumi.lv.

In Old Riga, access for transport and deliveries is regulated, pedestrian space dominates and parking is scarce. So send drawings and old diagrams, if there are any, with your request: then they can be compared with the actual board during the site visit, and no second trip is needed for a missing paper. Solar panels on a historic building: approval first

Old Riga is the historic core of the city with protected buildings, narrow streets and courtyards. Riga municipality explained on 28 July 2022 that in the historic centre and its protection zone panel placement has to be approved by the City Development Department. If the building is a cultural monument or located on monument territory, approval is also needed from the National Cultural Heritage Board. Before placing panels the owner has to have a technical survey of the building done to establish the load-bearing capacity of the structures.

The municipality also explained on 23 March 2022 that in Riga a building authority permit is not needed to install panels if no building work is required, such as strengthening load-bearing structures or building an engineering network connection. In Old Riga this does not mean that approval can be skipped: approval of the placement is a separate requirement.

Choose an installer with a BLEEA or LEB generation certificate, because without it Sadales tīkls allows neither installation nor inverter setup. We check the work done and prepare the documents, including the settings protocol. For what these papers include, see the page solar panel papers for Sadales tīkls.

Questions about these documents

What to do if an old building has no electrical diagram at all?

A diagram can be prepared from scratch after the site visit: the electrician traces the circuits from the board to the rooms and draws what is actually there. This is called an as-built diagram, or wiring diagram. The price depends on the building area, the number of boards and how many cable routes have to be traced on site.

Can solar panels be put up in Old Riga without approval?

No. According to Riga municipality’s explanation (28 July 2022), in the historic centre and its protection zone panel placement has to be approved by the City Development Department, and for a cultural monument also by the National Cultural Heritage Board. Ask the department about particular buildings.

What should be prepared for a site visit to a building with several users?

Arrange with the administrator access to all boards and technical rooms, and warn users that power will be switched off briefly during measurements. In your request, write how many users there are and whether the report is needed for the whole building or only one part.
