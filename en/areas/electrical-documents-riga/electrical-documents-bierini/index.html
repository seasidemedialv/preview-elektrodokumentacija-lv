A self-built detached house: what to attach to Part II of the explanatory note

The typical buildings of Bieriņi are detached houses, gardens, low-rise residential buildings and a few new projects. If you are building the house yourself, self-built, that determines which electrical papers are needed. Sub-point 157.7.1 of the Building Construction Regulations provides that if the owner built a second-group residential house themselves, electrical installation test documentation is attached to Part II of the explanatory note to complete the construction works. The table shows the electrician’s part and the papers connected with it:

Document What it shows Who prepares it
Electrical installation test documentation Measurements (insulation, phase-neutral loop, earthing), protocol and diagram of the installation as built The electrician; under LBN 261-15 the measurements are carried out by a certified electrical specialist
Statement on utility readiness That the connection has been built according to the technical conditions The body that issued the technical conditions, for example Sadales tīkls
As-built survey plan Where on the plot the house and the networks are Surveyor

Checked 25 November 2026: Cabinet Regulation No. 529 of 2 September 2014 “Building Construction Regulations”, point 157 and sub-point 157.7.1, likumi.lv. The building authority sets the other documents.

In Riga the municipality’s City Development Department examines this putting into use, and the municipality’s document list also includes concealed works acceptance records. So arrange the electrician before the walls are closed up, not just before applying in BIS.

A sauna or garage in the garden: its own board and circuits

If your garden in Bieriņi has a sauna, garage or outbuilding fed by an underground cable from the house distribution board, it may have its own distribution cabinet. That is a separate board with its own circuits, and it has to be shown separately in the diagram and the measurement report. A sauna has a sauna heater, and the construction standard sets special requirements for such locations.

If the sauna or garage was built with a building authority case, its electrical papers may be part of putting it into use; ask the building authority what exactly it requires. In your request, write how many buildings are in the garden, whether they have their own cabinets and how long the cable is. For these buildings the price is set after the site visit. More: sauna, garage and outbuilding.

Solar panels on a detached house: microgenerator papers

Bieriņi is a green neighbourhood of detached houses in Pārdaugava along the Mārupīte. If you plan solar panels on the house roof or in the garden, it is useful to know the limits first. For Sadales tīkls, a microgenerator is a unit of up to 11.1 kW on a three-phase or 3.7 kW on a single-phase connection (16 A per phase). Before applying, find out the inverter capacity and whether it is on the Sadales tīkls list of recognised inverters.

Sadales tīkls examines an application on e-st.lv within 3 working days if the inverter is on the list, and the assessment fee is €78.66 under the Sadales tīkls price list, checked 25 November 2026. After installation the client submits the confirmation with the inverter settings protocol, signed by a specialist whose certificate covers generating equipment. The person who mounts the panels and sets up the inverter needs the same specialisation, so clarify that before signing a contract with an installer. We do not do the installation; we check what has been done and handle the documents. More: microgenerator connection papers.

If you want to add a battery later, an application to change the microgenerator’s technical parameters is submitted to Sadales tīkls on e-st.lv, and each inverter needs its own settings protocol.

An as-built diagram for a new house while the wires are still visible

In a new detached house, the as-built diagram, or electrical installation diagram, is a drawing from the incoming supply to each circuit: which circuit feeds which rooms, which protective devices there are, where the cables run to the garden buildings. It is part of the test documentation, and every electrician who services the house later works with it.

The diagram is easiest to prepare while the wires are not yet covered: the routes can then be photographed and entered precisely. If the house is already finished, the diagram can also be drawn after a site visit by tracing the circuits from the board. Attach the design to your request if there is one. For this work, see the page as-built diagram.

Questions about these documents

What electrical documentation is required for Part II of the explanatory note?

If the owner built a second-group residential house themselves, sub-point 157.7.1 of the Building Construction Regulations requires electrical installation test documentation. In practice this is a measurement report and protocol on insulation, loop and earthing, and a diagram. The building authority sets the rest of the package.

Does a garden sauna need a separate diagram?

If the sauna has its own distribution cabinet, the diagram has to show it as a separate board with its own circuits and the cable from the house. A separate sheet is not compulsory if everything is in one clear diagram. If the sauna was built with its own building authority case, ask the building authority which papers it requires for it.

What has to be established before a microgenerator is connected to a private house?

The inverter capacity against the limit (11.1 kW on a three-phase or 3.7 kW on a single-phase connection), whether the inverter is on the Sadales tīkls list of recognised inverters and whether the house’s incoming supply and board are suitable. A battery counts as generating equipment, so it too has to be included in the total capacity.
