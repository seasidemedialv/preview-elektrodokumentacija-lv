The building authority, in Riga the City Development Department, asks for electrical installation test documentation when putting the building into use if the owner built the house themselves. Sadales tīkls asks, for a new connection, for a confirmation on e-st.lv that the installation on your side is ready. If there will be solar panels on the roof, a third paper is added: the inverter settings protocol.

A house you built yourself: electrical papers for putting it into use

The typical buildings of Berģi are detached houses, newer private house projects, low-rise buildings and roadside commercial properties. If you are building the new house yourself, one legal provision matters. Section 19.1, paragraph three of the Construction Law allows an owner to build a house of up to 400 m² on their own land, acting as the builder. For such a second-group residential house, the Building Construction Regulations require electrical installation test documentation at acceptance.

In practice this means measurements (insulation, phase-neutral loop, earthing, residual current devices), a report with the results and an electrical installation diagram. Under LBN 261-15, the measurements are carried out by a certified electrical specialist. In Riga, for construction cases opened after 31 December 2019, acceptance can be requested in BIS, and the documents are added to the case there. Arrange the measurements while the board and circuits are still accessible, not the day before applying. Read all about this case on the page electrical installation test documentation for a self-built house.

Checked 25 November 2026: Construction Law, Section 19.1, paragraph three (likumi.lv/ta/id/258572); Building Construction Regulations, sub-points 157.7.1 and 168.1 (likumi.lv/ta/id/269164).

A new connection towards Brīvības gatve: the steps on your side

Berģi is reached via Brīvības gatve and the A2 direction, and in a new project the plot may not yet have an address at first. In that case give the cadastral designation and the nearest junction in your request to Sadales tīkls and to us, so the property can be found. For a permanent connection the Sadales tīkls procedure is this:

  1. Application on e-st.lv If the network does not need rebuilding, Sadales tīkls offers the final costs and a contract within 3 days; if it does, it issues technical requirements.
  2. Installation up to the Sadales tīkls distribution cabinet A qualified electrical specialist or electrical installation company installs the cable to the cabinet and the indoor installation.
  3. Confirmation You submit a confirmation on e-st.lv that the installation is ready. The capacity maintenance fee starts from this moment.
  4. Meter Sadales tīkls gets in touch within 2 working days to install the meter.

The confirmation and the measurement protocol are different documents: you submit the confirmation to Sadales tīkls, while the measurement report is added to the building authority documents. More detail: Sadales tīkls new connection.

Solar panels on the roof of a new house

Berģi is a green eastern suburban neighbourhood near Brīvības gatve and the area around Lake Jugla, with detached houses and private roofs. If solar panels of up to 11.1 kW on a three-phase connection (3.7 kW single-phase) are planned for the house, it is a microgenerator connection with its own Sadales tīkls procedure. The application is assessed for €78.66 under the Sadales tīkls price list, checked 25 November 2026. If you plan panels later, mention it already in the connection request: on a single-phase connection the microgenerator limit is 3.7 kW, on a three-phase connection 11.1 kW.

The inverter settings protocol is signed by a specialist whose certificate covers generating equipment. On 23 March 2022 the Riga municipality explained that no building authority permit is needed to install panels if no construction work, such as reinforcing load-bearing structures, is required. The installer who mounts the panels and sets up the inverter must hold the same kind of certificate. We do not do the mounting, but we check the result and draw up the papers. The steps are described on the page connecting a microgenerator.

As-built diagram: when the building authority also benefits from a drawing

An as-built diagram is an electrical installation diagram that shows how the installation was actually built: incoming supply, distribution boards, protective devices and circuits. Berģi serves housing, roadside commerce, education and suburban services, so one plot may have a house and a small commercial building with a shared incoming supply. The diagram then has to show which board feeds which building.

For acceptance of a building, LBN 261-15, point 53, lists both drawings and switching diagrams and reports on measurements as separate attachments, if the work concerned has been carried out. A measurement report shows the values, but only the diagram lets the examiner understand which circuit they belong to. If the design diagram was changed during construction, the as-built diagram is drawn from the actual state. More on this: as-built diagram for electrics.

Questions about these documents

May an owner build their own house, and what rules then apply?

Yes: Section 19.1, paragraph three of the Construction Law allows an owner to build a house of up to 400 m² on their own land, acting as the builder. At acceptance, the Building Construction Regulations then require electrical installation test documentation. The building authority sets the rest of the document list.

What are the steps for a new permanent connection?

An application on e-st.lv, the Sadales tīkls offer or technical requirements, the installation on your side up to the network cabinet, a confirmation on e-st.lv and meter installation. After the confirmation, Sadales tīkls gets in touch about the meter within 2 working days, and the capacity maintenance fee starts.

What is the diagram for if there are already measurements?

Measurements show the values, but the diagram shows which board and circuit they belong to. For acceptance, LBN 261-15, point 53, lists both diagrams and reports on measurements as separate documents. Without a diagram, the report cannot be compared with the actual installation.
