For a business the main document is the electrical installation test report, and its interval depends on the environment in the premises. Large buildings add lightning protection testing and a diagram, while a new warehouse on a construction site needs a temporary connection.

Plant or warehouse: the test interval depends on the environment

Šķirotava’s functions are logistics, warehouses, manufacturing, wholesale and technical services. In such premises the test interval is not the same for all. Point 56 of the fire safety regulations currently in force (MK 238, in force until 1 January 2027) sets:

  • testing of the electrical installation, earthing and lightning protection once every 10 years in an ordinary environment;
  • once every two years if the property or territory has an explosive environment;
  • once a year if the property or territory has a chemically aggressive environment.

Which environment applies to your premises is determined by the company’s activity and fire safety documentation, and the electrician does not decide it. If the fire safety documentation sets an explosive or chemically aggressive environment for part of the premises, write that in your request, because these zones have a shorter interval than the rest of the building. From 2 January 2027 MK 238 is no longer in force, and the procedure after that had not yet been published on 25 November 2026. The work procedure in business premises is described on the page electrical installation testing for a business.

Together with the periodic test, the regulations also provide for inspecting contact connections with a thermal camera and a separate report on it. In a plant this is planned during a shift, when equipment runs at full load, and insulation measurements at a time when lines can be switched off.

Checked on 25 November 2026: Cabinet Regulation of 19 April 2016 No. 238 “Fire Safety Regulations”, point 56 (likumi.lv/ta/id/281646). In force until 1 January 2027.

Lightning protection for large warehouse and production buildings

The typical development of Šķirotava is railway infrastructure, warehouses, manufacturing, commercial sites and scattered housing. If a building has a lightning protection system, testing it is part of the periodic test, and the result goes in the same report (MK 238 Annex 6) as the insulation, phase-neutral loop, earthing installation and earthing conductor bond. The report also records measuring instrument details and the calibration or verification certificate number.

A large building has several down conductors and earth electrodes in its lightning protection, and each has to be found and measured. If there is a building design or a previous report showing the down conductor layout, attach it to your request and write whether the roof can be reached safely. Without a diagram the down conductors have to be traced on site. What the measurement contains is described on the page lightning protection testing. If a building has no lightning protection, it is not measured in the test; whether the building needs a system is a design question.

A diagram based on the existing boards on a site with several buildings

Access to Šķirotava is via Lubānas iela and Krustpils iela, the railway and the Dienvidu tilts connections, and one company site may have several buildings with separate distribution boards. If the old diagram has not survived or no longer matches after alterations, an as-built diagram (an electrical diagram showing the actual state) can be prepared from the existing boards: from the incoming supply to each board and its circuits. If the buildings are let to different companies, the diagram can mark which board feeds which tenant’s premises, and this later makes it easier to divide testing and responsibility between tenants.

The scope of work is set by the number of distribution boards and circuits and how many cable routes have to be traced between buildings. In your request, write the number of buildings on the site, where the incoming supply is and whether there is any plan with cable routes. The diagram is also useful for periodic testing, because report entries are made according to it. More detail: as-built wiring diagram.

Temporary connection for a new warehouse

Šķirotava has the railway, freight traffic and an industrial environment, and new warehouses may be built on vacant land. For a construction site Sadales tīkls sets up a temporary connection for up to 24 months if there is a valid building permit. The set-up fee is €117.21, or €217.52 with additional installation work, under the Sadales tīkls price list, checked on 25 November 2026.

On your side a construction site distribution board and the electrician’s work to connect it are needed. In your request, write the building permit number or BIS case, the required capacity and how long the connection will be needed. The procedure is described in more detail on the page temporary electricity connection for a construction site. Once the warehouse is built, for the permanent connection you submit a confirmation of installation readiness on e-st.lv, while acceptance of the building in Riga also requires an opinion on the readiness of the engineering network connection.

Questions: Electrical documents in Šķirotava

What is the test interval in a chemically aggressive environment?

Once a year. The fire safety regulations currently in force (MK 238, in force until 1 January 2027) provide for testing once every two years in an explosive environment and once every 10 years for other properties. Which environment applies to your premises is determined by the company’s activity and fire safety documentation.

What has to be entered in a lightning protection test report?

The result of testing the lightning protection system is part of the MK 238 Annex 6 report, which also includes the insulation, phase-neutral loop, earthing installation and earthing conductor continuity bond. The report records measuring instrument details and the calibration or verification certificate number.

Can a diagram for a plant be prepared from the existing distribution boards?

Yes. The as-built diagram is drawn from the incoming supply to each board and its circuits, tracing the cables on site. If there is an old diagram or design, the work is shorter, because it is compared with the actual state. We quote the price after the site visit.
