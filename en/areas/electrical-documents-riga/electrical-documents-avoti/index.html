Each case calls for a different paper. For a flat it is a measurement report, for a buyer or landlord a report on the condition, for a building a report in the form set by the fire safety regulations.

A measurement report for a flat in a street or courtyard wing

The typical buildings of Avoti are pre-war tenements, courtyard wings, renovated masonry houses and small commercial premises on the ground floors. One address may have a street wing and one or more courtyard wings, each with its own stairwell and its own riser. A shop on the ground floor may be connected to the same incoming distribution board as the flats.

A flat measurement report describes the flat’s distribution board and its circuits: insulation resistance, phase-neutral loop resistance, the connection of the protective conductor to earth and the tripping of the residual current device (RCD), if one is installed. The stairwell distribution board and the riser belong to the building and are measured in the building test. For the process and the report, see the page electrical installation testing in a flat.

After the measurements you receive a report with the results for each circuit. If a circuit does not meet the standard, the report shows it, and an electrician puts that circuit right before repeat measurements. The report stays with the flat owner and can be shown to a buyer, a tenant or the building manager.

If the wiring in the flat has not been replaced, mention it in your request. It is then clear in advance that the measurements may reveal weak points of the old installation, and afterwards you will have to decide what to do next.

A report before buying or letting a flat

Alongside housing, Avoti has retail, catering, creative services and an active rental market. A buyer or landlord wants to know the condition of the wiring before signing a contract or before moving a tenant in. The report is ordered by choice, and it can be useful in price negotiations and for knowing what work will need to be done.

In the report the electrician describes the distribution board, protective devices and visible wiring and writes a conclusion; we tell you which measurements are needed after the site visit. Before a purchase, agree with the seller on access to the flat and the board and on the power being switched off briefly during measurements. For how this report is prepared, see the page electrical installation testing before buying a property.

If a bank is financing the purchase or you will let the flat through an agency, ask whether they have their own form or specific questions about the electrics. Then the report can be prepared so that it answers exactly those, and no second document is needed.

Periodic testing of a whole building: preparing access

The fire safety regulations currently in force (Cabinet Regulation No. 238, in force until 1 January 2027) require periodic testing of the electrical installation. Talk to the building manager about testing the common part of an apartment building. What this test includes and which report is drawn up is explained on the page periodic electrical installation testing.

In Avoti, buildings are reached via Aleksandra Čaka iela or Avotu iela, and there is a dense public transport network nearby. For a building with courtyard wings, what happens behind the gate matters more: which entrance leads into each wing, where the incoming distribution board is and who will unlock the cellar. Write this in your request, and the site visit can be planned so that all the wings are seen in one go.

Before you let an electrician into the common board: check the certificate

Avoti has dense development, limited access to courtyards and few parking spaces, so an electrician’s visit is arranged in advance and with the help of a neighbour or the building manager. It is also a good moment to check whether the person who will open the common distribution board and sign the documents holds a suitable certificate.

In its advice Sadales tīkls states that certified specialists can be found in the BIS register of construction specialists, where they can be filtered by field of work. It shows construction practice certificates, while measurement certificates issued by BLEEA or LEB are checked with the issuer. In the register, look at:

  • whether the name and surname match those of the person who will sign the report;
  • what the field of work is, for example managing or designing electrical installation works;
  • whether the certificate is currently valid.

Checked 25 November 2026: the Sadales tīkls advice on how to find a good electrician (sadalestikls.lv) names the BIS register of construction specialists as the database of certified specialists.

Questions: Electrical documents in Avoti

How do you check an electrician’s certificate before ordering?

First ask what type of certificate it is. A construction practice certificate can be found in the register of construction specialists in BIS (bis.gov.lv) by name and surname or number, while a BLEEA or LEB certificate is checked against the issuer’s data. In both cases look at the field of work and the validity.

Does the law require an electrical report for a rented flat?

We cannot support a requirement for a separate report for a rental contract with any point of any regulation. A report can be useful to a landlord, to know the condition of the installation before a tenant moves in, and to a tenant as a clear description of what is in order and what is not. Who orders the test can be agreed in the contract.

What should you write in a request about a flat in a courtyard building?

Write the address and wing (street or courtyard), the floor, whether the gate is locked and who will open it. Attach a photo of the flat’s distribution board and, if you know, when the wiring was replaced. This helps to work out whether a measurement report will be enough or a condition report is needed.
