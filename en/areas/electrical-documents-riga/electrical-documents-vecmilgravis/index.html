If a flat needs more capacity, Sadales tīkls requires the building manager’s written consent, and a site visit shows whether the flat board and circuits can take it. If the building authority asks for papers after alterations, a measurement report is needed and, if it requests, a diagram. For a warehouse or shop a report is provided for by the fire safety regulations currently in force (MK 238, in force until 1 January 2027), and whoever is responsible for the premises orders it.

A flat in an estate building: measurement report after circuit replacement

The typical development of Vecmīlgrāvis is apartment estates, low-rise buildings, port, warehouse and industrial sites. In an estate building a flat renovation may replace everything: the board, circuits and socket positions. If that is what happened in your flat, order measurements while the renovation is not yet finished and the board is accessible. The report shows the insulation resistance and phase-neutral loop result for each circuit.

Such a report is useful if you sell the flat, let it or the building manager wants to know what has been changed. For how the work goes and what the report contains, see the page electrical installation testing in a flat. In your request, write the floor, whether the flat board is in the hallway or stairwell and whether the circuits in the board are labelled.

A flat report does not cover the stairwell board and rising main. If the measurements on the flat circuits are good but the problem is in the feed from the rising main, this is seen at the site visit, and then the decision rests with the building manager. So the report must show from which point the measured part begins.

More capacity for a flat: consent, check, application

If you plan an electric cooker, water heater or heat pump in the flat, you may need more permitted capacity. Sadales tīkls states that a flat owner must attach the building manager’s written consent to a load change application. This requirement is part of the application itself, so ask for the consent in good time.

  1. Find out the current capacity Photograph the main protective device in the flat board or near the meter, and your bill.
  2. Site visit to the flat The electrician assesses whether the flat board and circuits suit the new load and what has to be changed.
  3. Consent from the building manager Ask for written consent stating the desired capacity; it is an attachment to the application.
  4. Application on e-st.lv The application to Sadales tīkls is submitted with the consent attached; the fee follows the Sadales tīkls price list.

Checked on 25 November 2026: Sadales tīkls, “Load changes”; flat owners attach the building manager’s written consent.

Remember the opposite direction too: Sadales tīkls charges no fee for reducing the permitted capacity, but restoring it later is paid. If the capacity of a flat was once reduced, first find out what it is now before adding new appliances.

For the whole process, see the page increasing the permitted capacity.

Stairwell distribution board and rising main: what belongs to the building

Vecmīlgrāvis is a large northern residential and port neighbourhood by the Mīlgrāvis canal, and its typical development is apartment estates. In every such building the boundary between the flat installation and the building installation has to be understood before you order anything. A flat owner can order measurements of their own circuits. Stairwell boards, rising mains and the incoming supply are tested for the whole building, and the manager organises this.

If your work affected a rising main or stairwell board, talk to the building manager before the measurements, not after them. For how testing the building’s shared installation goes, see the page apartment building electrical installation testing.

If you represent the building, state in your request the number of stairwells and floors, whether the electrical room is in the basement and when the last building test was. This helps to understand whether everything can be measured in one visit or several are needed.

Warehouse or shop: a report together with a diagram

The neighbourhood’s functions are housing, port, logistics, manufacturing, trade and services. For a warehouse or shop the test report records measurements circuit by circuit. If the circuits are not labelled and there is no diagram, it is hard to tie the results to particular lines, and at the next test they cannot be compared. So for premises without a diagram it is worth ordering an as-built diagram together with the measurements.

If a shop or warehouse is on the ground floor of an apartment building, the premises may have their own meter and board, but the incoming supply is shared with the building. Then the report has to state that only the circuits of these premises were measured, while the building part is decided by the manager.

In your request, state the area, the number of boards and when circuits may be switched off, because cooling or till equipment may not be possible to switch off at any moment. For what the work contains, see the page electrical installation testing for a business.

If a warehouse has large consumers, such as cold rooms or forklift charging points, testing can be supplemented with a thermal camera. It shows overheated connections in the board under load, and the result is recorded in a separate report.

Questions: Electrical documents in Vecmīlgrāvis

How does a flat owner get consent for a capacity change?

In writing from the building manager, stating the desired capacity. Sadales tīkls requires this consent as an attachment to a flat load change application. If the manager wants to know whether the flat side is suitable, the result of the site visit helps to answer.

Is a diagram added to the measurement report for a warehouse?

The report records measurements circuit by circuit, and without a diagram they are hard to compare at the next test. If the warehouse has no diagram, it can be prepared together with the measurements as an as-built diagram. The price is determined by the number of boards and how many routes have to be traced.

What should be written in the form about the floor and distribution board?

The floor, whether there is a lift, where the flat board and meter are (in the flat or stairwell) and whether the circuits are labelled. Attach a photo of the opened board. After you send the form, we contact you within 5 minutes.
