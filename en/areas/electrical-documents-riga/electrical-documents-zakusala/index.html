Office and media buildings: what testing of premises includes

The typical development of Zaķusala is the television complex, office sites, infrastructure and limited housing. In such buildings the electrical installation consists of the incoming supply, the main distribution board and floor or zone boards that feed offices, server rooms and technical equipment. Testing covers this whole chain, not only the tenant’s premises, unless the client has limited the scope.

An office building has equipment that cannot be switched off while people are working, such as servers, communications equipment, broadcasting or security systems. A line is disconnected for insulation measurement, so these circuits are measured outside working hours or by separate agreement, and some may be left for another time. In your request, write which lines are critical, whether the building has a backup supply and who in the building is responsible for the power supply. If you rent the premises, find out whether the tenant or the building owner orders the testing. The work procedure is explained on the page electrical installation testing for a business.

If a value does not comply, the report notes it with the board and circuit. Fixing it is the electrician’s work, and afterwards the line concerned is measured again so that the report has a compliant result. For the building manager such a report is also useful as a basis for a repair plan, because it shows which zones need attention.

The periodic test report and its validity

Zaķusala’s functions are media, offices, infrastructure, events and property development. In a building that has been working for many years, the question of whether the previous report is still valid matters. Point 525 of the fire safety regulations currently in force (MK 238, in force until 1 January 2027) sets that a report drawn up after 19 April 2016 is valid until the next test. One report covers the following measurements:

Measurement What it shows in an office building
Insulation resistance Whether floor and zone lines have no leakage path
Phase-neutral loop Whether the protection will disconnect a fault on the longest lines too
Earthing installation resistance The condition of the building’s earth electrode
Earthing conductor continuity bond Whether the protective conductor is unbroken between the boards
Lightning protection system The result, if the building has such a system

The test interval is currently once every 10 years, but MK 238 ceases to be in force on 2 January 2027, and the text of the next fire safety regulations had not been published on 25 November 2026. If zones in the building have been altered since the last report, they need new measurements now. The intervals are explained on the page periodic electrical installation testing. In a building with several tenants, one report for the whole building divided into zones can be prepared: each tenant sees their part, and the owner has the full picture.

Checked on 25 November 2026: Cabinet Regulation of 19 April 2016 No. 238 “Fire Safety Regulations”, points 56, 57 and 525 (likumi.lv/ta/id/281646). In force until 1 January 2027.

Thermal camera for distribution boards: a second report, not a substitute

Zaķusala is a long Daugava island with television, office, transport and development areas, and in large buildings the boards work under constant load. A thermal camera shows connections in boards, junction boxes and at protective devices that heat up more than others. This can be seen only during working hours, when current flows through the connections.

The regulations provide for the thermal camera inspection to be done together with the periodic test and recorded in a separate report (MK 238 point 58, Annex 7). It does not replace insulation measurements: a thermal camera does not see damaged insulation in a line, and an insulation tester does not see a loose screw in a board. In practice this means two times at one site: the thermal camera on a working day, measurements in a shutdown window. How it goes: thermal camera inspection of a distribution board. Before the inspection, arrange with the building’s technical staff that board covers can be removed and there is clear access to them.

Questions about these documents

Which measurements does one periodic test report cover?

Insulation resistance, phase-neutral loop impedance, earthing installation resistance, the earthing conductor continuity bond and the lightning protection system, if there is one. The report also records measuring instrument details. The thermal camera inspection has a separate report (MK 238, in force until 1 January 2027).

Does a thermal camera inspection replace insulation measurements?

No. A thermal camera shows overheated connections in boards, but not the condition of line insulation. The fire safety regulations currently in force provide for both checks to be done together and each recorded in its own report. Both jobs are planned at one site but at different times: the thermal camera under load, measurements when lines can be switched off.

How to describe access to distribution boards at an island site?

In your request, state the building and entrance, the pass procedure, the contact of the responsible person in the building and where the main distribution board is. Also write at what times lines can be switched off. That way the site visit can be arranged in one go.
