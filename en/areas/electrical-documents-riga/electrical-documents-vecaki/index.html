Electrical measurements in Vecāķi have to be planned with summer in mind: access via Vecāķu prospekts and parking are then at their busiest, and that is exactly when cafés have their working season.

Circuits have to be dead during measurements, so for a café or guest house it is more convenient to do them before the season. For a house or summer house the timing is more flexible, but summer traffic is worth allowing for there too.

A café or guest house before the season: a report and more capacity

Vecāķi’s functions are housing, hospitality, catering, recreation and seasonal services. For a café or guest house a measurement report shows that the circuits and earthing of the premises are in order, and it can be shown to the premises owner or tenant. If new equipment arrives in the kitchen before the season, it should also be established whether the permitted capacity is enough.

If the connection is suitable and the network does not have to be rebuilt, Sadales tīkls is paid only for connection, €117.21 (0.4 kV) under the Sadales tīkls price list, checked on 25 November 2026. If the network has to be rebuilt, the timing and fee are different. It is worth applying in good time, because before that the electrician has to check whether the board and incoming supply suit the new capacity.

Checked on 25 November 2026: Sadales tīkls, “Load changes” and the price list; connection fee if the network does not have to be rebuilt.

Useful to us in your request:

  • a list of the largest appliances with their power (cookers, ovens, coolers, water heaters);
  • photos of the main protective device and main distribution board;
  • the time when power may be switched off in the premises;
  • whether the terrace or outdoor area has separate circuits.

For what the work contains in business premises, see the page electrical installation testing for a business.

A summer house you plan to live in all year

The typical development of Vecāķi is detached houses, summer houses, low-rise housing, leisure and catering sites. If a summer house is converted into a year-round dwelling, both the load and the paperwork route change. Electric heating or a heat pump needs a different capacity, and if the conversion goes through the building authority, the electrical documents have to match what it requires.

If the summer house has a single-phase incoming supply, more capacity may also mean moving to three phases. The site visit shows whether the incoming supply and board allow this, and it is worth knowing before you buy heating equipment.

If the summer house is not being converted and you simply want to know what condition the installation is in before winter, a measurement report can be useful. For both routes for garden and summer houses, see the page electrical documents for a garden house. In your request, write whether the house has one or three phases and what the main protective device is.

Old wiring in a detached house: a report before deciding

If the wiring in your detached house has not been replaced for decades, or the house has been altered in parts at different times, an electrical installation report helps to understand what can stay and what needs changing. The report describes what was inspected and gives recommendations; the need for measurements is determined after the site visit. It is not a building authority paper, but it is useful when planning renovation, before selling or before letting the house to holidaymakers.

If you let the house to holidaymakers, the report can be useful in a conversation with your insurer. It shows you which circuits are overloaded if several appliances run at once.

If faults are found, the electrician fixes them; we test and prepare the papers. For what the report includes, see the page old electrical installation report. Attach to your request a photo of the board and the old diagram, if there is one.

A seasonal site after a long break without electricity

A seaside café or house may stand without an electricity contract for some time. If the connection has to be restored, Sadales tīkls advises testing a long-untested internal installation first, because it does not connect an unfit one. So plan the testing before the application, not before opening day.

For how reconnection works and what the Sadales tīkls fee depends on, see the page reconnection. Access to Vecāķi is by railway and Vecāķu prospekts; in summer access and parking are busier, so state in the form whether a car can be parked at the property.

If the owner of the property changed during the break, the electricity supplier chosen by the new owner applies to Sadales tīkls for reconnection. Start with testing and then agree with the supplier.

Price for a café, a house and a report

For measurements and documents for a café, guest house or other business premises the price is set after the site visit, because it is determined by the floor area and the number of distribution boards and circuits. Measurements and documentation for a private house cost from €230. An electrical installation report costs from €180. The site visit is paid: €40.

Prices cover the electrician’s work and the documents. Sadales tīkls and building authority fees are separate. After the site visit we tell you which papers are needed and quote the price.

Questions: Electrical measurements in Vecāķi

How fast does Sadales tīkls increase capacity if the connection is suitable?

It depends on the particular connection, so find out the date from Sadales tīkls after applying. If the existing connection is suitable and the network does not have to be rebuilt, the connection fee is €117.21 (0.4 kV) under the price list, checked on 25 November 2026. In the case of reconstruction, expect building work too.

Does a seasonal café need measurements before opening?

A measurement report shows the condition of the installation in the premises, and it is easier to do before the season, while the premises are not yet working. If more than 10 years have passed since the last test (the fire safety regulations currently in force, MK 238, in force until 1 January 2027) or there is no report, start there. If you rent the premises, ask the owner whether there already is a report.

How to plan the site visit around summer traffic?

In the form, give several suitable dates, say whether there is parking at the property and who will open the premises. For a café it is better to choose a day before the season or a time when it is not yet open. After you send the form, we contact you within 5 minutes.
