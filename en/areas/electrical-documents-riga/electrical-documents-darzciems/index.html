For a detached house owner the measurements are a document for the house: for a report on the circuits or papers for putting into use. For a business in a warehouse they are a report on the premises that can be split by distribution board and is priced after the site visit. In both cases insulation measurements have the same minimum limit.

In detached house blocks: from the site visit to the report

The typical buildings of Dārzciems are blocks of detached houses, apartment buildings, warehouses and commercial areas. For a detached house the work starts with the question of why the document is needed. If the house is being put into use, the report has to fit into the building authority set of papers; if it has been lived in for a long time, the report shows the condition of the installation before a sale or major renovation. The order is similar in both cases:

  1. Request Write the address, the floor area, the number of distribution boards and who the document is for; attach a photo of the incoming board.
  2. Site visit The electrician looks at the boards and circuits, says which measurements and documents are needed and gives the price.
  3. Testing Insulation resistance, phase-neutral loop, earthing and testing of residual current devices (RCD) for each circuit.
  4. Report The results with a circuit list and notes on which values do not comply; if something has to be repaired, an electrician does it, and the circuits concerned are then measured again.

If the house is newly built, in Riga it is put into use by the City Development Department of the Riga State City Municipality. For construction cases opened after 31 December 2019, acceptance can be requested in BIS, and the measurement documents are uploaded with the other papers for putting into use. So mention in your request whether the case is in BIS and who is running it.

For what a house test includes in more detail, read the page electrical installation testing in a private house.

A warehouse, car repair shop or shop: premises report and the insulation limit

Dārzciems serves housing, retail, logistics, car repair shops and warehouses. In business premises a three-phase 230/400 V network is below the 500 V limit, so the same insulation standard applies as for a house. Table 2 of Annex 6 to LBN 261-15 provides: in a network up to 500 V insulation is tested with a 500 V test voltage, and the resistance must be greater than 1.0 MΩ. For safety extra-low voltage (SELV, PELV) circuits the test is done at 250 V, and the limit is 0.5 MΩ.

In a car repair shop or warehouse the scope differs, not the limit: more distribution boards, longer lines, equipment that has to be disconnected before measuring. In your request, write which equipment may be switched off and when. How premises are tested is explained on the page electrical installation testing for businesses.

Checked 25 November 2026: LBN 261-15, point 54 and Annex 6, Table 2, minimum insulation resistance values (likumi.lv/ta/id/274674).

Lightning protection for a warehouse or house roof

If a building has a lightning protection system installed, its test is part of the periodic electrical installation testing, and the result is in the same report as insulation and earthing. This is set by the fire safety regulations currently in force (Cabinet Regulation No. 238, in force until 1 January 2027). For a warehouse and commercial building in Dārzciems this means lightning protection does not have to be ordered as separate work if the tests are planned together.

Before the site visit, find out whether there is a building design or diagram showing the earth electrodes and down conductors, and whether they can be reached without a lift. Without a diagram the electrician traces the down conductors on site, which adds time. For what is measured and what the report is like, read the page lightning protection testing.

When a workshop or house needs more permitted capacity

Increasing permitted capacity is a paid Sadales tīkls service requested on e-st.lv. The electrician’s work on your side is to check whether the incoming supply and distribution board match the new capacity, whether the main protective device has to be changed and whether a move to three phases is needed. If the network does not need rebuilding, you pay Sadales tīkls for connecting; ask it about the completion time after applying.

Dārzciems is reached by Dārzciema iela, Lubānas iela and the Dienvidu Bridge corridor, and land use in the neighbourhood is mixed: houses, warehouses and commercial areas lie side by side. In your request, state where the meter and the incoming board are and whether they can be accessed without the owner. What has to be prepared is described on the page increasing electrical capacity.

Questions: Electrical measurements in Dārzciems

What is the minimum insulation resistance allowed in a 400 V network?

In a network up to 500 V, which includes 230/400 V, insulation is tested with a 500 V test voltage, and the resistance must be greater than 1.0 MΩ. This is set by Table 2 of Annex 6 to LBN 261-15. For a network above 500 V the test voltage is 1000 V, and the limit is also 1.0 MΩ.

When does a building need lightning protection testing?

If the building has a lightning protection system, it is tested together with the electrical installation once every 10 years, as the fire safety regulations currently in force (Cabinet Regulation No. 238, in force until 1 January 2027) provide. In an explosive environment the interval is two years, in a chemically aggressive environment one year.

Can warehouse measurements be split by distribution board?

Yes. The results in the report can be arranged by board, and the work can be planned in parts if all equipment cannot be switched off at once. We agree the order at the site visit and give the price after it, because it depends on the number of boards and circuits.
