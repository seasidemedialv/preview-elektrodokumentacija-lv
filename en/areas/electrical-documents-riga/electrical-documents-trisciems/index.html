New connection for a plot: what you must do and what Sadales tīkls does

Trīsciems is made up of detached houses, gardens, low-rise buildings and large natural areas, and if your plot has no electricity yet, the first step is a connection. A new connection is a job for two parties. Sadales tīkls builds the network up to its distribution cabinet and installs the meter. Everything beyond the boundary, that is, the cable to your distribution board and the internal installation, is the customer’s part, prepared by an electrician the customer chooses.

A garden house getting electricity for the first time

If a garden plot has had no electricity so far, the garden house is the first building to get a distribution board. Then it is worth thinking ahead: whether there will later be a dwelling house, sauna or charger on the plot. The board and number of phases are chosen accordingly, and the as-built diagram shows from the start where the cables run and which circuits are where.

If the garden house is later put into use or altered, this diagram and the first measurements make the papers easier. For how electrical documents for a garden house differ from those for a dwelling house, see the page electrical documents for a garden house.

A sauna, garage or shed with its own line

On detached house plots in Trīsciems there may be a sauna, garage or shed next to the house. If they are fed from the house distribution board, the line to them is part of the house installation and is measured with the house. If an outbuilding has its own board, the measurement report describes it as a separate part with its own circuits.

A sauna has a damp environment and a heater, so the test pays special attention to the residual current device (RCD) and its operation. In a garage or shed, earthing and cable condition matter, especially if the cable runs underground. For what outbuilding papers contain, see the page electrical documents for a sauna, garage or outbuilding. In your request, write how far the building is from the house and whether the cable is laid underground.

Houses you built yourself: what has to be measured

If a second-group residential house has been built by the owner, electrical installation test documentation is needed for acceptance into use (Building Construction Regulations, points 157.7.1 and 168.1). This applies to a self-built house, not to every detached house. If a construction company with a construction manager built the house, the electrical documents are part of its as-built documentation, and ask it for them first.

In a self-built house the whole installation is measured:

  • insulation resistance for each circuit before it is switched into use;
  • phase-neutral loop impedance, to see whether the circuit breakers will disconnect a fault;
  • earthing resistance and the protective conductor bond to the parts to be earthed;
  • operation of residual current devices.

It is worth ordering an as-built diagram together with the measurements to show what was actually built, because for a self-built house the design diagram and reality may differ. For the full list, see the page electrical documents for a self-built house. In Riga, putting into use is handled by the municipality’s City Development Department, and in cases started after 31 December 2019 the application can be submitted in BIS.

Questions about these documents

In what order is a new connection for a garden plot arranged?

An application on e-st.lv, then Sadales tīkls builds the network up to the boundary, after that the electrician builds the cable to your distribution board and the internal installation. When that is ready, you submit a confirmation on e-st.lv, and Sadales tīkls makes contact within 2 working days about installing the meter.

Does a shed with electricity need a separate report?

If the shed is fed from the house distribution board, its line is measured with the house and entered in the same report. If it has its own board, the report describes it as a separate part. A separate report can be prepared if someone asks for it, for example a buyer.

What to measure in a house the owner built?

The whole installation: insulation resistance, phase-neutral loop impedance, earthing and the protective conductor bond, and operation of residual current devices. Together with the as-built diagram this makes up the electrical installation test documentation that the building authority requires for a self-built house.
