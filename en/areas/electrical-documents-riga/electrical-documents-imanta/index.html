Flat in a panel building: a report after rewiring

The typical development of Imanta is extensive apartment estates, detached house areas, shopping centres and new projects. In a panel building the wiring runs through channels in the walls and ceilings, and when it is replaced, the new circuits may take different routes from the old ones. After such work the report shows that each circuit meets the standard, and the diagram shows where the circuits run.

In a flat the measurements cover insulation resistance, phase-neutral loop impedance, the protective conductor connection and the tripping of the residual current devices. If some of the old wiring has been left in place, write this in your request: it can be looked at separately during the measurements, and it will be clear whether to keep it.

If the work affected the riser or the stairwell distribution board, it is no longer a matter for your flat alone, and you should talk to the building manager first. For how a flat is tested, see electrical installation testing in a flat.

A detached house switches to three phases: what the building standard says

Imanta is a large residential neighbourhood in the west of the left bank of the Daugava, and in its detached house areas a heat pump, electric heating or an electric car may need more capacity than a single-phase supply provides. Sub-point 8.3 of the building standard for internal electrical installations, LBN 261-15, states that in residential buildings an installation with a main protective device above 32 A is connected to the system operator’s network only as a three-phase installation.

Checked on 25 November 2026: sub-point 8.3 of LBN 261-15 (Cabinet Regulation of 9 June 2015 No. 294), likumi.lv.

  1. Capacity needed Make a list of appliances with their power in kW; the main protective device you need follows from it.
  2. The 32 A limit If the main protective device is above 32 A, a residential building needs three phases.
  3. Application to Sadales tīkls A load change is applied for at e-st.lv; Sadales tīkls assesses whether the existing connection is suitable.
  4. Incoming supply and distribution board The electrician rebuilds the incoming supply and the distribution board for three phases and spreads the circuits across the phases.
  5. Confirmation Once the installation is ready, the customer submits the confirmation at e-st.lv and Sadales tīkls gets in touch to agree on connecting it.

We check the incoming supply and the distribution board before and after the rebuild and prepare the electrician’s papers. For this route, see from one phase to three.

Self-built house in a detached house area: earthing and papers for putting into use

If you built a detached house in Imanta yourself, the Building Construction Regulations require electrical installation test documentation for acceptance into use. It includes measurements showing that the installation is safe: insulation resistance, the phase-neutral loop and earthing resistance. A detached house is earthed through its own earth electrode, and its resistance is measured on site.

If there is a garage or sauna next to the house with a separate cable from the house distribution board, it is also part of the installation, and measurements are taken there too. Write this in your request, so that no building is left unexamined during the site visit.

Before the measurements it helps to know where the earth electrode was installed and whether it has a test point. If a builder installed the electrode, ask for photos or a description. In Riga the application for putting into use is submitted in BIS if the case was opened after 31 December 2019. For the other papers needed, see self-built house: electrical documents for putting into use.

Confirmation at e-st.lv: when to submit it

For a new connection or a capacity increase, Sadales tīkls requires the customer to submit a confirmation at e-st.lv that their internal installation has been built and is ready to receive voltage. Sadales tīkls states that only a qualified electrical specialist or an electrical installation company may build this installation.

If you submit the confirmation after switching to three phases, first make sure that all three phases are connected in the distribution board and the circuits are spread so that the load on each phase is similar. Measurements and an inspection of the board before submitting show this.

Submit the confirmation only when everything really is ready. Once the confirmation for a new connection has been submitted, the monthly capacity maintenance fee starts, even if the electricity has not yet been switched on. The confirmation is not the same as a measurement protocol; they are different documents. More: confirmation to Sadales tīkls.

Questions: Electrical measurements in Imanta

When does a supply above 32 A need three phases in a residential building?

In every case where the main protective device is above 32 A. Sub-point 8.3 of LBN 261-15 states that in residential buildings such an installation is connected to the system operator’s network only as a three-phase installation. Up to and including 32 A this sub-point does not require three phases.

Who has to submit the confirmation of readiness at e-st.lv?

The confirmation is submitted by the Sadales tīkls customer, that is, the property owner or the user of the connection, once the internal installation has been built. A qualified electrical specialist or an electrical installation company prepares the installation. An electrician can help to prepare the information, but the confirmation is the customer’s statement to Sadales tīkls.

Does a private house in Imanta need an earthing measurement for putting into use?

If a house is being put into use, sub-point 53.4 of LBN 261-15 also mentions reports on testing the resistance of earthing loops, if the relevant work has been carried out. For a self-built house the Building Construction Regulations require electrical installation test documentation, and the earthing measurement is part of it.
