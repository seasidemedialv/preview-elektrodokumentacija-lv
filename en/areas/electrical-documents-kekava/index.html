The Ķekava Municipality list: which measurements have to be submitted

Ķekava has detached-house areas, newer residential projects, a local centre and commercial development along the main roads. For owners of detached houses building for their own needs without construction supervision, the Ķekava Municipality document list names the electrical part precisely, referring to sub-point 53.4 of LBN 261-15.

The list includes electrical installation test documentation issued by a person with a certificate in the relevant field, containing reports on:

  • conductor and cable resistance testing;
  • phase-neutral loop measurements;
  • earthing loop resistance;
  • bonding resistance between earth electrodes and the parts to be earthed.

All four are measured during one site visit and go into one protocol with a circuit table. In Ķekava Municipality documents are submitted only in BIS, so receive the protocol electronically. What this looks like in a case is described on the page electrical installation documents for the building authority.

Building yourself in one of the municipality’s villages: concealed works records in time

Ķekava’s main connections are the A7 (Via Baltica) and the Riga bypass, and the municipality has a wide network of villages. Whichever village the house is in, the building authority and list are the same. What differs is only how far the construction has progressed by the time you remember the papers.

Sub-point 53.3 of LBN 261-15 states that concealed works acceptance records on the installation of electrical equipment are attached to the acceptance statement, if such works have been carried out. Concealed works are cables in walls, floors or under plaster that are no longer visible after finishing. A concealed works record is not recreated once the work has been covered up; ask the building authority what it accepts instead.

Production facility, shop or logistics premises: what to prepare for putting into use

Ķekava’s functions are a municipal centre, production and the food industry, retail, logistics and commuting to Riga. If a business alters premises under a building authority case, the electrical part is the same as for a house, only larger: measurements by distribution board, diagrams and concealed works records if cables are hidden. If the premises will have powerful three-phase equipment, attach a list of it: it shows whether the existing connection is sufficient.

In a food production facility or a warehouse with freezers, say which equipment must not be switched off so that the measurements can be planned in parts. If you rent the premises, agree with the owner in whose name the documents will be submitted to the building authority. More: putting commercial premises into use.

A solar station on a business roof: above 11.1 kW, a different route

Production and logistics buildings in Ķekava can have large roofs, and a business may want a station bigger than a microgenerator. Sadales tīkls defines a microgenerator as up to 11.1 kW on three phases. Above that it is a power station with a separate process: Sadales tīkls issues technical requirements and a contract, and a building design in BIS is needed if a new connection has to be installed or the load changed. If the station does not exceed 11.1 kW, the microgenerator route with an e-st.lv application and an inverter settings protocol suits the business.

For a power station above 11.1 kW, Sadales tīkls accepts only a regulated-sphere certificate “Elektroietaišu izbūves darbu vadīšana” (managing electrical installation construction works) or “Elektroietaišu izbūves darbu būvuzraudzība” (supervising electrical installation construction works) with a generating equipment specialisation. A State Environmental Service permit is needed only from 500 kW. A specialist with this certificate installs the station and signs the test documents; we do not mount the station, but we prepare the measurements and documents on the property side for the business building. The process is described on the page connecting a business solar power station.

Questions: Electrical documents in Ķekava

Which measurements have to be included in Ķekava Municipality electrical documentation?

The Ķekava Municipality list has four reports under sub-point 53.4 of LBN 261-15: conductor and cable resistance, phase-neutral loop, earthing loop resistance and bonding resistance between earth electrodes and the parts to be earthed. They are issued by a person with a certificate in the relevant field.

Can the Sadales tīkls report and the electrician’s report be confused?

They are different papers. The report on utility network readiness comes from the issuer of technical conditions, for example Sadales tīkls, and concerns the connection. The electrician’s protocol describes the house’s internal installation. The Ķekava Municipality list requires both.

What is needed if panels on a business roof exceed 11.1 kW?

Then it is no longer a microgenerator connection but a power station connection, and a building design via BIS is required if a new connection or a load change is needed. Sadales tīkls accepts only a regulated-sphere certificate with a generation specialisation, and from 500 kW a State Environmental Service permit is also needed.
