The charger is installed by an installer; we test the existing electrical installation, measure the new circuit and prepare the papers. If capacity has to be increased, we prepare the customer-side documents for Sadales tīkls.

What is checked before a charger is connected

  • The connection capacity: the current of the main protective device and the number of phases.
  • The distribution board: whether it has room for a new circuit and a residual current device (RCD).
  • The distance from the board to the charger location and the cable route.
  • The other loads in the house: heating, heat pump, cooker, sauna.
  • Earthing and the condition of the existing electrical installation.

Sadales tīkls writes in its advice that a certified electrician checks the existing electrical installation, chooses the cable and protection and puts the charger into use. We do the testing and document part. If the charger will be outdoors or in a garage, the site visit also looks at where the cable will run and how it will be protected.

Does an application have to be submitted to Sadales tīkls

If the capacity of the existing connection is enough, no application to Sadales tīkls is needed: the charger is an ordinary load. Sadales tīkls states that a typical private house connection is three-phase 20–25 A, that is 13.8–17.3 kW, and a smart charger with dynamic load management usually makes it possible to manage without increasing capacity.

Sadales tīkls advice “Electric car charging at a private house”, sadalestikls.lv/lv/padoms/elektroauto-uzlade-privatmaja (published on 7 September 2026). Checked on 25 November 2026.

If capacity does have to be increased

A capacity increase is requested on e-st.lv. If the network does not have to be rebuilt, only the connection is charged: €117.21 (0.4 kV) according to the Sadales tīkls price list, checked on 25 November 2026. For a capacity increase, a confirmation that the customer’s electrical installation is ready is also submitted on e-st.lv. After applying, ask Sadales tīkls for the timeframe for your connection. More: increasing electrical capacity.

Sadales tīkls, sadalestikls.lv/lv/slodzes-izmainas and /lv/cenradis. Checked on 25 November 2026.

Papers after the charger is installed

After installation, the insulation resistance and phase-neutral loop of the new circuit can be measured and the residual current device checked, a measurement report prepared and the electrical installation diagram added to. You then have a document showing that the circuit has been tested and a diagram that matches what is in the distribution board. The documents are prepared and signed by a certified electrical specialist.

If you are still choosing a charger or looking for someone to install it, see electric car charger installation.

A charger in an apartment building

If the charger is connected to the flat’s meter and capacity has to be increased, Sadales tīkls requires the building manager’s written consent to be attached. A cable route through a stairwell or courtyard also affects the building’s common property, so talk to the building manager first.

What to prepare

  • The address and whether it is a private house or a flat.
  • The charger model and power, if already chosen.
  • A photo of the distribution board and the main protective device (the circuit breaker next to the meter).
  • Where the charger is planned and how far it is from the distribution board.
  • If you already have an installer’s offer, a copy of it.

Questions about these documents

Do you install electric car chargers?

No. Installation is done by an installer; we test the existing electrical installation, measure the new circuit and prepare the documents.

Does a home charger need permission from Sadales tīkls?

If the existing capacity is enough, no application to Sadales tīkls is needed. If capacity has to be increased, it is requested on e-st.lv.

What is dynamic load management?

It is a charger function that reduces the charging current when other large loads are switched on in the house, so that the total current does not exceed the main protective device. Sadales tīkls states that such a charger usually makes it possible to manage without increasing capacity.

Can a charger be connected to a single-phase connection?

That depends on the charger power and the other load. After the site visit we tell you whether the existing connection is enough or whether you need to switch to 3 phases, see 1 phase to 3 phases.

Can the check be done before the charger is bought?

Yes, and that is the most logical moment: then you know what charger power will suit and whether a capacity increase is needed.
