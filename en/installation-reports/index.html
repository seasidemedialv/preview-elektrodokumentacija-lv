Which page for which situation

Each situation has its own page: who asks for the report, what you need to prepare and what is looked at first during the site visit.

Before buying a house: the buyer wants to know what they are buying before signing the contract. It also suits taking over a new flat from a developer.

For insurance: after damage, the insurer asks for an electrician’s written assessment of what has been damaged.

For a bank: for the loan file, the bank asks for a document on the condition of the electrical installation.

After a fire and after flooding: you need to understand which circuits can be switched on and which have to be replaced.

Old wiring: an inherited or old house, aluminium conductors, no earthing.

VUGD requirements: periodic testing under the fire safety regulations currently in force.

Electric car charger connection: whether the existing electrical installation and capacity suit a charger.

Report, protocol or test report: what is the difference

These words are often confused, but each document has its own source and its own place. The name is chosen according to who is asking for the document.

Document Where it is laid down When it is needed
Test report (insulation, phase-neutral loop, earthing, lightning protection) Regulation No. 238, point 57, form in Annex 6 Periodic testing
Contact connection test report (thermal camera) Regulation No. 238, point 58, Annex 7 Together with periodic testing
Protocol with documents for the acceptance statement LBN 261-15 point 53 Putting a building into use
Approved reports on measurements LBN 261-15 sub-point 53.4 Putting a building into use
Inverter settings protocol Sadales tīkls requirements for a microgenerator Solar panels, battery
Electrical installation report on its condition An electrician’s assessment when no form is prescribed A purchase, a bank, after damage, old wiring

Cabinet Regulation No. 238 “Fire Safety Regulations” (in force until 1 January 2027), points 57–58; LBN 261-15 point 53; the Sadales tīkls page on connecting a microgenerator. Checked on 25 November 2026.

If a bank, a buyer or another party asks for the document, ask what it has to contain and write it in the form. If measurements are also needed, they can be added to the report.

What is in the report

  • The property address, the date and what was inspected: distribution boards, circuits, rooms.
  • The condition found: protective devices, type of conductors, whether there is earthing and a residual current device (RCD).
  • Measurement results, if measurements were taken.
  • The defects found and what has to be put right before the installation can be used or other documents prepared.
  • The specialist’s signature. The documents are prepared and signed by a certified electrical specialist.

The report describes the condition on the day of the site visit. If something is changed in the electrical installation afterwards, for example, circuits are added, the distribution board is replaced or defects are put right, the earlier report no longer describes what is there. Then ask for a new site visit or measurements after the changes.

When a report is not the right document

A report does not replace documents that have their own name and content in the regulations. If a house has to be put into use, the building authority needs the electrical installation test documentation and the documents named in LBN 261-15 point 53: see putting a house into use: electrics. If periodic testing is due, a test report under Annex 6 of Regulation No. 238 is needed. If the connection changes, a confirmation is submitted to Sadales tīkls on e-st.lv.

If you do not know which of these you need, write in the form who is asking for the document and what it is called in the letter or requirements. After the site visit we tell you which documents are needed and quote the price.

Questions: Electrical installation report

Why order a report when buying a house?

The buyer orders the report themselves to understand what they are buying. It can also be useful in talks with the seller.

How does a report differ from a test report?

The fire safety regulations currently in force (Cabinet Regulation No. 238) prescribe a form with measurement results for the test report. A report is an electrician’s assessment of the condition when no particular form is required.

Can a report be written from photos?

No. A report describes the particular electrical installation, so a site visit is needed. Photos help to prepare but do not replace the inspection.

Will the report also include measurements?

If whoever is asking for the report needs them, or if the condition cannot be assessed without them. We tell you that after the site visit.

What if the report shows defects?

An electrician puts the defects right. We do not carry out repairs, but after the correction we can take measurements and prepare the documents.
