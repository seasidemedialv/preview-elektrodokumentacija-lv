Electrical installation testing after a fire

Electrical installation testing after a fire answers the first question after the fire has been put out: which electrical circuits may be switched on and which must stay off until repaired.

Heat and smoke can damage conductors and connections even where no flames were seen. After the site visit you receive an electrical installation report with the condition found.

Before switching on

If the distribution board, conductors or sockets were in fire, heat or water from extinguishing, do not switch all circuits on straight away. After the site visit it will be possible to say which circuits are untouched and which must stay off. The fire safety regulations currently in force (Cabinet Regulation No. 238) require the electrical installation to be kept in working order and operated according to the installation rules, and point 52 of LBN 261-15 provides for electrical equipment to be tested after significant changes.

Cabinet Regulation No. 238 “Fire Safety Regulations” (in force until 1 January 2027), point 54; LBN 261-15 point 52; likumi.lv. Checked on 25 November 2026.

A fire in a flat: the installation of the flat and of the building

In a flat, the electrical installation is tested from the flat’s distribution board to the sockets and light fittings. If fire or smoke affected the stairwell, rising mains or the stairwell distribution board, that is part of the building’s common property, and its testing has to be discussed with the building manager. Write in the form which rooms are affected and, if you know, whether the damage also reached the stairwell.

What a report after a fire shows

  1. Which circuits and distribution boards were affected by fire, heat or extinguishing water.
  2. Which circuits are untouched and which must stay off until repaired.
  3. Measurement results, for example, insulation resistance, if measurements can be taken.
  4. What has to be replaced before the electrical installation can be used.

If a third party asks for the report, attach its questions to the form. The documents are prepared and signed by a certified electrical specialist.

If the electricity was off for a long time

If the electricity supply to the property was interrupted for a long time after the fire, Sadales tīkls recommends choosing a certified electrician and checking the internal electrical installation before reconnection. If it is not fit for use, Sadales tīkls cannot restore the connection. For the reconnection procedure, see restoring an electricity connection.

Sadales tīkls, sadalestikls.lv/lv/piesleguma-atjaunosana. Checked on 25 November 2026.

After repairs: measurements and a test report

We do not carry out repairs. An electrician replaces or repairs the damaged circuits and distribution board, for example, electrician’s repair work after damage. After that the insulation resistance, phase-neutral loop and earthing can be measured and a test report prepared showing the condition after the repair.

Once the electrical installation is working under load again, the connections in the distribution board and junction boxes can also be checked with a thermal camera. Point 58 of Regulation No. 238 provides for such an inspection together with the periodic test, and a report under Annex 7 is drawn up on it. Thermal camera inspection of a distribution board: price after the site visit, because it depends on the number of boards and whether it is done together with measurements.

What to prepare for the site visit

  • The address and which rooms the fire affected.
  • Which circuits are currently switched off and whether the meter is sealed or disconnected.
  • Photos of the distribution board and damaged places before cleaning is started.
  • If the building manager or another party asks for the document, its requirements.

Questions about these documents

Can the circuits that look untouched be switched on?

A circuit that looks untouched may run through a damaged spot or distribution board. It is safer to inspect the board first and then decide which circuits to switch on.

After a fire in a flat, does the neighbours’ electrical installation also have to be tested?

If the damage affected common parts, such as rising mains or the stairwell distribution board, it has to be discussed with the building manager. Testing a neighbour’s flat is ordered by its owner.

Will the report establish the cause of the fire?

The report describes the condition of the electrical installation. The cause of a fire is established by the institutions responsible for that; if a party asks about the electrical installation, write its questions in the form.

Is a new electrical installation diagram needed after repairs?

If circuits or the distribution board change during the repair, the old diagram no longer matches what is there. It is then worth drawing a new as-built diagram; we quote its price after the site visit.

When can testing be requested after a fire?

When the property can be entered safely and the distribution board accessed. After you send the form, we contact you within 5 minutes and agree on the site visit.
