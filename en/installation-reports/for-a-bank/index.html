Before ordering, find out exactly which document the bank will accept: sometimes an existing test report is enough, sometimes a new report or measurements are needed. We visit the property and prepare what the bank asks for.

Why a bank asks for an electrical installation paper

A bank may ask for documents on the technical condition of the collateral, for example, if the house is old or has not yet been put into use. Which papers and with what content the bank requires is decided by the bank itself, so the only reliable source is the bank’s letter or the adviser’s list. An electrical installation report can be useful even if the bank does not require it: you will know whether money will have to be put into the electrics after the purchase.

Perhaps an existing test report is enough

If the electrical installation was tested recently, ask the seller or building manager for the test report. The fire safety regulations currently in force (Cabinet Regulation No. 238, in force until 1 January 2027) provide for testing once every 10 years and a report under Annex 6 with insulation resistance, phase-neutral loop, earthing and lightning protection results. Show this report to the bank and ask whether it will do.

Cabinet Regulation No. 238 “Fire Safety Regulations”, points 56–57 and Annex 6, likumi.lv. Checked on 25 November 2026.

What to find out at the bank before ordering

  • The name of the document: report, test report or measurement protocol.
  • Whether measurements are needed or an inspection is enough.
  • Whether the document has to carry the signature and certificate number of a certified specialist.
  • Whether the bank accepts the document electronically or on paper.
  • The date by which the document is needed.

Copy the bank’s requirement into the form. That way we know before the site visit what to prepare.

How a report for a bank is prepared

  1. Request In the form, state the address, the bank’s requirement and the deadline.
  2. Site visit The electrician looks at the distribution board, circuits, protective devices and earthing.
  3. Measurements If the bank requires measurements, they are taken when the electrical installation is ready for them; if defects are found, they have to be put right first.
  4. Document The report or test report is prepared with the condition found. The documents are prepared and signed by a certified electrical specialist.

If the report shows defects

A report may show that the distribution board is outdated, there are no residual current devices (RCD) or the insulation resistance is too low. That does not mean the deal has to be called off, but the bank and you need to know that there will be work after the purchase. What to do about it is decided by the bank and the parties to the deal.

An electrician puts the defects right. We do not carry out repairs, but after the repair the electrical installation can be measured and a test report prepared. Point 52 of LBN 261-15 provides for electrical equipment to be tested after significant changes too, so after repairs a new test report is a logical document to show the bank. What insulation measurements involve is explained on the page insulation resistance measurements.

LBN 261-15 (Cabinet Regulation No. 294) point 52, likumi.lv. Checked on 25 November 2026.

If the bank also asks for putting-into-use papers

If the loan is for a new build or a house whose construction is not finished, the bank may ask for the house to be put into use. Then a different set of documents is needed: the documents named in LBN 261-15 point 53 are attached to the acceptance statement if the relevant work has been done, and for a self-built house Regulation No. 529 requires electrical installation test documentation. This is covered on the page putting a house into use: electrics.

Questions about these documents

Does a bank necessarily require electrical installation testing?

That is decided by the particular bank. A bank may ask for such a document; ask the bank in writing which one exactly is needed.

Will a bank accept a test report drawn up several years ago?

The bank decides that. The fire safety regulations currently in force provide for testing once every 10 years, but whether the bank will accept an older report or ask for a new one is a question for the bank.

Who pays for testing for the bank, the buyer or the seller?

The buyer and seller agree on that. State in the form who is ordering the work.

Can a report be prepared without the seller present?

Access to the property and the distribution board is needed, so the seller or their representative has to open the premises during the site visit.

Is the report also useful to the buyer?

Yes, the same document shows you the condition too. For testing in the buyer’s interest, see testing before buying a house.
