Electrical installation testing for VUGD requirements - a report under the fire safety regulations

Electrical installation testing for State Fire and Rescue Service (VUGD) requirements is the test set by the fire safety regulations currently in force (Cabinet Regulation No. 238): once every 10 years the insulation, phase-neutral loop, earthing and lightning protection have to be tested, and the results recorded in a test report.

These regulations are in force until 1 January 2027. We take the measurements and prepare the report for a flat, house or business premises.

What the fire safety regulations require of an electrical installation

Point 54 of Regulation No. 238 requires the electrical installation, including the earthing and lightning protection equipment, to match the solution in the construction intention documentation, to be kept in working order and to be operated in line with legislation and the manufacturer’s requirements. Point 56 sets the testing frequency, point 57 the report, point 58 the contact check with a thermal camera.

The regulations apply to properties regardless of the form of ownership: a flat, private house or business premises. The regulations do not name a construction specialist certificate for whoever carries out the test. However, attached to the Annex 6 report are a copy of the electrical safety group card of the person doing the work, a copy of the measuring instrument’s calibration or verification certificate and the electrical installation diagram (points 1.1–1.3 of the notes to the annex).

Cabinet Regulation No. 238 “Fire Safety Regulations” of 19 April 2016, points 54–58, likumi.lv. In force until 1 January 2027. Checked on 25 November 2026.

How often testing is needed

Environment in the property or territory Testing
Ordinary environment (flats, houses, offices, shops) Once every 10 years
Explosive environment Once every two years
Chemically aggressive environment Once a year

If the date of the last report is unknown, it is worth testing now and keeping the report for next time.

What is in the test report and the thermal camera report

  1. Test report (Annex 6) The results of testing the electrical installation’s insulation resistance, the phase-neutral loop impedance, the earthing resistance, the earth conductor continuity and the lightning protection system. The report also has the measuring instrument data and calibration certificate number.
  2. Contact connection test report (Annex 7) The quality of connections in junction boxes, the distribution board and where protective devices are installed is checked with a thermal camera, together with the tests under point 56.

The test report is based on the electrical installation diagram. If there is no diagram, one can be drawn from what is there: as-built diagram.

What will change after 1 January 2027

Regulation No. 238 ceases to be in force on 2 January 2027. As at 25 November 2026 the new fire safety regulations have not yet been published, so it cannot be said whether the 10-year period and the same report form will remain. If you test now, the report is prepared under the regulations currently in force.

Flat, house or business

  • Flat: the flat’s electrical installation is tested from the flat’s distribution board. Testing of the building’s common property is organised by the building manager: testing in an apartment building.
  • Private house: the whole electrical installation, earthing and, if there is one, lightning protection: periodic testing.
  • Business: offices, shops, warehouses, production premises: testing for businesses.

What to prepare for the test

  • The previous test report, if there is one: it shows when the last test was.
  • The electrical installation diagram or electrical design.
  • Information on lightning protection: whether there is any and where the earth electrodes are.
  • Access to all distribution boards and rooms; in a business, agree which equipment may be switched off briefly during the test.
  • If a tenant or building manager asks for the report, their requirements.

Questions about these documents

Does the VUGD carry out electrical installation testing itself?

No, the test is ordered by the property owner or building manager. The regulations lay down that the test has to be carried out and a report drawn up on the results.

Does a flat also need the 10-year test?

The fire safety regulations currently in force provide for testing once every 10 years regardless of the type of property, so for a flat too. The regulations are in force until 1 January 2027.

Is a thermal camera inspection compulsory?

Point 58 of Regulation No. 238 provides for checking contact connections with a thermal camera together with the tests under point 56, and a report under Annex 7 is drawn up on it.

Does lightning protection also have to be tested?

Yes, if there is any. Point 56 of Regulation No. 238 includes earthing and lightning protection equipment in the test, and the report contains the results of the lightning protection system test. More: lightning protection testing.

What should I do if the measurements do not meet requirements?

An electrician puts the defects right, and the measurements are then repeated. We do not carry out repairs, but after the repair we prepare the report.
