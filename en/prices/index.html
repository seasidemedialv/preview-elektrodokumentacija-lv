What changes the price

  • Type and floor area of the property: flat, private house, sauna, commercial premises.
  • Number of distribution boards and electrical circuits.
  • Which documents are needed: measurement report, as-built diagram, installation report or papers for Sadales tīkls.
  • Whether there is earlier documentation, such as a design or an old diagram.
  • Distance from Riga.

That is why the price can differ for items with the same name. A two-room flat with one distribution board and new wiring means less work than a house with several distribution boards, no diagram at all and cable routes that have to be traced on site.

Building authority fees and procedure are set by the particular municipality. In Riga, putting a building into use is handled by the City Development Department of the Riga State City Municipality; in the Riga region, by the building authority of the area concerned. For the fee, ask the building authority to which you submit the documents.

Why the site visit is paid

The scope of electrical installation documents cannot be determined from a phone conversation. The electrician has to see the distribution board, work out how many circuits there are, whether there is earthing, whether the old diagram matches what is on site and which document is needed at all. That involves a call-out and work, so the site visit costs €40.

After the site visit we tell you which documents are needed and quote the price. If defects are found that must be put right before the documents are prepared, we tell you that too.

Questions: Electrical installation testing prices

Are Sadales tīkls fees included in the price?

No. Prices cover the electrician’s work and the documents. Sadales tīkls and building authority fees are separate, and you pay them according to the invoice from Sadales tīkls or the building authority.

Why is there no price for the as-built diagram and the Sadales tīkls papers?

The scope of this work varies widely: one house has an old diagram, another has nothing, and the routes have to be traced room by room. That is why we quote the price after the site visit, when it is clear what has to be done.

Is the price in the Riga region the same as in Riga?

Distance from Riga is one of the factors that change the price. Give the address in the form, and after the site visit we quote the price for the particular property.

How much does the electrical part of putting a house into use cost?

Measurements and documentation for putting a private house into use: from €230. If an as-built diagram is also needed, we quote its price after the site visit. Building authority fees are separate.

Can I order just one measurement, for example earthing?

Yes. We quote the price of a single measurement after the site visit, because the scope can only be seen on site. If you need a report for a flat, there is also a package price from €170.
